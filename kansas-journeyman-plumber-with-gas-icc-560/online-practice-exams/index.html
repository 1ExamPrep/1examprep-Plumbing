Exam Details

The 560 Kansas Standard Journeyman Plumber with Gas examination is administered through the International Code Council contractor and trade examination program. ICC identifies the examination as consisting of 70 multiple-choice questions with a 3-hour time limit.

The exam combines plumbing-code knowledge with fuel gas requirements. The current ICC examination bulletin for this exam identifies the following content distribution:

  • Plumbing Fixtures and Terminology: 4%
  • Plumbing Fundamentals: 14%
  • Traps, Cleanouts and Interceptors: 4%
  • Special Requirements: 4%
  • Drainage Requirements: 14%
  • Vent Requirements: 14%
  • Water Distribution: 12%
  • Plan Reading: 14%
  • Gas: 20%

This distribution shows why candidates need to prepare across the entire plumbing system rather than concentrating on a single chapter. Drainage, venting, water distribution, plan reading, plumbing fundamentals, and gas together make up a substantial portion of the examination.

Fuel gas represents 20 percent of the published examination content, making the 2015 International Fuel Gas Code an important part of preparation. Candidates should be prepared to work with fuel gas piping, installation requirements, appliance-related requirements, combustion air, venting concepts, and other subjects addressed by the approved reference.

Plan reading is another significant portion of the examination. Candidates should be comfortable interpreting information from plumbing plans and applying code requirements to practical layouts. Practice that combines code knowledge with real-world installation thinking can be valuable for this part of the exam.

Open Book Test

The Kansas Journeyman Plumber with Gas ICC 560-KS examination is an open book test. ICC identifies a three-hour time limit for the 70-question examination and lists the 2015 International Plumbing Code and 2015 International Fuel Gas Code as the approved references.

An open-book examination still requires significant preparation. Seventy questions in three hours provides an average of approximately two and a half minutes per question. Spending too much time searching through a code book can quickly use up valuable testing time.

A strong study approach is to become familiar with the structure of both references before the examination. Learn where major subjects are located, recognize common terminology, and understand which code book applies to the question being asked.

For the International Plumbing Code, practice navigating subjects such as fixtures, water supply, sanitary drainage, traps, cleanouts, interceptors, vents, and general installation requirements. For the International Fuel Gas Code, focus on finding provisions related to fuel gas systems and the gas-related subjects identified in the examination outline.

Use practice questions to develop a repeatable process: read the entire question, identify the subject, determine whether you know the answer directly, and use the appropriate reference when confirmation is needed. The goal is not simply to locate words in the book but to understand how the code applies to the situation described.

Licensing Steps

Kansas plumbing licensure is administered through cities and counties rather than through a single statewide plumbing licensing office. Candidates should follow the requirements of the city or county where they plan to obtain a journeyman credential.

  1. Determine the licensing jurisdiction. Contact the Kansas city or county where you intend to work and determine its journeyman plumber licensing requirements and accepted examination.
  2. Complete the required field experience. Kansas law requires the issuing jurisdiction to verify documented experience before issuing a journeyman certificate.
  3. Confirm the required examination. Determine whether the jurisdiction accepts or requires the ICC Kansas Standard Journeyman Plumber with Gas examination identified as 560-KS.
  4. Prepare for the examination. Study the approved references and practice plumbing, fuel gas, plan-reading, drainage, venting, and water-distribution questions.
  5. Register for the ICC examination. Complete the applicable ICC registration and scheduling process.
  6. Take the examination. The 560-KS examination contains 70 multiple-choice questions with a three-hour time limit and is administered as an open-book exam.
  7. Complete the local licensing process. After satisfying examination and experience requirements, submit the documentation and fees required by the applicable Kansas city or county.
  8. Maintain the credential. Follow the renewal and continuing-education requirements that apply to the license issued by the jurisdiction.

State Requirements

Kansas statutes establish a framework for plumbing competency examinations and local licensure, while cities and counties issue plumber licenses. This means candidates should always identify the specific jurisdiction in which they intend to work.

For a journeyman certificate, Kansas law requires the issuing jurisdiction to verify documented proof of a minimum of two years of field experience. Field experience includes work performed under the direct supervision of a person holding a valid journeyman or master certificate and qualifying trade-related schooling.

No more than one year of the required experience may be satisfied through trade-related schooling. When schooling is used toward that allowance, Kansas law specifies a program consisting of at least 930 hours documented by a certificate of completion.

Kansas law also establishes requirements for jurisdictions that use designated standard plumbing examinations. A city or county using those examinations must prescribe a minimum passing score of 75 percent and establish applicable licensing rules and fees.

Continuing education is also part of the Kansas statutory framework for locally issued plumbing licenses. Jurisdictions using the statutory licensing system require continuing education that includes code education along with construction, maintenance, and code-update training.

Because requirements are administered locally, candidates should verify application procedures, experience documentation, licensing fees, renewal procedures, and any additional administrative requirements directly with the city or county in which they plan to become licensed.

Test Information and Study Materials

The best preparation for the Kansas Journeyman Plumber with Gas exam combines code familiarity with repeated question practice. Reading the books is important, but candidates should also spend time applying the requirements to exam-style problems.

Start with plumbing fundamentals. Review the basic rules and terminology that support the rest of the plumbing code. Understanding definitions, general installation principles, materials, and basic system relationships can make more specialized questions easier to solve.

Spend significant time on drainage and venting. Drainage Requirements and Vent Requirements each account for 14 percent of the published examination content. Candidates should practice navigating sanitary drainage provisions and understanding how venting requirements relate to properly functioning drainage systems.

Study water distribution. Water Distribution represents 12 percent of the exam. Review the code requirements that affect potable water systems, distribution piping, sizing concepts, installation, and protection of the water supply.

Practice plan reading. Plan Reading accounts for 14 percent of the published examination. Learn to interpret plumbing layouts and connect information shown on a plan with the applicable code requirement.

Do not neglect fuel gas. Gas represents 20 percent of the examination, making it the largest single published content category. Become familiar with the 2015 International Fuel Gas Code and practice determining where different gas-system requirements are located.

Use timed practice sessions. The actual examination allows three hours for 70 questions. Practice answering groups of questions under a time limit so you can develop a comfortable testing pace. When a question requires code research, monitor how long it takes you to locate the relevant information.

Review mistakes deliberately. When you miss a practice question, do more than memorize the correct response. Identify why the answer is correct, find the applicable code topic, and determine what clue in the question should have directed you toward the right section.

Learn the books instead of trying to memorize every section. For an open-book examination, knowing how the reference is organized can be more practical than attempting to memorize hundreds of provisions. Build familiarity with chapters, tables, definitions, and commonly tested subjects.
