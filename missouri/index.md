## How does plumber licensing work in Missouri?

The city or county where you work licenses plumbers here. The board approves your application first; then you schedule the exam and bring only the references the candidate bulletin allows.

- Missouri has no statewide plumber license; cities such as Springfield and Kansas City license plumbers.
- Springfield and Kansas City use ICC exams written for their codes.
