About this St. Paul Plumbing Contractor package

Prepare for plumbing contractor work in Saint Paul, Minnesota with a focused reference package built around the codes and regulations identified by the City of Saint Paul for its Plumbing Certificate of Competency examinations. The St. Paul Minnesota Plumbing Contractor Book Package brings together the local water requirements, Minnesota accessibility provisions, Minnesota plumbing regulations, and mechanical and fuel gas requirements that plumbing professionals need to study for Saint Paul plumbing competency testing and local code work.

The City of Saint Paul Department of Safety and Inspections administers Plumbing Certificate of Competency examinations at both the Journey and Master levels. Candidates applying for the Master-level examination must already possess a State of Minnesota master-level plumbing license and satisfy the City’s application requirements. The City identifies specific reference materials from which examination questions are derived, and only those listed references are allowed for use during the examination.

This book package is especially useful for plumbers preparing for the Saint Paul Master-level Plumbing Certificate of Competency examination and for professionals who want a consolidated set of references covering local water regulations, accessibility, plumbing, mechanical, and fuel gas requirements. The package also supports contractors who need to become familiar with the code framework used for plumbing work in Saint Paul.

Because Saint Paul plumbing requirements combine state and local provisions, effective preparation requires more than reviewing a single plumbing code. Candidates should understand how the Minnesota Plumbing Code works together with the Saint Paul Water Code, accessibility requirements, mechanical and fuel gas provisions, and state plumbing statutes.

Exam Details

The City of Saint Paul Department of Safety and Inspections offers Plumbing Certificate of Competency examinations at both Journey and Master levels. The St. Paul Minnesota Plumbing Contractor Book Package is particularly relevant to candidates preparing for the Master-level examination and contractors who need to understand Saint Paul plumbing requirements.

  • Exam Authority: City of Saint Paul Department of Safety and Inspections
  • Exam Levels: Journey Level and Master Level
  • Passing Score: 70% for both Journey and Master levels
  • Application Deadline: Applications are due 30 days before the examination date.
  • Application Review: Applications are subject to approval by the Plumbing Examination Board.
  • Testing Frequency: Exams are held a minimum of three times per year as scheduled and may be held more frequently when required and approved by the Plumbing Examination Board.

After an application is approved, the candidate receives information regarding the exact examination place, time, and date. The City also publishes scheduled Plumbing Certificate of Competency examination dates and application deadlines through its Department of Safety and Inspections.

The City requires candidates to bring valid state-issued photo identification or a driver’s license, the applicable Minnesota state plumbing license, two #2 lead pencils, a black or blue ballpoint pen, and a basic-function standard calculator. Construction calculators, engineering calculators, scientific calculators, and phone calculators are not allowed. Cell phones are not allowed for use during the examination.

The City has not published a question count or time limit in the current Plumbing Certificate of Competency examination guidelines, so preparation should focus on complete familiarity with the listed references and efficient navigation of the code materials.

Open Book Test

The Saint Paul Plumbing Certificate of Competency examination is an open book test. The City specifies that examination questions are derived from the listed references and that only those identified references are allowed for use during the examination.

This makes code navigation an important part of preparation. Candidates should not rely on simply bringing the books into the examination room. Effective preparation involves learning where major subjects are located, understanding terminology, recognizing how local and state rules interact, and practicing how to move between references when a question involves more than one code requirement.

The allowed examination references include the Saint Paul Water Code, 2020 Minnesota Accessibility Code, 2020 Minnesota Plumbing Code and related Minnesota statutes and rules, and the 2020 Minnesota Mechanical and Fuel Gas Code with ANSI/ASHRAE Standard 154-2011.

During preparation, candidates should practice identifying whether a question is primarily a local water issue, plumbing-code issue, accessibility requirement, gas-related requirement, mechanical provision, or state licensing and regulatory issue. Correctly identifying the governing reference can reduce unnecessary searching during an open-book test.

Licensing Steps

  1. Obtain the appropriate Minnesota plumbing license. Candidates for the Saint Paul Master-level Plumbing Certificate of Competency must possess a State of Minnesota master-level plumbing license.
  2. Meet the City’s Master-level qualifications. The City requires Master-level candidates to have notarized vouchers signed by two State of Minnesota master-level plumbing license holders.
  3. Prepare the City examination application. Applications are submitted through the City of Saint Paul’s PAULIE system.
  4. Submit the application by the deadline. Plumbing Certificate of Competency applications are due 30 days before the examination date.
  5. Wait for Plumbing Examination Board approval. Applications are reviewed before candidates are authorized to sit for the examination.
  6. Study the official examination references. Use the Saint Paul Water Code, Minnesota Accessibility Code, Minnesota Plumbing Code, and Minnesota Mechanical and Fuel Gas Code included in this package.
  7. Practice code navigation. Become familiar with the organization, chapters, tables, and major subject areas within each reference.
  8. Bring the required examination materials. Candidates must bring appropriate identification, their state plumbing license, writing instruments, an approved basic calculator, and any of the allowed references they plan to use.
  9. Complete the Saint Paul examination. The Master-level passing score is 70%.
  10. Complete applicable City competency requirements. Candidates who successfully complete the process may proceed through the City’s applicable Certificate of Competency requirements.
  11. Maintain the required state contractor structure. Minnesota plumbing contractors must maintain a responsible licensed individual who holds an active Master Plumber license and satisfy applicable state contractor requirements.
  12. Maintain local registrations and permits when required. Contractors performing plumbing work in Saint Paul must follow applicable City registration, permitting, inspection, and competency requirements.

State Requirements

Minnesota regulates plumbing contractors separately from individual plumber licenses. A plumbing contractor may contract to perform plumbing work throughout the state when the business maintains the required responsible licensed individual and satisfies applicable state licensing requirements.

A Minnesota plumbing contractor must maintain an active Master Plumber as the responsible licensed individual. That person must be an owner, officer, or member of the business, or a qualifying W-2 employee who is actively engaged in the company’s plumbing work and meets state requirements.

For the Saint Paul Master-level Plumbing Certificate of Competency examination, the applicant must already possess a State of Minnesota master-level plumbing license. The City also requires notarized vouchers signed by two Minnesota master-level plumbing license holders.

Minnesota Master Plumber applicants generally must qualify for and pass the state Master Plumber examination before receiving the state license. One qualifying route requires at least one year of practical plumbing experience as a licensed Journeyworker Plumber. Other state-recognized pathways may apply to qualifying applicants.

At the contractor-business level, Minnesota requires a plumbing contractor license for businesses and individuals contracting to perform plumbing work in communities with populations greater than 5,000. Saint Paul falls within the statewide plumbing contractor licensing framework.

Licensed plumbing contractors are responsible for ensuring that employees performing plumbing work hold the appropriate licenses or registrations, obtaining required permits, arranging inspections, and complying with applicable plumbing codes and regulatory requirements.

Within Saint Paul, plumbing and gas work may also require City permits and compliance with local requirements. Licensed Plumbing/Gas Fitter Contractors may apply for applicable plumbing and gas permits through the City’s permitting system.

The City’s Certificate of Competency requirements add a local layer to the state licensing structure. Candidates therefore need to understand the distinction between a Minnesota state plumbing license, the Minnesota plumbing contractor business license, and the City of Saint Paul Plumbing Certificate of Competency.

Test Information and Study Materials

Saint Paul Water Code: Give particular attention to local water utility rules. Review water service requirements, meter-related provisions, connections, approved arrangements, backflow-related requirements, and the responsibilities associated with work involving Saint Paul Regional Water Services.

Minnesota Plumbing Code: Study the principal plumbing subjects in Chapter 4714, including plumbing fixtures, water supply and distribution, sanitary drainage, vent systems, traps, cleanouts, indirect waste, interceptors, storm drainage, and applicable materials and installation requirements.

Water Distribution: Review pipe sizing principles, fixture demand, pressure requirements, valves, materials, cross-connection control, and backflow prevention. Water distribution questions may involve both state plumbing rules and Saint Paul-specific water requirements.

Sanitary Drainage: Review drainage fixture units, pipe sizing, slopes, cleanouts, drainage connections, building drains, building sewers, and permitted drainage configurations.

Venting: Become familiar with vent sizing, vent connections, individual and common vents, wet venting, circuit venting, and other approved methods recognized under the Minnesota Plumbing Code.

Fixtures and Accessibility: Study plumbing fixture requirements together with the Minnesota Accessibility Code. Candidates should recognize situations where plumbing-code compliance and accessibility requirements apply simultaneously.

Backflow Prevention: Review cross-connection control, backflow preventers, installation requirements, and the relationship between plumbing-code requirements and local water-utility requirements.

Fuel Gas: Use the Minnesota Mechanical and Fuel Gas Code to review fuel gas piping, equipment connections, shutoff requirements, combustion considerations, ventilation, and other gas-related provisions relevant to plumbing and gas-fitting work.

State Plumbing Regulation: Review Minnesota Statutes Chapter 326B.41 through 326B.59 and related rules for licensing, responsibilities, and regulatory requirements that may appear on the examination.

Code Navigation: Because the examination is open book, navigation skill is essential. Practice identifying the subject first, selecting the correct reference, locating the applicable chapter or section, and confirming the exact requirement before selecting an answer.

Local Versus State Requirements: Candidates should learn to distinguish Saint Paul-specific water requirements from statewide Minnesota plumbing provisions. Local requirements can supplement state plumbing rules in areas under City and regional water authority.

Test Information and Study Materials (continued)

Calculator Practice: The City permits only a basic-function standard calculator. Practice plumbing calculations using a simple calculator rather than depending on construction, engineering, scientific, or phone-based calculator functions.

Exam-Day Organization: Candidates should arrive with the required identification, applicable state plumbing license, pencils, pen, basic calculator, and examination references. Familiarity with the physical layout of the books can reduce time spent searching during the test.
