How does the Journeyman Plumber exam work?

The Journeyman Plumber license comes from the Nevada State Contractors Board (NSCB). PSI gives the exam. The board approves your application first; then you schedule the exam and bring only the references the candidate bulletin allows.

  • The Nevada State Contractors Board licenses C-1 plumbing and heating contractors; every applicant also takes the business and law exam.
  • Nevada journeyman plumber cards are local; Reno and Clark County set their own exams.
