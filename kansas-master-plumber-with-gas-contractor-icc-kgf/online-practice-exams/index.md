## Exam Details

The **KGF Kansas Standard Master Plumber with Gas** examination is administered through the International Code Council contractor and trade examination program. The exam contains **100 multiple-choice questions** and has a **4-hour time limit**.

The published ICC examination outline divides the KGF exam into the following content areas:

- **Administration and General Regulations:** 36%
- **Plumbing Fixtures:** 13%
- **Water Supply and Distribution:** 9%
- **Sanitary Drainage:** 8%
- **Plumbing Vent Requirements:** 6%
- **Traps, Interceptors, and Separators:** 5%
- **Storm Drainage:** 2%
- **Gas Piping:** 21%

Administration and General Regulations is the largest published category at 36 percent of the examination. This section includes general installation requirements, code definitions and terms, plan reading, testing, and materials. Candidates should devote substantial study time to these subjects because they can affect many different plumbing and gas questions.

Gas Piping accounts for 21 percent of the published examination content. This portion covers gas piping for service and distribution, gas piping requirements involving specific appliances and equipment, sources of combustion air and duct openings, and fuel-burning appliance vent and connector requirements.

Plumbing Fixtures accounts for 13 percent of the exam and includes subjects such as water heaters, water closets and urinals, specialty fixtures, bathtubs, showers, sinks, and lavatories.

Water Supply and Distribution makes up 9 percent of the examination. Candidates should be prepared for questions concerning service and distribution piping, water supply and pressure, and protection of the potable water supply.

Sanitary Drainage accounts for 8 percent, while Plumbing Vent Requirements account for 6 percent. Traps, Interceptors, and Separators represent 5 percent, and Storm Drainage accounts for 2 percent.

The breadth of this outline means candidates should avoid preparing for only one or two chapters. A strong study plan should cover the full range of plumbing and fuel gas subjects while placing additional emphasis on the larger content categories.

## Open Book Test

The Kansas Master Plumber with Gas ICC KGF examination is an **open book test**. ICC identifies the examination as open book with a four-hour time limit.

The approved references for this exam include the **2018 International Plumbing Code** and the **2018 International Fuel Gas Code**.

Although the exam is open book, candidates should not depend on looking up every answer from scratch. With 100 questions and four hours available, there is an average of approximately 2.4 minutes per question if time is divided evenly.

Effective preparation should therefore include repeated code-navigation practice. Candidates should become familiar with chapter organization, definitions, tables, common terminology, and the general location of important plumbing and fuel gas requirements.

For plumbing questions, practice moving quickly to the correct chapter of the International Plumbing Code. For gas questions, develop the habit of recognizing when the International Fuel Gas Code is the correct reference before beginning a lookup.

Practice questions can help build this process. Read the question carefully, identify the system or subject involved, determine which reference applies, and then locate the applicable requirement. This is generally more efficient than searching broadly through both code books.

## State Requirements

Kansas statutes recognize standard plumbing examinations administered by organizations including the International Code Council for determining the competency of plumbing contractors, master plumbers, and journeyman plumbers.

Plumbing licenses are issued through Kansas cities and counties that require licensure. A candidate should therefore distinguish between passing the ICC examination and receiving a local plumbing license. The ICC exam demonstrates examination competency, while the applicable jurisdiction determines whether all licensing requirements have been met.

Before issuing a master certificate, Kansas law requires the issuing jurisdiction to verify documented proof that the applicant has held a valid journeyman certificate for at least **two years** or has at least **four years of qualifying field experience**.

Kansas law also establishes a minimum passing score of **75 percent** for the designated plumbing examinations used by jurisdictions under the statutory framework.

Continuing education is part of the Kansas licensing structure as well. Jurisdictions using this system require licensees to obtain continuing education, including code education and training related to construction, maintenance, and code updates.

Because licenses are administered locally, applicants should review the exact application procedures, fees, documentation requirements, renewal rules, and other administrative requirements imposed by the city or county where they intend to become licensed.
