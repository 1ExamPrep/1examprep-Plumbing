## State Requirements

Georgia Master Plumber Class I licensing is regulated by the State Construction Industry Licensing Board, Division of Master and Journeyman Plumbers.

**Qualifying Experience:** Under Georgia's requirements effective July 1, 2026, applicants must complete at least four years of qualifying full-time plumbing experience.

**Licensed Journeyman Experience:** At least three of those years must generally be completed while licensed as a Journeyman Plumber. A transitional provision applies to applicants licensed as Journeymen before March 1, 2025.

**Licensed Supervision:** Qualifying experience must be obtained under a licensed Master Plumber and relate to work within the Class I restricted scope.

**Experience Verification:** Applicants must provide the required signed supervisory affidavits and supporting documentation.

**Examination Requirement:** Applicants must pass the approved Master Plumber Class I examination with a minimum score of 70.

**Restricted License Scope:** Georgia Master Plumber Class I authorizes plumbing work and contracting involving residential single-family dwellings, townhomes, and multifamily dwellings, including duplexes, hotels, motels, and apartment buildings not exceeding three stories, as well as commercial buildings not exceeding 20,000 square feet in total building area.

**Class II Classification:** Master Plumber Class II provides unrestricted plumbing authority. Work exceeding Class I limitations requires the appropriate classification.

**Business Operations:** Establishing an LLC or corporation does not independently provide a plumbing license. Plumbing contracting must be performed under the appropriate professional licensing authority and applicable legal requirements.
