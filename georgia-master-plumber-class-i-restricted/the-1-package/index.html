Exam Details

The Georgia Master Plumber Class I (Restricted) examination evaluates advanced plumbing knowledge, technical calculations, installation practices, code interpretation, construction safety, and contractor business responsibilities.

Georgia plumbing licensing is administered by the State Construction Industry Licensing Board, Division of Master and Journeyman Plumbers. The licensing examination is delivered through PSI.

Georgia Master Plumber Class I Examination Overview:

  • License Classification: Master Plumber Class I (Restricted)
  • Licensing State: Georgia
  • Licensing Authority: Georgia State Construction Industry Licensing Board, Division of Master and Journeyman Plumbers
  • Examination Provider: PSI
  • Examination Format: Computer-based, multiple-choice examination
  • Testing Method: Closed book
  • Scored Questions: 120
  • Unscored Pretest Questions: 24
  • Total Questions Presented: 144
  • Time Allowed: Seven hours, divided into two testing parts
  • Passing Standard: Minimum final scaled score of 70

The examination addresses plumbing regulations, work planning, pipe cutting and joining, installation, system testing, maintenance, repairs, safety, calculations, business laws, administrative requirements, and plumbing system design.

Candidates benefit from understanding practical plumbing principles and professional responsibilities. The online course and rental references support preparation across these important examination subjects.

Closed Book Test

The Georgia Master Plumber Class I (Restricted) examination is a closed-book test. Candidates cannot consult plumbing codes, technical manuals, personal study notes, or other reference publications during testing.

This examination format places particular importance on understanding and remembering technical information. Candidates must recognize plumbing problems, apply relevant principles, and demonstrate professional knowledge without relying on reference materials.

For example, examination questions may involve pipe sizing, drainage arrangements, venting principles, fuel gas installations, plumbing calculations, or contractor administrative responsibilities.

State Requirements

Georgia Master Plumber Class I licensing is regulated by the State Construction Industry Licensing Board, Division of Master and Journeyman Plumbers.

Qualifying Experience: Under Georgia's requirements effective July 1, 2026, applicants must complete at least four years of qualifying full-time plumbing experience.

Licensed Journeyman Experience: At least three of those years must generally be completed while licensed as a Journeyman Plumber. A transitional provision applies to applicants licensed as Journeymen before March 1, 2025.

Licensed Supervision: Qualifying experience must be obtained under a licensed Master Plumber and relate to work within the Class I restricted scope.

Experience Verification: Applicants must provide the required signed supervisory affidavits and supporting documentation.

Examination Requirement: Applicants must pass the approved Master Plumber Class I examination with a minimum score of 70.

Restricted License Scope: Georgia Master Plumber Class I authorizes plumbing work and contracting involving residential single-family dwellings, townhomes, and multifamily dwellings, including duplexes, hotels, motels, and apartment buildings not exceeding three stories, as well as commercial buildings not exceeding 20,000 square feet in total building area.

Class II Classification: Master Plumber Class II provides unrestricted plumbing authority. Work exceeding Class I limitations requires the appropriate classification.

Business Operations: Establishing an LLC or corporation does not independently provide a plumbing license. Plumbing contracting must be performed under the appropriate professional licensing authority and applicable legal requirements.

Test Information and Study Materials

Successful examination preparation requires a strong understanding of plumbing systems, practical installation techniques, safety regulations, and business administration responsibilities.

Water Supply and Distribution: Study potable water piping, service connections, valves, system protection, and distribution layouts.

Sanitary Drainage: Review drainage pipe sizing, slopes, fittings, cleanouts, traps, and waste piping installation.

Venting Systems: Study vent arrangements, fixture connections, and appropriate installation principles.

Fuel Gas Systems: Review piping materials, appliance connections, combustion air, venting, and safety requirements.

Plumbing Mathematics: Practice measurements, conversions, geometry, fractions, decimals, slopes, and pipe calculations.

Installation and Maintenance: Review pipe joining, material selection, system testing, troubleshooting, and repairs.

Construction Safety: Study OSHA requirements involving excavation, trenching, scaffolding, and fall protection.

Business Administration: Review contractor responsibilities, financial practices, construction contracts, and project management.

Georgia Requirements: Become familiar with the state's plumbing and fuel gas code amendments and the responsibilities associated with restricted Master Plumber licensing.
