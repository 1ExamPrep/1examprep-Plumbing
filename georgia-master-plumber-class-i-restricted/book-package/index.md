## State Requirements

Georgia Master Plumber Class I licensing is administered by the State Construction Industry Licensing Board, Division of Master and Journeyman Plumbers.

**Experience Requirement:** Under Georgia's requirements effective July 1, 2026, Master Plumber Class I applicants must complete at least four years of full-time qualifying plumbing experience.

**Licensed Journeyman Experience:** At least three of those four years must generally be completed while licensed as a Georgia Journeyman Plumber. Applicants licensed as Journeyman Plumbers before March 1, 2025, qualify under a transitional provision requiring at least one year of licensed Journeyman experience within the four-year total.

**Licensed Supervision:** Qualifying experience must be obtained under the supervision of a licensed Master Plumber and must be relevant to the restricted Class I scope.

**Experience Verification:** Applicants must provide signed affidavits from licensed Master Plumbers who supervised or oversaw their qualifying plumbing work.

**Technical Competency:** Qualifying activities include plumbing system installation, maintenance, alteration, service, repair, layout, pipe sizing, material selection, testing, inspection, and troubleshooting.

**Examination Requirement:** Applicants must pass the Board-approved Master Plumber Class I examination with a minimum score of 70.

**License Classification:** The Class I license permits plumbing contracting within its restricted scope. Work outside that scope requires the appropriate license classification.

Master Plumber licensing carries professional responsibilities involving code compliance, safe installation practices, supervision, and the appropriate performance of plumbing contracting activities.

Studying applicable state plumbing codes, fuel gas requirements, and technical standards provides a useful foundation for understanding these responsibilities.
