State Requirements

Georgia Master Plumber Class I licensing is regulated by the State Construction Industry Licensing Board, Division of Master and Journeyman Plumbers.

Qualifying Experience: Under Georgia's licensing requirements effective July 1, 2026, Master Plumber Class I applicants must complete a minimum of four years of qualifying full-time plumbing experience.

Licensed Journeyman Experience: At least three of those years must be completed while licensed as a Journeyman Plumber. Applicants who obtained their Journeyman Plumber license before March 1, 2025, are covered by a transitional provision requiring at least one year of licensed journeyman experience within the four-year total.

Licensed Supervision: Qualifying experience must be obtained under the supervision of a licensed Master Plumber and must relate to plumbing work within the Class I scope.

Relevant Plumbing Work: Experience may involve installation, maintenance, alteration, service, repair, system layout, pipe sizing, material selection, inspections, testing, and troubleshooting.

Experience Verification: Applicants must provide signed affidavits from licensed Master Plumbers who supervised or oversaw their qualifying work.

Application Requirements: Applicants must complete the prescribed licensing application and submit the required identity, experience, and supporting documentation.

Examination Requirement: Candidates must pass the Board-approved Master Plumber Class I examination with a minimum score of 70.

Restricted License Scope: Georgia Master Plumber Class I licensing covers plumbing involving single-family dwellings, one-level dwellings designed for no more than two families, and commercial structures not exceeding 10,000 square feet in area.

Class II Distinction: Master Plumber Class II is the unrestricted classification. Professionals intending to undertake plumbing contracting beyond the Class I scope require the appropriate authorization.

Understanding these requirements helps candidates plan their licensing pathway and prepare for the responsibilities associated with master-level plumbing work.
