Exam Details

The Georgia Master Plumber Class I (Restricted) examination evaluates advanced plumbing knowledge, including installation practices, code requirements, calculations, system design, professional responsibilities, and contractor business principles.

Licensing is administered by the Georgia State Construction Industry Licensing Board, Division of Master and Journeyman Plumbers. The examination is delivered through PSI.

Georgia Master Plumber Class I Examination Information:

  • License Classification: Master Plumber Class I (Restricted)
  • Licensing State: Georgia
  • Licensing Authority: Georgia State Construction Industry Licensing Board, Division of Master and Journeyman Plumbers
  • Examination Provider: PSI
  • Examination Format: Computer-based, multiple-choice examination
  • Testing Method: Closed book
  • Scored Questions: 120
  • Unscored Pretest Questions: 24
  • Total Questions: 144
  • Examination Time: Seven hours, divided into two parts
  • Passing Standard: Minimum final scaled score of 70

State Requirements

Georgia Master Plumber Class I licensing is regulated by the State Construction Industry Licensing Board, Division of Master and Journeyman Plumbers.

Experience Requirement: Under Georgia's licensing requirements effective July 1, 2026, applicants generally must complete at least four years of qualifying full-time plumbing experience.

Licensed Journeyman Experience: At least three years must be completed while licensed as a Journeyman Plumber. A transitional provision allows applicants licensed as Journeymen before March 1, 2025, to qualify with at least one year of licensed Journeyman experience within the four-year total.

Licensed Supervision: Qualifying experience must be obtained under a licensed Master Plumber and must relate to plumbing work within the Class I restricted scope.

Experience Verification: Applicants must submit required documentation and signed supervisory affidavits supporting their qualifying plumbing experience.

Examination Requirement: Applicants must pass the approved Master Plumber Class I examination with a minimum score of 70.

Restricted License Scope: Master Plumber Class I licensing is limited to plumbing involving single-family dwellings, one-level dwellings designed for no more than two families, and commercial structures not exceeding 10,000 square feet in area.

Professional Responsibilities: Licensed Master Plumbers must comply with applicable plumbing codes, safety standards, and professional requirements.

Master Plumber Class II is the unrestricted classification. Applicants should select the license classification that corresponds to their qualifications and intended plumbing work.
