Exam Details

The Georgia Master Plumber Class I (Restricted) examination evaluates technical plumbing knowledge, code compliance, system installation, maintenance, business practices, and administrative responsibilities associated with master-level plumbing work.

Georgia licensing is administered by the State Construction Industry Licensing Board, Division of Master and Journeyman Plumbers. The examination is delivered through PSI.

Georgia Master Plumber Class I Examination Overview:

  • License Classification: Master Plumber Class I (Restricted)
  • Licensing State: Georgia
  • Licensing Authority: Georgia State Construction Industry Licensing Board, Division of Master and Journeyman Plumbers
  • Examination Provider: PSI
  • Examination Format: Computer-based examination
  • Testing Method: Closed book
  • Scored Questions: 120
  • Additional Unscored Questions: 24
  • Total Questions: 144
  • Passing Standard: Minimum final scaled score of 70

The examination includes technical plumbing subjects and a separate administrative, business, and design component. Candidates should prepare for questions involving plumbing regulations, work planning, materials, installation, maintenance, troubleshooting, construction safety, and business responsibilities.

Important areas include water distribution, drainage, venting, fuel gas piping, plumbing fixtures, calculations, and applicable Georgia regulations.

The 2024 International Plumbing Code and International Fuel Gas Code, together with Georgia amendments, provide important technical study material for the current examination.

State Requirements

Georgia Master Plumber Class I licensing is regulated by the State Construction Industry Licensing Board, Division of Master and Journeyman Plumbers.

Experience Requirement: Under Georgia's requirements effective July 1, 2026, applicants must complete at least four years of qualifying full-time plumbing experience.

Licensed Journeyman Experience: At least three years must be completed while licensed as a Journeyman Plumber. A transitional provision applies to applicants licensed as Journeyman Plumbers before March 1, 2025, requiring at least one licensed journeyman year within the four-year total.

Licensed Supervision: Qualifying plumbing experience must be obtained under the supervision of a licensed Master Plumber and relate to the restricted Class I scope.

Experience Verification: Applicants must provide signed affidavits from licensed Master Plumbers who supervised qualifying work.

Examination Requirement: Applicants must achieve a minimum score of 70 on the approved Master Plumber Class I examination.

Restricted License Scope: Master Plumber Class I covers plumbing involving single-family dwellings, one-level dwellings designed for no more than two families, and commercial structures not exceeding 10,000 square feet in area.

Class II Distinction: Master Plumber Class II is the unrestricted classification. Plumbing work outside the Class I scope requires the appropriate licensing authority.

Professional Responsibilities: Licensed Master Plumbers must comply with applicable plumbing regulations, construction safety requirements, and professional standards.

Understanding these requirements helps applicants prepare for the qualifications and responsibilities associated with restricted master-level plumbing contracting.
