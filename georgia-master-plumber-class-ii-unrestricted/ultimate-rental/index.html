Exam Details

The Georgia Master Plumber Class II (Unrestricted) examination evaluates advanced plumbing knowledge, installation practices, technical calculations, construction regulations, and professional responsibilities associated with unrestricted plumbing contracting.

Georgia plumbing licensing is administered by the State Construction Industry Licensing Board, Division of Master and Journeyman Plumbers. Licensing examinations are delivered through PSI.

Georgia Master Plumber Class II Examination Information:

  • License Classification: Master Plumber Class II (Unrestricted)
  • Licensing State: Georgia
  • Licensing Authority: Georgia State Construction Industry Licensing Board, Division of Master and Journeyman Plumbers
  • Testing Provider: PSI
  • Examination Format: Computer-based examination
  • Testing Method: Closed book
  • Scored Questions: 140
  • Additional Pretest Questions: 28
  • Total Questions Presented: 168
  • Time Allowed: 7 hours, divided into two 3.5-hour parts
  • Passing Standard: Minimum final scaled score of 70

The examination covers general plumbing laws, regulations, work planning, materials, pipe installation, testing, maintenance, repair, safety, business administration, and plumbing system design.

Master Plumber Class II candidates should also understand plumbing principles associated with commercial, industrial, and specialized installations.

The 2024 International Plumbing Code and International Fuel Gas Code, together with Georgia amendments, provide important technical subjects for examination preparation.

Closed Book Test

The Georgia Master Plumber Class II (Unrestricted) examination is a closed-book test. Candidates cannot consult plumbing codes, reference books, technical manuals, or personal study notes during testing.

This examination format makes technical understanding and knowledge retention especially important. Candidates must recognize plumbing problems, apply relevant principles, and answer questions using their professional knowledge.

For example, a water distribution question may involve understanding pipe sizing, pressure, or system protection. Drainage questions may address slopes, venting arrangements, or fixture connections.

Fuel gas questions may involve piping materials, appliance installations, combustion air, or venting requirements. Business questions may involve administrative responsibilities, construction contracts, or project organization.

Effective closed-book preparation strategies include:

  • Reviewing important plumbing code concepts and applications.
  • Studying water distribution, sanitary drainage, and venting systems.
  • Practicing plumbing mathematics without reference examples.
  • Understanding fuel gas system installation and safety requirements.
  • Reviewing commercial and industrial plumbing principles.
  • Studying construction safety and contractor business responsibilities.
  • Practicing technical questions independently after reviewing each subject.

The rental reference books support preparation before examination day. Students can review technical tables, diagrams, and code provisions while strengthening their understanding of important plumbing principles.

A useful study approach involves reviewing each technical subject and then practicing how to explain or apply the material without consulting references.

State Requirements

Georgia Master Plumber Class II licensing is regulated by the State Construction Industry Licensing Board, Division of Master and Journeyman Plumbers.

Qualifying Experience: Georgia's licensing requirements effective July 1, 2026, establish at least five years of qualifying full-time plumbing experience.

Licensed Journeyman Experience: At least four of the five years must be completed while licensed as a Journeyman Plumber. A transitional provision applies to applicants licensed as Journeymen before March 1, 2025, requiring at least two licensed journeyman years within the five-year total.

Unrestricted-Scope Experience: At least two years of qualifying experience must involve plumbing work within the unrestricted Class II scope.

Professional Supervision: Qualifying experience must be obtained under a licensed Master Plumber.

Experience Verification: Applicants must provide the required signed supervisory affidavits and supporting documentation.

Examination Requirement: Applicants must pass the Board-approved Master Plumber Class II examination with a minimum score of 70.

License Classification: Master Plumber Class II is Georgia's unrestricted master plumbing classification. It does not carry the building-size limitations associated with Class I.

Professional Responsibilities: Licensed Master Plumbers must comply with applicable plumbing codes, construction requirements, and professional licensing obligations.

These requirements reflect the advanced experience and professional responsibilities associated with unrestricted plumbing contracting in Georgia.
