## State Requirements

Georgia Master Plumber Class II licensing is regulated by the State Construction Industry Licensing Board, Division of Master and Journeyman Plumbers.

**Qualifying Experience:** Georgia's licensing requirements effective July 1, 2026, establish at least five years of qualifying full-time plumbing experience.

**Licensed Journeyman Experience:** At least four of the five years must be completed while licensed as a Journeyman Plumber. A transitional provision applies to applicants licensed as Journeymen before March 1, 2025, requiring at least two licensed journeyman years within the five-year total.

**Unrestricted-Scope Experience:** At least two years of qualifying experience must involve plumbing work within the unrestricted Class II scope.

**Professional Supervision:** Qualifying experience must be obtained under a licensed Master Plumber.

**Experience Verification:** Applicants must provide the required signed supervisory affidavits and supporting documentation.

**Examination Requirement:** Applicants must pass the Board-approved Master Plumber Class II examination with a minimum score of 70.

**License Classification:** Master Plumber Class II is Georgia's unrestricted master plumbing classification. It does not carry the building-size limitations associated with Class I.

**Professional Responsibilities:** Licensed Master Plumbers must comply with applicable plumbing codes, construction requirements, and professional licensing obligations.

These requirements reflect the advanced experience and professional responsibilities associated with unrestricted plumbing contracting in Georgia.
