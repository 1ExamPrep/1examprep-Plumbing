Exam Details

The Georgia Master Plumber Class II (Unrestricted) examination evaluates advanced plumbing knowledge, practical installation principles, code requirements, safety procedures, technical calculations, and professional business responsibilities.

Georgia plumbing licensing is administered by the State Construction Industry Licensing Board, Division of Master and Journeyman Plumbers. Licensing examinations are delivered through PSI.

Georgia Master Plumber Class II Examination Overview:

  • License Classification: Master Plumber Class II (Unrestricted)
  • Licensing State: Georgia
  • Licensing Authority: Georgia State Construction Industry Licensing Board, Division of Master and Journeyman Plumbers
  • Examination Provider: PSI
  • Examination Format: Computer-based examination
  • Testing Method: Closed book
  • Examination Sections: Technical plumbing and administrative, business, and design subjects
  • Examination Time: Seven hours, divided into two parts
  • Passing Standard: Minimum examination score of 70

Master Plumber Class II preparation involves understanding plumbing systems in residential, commercial, and industrial settings. The examination also addresses administrative responsibilities and plumbing design considerations. Candidates benefit from reviewing contractor business practices, employment requirements, project organization, and technical planning.

State Requirements

Georgia Master Plumber Class II licensing is regulated by the State Construction Industry Licensing Board, Division of Master and Journeyman Plumbers.

Qualifying Experience: Georgia requires Master Plumber applicants to document a minimum of five years of plumbing experience involving work covered by the Georgia State Plumbing Code.

Licensed Journeyman Experience: At least two years of the required plumbing experience must be completed while licensed as a Journeyman Plumber.

Commercial or Industrial Experience: Class II applicants must also document qualifying experience involving commercial or industrial plumbing work.

Professional References: Georgia requires references from licensed plumbers who can attest to the applicant's experience. At least two references for Class II applicants must come from licensed Master Plumbers.

Examination Requirement: Applicants must obtain a minimum score of 70 on the approved licensing examination.

Unrestricted Classification: Master Plumber Class II is Georgia's unrestricted master plumbing classification. It does not carry the building-size and occupancy restrictions associated with Master Plumber Class I.

Professional Responsibilities: Licensed Master Plumbers must comply with applicable plumbing regulations, safety requirements, and professional licensing obligations.

The Class II classification supports plumbing work across residential, commercial, industrial, and specialized facilities. Understanding advanced plumbing systems and professional responsibilities is important for candidates preparing to pursue unrestricted licensing.
