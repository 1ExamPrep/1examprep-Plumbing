Exam Details

The Georgia Master Plumber Class II (Unrestricted) examination evaluates advanced plumbing knowledge, installation practices, technical calculations, regulatory responsibilities, and professional business principles associated with unrestricted plumbing contracting.

Georgia plumbing licensing is regulated by the State Construction Industry Licensing Board, Division of Master and Journeyman Plumbers. The licensing examination is administered through PSI.

Georgia Master Plumber Class II Examination Information:

  • License Classification: Master Plumber Class II (Unrestricted)
  • Licensing State: Georgia
  • Licensing Authority: Georgia State Construction Industry Licensing Board, Division of Master and Journeyman Plumbers
  • Examination Provider: PSI
  • Examination Format: Computer-based examination
  • Testing Method: Closed book
  • Scored Questions: 140
  • Unscored Pretest Questions: 28
  • Total Questions Presented: 168
  • Examination Time: Seven hours, divided into two testing parts
  • Passing Standard: Minimum final scaled score of 70

The examination addresses technical plumbing knowledge, general laws, applicable regulations, work planning, material selection, installation methods, testing, maintenance, repair, safety, business administration, and plumbing design.

Master Plumber Class II preparation also involves understanding plumbing systems associated with commercial and industrial facilities, larger buildings, and complex piping installations.

The online course and reference collection help students organize their preparation around these important technical and professional subjects.

Closed Book Test

The Georgia Master Plumber Class II (Unrestricted) examination is a closed-book test. Candidates cannot consult plumbing codes, technical manuals, personal notes, or reference books during the examination.

This examination format places particular importance on technical understanding, knowledge retention, and the ability to apply plumbing principles independently.

For example, a question involving water distribution may require knowledge of system demand, piping arrangements, or pressure considerations. Drainage and venting questions may involve installation principles, pipe sizing, or fixture connections.

Fuel gas questions can involve appliance installation, piping materials, combustion air, and venting requirements. Business questions may address professional administration, contracts, or project responsibilities.

Effective closed-book preparation strategies include:

  • Studying important plumbing and fuel gas code concepts.

  • Practicing calculations without relying on reference examples.
  • Understanding commercial and industrial plumbing applications.

  • Studying construction safety and contractor responsibilities.
  • Practicing technical questions from memory.

State Requirements

Georgia Master Plumber Class II licensing is administered by the State Construction Industry Licensing Board, Division of Master and Journeyman Plumbers.

Qualifying Experience: Under Georgia's licensing requirements effective July 1, 2026, applicants must complete at least five years of qualifying full-time plumbing experience.

Licensed Journeyman Experience: At least four of the five years must be completed while licensed as a Journeyman Plumber. A transitional provision applies to individuals who obtained their Journeyman Plumber license before March 1, 2025, requiring at least two licensed journeyman years within the five-year total.

Unrestricted-Scope Experience: At least two years of qualifying experience must involve plumbing work within the unrestricted Class II scope.

Licensed Supervision: Qualifying experience must be obtained under the supervision of a licensed Master Plumber.

Experience Verification: Applicants must provide signed supervisory affidavits documenting their qualifying plumbing work.

Examination Requirement: Applicants must pass the approved Master Plumber Class II examination with a minimum score of 70.

Unrestricted Classification: Georgia Master Plumber Class II does not carry the building-size and occupancy restrictions associated with Master Plumber Class I.

Professional Responsibilities: Licensed Master Plumbers must comply with applicable plumbing codes, safety standards, permitting requirements, and professional regulations.

These requirements reflect the technical experience and professional responsibilities associated with unrestricted master-level plumbing work.
