## State Requirements

Georgia Master Plumber Class II licensing is regulated by the State Construction Industry Licensing Board, Division of Master and Journeyman Plumbers.

**Qualifying Experience:** Under Georgia's licensing requirements effective July 1, 2026, applicants must complete at least five years of qualifying full-time plumbing experience.

**Licensed Journeyman Experience:** At least four of those five years must be completed while licensed as a Journeyman Plumber. Applicants who obtained their Journeyman Plumber license before March 1, 2025, are subject to a transitional provision requiring at least two years of licensed journeyman experience.

**Unrestricted-Scope Experience:** At least two years of the required plumbing experience must involve work within the unrestricted Class II scope.

**Professional Supervision:** Qualifying experience must be obtained under the supervision of a licensed Master Plumber.

**Experience Documentation:** Applicants must provide the required signed supervisory affidavits and supporting work experience information.

**Examination Requirement:** Applicants must pass the Board-approved Master Plumber Class II examination with a minimum score of 70.

**Unrestricted Classification:** Georgia Master Plumber Class II is the unrestricted master plumbing classification. It does not carry the building-size limitations associated with Class I.

**Professional Responsibilities:** Licensed Master Plumbers must comply with applicable plumbing codes, construction safety standards, and professional licensing requirements.

These qualifications reflect the advanced technical experience associated with unrestricted plumbing work, including commercial, industrial, and specialized installations.
