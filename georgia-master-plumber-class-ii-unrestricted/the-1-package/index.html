Exam Details

The Georgia Master Plumber Class II (Unrestricted) examination evaluates advanced plumbing knowledge, installation practices, technical calculations, applicable regulations, safety procedures, and professional responsibilities associated with unrestricted plumbing contracting.

Georgia plumbing licensing is administered by the State Construction Industry Licensing Board, Division of Master and Journeyman Plumbers.

Georgia Master Plumber Class II Examination Overview:

  • License Classification: Master Plumber Class II (Unrestricted)
  • Licensing State: Georgia
  • Licensing Authority: Georgia State Construction Industry Licensing Board, Division of Master and Journeyman Plumbers
  • Testing Method: Closed book
  • Examination Requirement: Board-approved Master Plumber Class II examination
  • Passing Standard: Minimum examination score of 70

Master Plumber Class II preparation involves studying plumbing systems and understanding professional responsibilities associated with commercial, industrial, residential, and specialized installations.

Important technical areas include potable water distribution, sanitary drainage, venting, fuel gas piping, plumbing fixtures, material selection, system testing, maintenance, and applicable construction requirements.

Business-related preparation is also valuable for individuals pursuing plumbing contracting responsibilities. Contractor administration, construction contracts, project organization, and workplace safety are relevant areas of professional knowledge.

The reference collection and online course support preparation across these subjects through organized technical review and practical learning.

Closed Book Test

The Georgia Master Plumber Class II (Unrestricted) examination is a closed-book test. Candidates prepare using plumbing codes, technical manuals, and study references before examination day, then demonstrate their understanding without consulting those materials during the test.

This format places particular importance on technical knowledge, practical reasoning, and the ability to apply plumbing principles independently.

For example, water distribution questions can involve system demand, piping arrangements, pressure considerations, or installation principles. Drainage and venting questions can involve pipe sizing, fixture connections, or system protection.

Fuel gas subjects involve piping materials, appliance connections, combustion air, venting, and safety. Plumbing mathematics can involve measurements, conversions, slopes, and practical calculations.

Effective closed-book preparation strategies include:

  • Reviewing important plumbing code concepts and their applications.
  • Studying water distribution, drainage, and venting systems.
  • Practicing plumbing calculations without reference examples.
  • Understanding fuel gas installation and safety principles.
  • Reviewing commercial and industrial plumbing applications.
  • Studying construction safety and professional responsibilities.
  • Testing technical knowledge after completing each study section.

Professionally highlighted and tabbed rental references help organize preparation before testing. Students can use these materials to study important provisions, review diagrams and tables, and strengthen familiarity with technical terminology.

As their knowledge develops, candidates can transition toward explaining concepts and solving problems without consulting their books.

State Requirements

Georgia Master Plumber Class II licensing is regulated by the State Construction Industry Licensing Board, Division of Master and Journeyman Plumbers.

Experience Requirement: Georgia's requirements effective July 1, 2026, establish a minimum of five years of qualifying full-time plumbing experience.

Licensed Journeyman Experience: At least four of those five years must be completed while licensed as a Journeyman Plumber. Applicants licensed as Journeyman Plumbers before March 1, 2025, are subject to a transitional provision requiring at least two licensed journeyman years within the five-year total.

Unrestricted-Scope Experience: At least two years of the required experience must involve plumbing work within the unrestricted Class II scope.

Licensed Supervision: Qualifying plumbing experience must be obtained under the supervision of a licensed Master Plumber.

Professional References: Applicants must provide three references from licensed plumbers, including at least two licensed Master Plumbers.

Examination Requirement: Applicants must pass the approved Master Plumber Class II examination with a minimum score of 70.

Unrestricted Classification: Master Plumber Class II is Georgia's unrestricted master plumbing classification and does not carry the building-size restrictions associated with Class I.

Business Operations: Establishing an LLC or corporation does not independently authorize plumbing contracting. The business must operate in compliance with applicable professional licensing and other legal requirements.

Installation and Maintenance: Review pipe cutting, joining methods, fitting selection, fixture installation, system testing, inspection, and repairs.
