## State Requirements

Georgia Master Plumber Class II licensing is administered by the State Construction Industry Licensing Board, Division of Master and Journeyman Plumbers.

**Qualifying Experience:** Under Georgia's licensing requirements effective July 1, 2026, Master Plumber Class II applicants must complete at least five years of qualifying full-time plumbing experience.

**Licensed Journeyman Experience:** At least four of the five years must be completed while licensed as a Journeyman Plumber. Applicants who obtained their Georgia Journeyman Plumber license before March 1, 2025, are subject to a transitional requirement of at least two licensed journeyman years within the five-year total.

**Unrestricted-Scope Experience:** At least two years of the required experience must involve plumbing work within the unrestricted Class II scope.

**Licensed Supervision:** Qualifying experience must be obtained under the supervision of a licensed Master Plumber.

**Experience Verification:** Applicants must provide signed affidavits from the licensed Master Plumbers who supervised or oversaw their qualifying plumbing work.

**Examination Requirement:** Applicants must pass the Board-approved Master Plumber Class II examination with a minimum score of 70.

**Unrestricted License Scope:** Class II permits plumbing work and contracting without the building-size and occupancy limitations associated with Class I. Its scope includes commercial buildings of any size, industrial facilities, institutional occupancies, multistory structures, and specialized-use facilities.

**Professional Responsibilities:** Licensed Master Plumbers are responsible for complying with applicable plumbing regulations, construction requirements, and professional licensing obligations.

These qualifications reflect the advanced experience and technical knowledge associated with unrestricted master-level plumbing work in Georgia.
