How does the Plumbing, Including Solar Residential Contractor (R-37) exam work?

The Plumbing, Including Solar Residential Contractor (R-37) license comes from the Arizona Registrar of Contractors (ROC). The board approves your application first; then you schedule the exam and bring only the references the candidate bulletin allows.

  • The Arizona Registrar of Contractors licenses plumbing contractors in commercial (C-37), dual (CR-37) and residential (R-37R) classes, plus solar plumbing classes.
  • Arizona has no statewide journeyman plumber license; cities that license journeymen set their own exams.
