## Exam Details

The Arizona R-37 Plumbing, Including Solar residential examination evaluates knowledge connected with water and gas piping systems, fire-protection systems, sewage treatment systems, plumbing fixtures, vents, devices common to the plumbing trade, and applicable solar work. The classification includes the residential plumbing work covered by the related R-37R subclassifications.

The R-37 trade examination contains 100 questions. Candidates are allowed 240 minutes to complete the test and must earn a minimum score of 70 percent to pass.

Preparation may include drainage, waste and vent systems, water supply, backflow prevention, plumbing fixtures, water heaters, storm drainage, specialty plumbing, interceptors, traps, isometric analysis, fuel gas, septic and sewer systems, fire sprinklers, excavation, mechanical systems, hydronic heating, boilers, construction safety, and solar thermal applications.

Exam questions may require candidates to identify a code requirement, interpret a definition, select an approved material, use a technical table, calculate a measurement, evaluate a piping arrangement, identify a safe work practice, or determine which publication applies to the situation described.

Solar-related preparation may include solar water heating, solar pool heating, collectors, storage, system piping, circulation, pumps, controls, heat transfer, system configuration, installation practices, operation, maintenance, and safety considerations.

Candidates should practice identifying the subject of a question before opening a book. A water distribution or drainage question may require the International Plumbing Code, while a fuel gas question may lead to the International Fuel Gas Code. A solar thermal question may require the Solar Water and Pool Heating Design and Installation Manual.

## Licensing Steps

Begin by confirming that the R-37 Plumbing, Including Solar residential classification matches the work the applicant or business plans to perform. The classification applies to residential plumbing and applicable solar work within its authorized scope.

Identify the qualifying party for the contractor license. The qualifying party is responsible for meeting the experience, knowledge, and examination requirements associated with the requested classification and maintaining the required relationship with the applicant business.

Complete the R-37 trade examination unless an authorized waiver applies. The qualifying party may also need to complete the applicable Arizona Statutes and Rules requirement as part of the licensing process.

Complete the required background checks for the individuals identified by the application requirements. Applicants should coordinate background-check timing with examination completion and document preparation.

Obtain the required contractor bond and gather the documents associated with the applicant’s business structure. Arizona contractor licenses may be issued to eligible business entities such as sole proprietorships, partnerships, limited liability companies, and corporations.

Prepare the application using consistent business names, ownership information, qualifying-party records, identification, examination results, experience documentation, background-check information, bond paperwork, and required forms.

Because the R-37 classification includes solar work, applicants must also provide the applicable solar warranty documentation with the license application.

Submit the completed application to the Arizona Registrar of Contractors for review. Passing the examination is an important step, but it does not automatically result in license issuance. The application must satisfy the state’s licensing requirements before approval.
