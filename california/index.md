## How does plumber licensing work in California?

The Contractors State License Board (CSLB) licenses plumbers here. The board approves your application first; then you schedule the exam and bring only the references the candidate bulletin allows.

- The CSLB licenses C-36 plumbing contractors; applicants take the Law and Business exam as well as the trade exam.
- California has no statewide journeyman plumber license, so the journeyman packages suit local and union exams built on the UPC.

### Can you bring books into the California exam?

It depends on the exam. Many plumbing trade exams allow the code book, but some are closed book. Your candidate bulletin lists the references allowed; if books are allowed, highlighted and tabbed sets save the most time.
