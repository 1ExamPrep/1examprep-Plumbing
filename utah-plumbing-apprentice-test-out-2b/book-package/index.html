Exam Details

The Utah Plumbing Apprentice Test Out 2B examination is part of the Utah Plumbing & Heating Contractors Association Apprentice Test-Out Exam Program. Prov administers the written examinations for the program.

The Test Out 2B examination contains 60 questions. Candidates are allowed 3 hours to complete the examination.

The exam is identified as the Utah Plumbing Competency Exam – 2B. Its published learning objectives draw from plumbing codes and standards, mathematics, and plumbing trade knowledge associated with apprenticeship education.

The published Test Out 2B objectives identify the following chapters of the 2018 International Plumbing Code:

  • Chapter 11
  • Chapter 12

Chapter 11 of the 2018 International Plumbing Code addresses storm drainage. Candidates should understand the code structure and requirements governing the collection, conveyance, sizing, and disposal of storm water from building roof and drainage systems.

Chapter 12 addresses special piping and storage systems. Candidates should become familiar with how this chapter is organized and how its requirements relate to specialized piping applications.

The published learning objectives for Test Out 2B also identify a substantial mathematics component as well as additional plumbing trade subjects. Mathematics units associated with the examination include material involving practical calculations used throughout plumbing and pipe trades.

Residential fire-sprinkler concepts are also identified in the Test Out 2B learning objectives through material associated with NFPA 13D. Candidates preparing for the complete examination should therefore understand that Test Out 2B extends beyond only the books included in this particular package.

With 60 questions and three hours available, candidates average approximately three minutes per question. Efficient reference navigation can help preserve time for mathematical problems and detailed technical questions.

Open Book Test

The Utah Plumbing Apprentice Test Out 2B examination is an open book test. Candidates may use permitted reference materials according to the testing program's rules.

Prov allows candidates to prepare approved reference materials in advance with highlighting and permanent indexing or tabs. Candidates should use the preparation period to become familiar with their books instead of depending on the open-book format as a substitute for studying.

Permanent tabs are most effective when they identify meaningful locations such as major chapters, frequently used tables, definitions, fuel-gas sections, mechanical sections, and Utah amendments. Too many tabs can make navigation slower rather than faster.

When working through a practice question, identify the subject before searching. A storm-drainage problem should immediately suggest Chapter 11 of the International Plumbing Code. A special-piping question may suggest Chapter 12. Fuel-gas questions should direct attention toward the applicable fuel-gas reference, while mechanical-system questions may require the International Mechanical Code.

Candidates should also become comfortable distinguishing between model-code provisions and Utah-specific modifications. State amendments can alter the way a model-code provision applies in Utah, making familiarity with applicable amendment material an important part of code-based preparation.

Timed code-navigation exercises are useful. Pick a plumbing topic, start a timer, locate the relevant chapter or section, and confirm the requirement. Repeating this process helps build the ability to move through technical references more confidently during the actual examination.

Licensing Steps

The Utah Plumbing Apprentice Test Out 2B examination is an apprenticeship competency examination. It is not the Utah Journeyman Plumber licensing examination. Passing Test Out 2B demonstrates competency in designated apprenticeship learning objectives but does not independently issue a plumbing license.

  1. Enter or plan to enter a Utah plumbing apprenticeship program. The competency examinations are intended for students pursuing plumbing apprenticeship education in Utah.
  2. Maintain apprentice licensure as applicable. Utah regulates apprentice plumbers through the Division of Professional Licensing.
  3. Complete the competency exams in sequence when using the test-out pathway. Utah's rules identify a sequence beginning with Test Out 1A and continuing through Test Out 2B.
  4. Meet the passing requirement for the preceding competency exam. Admission to a subsequent competency examination requires successful completion of the previous exam in the sequence.
  5. Prepare for Test Out 2B. Review storm drainage, special piping, mathematics, plumbing trade knowledge, residential fire-sprinkler concepts, and related code material.
  6. Practice open-book reference navigation. Become familiar with the organization of the International Plumbing Code, International Mechanical Code, fuel-gas code references, and applicable Utah amendments.
  7. Register with the approved examination provider. Utah's competency-exam rules allow qualified applicants to register directly with the approved exam provider.
  8. Complete the Test Out 2B examination. Candidates have three hours to answer 60 questions.
  9. Continue satisfying apprenticeship and experience requirements. Successful competency testing addresses the educational or placement component identified by Utah rules but does not replace required plumbing experience.
  10. Complete journeyman licensing requirements when eligible. Applicants must satisfy the applicable Utah requirements for journeyman licensure, including the required licensing examinations.

State Requirements

Utah administrative rules establish a plumbing apprentice competency-examination pathway beginning with Test Out 1A and continuing through Test Out 2B. A licensed apprentice plumber may use the approved competency exams to satisfy applicable education requirements or determine placement in an approved training program.

The competency exams must be taken in sequence. A candidate must obtain a minimum score of 70% before proceeding to the subsequent competency examination.

Utah rules permit an applicant to attempt each competency examination one time. When a candidate scores below 70% on a competency exam, placement in the planned program of training is determined at that point.

Division pre-approval is not required merely to sit for an approved plumbing competency examination. An eligible applicant may register directly with the Division's approved examination provider.

The competency-exam pathway addresses apprenticeship education and placement. It does not eliminate Utah's separate work-experience requirements for plumbing licensure.

A candidate working toward Journeyman Plumber status must ultimately satisfy Utah's applicable training and experience pathway and pass the licensing examinations required for journeyman applicants. Utah requires Journeyman Plumber applicants to obtain passing scores on the Utah Plumber Theory Exam and the Utah Plumber Practical Exam.

Test Out 2B is therefore an important milestone for candidates using the competency pathway, but it remains one component of a broader apprenticeship and licensing process.

Test Information and Study Materials

A productive Test Out 2B study plan should begin with Chapter 11 of the 2018 International Plumbing Code. Storm drainage is an important technical subject involving roof drainage, conductors, leaders, storm drains, sizing, and related methods for handling storm water.

Candidates should understand that storm drainage questions can involve both code navigation and calculations. Study the applicable tables and learn how drainage areas, pipe sizes, and system arrangements relate to one another.

Chapter 12 covers special piping and storage systems. Learn how this chapter is organized and practice locating its individual requirements. Understanding where a subject appears in the code can save significant time during an open-book examination.

Mathematics is another important component of Test Out 2B preparation. Published learning objectives identify multiple mathematics units, reflecting the continued importance of practical calculation skills in plumbing work.

Plumbing mathematics may require candidates to work carefully with measurements, geometry, pipe calculations, offsets, areas, volumes, grade, and other trade-related applications. Practice calculations by hand and make sure units remain consistent throughout each problem.

The Test Out 2B learning objectives also identify additional plumbing trade chapters beyond the International Plumbing Code. Candidates should continue reviewing practical system knowledge, materials, installation procedures, and terminology gained throughout apprenticeship education.

Residential fire-sprinkler material associated with NFPA 13D is also identified in the published learning objectives. Candidates preparing for the complete Test Out 2B examination should include residential fire-sprinkler concepts in their broader study plan.

Open-book practice should be incorporated into every study session. Instead of simply reading a chapter, create or use practice questions that force you to locate the applicable section. This makes the books active study tools rather than passive references.

When working with the International Plumbing Code, develop immediate topic-to-chapter associations. Storm drainage should suggest Chapter 11. Special piping and storage systems should suggest Chapter 12. This mental organization reduces unnecessary searching.

Use the International Mechanical Code and fuel-gas references regularly enough that their organization also becomes familiar. Even when a candidate knows the underlying trade concept, finding the precise code provision may require efficient navigation.

Review definitions carefully. Code terminology can have a precise meaning that differs from everyday trade language. Understanding defined terms can make technical questions easier to interpret and prevent mistakes caused by assuming a familiar word has its ordinary meaning.

When a practice question is missed, identify whether the problem was technical knowledge, mathematics, code navigation, or interpretation. Different types of mistakes require different study responses. A calculation error calls for more math practice, while a slow lookup may indicate that the candidate needs greater familiarity with the code's organization.

Timed practice becomes increasingly important as the examination approaches. With three hours for 60 questions, candidates should handle straightforward questions efficiently and reserve additional time for calculations and detailed code searches.

The Test Out 2B examination sits at the end of the current Utah apprentice competency-exam sequence, so candidates should also continue using knowledge developed while preparing for 1A, 1B, and 2A.
