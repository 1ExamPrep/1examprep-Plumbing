## Exam Details

The Delaware Master Plumber examination evaluates the knowledge needed to provide plumbing services involving the design, installation, construction, replacement, service, repair, alteration, and modification of pipes, fixtures, and related apparatus used to bring water into a building and remove liquid and water-carried waste. Delaware Master Plumber services also include the installation and connection of gas piping.

The examination contains 100 questions and allows three hours for completion. The published examination outline includes:

- General Knowledge: 16 questions
- Isometrics and Plan Reading: 8 questions
- Plumbing Math: 8 questions
- Drain, Waste, and Vent Systems: 14 questions
- Water Supply and Distribution: 14 questions
- Fixtures and Fittings: 8 questions
- Water Heaters: 10 questions
- Fuel Gas Systems: 10 questions
- Storm and Roof Drainage: 6 questions
- Safety: 6 questions

General knowledge questions may involve plumbing terminology, materials, fittings, tools, installation methods, system components, inspections, testing, and standard trade practices. Candidates should understand the purpose of each component and how it affects the operation of the complete plumbing system.

Isometric and plan-reading questions may require candidates to interpret drawings, symbols, dimensions, elevations, piping routes, fixture locations, vent connections, and system layouts. Candidates should practice moving between a drawing and the applicable code requirements while identifying the information needed to solve the problem.

Plumbing mathematics may involve measurements, developed lengths, areas, volumes, slopes, offsets, pressure, flow, fixture units, pipe sizing, and other trade calculations. Candidates should practice identifying the required formula, organizing the information provided, and checking whether the final answer is reasonable.

Drain, waste, and vent preparation should include drainage materials, fixture connections, traps, cleanouts, horizontal and vertical piping, slope, venting methods, drainage sizing, and installation requirements. Candidates should understand how proper venting protects trap seals and supports reliable drainage-system operation.

Water-supply and distribution questions may cover approved materials, service piping, distribution systems, valves, backflow protection, pressure control, sizing, fixture demand, water hammer, and installation practices. Candidates should be prepared to use code tables and apply the conditions or limitations associated with them.

## Open Book Test

The Delaware Master Plumber examination is an open-book, timed test. Candidates may use authorized references during the examination while following the testing provider’s current rules for approved titles, editions, highlighting, permanent tabs, notes, inserts, calculators, and other materials.

An open-book examination still requires thorough preparation. Candidates must complete 100 questions within three hours. That schedule leaves limited time for searching unfamiliar books, so successful preparation should combine plumbing knowledge with efficient reference navigation.

The *International Plumbing Code, 2018* supports questions involving general plumbing requirements, fixtures, water supply, sanitary drainage, vents, traps, storm drainage, water heaters, materials, testing, and system installation. Develop a repeatable search process before test day. Begin by identifying the primary subject and important terms in the question. Choose the reference most likely to contain the answer, use the index or table of contents, and read the complete applicable section.

Do not rely only on the first sentence or table entry that appears relevant. Review nearby definitions, exceptions, diagrams, notes, and cross-references. A small condition in the question may change which code provision or calculation applies.

## Licensing Steps

The Delaware Board of Plumbing, Heating, Ventilation, Air Conditioning and Refrigeration Examiners regulates Master Plumber licenses. Applicants must complete the state licensing process and receive Board approval before taking the Master Plumber examination.

1. **Create or access a DELPROS account.** Delaware professional license applications are submitted through the Delaware Professional Regulation Online Services system.
2. **Complete the Master Plumber application.** Provide the requested personal, apprenticeship, employment, experience, examination, and licensing information.
3. **Submit license verifications.** Arrange for verification from each jurisdiction where you currently hold or have previously held a professional license, when applicable.
4. **Document plumbing experience.** Submit the employment verification and supporting records required for the licensing pathway under which you are applying.
5. **Provide apprenticeship records when applicable.** Applicants using the journeyperson pathway must provide the required apprenticeship completion documentation.
6. **Complete the Apprenticeship By-Pass Exam when required.** Applicants without a qualifying journeyperson certificate must first receive Board approval and satisfy the applicable bypass examination requirement.
7. **Receive approval for the Master Plumber exam.** The Board reviews the completed application and supporting documentation before authorizing the applicant to test.
8. **Register with the testing provider.** Follow the registration and scheduling instructions included with the candidate information provided after approval.
9. **Take the Master Plumber examination.** Follow the current identification, reference-book, calculator, appointment, and testing-conduct requirements.
10. **Complete all remaining requirements.** The Board must receive the required examination result and supporting records before issuing the professional license.

Applicants should begin collecting official records early. Apprenticeship certificates, employment verification, and license verification from other jurisdictions may require action by employers, supervisors, training programs, or licensing authorities.

Purchasing this exam book package does not establish eligibility for Delaware licensure or register the buyer for the examination. Each applicant remains responsible for completing the application, documenting qualifications, paying applicable fees, receiving approval, and satisfying all licensing requirements.

## State Requirements

Delaware provides two primary experience pathways for Master Plumber applicants applying by examination. The documentation required depends on whether the applicant possesses a journeyperson certificate issued through a federally approved plumbing apprenticeship program.

An applicant with a qualifying journeyperson certificate must submit a copy of the apprenticeship program Certificate of Completion and proof of at least two years of plumbing work experience under the supervision of a master licensee after receiving the certificate.

An applicant without a qualifying journeyperson certificate must provide proof of at least seven years of plumbing work experience under the supervision of a master licensee. The Board reviews the experience before authorizing the applicant to take the Apprenticeship By-Pass Exam.

After receiving authorization, an applicant using the experience pathway must pass the Apprenticeship By-Pass Exam with a score of at least 70 percent and arrange for the Board to receive the examination results. The applicant may then continue through the approval process for the Master Plumber examination.

Employment verification should document the dates, supervision, and nature of the applicant’s plumbing work. Acceptance of experience documentation is determined by the Delaware Board.

A Delaware Master Plumber can perform plumbing services involving water-supply piping, fixtures, sanitary drainage, waste removal, and gas piping. A separate Master HVACR or Master HVACR Restricted license is required for work involving products or equipment used in heating, air conditioning, refrigeration, ventilation, or process cooling or heating systems.

A Delaware professional Master Plumber license is separate from a Delaware business license. A plumber planning to operate a contracting company may also need entity registration, tax accounts, insurance, permits, and other state or local business credentials.

## Test Information and Study Materials

Create a study schedule based on the published examination categories. Divide general knowledge, plan reading, plumbing math, drainage, venting, water distribution, fixtures, water heaters, fuel gas, storm drainage, and safety into manageable review sessions.

Spend time practicing code navigation. Select common subjects such as cleanouts, vent sizing, fixture connections, water heaters, gas piping, or roof drainage and locate the applicable provisions without using an answer key.

Practice plumbing calculations separately from code searches. Write each step clearly, track units of measurement, and confirm that the result matches the type of answer requested. Review formulas and calculation methods until they can be applied without unnecessary trial and error.

For plan reading, practice interpreting symbols, elevations, dimensions, fixture locations, piping routes, branches, stacks, vents, and developed lengths. Translate the drawing into the information needed for sizing or code application.
