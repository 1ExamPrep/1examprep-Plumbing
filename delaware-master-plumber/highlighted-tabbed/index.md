## Exam Details

The Delaware Master Plumber examination evaluates the knowledge needed to provide plumbing services involving the design, installation, construction, replacement, service, repair, alteration, and modification of piping, fixtures, and related equipment used to bring water into a building and remove liquid and water-carried waste. The authorized plumbing scope also includes the installation and connection of gas piping.

The examination contains 100 questions and allows three hours for completion. The published subject areas include:

- General Knowledge: 16 questions
- Isometrics and Plan Reading: 8 questions
- Plumbing Math: 8 questions
- Drain, Waste, and Vent Systems: 14 questions
- Water Supply and Distribution: 14 questions
- Fixtures and Fittings: 8 questions
- Water Heaters: 10 questions
- Fuel Gas Systems: 10 questions
- Storm and Roof Drainage: 6 questions
- Safety: 6 questions

General knowledge questions may involve plumbing terminology, approved materials, fittings, tools, installation methods, testing procedures, inspections, system components, and standard trade practices. Candidates should understand how individual components contribute to the performance and safety of the complete plumbing system.

Isometric and plan-reading questions may require candidates to interpret symbols, dimensions, elevations, fixture locations, piping routes, branches, stacks, vents, developed lengths, and system layouts. Candidates should practice translating a drawing into the information needed for code application, sizing, or calculation.

Plumbing mathematics may involve measurements, areas, volumes, slopes, offsets, developed lengths, pressure, flow, fixture units, pipe sizing, and other trade calculations. Candidates should practice organizing the information provided, selecting the correct method, tracking units, and confirming that the final result is reasonable.

Drain, waste, and vent preparation should include drainage materials, horizontal and vertical piping, fixture connections, traps, cleanouts, slope, drainage sizing, venting methods, and installation requirements. Candidates should understand how proper drainage and venting support flow while protecting fixture trap seals.

Water-supply and distribution questions may address approved materials, service piping, distribution systems, valves, pressure control, backflow protection, sizing, fixture demand, water hammer, and installation practices. Candidates should become comfortable using code tables and applying the conditions and limitations connected with each table.

## Licensing Steps

The Delaware Board of Plumbing, Heating, Ventilation, Air Conditioning and Refrigeration Examiners regulates Master Plumber licenses. Applicants must complete the Delaware professional licensing process and receive Board approval before taking the Master Plumber examination.

1. **Create or access a DELPROS account.** Delaware professional license applications are submitted through the state’s online licensing system.
2. **Complete the Master Plumber application.** Provide the requested personal, apprenticeship, employment, experience, examination, and licensing information.
3. **Arrange for license verification.** Verification may be required from jurisdictions where the applicant currently holds or previously held a professional license.
4. **Document plumbing experience.** Submit employment verification and supporting records for the licensing pathway under which the application is filed.
5. **Provide apprenticeship documentation when applicable.** Applicants using the journeyperson pathway must submit the required apprenticeship completion records.
6. **Complete the apprenticeship bypass requirement when applicable.** Applicants without a qualifying journeyperson certificate must receive approval and complete the required bypass examination process.
7. **Receive approval for the Master Plumber examination.** The Board reviews the application and supporting records before authorizing the applicant to test.
8. **Register with the testing provider.** Follow the examination scheduling instructions provided after approval.
9. **Take the examination.** Follow the current identification, calculator, reference-book, appointment, and testing-conduct requirements.
10. **Complete remaining licensing requirements.** The Board must receive the required examination result and all supporting documentation before issuing the license.

Applicants should begin collecting official documentation early. Apprenticeship records, employment verification, and license verification from other jurisdictions may require action by employers, supervisors, training programs, or licensing authorities.

## State Requirements

Delaware provides different qualification pathways for Master Plumber applicants applying by examination. The required documentation depends in part on whether the applicant holds a journeyperson certificate issued after completion of a qualifying plumbing apprenticeship program.

An applicant with a qualifying journeyperson certificate must provide the applicable apprenticeship completion documentation and proof of the required plumbing experience under the supervision of a master licensee after receiving the certificate.

An applicant without a qualifying journeyperson certificate must document the required period of plumbing work experience under the supervision of a master licensee. This applicant must also complete the applicable apprenticeship bypass process before continuing to the Master Plumber examination.

Employment verification should describe the dates, supervision, and nature of the plumbing work performed. The Delaware Board determines whether submitted experience and supporting records satisfy the licensing requirements.

Applicable experience may involve water-supply piping, sanitary drainage, venting, fixtures, water heaters, gas piping, storm drainage, system testing, installation, service, repair, alteration, and related plumbing work performed under qualified supervision.

A Delaware Master Plumber license authorizes plumbing services within the scope established by the state. Work involving heating, air conditioning, refrigeration, ventilation, or process cooling and heating equipment may require a separate Master HVACR or Master HVACR Restricted license.

A Delaware professional license is separate from a Delaware business license. A plumber planning to operate a contracting company may also need entity registration, tax accounts, insurance, permits, and other state or local business credentials.
