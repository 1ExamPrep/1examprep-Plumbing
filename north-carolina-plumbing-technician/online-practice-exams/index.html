Licensing Steps

The North Carolina State Board of Examiners of Plumbing, Heating, and Fire Sprinkler Contractors administers the Plumbing Technician licensing process. Candidates should complete the applicable experience, application, examination, background, and license-activation requirements.

  1. Choose the appropriate Plumbing Technician classification. Determine whether the Class I or Class II technician qualification matches the work and experience associated with the license being pursued.
  2. Gain qualifying hands-on experience. Applicants must document applicable experience in the installation, maintenance, service, or repair of plumbing systems related to the category sought.
  3. Document qualifying employment. Experience documentation should accurately identify the work performed and the license holder under whose supervision the qualifying experience was obtained when applicable.
  4. Obtain the required reference books. Candidates should have the current required code books and Board reference materials before applying for examination.
  5. Submit the Plumbing Technician application. Provide the required application information, experience documentation, and supporting materials to the Board.
  6. Complete the applicable background process. Follow the current Board application instructions for background-report requirements.
  7. Receive examination approval. Candidates proceed to testing after their application and qualifications are reviewed and approved.
  8. Schedule the Plumbing Technician examination. Follow the examination scheduling instructions provided after eligibility is granted.
  9. Prepare for technical plumbing subjects. Review water supply, drainage, venting, fixtures, traps, piping, materials, and related code requirements.
  10. Prepare for fuel gas. Study gas piping, sizing, appliance connections, combustion air, venting, testing, and related requirements.
  11. Review North Carolina administrative requirements. Use the Administration Code and Policies reference for code-administration preparation.
  12. Study Board Laws and Rules. Become familiar with the licensing requirements, technician responsibilities, and regulatory provisions administered by the Board.
  13. Complete the examination. Satisfy the technical examination requirement for the technician classification sought.
  14. Complete final licensing requirements. Follow the Board's instructions for license issuance or activation after the applicable requirements have been met.

State Requirements

North Carolina offers Plumbing Technician qualifications with Class I and Class II distinctions. Applicants should select the technician classification that corresponds with their qualifying experience and intended scope of work.

For Plumbing Technician Class I eligibility, the standard experience requirement is 18 months, or 3,000 hours, of applicable on-site full-time experience in the installation, maintenance, service, or repair of plumbing systems related to the category sought.

For Plumbing Technician Class II eligibility, the standard experience requirement is 15 months, or 2,500 hours, of applicable on-site full-time experience related to the requested plumbing qualification.

Experience should be documented carefully. The Board's technician application process requires applicants to provide information supporting the qualifying experience claimed, and experience verification should accurately describe the work performed.

The technician credential differs from a contractor license. A technician qualification demonstrates technical competency in the licensed field, but it does not provide the same independent contracting authority associated with holding the applicable contractor qualification.

Candidates should understand the limitations of the technician credential and the supervision, employment, and licensing conditions that apply to work performed under that qualification.

North Carolina also requires applicants to follow current application and background procedures. Applicants should keep their documentation organized so experience verification, employment information, and other required materials are available during the licensing process.

The current Board Laws and Rules reference is especially important for understanding these professional responsibilities. Candidates should study the licensing material alongside technical plumbing codes rather than concentrating only on installation questions.

Candidates should practice deciding which book contains the most likely answer before beginning a lookup. This simple habit can improve reference efficiency and reduce unnecessary searching across several books.
