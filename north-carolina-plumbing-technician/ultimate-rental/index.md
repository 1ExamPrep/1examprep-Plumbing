## Exam Details

The North Carolina Plumbing Technician examination is part of the licensing process administered by the North Carolina State Board of Examiners of Plumbing, Heating & Fire Sprinkler Contractors. Examinations are computer based and are administered through PSI testing centers.

The Plumbing Technician technical examination has a four-hour testing period. Board examinations require a passing grade of 70. Applicants who receive examination authorization must schedule and take the examination within the authorization period provided by the Board, which is generally 90 days from the eligibility notification.

The examination process focuses on the technical knowledge required for plumbing work as well as applicable North Carolina Board laws and rules. Candidates should be prepared to work with plumbing code requirements, water distribution systems, drainage and venting, fixtures, materials, fuel gas provisions, residential applications, code administration, inspections, and regulatory responsibilities.

An important distinction applies to this credential: a North Carolina Plumbing Technician license is a technician license and is not itself a contracting license. A Plumbing Technician may work within the scope permitted by the Board while employed by and appropriately associated with a licensed plumbing contractor. Class I and Class II technician classifications have different scopes, so candidates should apply for the classification that corresponds to their experience and intended work.

## State Requirements

North Carolina Plumbing Technician applicants must meet experience requirements established by the State Board. For Class I technician licensure, applicants must have 18 months, or 3,000 hours, of qualifying on-site full-time experience in the installation, maintenance, service, or repair of plumbing systems related to the license category. Class II technician applicants must have 15 months, or 2,500 hours, of qualifying experience.

The Class I Plumbing Technician scope covers potable water and drain, waste, and vent piping systems. A Class I Plumbing Technician must operate within the technician licensing structure established by the Board and may serve as a sublicensee of a Plumbing Contractor where applicable.

The Class II Plumbing Technician classification also addresses potable water and drain, waste, and vent piping systems but is limited in scope. Class II technicians may supervise qualifying work in single-family detached dwellings within the limits of their classification.

A technician credential should not be confused with a North Carolina Plumbing Contractor license. The Board specifically identifies technician licensure as a separate credential. Candidates who intend to contract directly with the public should understand the difference between technician and contractor qualifications before selecting a licensing path.

The Board currently accepts applications throughout the year. Once approved, applicants receive examination authorization and scheduling information. Candidates who fail an examination must follow the Board's re-examination procedures and applicable waiting period before testing again.
