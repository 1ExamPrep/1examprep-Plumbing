Exam Details

The Oklahoma Plumbing Contractor and Natural Gas examination evaluates knowledge associated with plumbing installation, water supply, sanitary drainage, vents, piping materials, fixtures, fuel gas systems, and construction safety.

The Oklahoma Construction Industries Board administers plumbing contractor licensing, and PSI delivers the examination.

  • Exam Name: Oklahoma Plumbing Contractor and Natural Gas
  • Licensing Authority: Oklahoma Construction Industries Board
  • Testing Provider: PSI
  • Number of Scored Questions: 85
  • Time Allowed: 180 minutes
  • Passing Score: 75%
  • Minimum Correct Answers: 64
  • Exam Format: Open book

The technical examination includes ten principal subject areas:

  • General Plumbing Knowledge and Regulations: 10 questions
  • Piping, Valves, and Control Valves: 8 questions
  • Fixtures and Equipment: 8 questions
  • Water Supply: 10 questions
  • Drains and Sewers: 10 questions
  • Vents: 13 questions
  • Traps, Interceptors, Indirect, and Special Waste: 5 questions
  • Isometric Analysis: 7 questions
  • Fuel Gas: 10 questions
  • Safety: 4 questions

Venting represents the largest individual examination category, with 13 questions. General plumbing knowledge, water supply, drains and sewers, and fuel gas are also heavily represented.

Candidates should review the full examination outline while giving additional attention to venting systems, pipe sizing, drainage, and fuel gas installation requirements.

The contractor licensing process also includes a separate Plumbing Business and Law examination. This highlighted and tabbed package focuses on the four listed technical references.

The Oklahoma Plumbing Contractor and Natural Gas examination is an open-book test. Candidates may consult approved examination references according to PSI testing requirements.

Open-book testing requires candidates to understand technical requirements and locate relevant information efficiently. Familiarity with code chapters, indexes, technical tables, diagrams, and mathematical formulas is essential for preparing effectively.

For the Oklahoma Plumbing Contractor and Gas examination, the updated code examination transition began October 8, 2026, and continues through January 8, 2027. During this transition period, candidates may take the affected examination using the previously adopted or newly adopted codes.

Beginning January 9, 2027, the Oklahoma Plumbing Contractor and Gas examination will be based exclusively on the newly adopted codes.

State Requirements

Plumbing and natural gas contracting in Oklahoma is regulated by the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board under the state's plumbing licensing laws and administrative regulations.

Plumbing contractors perform work involving water supply systems, sanitary drainage, plumbing fixtures, venting, piping installations, and other regulated plumbing activities. Natural gas piping work also requires familiarity with fuel gas materials, sizing methods, regulators, testing, and installation requirements.

The Oklahoma Uniform Building Code Commission adopts statewide construction codes, including the International Plumbing Code and International Fuel Gas Code, with applicable Oklahoma amendments.

Contractors must understand the importance of properly installed systems, approved materials, appropriate testing procedures, and compliance with applicable technical requirements.

Contractor licensing is separate from journeyman licensing. Operating an independent plumbing contracting business requires the appropriate contractor authorization and compliance with applicable state requirements.

Construction safety remains an important professional responsibility. OSHA construction standards address excavation, personal protective equipment, fall protection, ladders, scaffolding, tools, and other jobsite hazards.

Test Information and Study Materials

Effective preparation for the Oklahoma Plumbing and Natural Gas Contractor examination combines plumbing knowledge, fuel gas code review, trade mathematics, and construction safety.

General Plumbing Knowledge: Review terminology, general installation requirements, permitting concepts, inspections, testing, measurements, and code definitions.

Water Supply Systems: Study water distribution, pipe sizing, materials, valves, backflow prevention, pressure, and applicable installation requirements.

Drainage and Sewer Systems: Review drainage piping, cleanouts, drainage fixture units, slopes, building drains, and sewer installation requirements.

Venting Systems: Study vent sizing, vent connections, wet venting, vent installation, and the purpose of plumbing vent systems. Venting is the largest individual examination category.

Fixtures and Equipment: Review plumbing fixtures, water heaters, installation requirements, clearances, and associated piping connections.

Traps and Interceptors: Develop familiarity with trap requirements, indirect waste, interceptors, and special waste provisions.

Fuel Gas Systems: Study gas pipe sizing, regulators, piping materials, valves, supports, appliance connections, combustion air, testing, and inspection.

Plumbing Mathematics: Practice measurements, area, volume, pipe offsets, unit conversions, and calculations associated with plumbing installations.

Isometric Analysis: Review plumbing isometric drawings and develop familiarity with interpreting piping layouts and connections.

Construction Safety: Study OSHA requirements covering excavation, protective equipment, fall protection, ladders, tools, and related jobsite hazards.

Reference Navigation: Practice locating requirements using highlighted sections, tabs, indexes, code tables, and diagrams.

Time Management: Complete timed study exercises involving code research and calculations to develop efficient examination habits.

Consistent preparation across these subjects helps candidates develop technical understanding and familiarity with the examination references.
