Exam Details

The Oklahoma Plumbing Contractor and Natural Gas examination is administered through PSI after the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board determines that an applicant meets the applicable licensing qualifications and is eligible to proceed with testing.

  • Exam: Oklahoma Plumbing Contractor and Natural Gas
  • Number of Questions: 85 scored questions
  • Passing Score: 75%
  • Passing Raw Score: 64 correct answers
  • Time Allowed: 180 minutes
  • Testing Provider: PSI

The technical examination is divided into the following major subject areas:

  • General Plumbing Knowledge and Regulations: 10 questions
  • Piping, Valves, and Control Valves: 8 questions
  • Fixtures and Equipment: 8 questions
  • Water Supply: 10 questions
  • Drains and Sewers: 10 questions
  • Vents: 13 questions
  • Traps, Interceptors, Indirect, and Special Waste: 5 questions
  • Isometric Analysis: 7 questions
  • Fuel Gas: 10 questions
  • Safety: 4 questions

Vents is the largest individual examination category with 13 questions. Candidates should understand vent installation, developed length, sizing, connections, and the relationship between vent systems and sanitary drainage.

General Plumbing Knowledge and Regulations contains 10 questions. Preparation should include head pressure, pipe joints, solvent-welded joints, calculations of area and volume, water-supply friction loss, permits and inspections, job costs, structural penetrations, fire integrity, pipe offsets, trenching, excavation, backfill, and fitting identification.

Water Supply contains 10 questions involving water distribution lines, protection of potable water supplies, and backflow. Drains and Sewers contains another 10 questions addressing drain and sewer piping, building sewers, cleanouts, sewage ejectors and sump pumps, drain and waste piping, and storm drainage.

Fuel Gas contains 10 questions and is a significant part of this contractor examination. Study should include gas distribution pipe sizing, regulators, hangers and supports, changes in direction, concealed piping, joints and connections, valves, outlets, drip legs and sediment traps, appliances, combustion air, chimneys and vents, outdoor gas piping, testing, inspection, and piping material requirements.

Isometric Analysis contains seven questions. Candidates should practice reading plumbing isometric drawings and identifying how fixtures, drainage, venting, and piping arrangements relate to code and sizing requirements.

Exam Details (continued)

Safety contains four questions covering construction jobsite responsibilities such as personal protective equipment, excavation safety, trench protection, ventilation, first aid, signs and barricades, tools, ladders, scaffolds, hazardous materials, material handling, and fall protection.

Contractor candidates also prepare for the separate Oklahoma Plumbing Business and Law examination.

  • Plumbing Business and Law Questions: 50 scored questions
  • Passing Score: 75%
  • Passing Raw Score: 37 correct answers
  • Time Allowed: 120 minutes

Business and Law preparation includes bidding and estimating, project management and supervision, contracts, financial management, labor and personnel, risk management, insurance, bonds, payroll, payroll taxes, liens, and Oklahoma plumbing licensing requirements.

Open Book Test

The Oklahoma Plumbing Contractor and Natural Gas technical examination is an open-book test. Candidates are responsible for bringing permitted references into the testing center and following the applicable examination rules for those materials.

The technical examination reference list includes the International Plumbing Code, 2024 edition with Oklahoma revisions; International Fuel Gas Code, 2024 edition with Oklahoma revisions; Mathematics for Plumbers and Pipefitters, 8th Edition; and Code of Federal Regulations - 29 CFR Part 1926 (OSHA) with applicable amendments.

Approved reference materials may be highlighted, underlined, and indexed before testing. Permanent tabs may be used. Temporary tabs such as Post-it notes are not permitted. Candidates should organize and practice with their references before examination day.

The International Plumbing Code should be used for plumbing-system preparation involving fixtures, water supply, drainage, vents, traps, piping, and related code requirements. Candidates should learn the organization of the code and become comfortable using the index, chapter structure, definitions, tables, and cross-references.

The International Fuel Gas Code should be used for gas piping, regulators, sizing, joints, valves, outlets, appliance-related requirements, combustion air, venting, testing, and inspection. Candidates should practice identifying gas-system questions quickly so they can move directly to the appropriate reference.

Mathematics for Plumbers and Pipefitters supports trade calculations involving measurements, area, volume, pipe offsets, and other mathematical applications. Repeated calculation practice is important because mathematical questions can consume valuable exam time if the candidate is unfamiliar with the process.

Code of Federal Regulations - 29 CFR Part 1926 supports construction-safety preparation. Candidates should become familiar with how the OSHA reference is organized and know where major safety subjects can be located.

Licensing Steps

The Oklahoma Construction Industries Board regulates plumbing contractor licensing. Contractor applicants must satisfy applicable experience requirements before proceeding through the application and examination process.

  1. Gain qualifying plumbing trade experience. Build verifiable experience while working within Oklahoma's plumbing licensing and supervision structure.
  2. Maintain apprentice registration when applicable. Oklahoma plumbing experience used toward licensing qualifications must be properly documented through apprentice registration unless the experience was lawfully obtained under an applicable exemption.
  3. Meet journeyman-level qualifications. Contractor applicants must satisfy the applicable requirements established for Oklahoma plumbing journeyman licensing.
  4. Gain additional contractor-level experience. Plumbing contractor applicants must meet the journeyman requirements plus an additional year of qualifying plumbing trade experience.
  5. Submit the plumbing contractor application. Provide the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board with the required application and supporting experience documentation.
  6. Receive examination authorization. The applicant's qualifications are reviewed before testing authorization is provided.
  7. Schedule the required examinations. Approved candidates arrange testing through PSI.
  8. Prepare for Plumbing Contractor and Natural Gas. Study plumbing systems, fuel gas, mathematics, isometric analysis, safety, and reference navigation.
  9. Prepare for Plumbing Business and Law. Review Oklahoma plumbing licensing law, regulations, lien law, estimating, contracts, project management, finances, and contractor responsibilities.
  10. Complete the required examinations. Achieve the applicable passing scores required for contractor licensing.
  11. Complete active contractor requirements. Address applicable bond, commercial general liability insurance, and other requirements associated with active contractor status.

State Requirements

Applicants for an Oklahoma plumbing contractor license must be at least 18 years old. Contractor applicants must meet the same basic qualifications as a plumbing journeyman and have an additional year of qualifying plumbing trade experience.

One standard journeyman pathway requires three years of experience in the plumbing trade while employed by a licensed plumbing contractor. Equivalent verifiable plumbing experience gained through qualifying United States military service may also be considered.

Applicants holding a current and good-standing out-of-state plumbing license may also qualify when the license and supporting experience meet Oklahoma requirements.

Approved plumbing education may substitute for part of the required trade experience. Candidates using education toward the qualification requirement should maintain appropriate documentation showing completion of the approved classroom program.

Oklahoma plumbing experience used toward licensing generally must be verifiable through apprentice registration unless the work was lawfully performed under an applicable exemption. Candidates should maintain organized records of employment, apprenticeship, education, and trade experience.

Contractor licensing extends beyond passing examinations. Active plumbing contractors must satisfy applicable bond and commercial general liability insurance requirements and maintain their licensing status in accordance with Oklahoma requirements.

Test Information and Study Materials

A productive Oklahoma Plumbing and Natural Gas Contractor study plan should divide preparation into plumbing code, fuel gas, mathematics, isometric analysis, OSHA safety, reference navigation, and Business and Law.

Prioritize venting. Vents is the largest individual exam category. Review vent installation, developed length, connections, sizing concepts, and how vent systems work with sanitary drainage and plumbing fixtures.

Strengthen water-supply and drainage knowledge. Review water distribution, potable water protection, backflow, sanitary drainage, building sewers, cleanouts, sewage ejectors, sump pumps, waste piping, and storm drainage.

Practice fuel-gas questions. Study gas distribution sizing, regulators, supports, joints, valves, outlets, sediment traps, appliances, combustion air, chimneys, vents, outdoor supply piping, inspections, testing, and materials.

Practice plumbing mathematics. Work through area, volume, friction-loss, pipe-offset, and measurement problems regularly. Develop a consistent method for identifying the values given in a question and selecting the appropriate formula or calculation process.

Work on isometric analysis. Practice interpreting piping drawings and identifying how fixtures, drains, vents, and piping arrangements connect. Use drawing practice to reinforce code navigation and system understanding.

Prepare for OSHA safety. Review excavation, trenching, PPE, ventilation, ladders, scaffolds, fall protection, signs and barricades, tools, hazardous materials, and material handling.

Study Business and Law separately. Use the Oklahoma statutes, administrative regulations, lien law, fine schedule, and NASCLA contractor guide to prepare for estimating, project management, contracts, finances, personnel, risk management, and licensing responsibilities.
