Exam Details

The Oklahoma Plumbing Contractor and Natural Gas examination evaluates technical knowledge involving plumbing systems, natural gas piping, code requirements, plumbing calculations, and construction safety.

The Oklahoma Construction Industries Board administers plumbing contractor licensing, while PSI administers the examination.

  • Examination: Oklahoma Plumbing Contractor and Natural Gas
  • Licensing Authority: Oklahoma Construction Industries Board
  • Testing Provider: PSI
  • Scored Questions: 85
  • Time Allowed: 180 minutes
  • Passing Score: 75%
  • Minimum Correct Answers: 64
  • Examination Format: Open book

The examination includes the following technical content areas:

  • General Plumbing Knowledge and Regulations: 10 questions.
  • Piping, Valves, and Control Valves: 8 questions.
  • Fixtures and Equipment: 8 questions.
  • Water Supply: 10 questions.
  • Drains and Sewers: 10 questions.
  • Vents: 13 questions.
  • Traps, Interceptors, Indirect, and Special Waste: 5 questions.
  • Isometric Analysis: 7 questions.
  • Fuel Gas: 10 questions.
  • Safety: 4 questions.

Venting represents the largest individual examination category. Water supply, drainage, general plumbing knowledge, and fuel gas are also important areas of preparation.

Contractor applicants also complete a separate Plumbing Business and Law examination. The five additional regulatory and business references included in this package support preparation involving contractor responsibilities beyond technical plumbing work.

Open Book Test

The Oklahoma Plumbing Contractor and Natural Gas examination is an open-book test. Candidates may use approved examination references according to PSI testing requirements.

Open-book testing requires both technical understanding and familiarity with reference organization. Candidates need to identify the subject of each question, locate the applicable provision, and interpret technical information within the available testing time.

Oklahoma adopted the 2024 International Plumbing Code and 2024 International Fuel Gas Code, with applicable state amendments effective September 14, 2026.

For the Oklahoma Plumbing Contractor and Natural Gas examination, the code transition period runs from October 8, 2026, through January 8, 2027. During this period, candidates may take the affected examination using either the previously adopted or newly adopted codes.

Beginning January 9, 2027, the affected examination will be based exclusively on the newly adopted codes.

Licensing Steps

Oklahoma Plumbing and Natural Gas Contractor licensing is administered by the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board. Applicants must meet the applicable qualifications and complete the required licensing procedures.

  1. Understand the Contractor Classification: Review the plumbing and natural gas work associated with the Oklahoma Plumbing Contractor license.
  2. Meet Experience Requirements: Satisfy the applicable plumbing trade experience or qualifying education requirements.
  3. Prepare for the Technical Examination: Study plumbing systems, fuel gas installation, construction mathematics, and safety.
  4. Review Business and Law: Study Oklahoma licensing regulations, contracts, construction liens, and contractor business responsibilities.
  5. Submit the Licensing Application: Complete the appropriate application through the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board.
  6. Obtain Examination Authorization: Complete the Board's application review process.
  7. Schedule Required Examinations: Arrange testing through PSI after authorization.
  8. Complete Licensing Requirements: Pass the required examinations and fulfill the remaining requirements for contractor licensure.

Oklahoma plumbing contractor applicants must meet journeyman-level eligibility requirements plus an additional year of qualifying plumbing trade experience. Approved plumbing educational programs may substitute for portions of the experience requirement under applicable state rules.

Understanding these steps helps applicants coordinate technical examination preparation with the administrative requirements of pursuing a contractor credential.

State Requirements

Plumbing and natural gas contracting in Oklahoma falls within the state's regulated plumbing industry. The Oklahoma Construction Industries Board administers licensing under the Oklahoma Plumbing License Law of 1955 and applicable Plumbing Industry Regulations.

Plumbing contractors need knowledge of water distribution, sanitary drainage, venting, plumbing fixtures, piping materials, testing, and installation requirements. Natural gas systems add responsibilities involving piping, regulators, sizing, connections, and related safety provisions.

Oklahoma's adopted plumbing and fuel gas codes establish technical requirements, subject to applicable state amendments. Contractors should understand how these provisions relate to installation practices and inspection responsibilities.

Contractor licensing differs from journeyman licensing. Operating an independent plumbing contracting business requires the appropriate contractor authorization and compliance with applicable professional obligations.

Business responsibilities can include contract administration, financial management, construction payment rights, and adherence to licensing regulations. The included Oklahoma legal references and NASCLA guide support study of these broader professional subjects.
