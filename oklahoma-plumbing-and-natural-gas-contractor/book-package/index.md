## Exam Details

The Oklahoma **Plumbing Contractor and Natural Gas** examination tests knowledge related to plumbing systems and natural gas piping within the scope of Oklahoma plumbing contractor licensing.

The technical examination contains **85 questions**. Candidates are allowed **180 minutes** to complete the test. The required passing score is **75%**.

The examination covers the following major content areas:

- **General Plumbing Knowledge and Regulations:** 10 questions
- **Piping, Valves, and Control Valves:** 8 questions
- **Fixtures and Equipment:** 8 questions
- **Water Supply:** 10 questions
- **Drains and Sewers:** 10 questions
- **Vents:** 13 questions
- **Traps, Interceptors, Indirect, and Special Waste:** 5 questions
- **Isometric Analysis:** 7 questions
- **Fuel Gas:** 10 questions
- **Safety:** 4 questions

Venting represents the largest individual portion of the examination, with 13 questions. Candidates should devote substantial preparation time to vent-system requirements while also studying the other major categories thoroughly.

General plumbing knowledge includes subjects such as plumbing calculations, pipe joints, permits and inspections, system testing, structural considerations, excavation, backfill, and other fundamental plumbing requirements.

Piping questions can involve the characteristics and installation requirements of multiple piping materials. Candidates should understand how different piping materials are used, joined, supported, and protected within plumbing systems.

Water supply, drains, and sewers each represent significant portions of the examination. Candidates should prepare for questions involving water distribution, potable water protection, backflow prevention, sanitary drainage, building drains, building sewers, cleanouts, waste piping, and other applicable plumbing requirements.

Fuel gas accounts for 10 questions and requires candidates to understand natural gas piping systems, sizing, materials, regulators, supports, joints, valves, outlets, combustion air, appliances, venting, testing, and inspection.

## Open Book Test

The Oklahoma Plumbing Contractor and Natural Gas examination is an **open-book test**. Candidates may use approved reference materials during the examination in accordance with testing-center requirements.

Approved references may be highlighted, underlined, and indexed before the examination session. Permanent tabs may be used to organize references. Temporary tabs such as Post-it notes are not permitted.

Reference materials may not contain written notes added before or during the examination. Candidates should prepare their books according to the applicable testing rules before arriving at the examination center.

The open-book format makes reference familiarity especially valuable. Candidates have 180 minutes to answer 85 questions, so searching through unfamiliar books for every question can consume valuable testing time.

During preparation, practice identifying the subject first. If a question involves sanitary drainage, water distribution, vents, or plumbing fixtures, the International Plumbing Code may be the appropriate reference. Questions involving natural gas piping, regulators, combustion air, or gas appliance venting may direct you toward the International Fuel Gas Code.

Mathematical questions may require calculations involving measurements, area, volume, piping, or other plumbing applications. Construction safety questions may require information contained in OSHA 29 CFR Part 1926.

Repeated practice using all four references can help candidates develop a more efficient lookup process and become comfortable moving between different types of technical information.

## State Requirements

Oklahoma plumbing contractor applicants must meet the qualification requirements applicable to a plumbing journeyman and complete an additional **one year of plumbing trade experience** for contractor-level licensing.

The state regulates plumbing contractors through the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board. Contractor candidates must complete the required application and examination process before qualifying for active contractor status.

Oklahoma plumbing contractor applicants are required to pass both the applicable plumbing examination and the Plumbing Business and Law examination. The technical examination focuses on plumbing and natural gas knowledge, while the Business and Law examination addresses contractor responsibilities and business-related requirements.

After successful completion of the required examinations, a contractor license remains inactive until the required bond and insurance documentation is received by the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board.

An active Oklahoma plumbing contractor must maintain a **$5,000 corporate surety bond** payable to the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board. The bond must meet applicable state requirements and remain current while the contractor maintains active status.

An active plumbing contractor must also provide evidence of at least **$50,000 in commercial general liability insurance**. Contractors are responsible for maintaining current insurance coverage to keep their license active.

Contractor licensing involves responsibilities beyond technical plumbing work. Licensed contractors must comply with applicable Oklahoma requirements related to supervision, licensing, insurance, contracts, advertising, and other regulated activities associated with operating a plumbing business.

## Test Information and Study Materials

A strong Oklahoma Plumbing and Natural Gas Contractor exam study plan should divide preparation across the major examination topics while including frequent reference-navigation practice.

**General plumbing knowledge** should include plumbing terminology, calculations, testing, pipe joints, structural considerations, permits and inspections, and other fundamental requirements. Candidates should understand both basic trade concepts and where supporting information is located in the references.

**Piping, valves, and control valves** require familiarity with common plumbing materials. Review installation and connection requirements for materials such as PVC, CPVC, copper, galvanized piping, polyethylene, PEX, cast iron, and steel where applicable.

**Fixtures and equipment** should be studied with attention to installation requirements and applicable code provisions. Candidates should know how to locate requirements affecting common plumbing fixtures and equipment.

**Water supply** accounts for 10 questions. Study water distribution, potable water protection, cross-connection control, backflow prevention, piping requirements, and related provisions. Practice finding applicable tables and code sections efficiently.

**Drains and sewers** also account for 10 questions. Candidates should review building drains, building sewers, waste piping, cleanouts, sewage ejectors, sump pumps, storm drainage, and other drainage-system requirements.

**Vents** represent the largest individual exam category. Review vent installation, sizing, developed length, connections, termination requirements, and other applicable provisions. Because venting accounts for 13 questions, this area deserves substantial study time.

**Traps, interceptors, indirect waste, and special waste** should also be reviewed. Candidates should understand the function of these components and know how to locate applicable code requirements.

**Isometric analysis** involves interpreting plumbing system layouts and applying technical knowledge to drawings. Mathematical skills can be useful when working through measurements, dimensions, offsets, and other information presented in isometric problems.

**Fuel gas** requires focused preparation using the International Fuel Gas Code. Review gas distribution pipe sizing, regulators, supports, direction changes, concealed piping, joints and connections, valves, outlets, drip legs, sediment traps, appliances, combustion air, chimneys, vents, testing, inspection, and piping materials.

Pipe sizing should be practiced directly from the code. Instead of memorizing every table, learn how to recognize which table applies, identify the required variables, and use the information accurately.
