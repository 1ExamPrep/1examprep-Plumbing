Exam Details

The Oklahoma Plumbing Contractor and Natural Gas examination evaluates technical knowledge associated with plumbing systems, fuel gas piping, installation requirements, trade calculations, and construction safety.

The Oklahoma Construction Industries Board administers the applicable contractor licensing program, while PSI delivers the licensing examination.

  • Exam Name: Oklahoma Plumbing Contractor and Natural Gas
  • Licensing Authority: Oklahoma Construction Industries Board
  • Testing Provider: PSI
  • Number of Scored Questions: 85
  • Time Allowed: 180 minutes
  • Passing Score: 75%
  • Minimum Correct Answers: 64
  • Exam Format: Open book

The technical examination includes ten principal content areas:

  • General Plumbing Knowledge and Regulations: 10 questions.
  • Piping, Valves, and Control Valves: 8 questions.
  • Fixtures and Equipment: 8 questions.
  • Water Supply: 10 questions.
  • Drains and Sewers: 10 questions.
  • Vents: 13 questions.
  • Traps, Interceptors, Indirect, and Special Waste: 5 questions.
  • Isometric Analysis: 7 questions.
  • Fuel Gas: 10 questions.
  • Safety: 4 questions.

Venting represents the largest individual examination category, followed by several important subjects involving water supply, drainage, general plumbing knowledge, and fuel gas systems.

Contractor applicants also complete a separate Plumbing Business and Law examination. The additional Oklahoma licensing and business references in this package support preparation for professional and administrative responsibilities.

Open Book Test

The Oklahoma Plumbing Contractor and Natural Gas examination is an open-book test. Candidates may consult approved examination references under PSI testing requirements.

Open-book examinations require familiarity with technical materials and the ability to locate code provisions efficiently. Candidates must identify the subject being tested, select the appropriate reference, and interpret requirements within the examination time limit.

Oklahoma adopted the 2024 International Plumbing Code and 2024 International Fuel Gas Code with state amendments effective September 14, 2026.

The updated examination transition period for the Oklahoma Plumbing Contractor and Natural Gas examination runs from October 8, 2026, through January 8, 2027. During this period, affected candidates may test using either the previously adopted or newly adopted code.

Beginning January 9, 2027, the affected examination will use the newly adopted codes exclusively.

PSI allows approved examination references to be highlighted, underlined, and indexed. Permanent tabs are permitted, while temporary removable tabs are not allowed.

State Requirements

Plumbing and natural gas contracting in Oklahoma operates within the state's regulated plumbing industry. The Oklahoma Construction Industries Board administers the applicable plumbing licensing laws and administrative regulations.

Plumbing contracting involves work associated with water supply, drainage, venting, fixtures, piping systems, and related installations. Natural gas work adds technical responsibilities involving fuel gas piping, regulators, system connections, testing, and safety.

Oklahoma's adopted plumbing and fuel gas codes establish installation requirements, subject to applicable state amendments.

Contractor licensing differs from journeyman licensing. Operating an independent plumbing contracting business requires the appropriate contractor authorization and compliance with applicable professional requirements.

Contractors also need familiarity with construction business management, legal payment rights, administrative regulations, and professional obligations.

The additional Oklahoma legal and business references included in this Ultimate Package provide resources for studying these responsibilities.
