## State Requirements

Plumbing and natural gas contracting in Oklahoma operates within the state's regulated plumbing industry. The Oklahoma Construction Industries Board administers applicable licensing laws and industry regulations.

Plumbing contractor applicants generally must meet journeyman qualifications plus an additional year of qualifying plumbing trade experience. Oklahoma also recognizes specified educational substitutions and other eligibility pathways under applicable rules.

Contractor licensing differs from journeyman licensing. A plumbing professional pursuing independent contracting responsibilities must satisfy the requirements associated with the contractor credential.

To maintain active contractor status, Oklahoma generally requires a **$5,000 surety bond** or an authorized alternative and proof of at least **$50,000 in commercial general liability insurance**.

Plumbing contractors must also understand applicable installation codes, licensing regulations, and professional responsibilities. Natural gas contracting involves additional attention to fuel gas piping, regulator installation, testing, and system safety.
