Georgia Master Plumber Exam Details

The Georgia Master Plumber licensing examinations are administered by PSI under the authority of the Georgia State Construction Industry Licensing Board, Division of Master and Journeyman Plumbers.

Georgia offers two Master Plumber license classifications, each with its own examination and scope of work.

Georgia Master Plumber Class I - Restricted

The Georgia Master Plumber Class I license covers plumbing involving single-family dwellings, one-level dwellings designed for no more than two families, and commercial structures not exceeding 10,000 square feet.

The Class I examination evaluates plumbing installation knowledge, regulations, mathematics, safety practices, maintenance, system testing, and professional responsibilities.

Georgia Master Plumber Class II - Unrestricted

The Georgia Master Plumber Class II license is unrestricted and permits plumbing contracting beyond the limits of the Class I classification.

Class II preparation includes technical plumbing knowledge applicable to larger and more complex systems, including commercial and industrial plumbing applications.

Georgia Master Plumber Examination Overview:

  • License Classifications: Class I Restricted and Class II Unrestricted.
  • Licensing Authority: Georgia State Construction Industry Licensing Board, Division of Master and Journeyman Plumbers.
  • Testing Provider: PSI.
  • Exam Format: Computer-based, multiple-choice examination.
  • Class I Exam: 80 scored questions.
  • Class II Exam: 100 scored questions.
  • Time Allowed: Seven hours, divided into two parts of three and one-half hours each.
  • Passing Score: Minimum final scaled score of 70.
  • Exam Type: Closed Book.

The examinations assess knowledge of general laws, regulations, work planning, installation practices, system testing, maintenance, safety, and plumbing calculations.

Closed Book Test

The Georgia Master Plumber Class I and Class II examinations are closed book tests. Candidates cannot use reference books or other study materials during these examinations.

Because reference materials are not available during testing, candidates should develop a strong working understanding of plumbing requirements before examination day.

Effective closed-book preparation includes reviewing frequently encountered code provisions, practicing plumbing calculations, understanding installation methods, and strengthening knowledge of safety and administrative responsibilities.

Students should concentrate on understanding how plumbing systems operate and why specific installation requirements apply. Reviewing technical scenarios and practicing questions without reference materials can help reinforce knowledge.

The plumbing codes and technical books listed below remain important resources for preparation. They help students build the knowledge required to answer examination questions independently.

Licensing Steps

Obtaining a Georgia Master Plumber license involves meeting the state's qualifications, completing the licensing application process, receiving examination approval, and passing the required examination.

  1. Select Your License Classification: Determine whether the restricted Class I or unrestricted Class II license matches your experience and intended plumbing contracting activities.
  2. Review Experience Requirements: Evaluate your qualifying plumbing experience, including time spent working as a licensed journeyman plumber.
  3. Gather Supporting Documentation: Prepare experience records, supervising master plumber affidavits, professional references, identification documents, and other required application materials.
  4. Complete the Georgia Application: Submit your application through the Georgia Secretary of State licensing system with the required documentation and applicable fees.
  5. Receive Examination Approval: The licensing board evaluates applications and determines eligibility for examination.
  6. Schedule Your Examination: After receiving approval, follow PSI's registration and scheduling instructions.
  7. Prepare for the Examination: Review plumbing references, technical calculations, business regulations, and examination subjects through live virtual and self-study training.
  8. Take the Licensing Examination: Complete the applicable Master Plumber examination and achieve the minimum passing score.
  9. Complete License Issuance Requirements: Follow the Board's instructions for completing the licensing process.

Organizing your documentation and preparation materials can make the licensing process easier to manage.

Georgia State Requirements

Georgia establishes specific experience and qualification requirements for Master Plumber Class I and Class II applicants.

Master Plumber Class I Requirements: Under requirements effective July 1, 2026, applicants must document at least four years of qualifying full-time plumbing experience, including at least three years while licensed as a journeyman plumber. Applicants who obtained their journeyman license before March 1, 2025, are subject to a transitional provision requiring one of the four years while licensed as a journeyman plumber.

Master Plumber Class II Requirements: Applicants must document at least five years of qualifying full-time plumbing experience, including at least four years while licensed as a journeyman plumber. Applicants licensed as journeyman plumbers before March 1, 2025, are subject to a transitional provision requiring two years while holding that license.

Class II applicants must also demonstrate at least two years of qualifying experience performing plumbing work within the unrestricted scope of Class II licensing.

Qualifying experience must meet applicable supervision requirements and be supported by signed affidavits from the licensed master plumbers who supervised the work.

Applicants must submit required identity documentation, satisfy applicable background check requirements, and successfully complete the Board-approved licensing examination with a minimum score of 70.

Georgia's Master Plumber licensing requirements distinguish between practical plumbing experience, professional supervision, and examination competency. Meeting these requirements establishes the foundation for pursuing statewide master plumber licensure.

Test Information and Study Materials

Georgia Master Plumber examination preparation should address both practical plumbing knowledge and the regulatory responsibilities associated with professional contracting.

The Georgia Master Plumber examinations cover several important technical and administrative categories.

  • General Laws and Regulations: Georgia licensing requirements, accessibility provisions, excavation regulations, plumbing codes, permits, and compliance responsibilities.
  • Work Planning and Organization: Interpreting drawings, preparing material quantities, understanding equipment information, and coordinating plumbing work.
  • Pipe Cutting and Joining: Plumbing materials, pipe preparation, joining methods, and appropriate installation techniques.
  • Plumbing System Installation: Water distribution, drainage, venting, fuel gas systems, fixtures, equipment, and specialized plumbing applications.
  • System Testing: Pressure testing, drainage testing, plumbing inspections, and verification of system performance.
  • Maintenance and Repair: Troubleshooting leaks, servicing plumbing components, identifying system defects, and evaluating repair methods.
  • Safety Practices: Construction hazards, trench safety, gas piping safety, personal protective equipment, and workplace regulations.
  • Plumbing Mathematics: Pipe dimensions, measurement conversions, slope calculations, volume, pressure, and practical mathematical applications.

Georgia's 2024 Plumbing Code and Fuel Gas Code, including applicable state amendments, are important preparation resources. The examination references identify these 2024 code editions for examinations beginning May 1, 2026.

Because the Master Plumber examinations are closed book, students should study for understanding and practice solving questions without immediately consulting reference materials.
