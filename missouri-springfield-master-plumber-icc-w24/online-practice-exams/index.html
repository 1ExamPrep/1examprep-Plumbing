Exam Details

The Missouri (Springfield) Master Plumber W24-MO-S examination is part of the International Code Council (ICC) Contractor/Trades examination program. Testing is administered through Pearson VUE.

The examination evaluates knowledge of plumbing codes and installation requirements, including general regulations, fixtures, water distribution, sanitary drainage, venting, traps, and storm drainage.

  • Exam Name: Missouri (Springfield) Master Plumber
  • Exam Code: W24-MO-S
  • Examination Organization: International Code Council (ICC)
  • Testing Provider: Pearson VUE
  • Examination Format: Multiple-choice questions
  • Number of Questions: 100
  • Time Limit: 4 hours (240 minutes)
  • Exam Type: Open book
  • Designated Reference: International Plumbing Code, 2018 Edition
  • Online Practice Access: 3 months

The ICC examination outline divides the Master Plumber examination into seven content categories:

  • Administration and General Regulations – 46%: General installation requirements, code definitions, plan reading, testing, and materials.
  • Plumbing – 14%: Water heaters, water closets, urinals, bathtubs, showers, sinks, and lavatories.
  • Water Supply and Distribution – 11%: Water services, distribution piping, water pressure, and potable water protection.
  • Sanitary Drainage – 11%: Building drainage, sumps, ejectors, and indirect or special waste systems.
  • Plumbing Vent Requirements – 8%: Vent connections, grading, and vent terminals.
  • Traps, Interceptors, and Separators – 8%: Trap installation and wastewater separation equipment.
  • Storm Drainage – 2%: Stormwater drainage requirements.

Administration and General Regulations is the largest category, accounting for 46% of the examination. This makes general code knowledge, installation principles, plan interpretation, and testing especially important areas of preparation.

Plumbing fixtures represent another 14%, while Water Supply and Distribution and Sanitary Drainage each account for 11%.

Together, these four categories represent 82% of the examination. Candidates should give them substantial attention while maintaining familiarity with all seven subjects.

The four-hour examination provides an average of 2.4 minutes per question. Developing familiarity with the International Plumbing Code helps reduce the time spent searching for technical requirements.
