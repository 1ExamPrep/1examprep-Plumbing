## How does plumber licensing work in Florida?

The Florida Construction Industry Licensing Board (CILB) licenses plumbers here. The board approves your application first; then you schedule the exam and bring only the references the candidate bulletin allows.

- The Florida Construction Industry Licensing Board certifies plumbing contractors statewide; Pearson VUE gives the state trade exam.
- Florida journeyman and master plumber cards are issued by counties, which use Prov or GITS exams.
- Florida also certifies plumbing inspectors and plans examiners using ICC exams (1P, 2P and 3P).

### Can you bring books into the Florida exam?

Yes, for most exams here. The trade exam allows the references listed in the candidate bulletin, so highlighted and tabbed books save the most time.
