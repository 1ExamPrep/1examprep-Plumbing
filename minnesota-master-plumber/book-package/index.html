About this Master Plumber package

Prepare for the Minnesota Master Plumber licensing examination with a focused reference package built around the plumbing code, statutes, and regulatory requirements used by the Minnesota Department of Labor and Industry. The Minnesota Master Plumber Book Package centers on the Minnesota Plumbing Code, 2020 and the applicable provisions of Chapter 4714, Chapter 144, Chapter 325, and Chapter 326 that form the foundation of the state plumbing licensing examination.

The Minnesota Master Plumber license represents an advanced level of individual plumbing licensure. A licensed Master Plumber is qualified to plan, supervise, and perform plumbing installations within the statewide scope authorized by Minnesota law. Master plumbers may work with interior plumbing as well as exterior water service, sanitary sewer, and storm sewer systems within property lines.

The licensing examination is administered by the Minnesota Department of Labor and Industry. The examination evaluates a candidate’s ability to apply plumbing code requirements, perform plumbing calculations, understand installation requirements, and interpret Minnesota laws and regulations governing plumbing work.

The 2020 Minnesota Plumbing Code is based on the 2018 Uniform Plumbing Code with Minnesota amendments. It regulates plumbing systems throughout the state and covers subjects including water supply and distribution, drain-waste-vent systems, plumbing fixtures, backflow prevention, materials, roof drainage, indirect waste, interceptors, traps, vents, and nonpotable water systems.

Exam Details

The Minnesota Master Plumber examination is administered by the Minnesota Department of Labor and Industry. It is intended to measure the advanced practical and code knowledge expected of an individual seeking Master Plumber licensure.

  • Exam: Minnesota Master Plumber Licensing Examination
  • Licensing Agency: Minnesota Department of Labor and Industry
  • Exam Value: 300 points
  • Time Allowed: 5.5 hours
  • Passing Score: 70%
  • Exam Type: Open book using Department-provided references
  • Primary Code: 2020 Minnesota Plumbing Code
  • Additional Examination Material: Applicable Minnesota laws and rules

The Master Plumber examination is broader than a basic code-recognition test. Candidates may encounter standard multiple-choice questions, questions requiring more than one correct response, and calculation problems. Questions can also contain information that is not necessary to solve the problem, requiring candidates to identify the relevant facts and apply the appropriate code provision.

The exam guide identifies numerous technical knowledge areas across the Minnesota Plumbing Code. Master-level preparation should include plumbing principles, definitions, general regulations, fixtures, water distribution, sanitary drainage, venting, traps, interceptors, indirect wastes, storm drainage, materials, testing, and system sizing.

Plumbing calculations are an important part of preparation. Candidates should be able to size water distribution piping, drain-waste-vent systems, and storm drainage systems using the applicable code requirements and tables.

Open Book Test

The Minnesota Master Plumber examination is an open book test.

The Minnesota Department of Labor and Industry provides the examination references, including the applicable 2020 Minnesota Plumbing Code and the Department’s current laws and rules material. The Department also supplies an electronic calculator for use during the examination.

Candidates are not permitted to bring outside reference materials or personal electronic devices into the examination area. The plumbing code supplied during the exam does not include personal tabs or other navigation aids.

For that reason, preparation should focus heavily on learning the structure of the code. Candidates should know which chapters address fixtures, water supply, sanitary drainage, venting, traps, storm drainage, and other major plumbing subjects before entering the examination.

Open-book testing rewards familiarity rather than dependence on the book. The Department’s examination guidance notes that many questions are intended to be answered without searching the reference material. A candidate who already understands the underlying subject can use the code to confirm a requirement instead of searching from the beginning for every question.

Licensing Steps

  1. Meet a qualifying pathway for the Master Plumber examination. Minnesota establishes specific experience or licensing pathways before an applicant can take the Master Plumber examination.
  2. Complete qualifying Journeyworker experience. One primary pathway requires at least one year of practical plumbing experience as a licensed Journeyworker Plumber.
  3. Use an equivalent out-of-state Master Plumber license when applicable. An applicant holding a current Master Plumber license from another state may qualify when the other jurisdiction’s requirements are determined by Minnesota DLI to be equivalent.
  4. Use the Restricted Master pathway when applicable. A current Minnesota Restricted Master Plumber may qualify after five years of verifiable experience in business as a plumbing contractor in Minnesota.
  5. Apply for the Master Plumber examination. Submit the examination application through the Minnesota Department of Labor and Industry.
  6. Receive examination approval. DLI reviews the applicant’s qualifications before authorizing testing.
  7. Schedule the examination. Once approved, follow the Department’s instructions to schedule the Master Plumber examination.
  8. Prepare with the Minnesota Plumbing Code. Study Chapter 4714 together with the applicable Minnesota laws and regulations included in the exam framework.
  9. Practice plumbing calculations. Work through water supply, DWV, vent, storm drainage, and other calculation problems before test day.
  10. Take the examination. Complete the 300-point Master Plumber examination within the 5.5-hour testing period.
  11. Earn the required passing score. Candidates must achieve at least 70%.
  12. Complete the licensing process. Successful candidates receive instructions from the Minnesota Department of Labor and Industry for obtaining the Master Plumber license.
  13. Maintain continuing education. Complete the continuing education required during each license period.
  14. Maintain the license through renewal. Master Plumber licenses expire according to Minnesota’s plumbing license renewal cycle and must remain active to continue performing work within the license scope.

State Requirements

A Minnesota Master Plumber is an individual skilled in planning, supervising, and performing the practical installation of plumbing. The license provides statewide authority for qualifying plumbing work, including interior plumbing and exterior water service, sanitary sewer, and storm sewer systems within property lines.

To qualify for the Master Plumber examination through the standard pathway, an applicant must have at least one year of practical plumbing experience as a licensed Journeyworker Plumber.

Other qualifying pathways include holding a current Master Plumber license from another jurisdiction whose requirements are determined to be equivalent to Minnesota’s requirements, or holding a Minnesota Restricted Master Plumber license with five years of verifiable experience in business as a plumbing contractor in the state.

The individual Master Plumber license should be distinguished from a Minnesota Plumbing Contractor license. A plumbing contractor is the business entity that contracts to perform plumbing work. The contractor must maintain a licensed Master Plumber as its responsible licensed individual and satisfy separate business licensing requirements.

The responsible Master Plumber for a plumbing contractor must be an owner, officer, or member of the business, or a qualifying W-2 employee actively engaged in the company’s plumbing work. That individual is responsible for the plumbing work performed by the company’s employees.

A Minnesota plumbing contractor must also maintain applicable liability insurance, workers’ compensation compliance, and a plumbing contractor surety bond as required by state law.

Master Plumber licenses are subject to continuing education requirements. Minnesota currently requires 16 hours of approved continuing education during each two-year license period. At least eight hours must relate to the State Plumbing Code, while the remaining required hours must address qualifying technical plumbing, State Building Code, or plumbing-law subjects.

Test Information and Study Materials

Basic Plumbing Principles: Review the fundamental principles behind safe plumbing installations, including potable water protection, sanitary waste disposal, proper venting, fixture operation, and protection of public health.

Definitions: Become familiar with terminology used throughout the Minnesota Plumbing Code. Correct interpretation of a defined term can determine how an entire code provision applies.

General Regulations: Study installation practices, protection of piping, workmanship, materials, supports, joints, testing, trenching, structural protection, and requirements affecting plumbing systems before individual chapters are applied.

Plumbing Fixtures: Review fixture installation, fixture fittings, accessibility, waste connections, water supplies, prohibited fixtures, special fixtures, and installation requirements.

Water Supply and Distribution: Study potable water systems, sizing, pressure, materials, valves, backflow prevention, cross-connection control, water-service piping, and distribution-system design.

Backflow Prevention: Review air gaps, reduced-pressure devices, double-check devices, atmospheric vacuum breakers, pressure vacuum breakers, hose connection protection, and the applications and limitations of each method.

Drain, Waste, and Vent Systems: Study drainage fixture units, pipe sizing, slope, drainage fittings, cleanouts, building drains, building sewers, vent sizing, vent connections, and approved venting methods.

Venting: Review individual vents, common vents, wet venting, circuit venting, relief vents, vent terminals, sizing, and developed-length requirements.

Traps and Interceptors: Study trap requirements, trap seals, prohibited traps, interceptors, grease removal, oil and flammable waste interceptors, and special waste arrangements.

Indirect Waste: Review indirect waste piping, air gaps, receptors, equipment connections, food-handling establishments, and protection against contamination.

Storm Drainage: Practice sizing roof drainage systems using roof area, rainfall rates, vertical conductors, horizontal drains, and related tables.

Water Pipe Sizing: Practice applying fixture-unit values, available pressure, elevation, developed length, and sizing tables to water distribution systems.

DWV Sizing: Practice calculating drainage loads and selecting appropriate pipe sizes for horizontal branches, stacks, building drains, and building sewers.

Testing: Review required tests for drainage, vent, water, and other piping systems, including acceptable test methods, pressures, and procedures.

Materials and Joints: Study approved pipe materials, fittings, joints, connections, transitions between different materials, corrosion protection, and installation restrictions.

Test Information and Study Materials (continued)

Code Navigation: Develop a chapter-by-chapter understanding of the Minnesota Plumbing Code. Because the examination copy does not include personal tabs, candidates should be able to locate important topics through the table of contents, chapter sequence, and index.

Timed Calculation Practice: The Master Plumber exam provides 5.5 hours for a 300-point examination. Candidates should practice calculations under timed conditions and develop a strategy for moving past difficult questions and returning to them later.

Who administers the Minnesota Master Plumber exam?

Is the Minnesota Master Plumber examination open book?

Yes. The examination is an open book test. The Minnesota Department of Labor and Industry supplies the applicable plumbing code, laws and rules material, and a calculator for use during testing.

Can I bring my own plumbing code into the exam?

No. The Department provides the examination reference materials. Outside materials and personal electronic devices are not permitted in the examination area.

How long is the Minnesota Master Plumber exam?

Candidates receive 5.5 hours to complete the Master Plumber examination.

How is the exam scored?

The Minnesota Master Plumber examination consists of 300 points, and a minimum score of 70% is required to pass.

What experience is required for the Master Plumber exam?

The standard qualification pathway requires at least one year of practical plumbing experience as a licensed Journeyworker Plumber. Minnesota also recognizes certain qualifying out-of-state and Restricted Master pathways.

Is a Master Plumber license the same as a Plumbing Contractor license?

No. The Master Plumber credential is an individual license. The Plumbing Contractor license applies to the business. A licensed plumbing contractor must maintain an eligible Master Plumber as its responsible licensed individual.

What plumbing subjects should I study?

Focus on water distribution, backflow prevention, fixtures, drainage, venting, traps, indirect waste, storm drainage, approved materials, installation practices, system testing, pipe sizing, plumbing calculations, and applicable Minnesota laws and regulations.

Does Minnesota require continuing education for Master Plumbers?

Yes. Minnesota Master Plumbers must complete 16 hours of approved continuing education during each two-year license period, including at least eight hours related to the State Plumbing Code.

Why should I study the code if the exam is open book?

The examination references are intended to support candidates, not replace preparation. Strong familiarity with the Minnesota Plumbing Code can help you recognize the governing subject quickly, navigate to the correct requirement, and reserve more examination time for calculations and complex questions.
