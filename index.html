What do people ask before buying?

Which plumbing license do I need?

Your state and the work you do decide that. Most states license journeyman and master plumbers, and many also license plumbing contractors. Each state page names the board and lists every license we have prep for.

Does my plumbing exam use the IPC or the UPC?

Your state or city adopts one of them. Every package on this site names its code in the title, such as "Based on 2024 IPC" or "Based on 2024 UPC", so match it to the editions in your candidate bulletin.

Is the plumbing exam open book?

It depends on the exam. Florida contractor and ICC inspector exams allow the listed references, for example, while some journeyman exams are closed book. Your candidate bulletin lists what you can bring.

What is in the 2026 journeyman packages?

The study guide is $59.99. The guide plus online course is $119.99. The complete package at $344.99 adds the code books with tabs already placed.

What do the Ultimate Package and The 1 Package include?

The Ultimate Package bundles highlighted and tabbed rental books, a year of online course access and help with your license application. The 1 Package adds LLC or corporation setup, EIN filing and compliance guidance.

Are these the real exam questions?

No. Licensing exam questions are confidential. The courses and practice questions are original material written to the same subjects and format, with explanations.

Does buying a package register me for the exam?

No. Your state board or city approves your application first, then the testing provider schedules the exam. A package gets you ready for the test itself.
