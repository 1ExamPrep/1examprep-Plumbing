How does the Plumbing Inspector exam work?

The Plumbing Inspector license comes from the New Mexico Construction Industries Division (Regulation and Licensing Department). PSI gives the exam. The board approves your application first; then you schedule the exam and bring only the references the candidate bulletin allows.
