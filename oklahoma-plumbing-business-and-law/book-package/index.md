Approved references may be highlighted, underlined, and indexed before the examination session. Permanent tabs may be used for organization. Temporary tabs such as Post-it notes are not permitted.

## State Requirements

Oklahoma plumbing contractor licensing is administered by the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board. Plumbing contractor licensing represents the contractor level of the state's plumbing licensing structure.

Applicants for a plumbing contractor license must meet the same qualification requirements applicable to a plumbing journeyman and complete an additional **one year of experience in the plumbing trade**.

The standard journeyman qualification pathway includes applicants who are at least **18 years old** and have three years of qualifying plumbing trade experience while employed by a licensed plumbing contractor. Oklahoma also recognizes certain qualifying military experience and current out-of-state plumbing licenses that meet applicable requirements.

Approved plumbing education can substitute for a portion of the required trade experience. Qualifying education must meet the standards established for plumbing licensing in Oklahoma.

Contractor applicants are required to pass both portions of the contractor examination: the applicable plumbing trade examination and the Plumbing Business and Law examination.

After the required examinations are completed, the contractor license remains inactive until the applicable bond and insurance requirements are satisfied.

An active Oklahoma plumbing contractor is required to maintain a **$5,000 corporate surety bond** or another qualifying form of security permitted under Oklahoma plumbing regulations.

Active plumbing contractors must also maintain at least **$50,000 in commercial general liability insurance**. Maintaining required coverage is part of keeping a plumbing contractor license active.

Licensed plumbing contractors also have continuing responsibilities involving apprentice supervision, licensing, advertising, contracts, bids, insurance, and compliance with Oklahoma plumbing laws and regulations.

This reference contains administrative rules governing the Oklahoma plumbing industry. Candidates can use it to study license qualifications, contractor responsibilities, examination procedures, apprentices, bond and insurance requirements, licensing procedures, and other state regulations.

This Oklahoma regulatory reference addresses fines associated with violations administered by the Construction Industries Board. It supports preparation involving contractor compliance, enforcement, and the consequences of regulated violations.
