Exam Details

The Oklahoma Plumbing Business and Law examination evaluates knowledge of contractor business operations, legal responsibilities, project management, financial administration, and Oklahoma plumbing licensing requirements.

The Oklahoma Construction Industries Board oversees plumbing contractor licensing, while PSI administers the examination.

  • Examination: Oklahoma Plumbing Business and Law
  • Licensing Authority: Oklahoma Construction Industries Board
  • Testing Provider: PSI
  • Number of Scored Questions: 50
  • Time Allowed: 120 minutes
  • Passing Standard: 75%
  • Examination Format: Open book
  • Examination Focus: Contractor business operations, management, regulations, and legal responsibilities

The examination covers eight major business and law subjects:

  • Bidding and Estimating: Project estimates, labor, materials, overhead, pricing, and bidding procedures.
  • Project Management and Supervision: Scheduling, budgets, quality control, materials management, safety, and project oversight.
  • Contracts: Contract terminology, agreements, change orders, warranties, and contractual obligations.
  • Financial Management: Accounting, cash flow, financial statements, business organization, and financing.
  • Labor and Personnel: Employment practices, personnel records, workplace requirements, and employee responsibilities.
  • Risk Management: Insurance, surety bonds, and business risk.
  • Payroll and Payroll Taxes: Payroll administration, tax obligations, reporting forms, and recordkeeping.
  • Licensing Requirements: Oklahoma plumbing contractor regulations, insurance, bonding, and license maintenance.

With 120 minutes available for 50 questions, candidates have approximately two minutes and 24 seconds per question. Familiarity with the references and their organization helps make preparation more effective.

The Business and Law examination is separate from the technical plumbing trade examination required for contractor licensing.

Open Book Test

The Oklahoma Plumbing Business and Law examination is an open-book test. Candidates may consult approved examination references according to PSI testing requirements.

Open-book testing requires more than access to legal and business publications. Candidates must recognize the subject of a question, identify the appropriate reference, locate relevant information, and apply the correct business or regulatory principle.

Questions involving plumbing licensing may require knowledge of the Oklahoma Plumbing License Law or Plumbing Industry Regulations. Questions involving estimating, accounting, contracts, and project management may draw on the NASCLA Contractors Guide.

Approved reference materials may be highlighted, underlined, and indexed before testing. Permanent tabs are permitted, while unauthorized handwritten notes, loose papers, and temporary removable tabs are prohibited.

State Requirements

Plumbing contracting in Oklahoma is regulated through the Oklahoma Plumbing License Law of 1955, Oklahoma Plumbing Industry Regulations, and applicable Construction Industries Board rules.

The Oklahoma Construction Industries Board establishes licensing qualifications and professional responsibilities for plumbing contractors.

Plumbing contractors must understand the requirements associated with authorized contracting activities, business operations, license maintenance, insurance, bonding, and applicable employee supervision.

Contractor licensing is separate from journeyman licensing. A contractor credential involves authority and responsibilities associated with operating a regulated plumbing contracting business.

Oklahoma plumbing contractors must maintain applicable contractor bond and insurance requirements. Compliance with these obligations is an important part of maintaining an active contractor license.

Plumbing contractors must also satisfy applicable continuing education and license renewal requirements.

Understanding the relevant state laws and regulations helps candidates recognize professional responsibilities and develop familiarity with the legal framework governing plumbing contracting.

Reference Books

The Oklahoma Plumbing Business and Law Online Exam Prep Course focuses on five important legal, regulatory, and business study references:

  • Oklahoma Plumbing License Law of 1955, Oklahoma Statutes, Title 59, Chapter 27
    Establishes Oklahoma's statutory framework for plumbing licensing and professional regulation. It addresses licensing authority, regulated plumbing activities, qualifications, professional responsibilities, and provisions governing plumbing contractors and journeymen. This reference supports preparation involving Oklahoma-specific plumbing licensing law.
  • Oklahoma Plumbing Industry Regulations, Oklahoma Administrative Code, Title 158, Chapter 30
    Contains administrative regulations governing plumbing license classifications, applications, examinations, contractor responsibilities, and professional compliance. It supports understanding of the rules applicable to plumbing contractors operating in Oklahoma.
  • Oklahoma Fine Schedule of the Construction Industries Board, Oklahoma Administrative Code, Title 158, Chapter 10
    Addresses administrative fines associated with Construction Industries Board enforcement. It helps candidates understand regulatory violations, enforcement-related requirements, and the importance of maintaining compliance with licensing laws.
  • Oklahoma Lien Law, Oklahoma Statutes, Title 42, Chapter 3
    Contains Oklahoma construction lien provisions relevant to payment rights and construction-related financial disputes. It supports study involving lien terminology, legal procedures, and contractor payment responsibilities.
  • NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, 14th Edition
    A comprehensive contractor business reference addressing estimating, bidding, project management, contracts, accounting, financial administration, personnel, insurance, risk management, payroll, and business operations. It supports preparation involving general contractor management principles and practical business responsibilities.
