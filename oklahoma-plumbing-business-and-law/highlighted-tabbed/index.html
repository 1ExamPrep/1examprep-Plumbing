Exam Details

The Oklahoma Plumbing Business and Law examination is part of the contractor licensing process for Oklahoma plumbing contractors.

The exam contains 50 questions. Candidates are allowed 120 minutes to complete the examination. The required passing score is 75%.

The examination covers eight major contractor business and law areas:

  • Bidding and Estimating: 10 questions
  • Project Management and Supervision: 7 questions
  • Contracts: 5 questions
  • Financial: 8 questions
  • Labor and Personnel: 5 questions
  • Risk Management: 4 questions
  • Payroll and Payroll Taxes: 6 questions
  • Licensing Requirements: 5 questions

Bidding and Estimating is the largest individual section of the examination. Candidates should understand general estimating concepts and the bidding process, including the business considerations involved in preparing a price for construction work.

Project Management and Supervision covers responsibilities such as project oversight, budgets, quality control, materials control, jobsite safety, scheduling, hazardous materials, environmental protection, submittals, reports, ethics, and liens.

Contracts can include terminology, required contract components, contract types, change orders, standardized documents, interpretation, warranties, document inclusions, and other obligations arising from construction agreements.

Financial subjects include business organization, business startup, accounting methods, cash-flow terminology, accounts receivable, accounts payable, balance sheets, income statements, company income taxes, financing, checking accounts, and financial ratios.

The remaining portions of the exam address labor and personnel, risk management, insurance, bonds, payroll, payroll taxes, and Oklahoma plumbing licensing requirements. Candidates should prepare across the entire outline rather than concentrating on a single reference.

Open Book Test

The Oklahoma Plumbing Business and Law examination is an open-book test. Candidates may use approved reference materials during the examination according to the testing provider's reference rules.

Reference materials may be highlighted, underlined, and indexed before the examination. Permanent tabs may be used to organize approved references. Temporary tabs such as Post-it notes are not permitted.

Written notes may not be added to the references before or during the examination. Candidates should make sure their books comply with applicable exam-center requirements before arriving for testing.

The open-book format makes reference navigation an important study skill. With 50 questions and 120 minutes available, candidates have an average of approximately two minutes and twenty-four seconds per question. Spending several minutes searching randomly through different books can quickly reduce the time available for the rest of the exam.

A more efficient approach is to identify the subject of the question first. A licensing question may require the Oklahoma Plumbing License Law or Plumbing Industry Regulations. A lien question may require Oklahoma Lien Law. Broader business questions involving estimating, contracts, financial management, labor, or project supervision may lead to the NASCLA Contractors Guide.

Practice this process while studying so selecting the appropriate reference becomes a familiar part of answering questions.

Licensing Steps

  1. Gain qualifying plumbing experience. Oklahoma plumbing contractor applicants must meet the applicable experience requirements for plumbing licensing.
  2. Meet journeyman-level qualifications. Contractor applicants must meet the qualification requirements applicable to an Oklahoma plumbing journeyman.
  3. Complete additional contractor experience. Oklahoma requires an additional year of qualifying plumbing trade experience for contractor-level licensing.
  4. Submit the plumbing contractor application. Plumbing licensing is administered through the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board.
  5. Receive examination eligibility. Applicants must receive approval before scheduling the required contractor examinations.
  6. Prepare for the technical plumbing examination. Contractor applicants must meet the applicable technical examination requirements.
  7. Prepare for the Plumbing Business and Law examination. Study estimating, project management, contracts, financial topics, labor, risk management, payroll, licensing, and related contractor responsibilities.
  8. Complete the required examinations. Candidates must satisfy the applicable testing requirements for contractor licensing.
  9. Complete active contractor requirements. After passing the required examinations, candidates must satisfy applicable Oklahoma requirements for an active contractor license.

State Requirements

Oklahoma plumbing contractor licensing is administered by the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board. Applicants for a plumbing contractor license must meet the qualification requirements applicable to a plumbing journeyman and complete an additional one year of experience in the plumbing trade.

The standard journeyman pathway includes applicants who are at least 18 years old and have three years of qualifying plumbing trade experience while employed by a licensed plumbing contractor. Oklahoma also recognizes qualifying alternatives provided under its licensing requirements.

Approved plumbing education may substitute for a portion of the required experience when the education meets Oklahoma requirements. Contractor applicants must still satisfy the additional contractor-level experience requirement.

Once the applicable examinations have been completed, additional requirements apply before the contractor license is active. An active Oklahoma plumbing contractor generally must maintain the required contractor bond and commercial general liability insurance.

The Oklahoma Construction Industries Board requires an active plumbing contractor to maintain a $5,000 corporate surety bond, subject to the alternatives and requirements established under Oklahoma plumbing regulations.

An active plumbing contractor must also provide evidence of at least $50,000 in commercial general liability insurance. Maintaining required coverage is an ongoing responsibility associated with active contractor status.

Oklahoma plumbing contractors are also responsible for complying with applicable requirements involving apprentice supervision, license display, advertising, contracts, bids, continuing education, and other obligations established by Oklahoma plumbing law and regulations.

Test Information and Study Materials

A productive Oklahoma Plumbing Business and Law study plan should organize preparation around the major exam categories instead of attempting to read every reference from beginning to end without a strategy.

Bidding and estimating should receive significant attention because it represents 10 questions. Review estimating concepts, bid preparation, project costs, labor, materials, overhead, and the business considerations that affect a contractor's proposal.

Project management and supervision requires candidates to think beyond the initial bid. Study budgets, quality control, materials management, scheduling, jobsite safety, environmental considerations, reports, ethics, and liens. Contractors must manage work after the contract is awarded, making this an important area of business preparation.

Contracts should be reviewed with attention to terminology, required elements, contract types, change orders, warranties, standardized documents, interpretation, and contractual responsibilities. Understanding contract principles helps candidates recognize the obligations created by construction agreements.

Financial management is one of the larger exam categories. Study business organization, accounting methods, cash flow, accounts receivable, accounts payable, balance sheets, income statements, financing, taxes, checking accounts, and financial ratios.

Labor and personnel can include labor standards, workers' compensation, workplace safety, hiring responsibilities, personnel records, and other requirements involved in employing and managing workers.

Risk management includes insurance and bonds. These topics are especially relevant to plumbing contractors because active contractor licensing includes ongoing bond and insurance responsibilities.

Payroll and payroll taxes should receive dedicated preparation. Contractors employing workers must understand the basic responsibilities associated with payroll administration, taxes, forms, and applicable deadlines.

Licensing requirements should be studied directly from Oklahoma-specific references. Become familiar with the Plumbing License Law and Plumbing Industry Regulations so you can locate provisions involving contractor qualifications, licensing status, bond and insurance requirements, and other state responsibilities.

Timed practice is useful because the examination contains 50 questions and allows 120 minutes. Practice identifying straightforward questions that can be answered quickly while reserving additional time for questions that require detailed reference research.
