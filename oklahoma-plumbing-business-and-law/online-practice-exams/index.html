Exam Details

The Oklahoma Plumbing Business and Law Contractor Exam is administered by PSI for the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board. Candidates must be approved by the State of Oklahoma before scheduling the examination. Once approved, candidates are responsible for scheduling their testing appointment with PSI.

The Plumbing Business and Law examination contains 50 scored items. Candidates are allowed 120 minutes to complete the exam, and the listed passing score is 70% correct. The examination is computer-based and is taken through an approved PSI testing location.

The exam content is centered on business and legal knowledge expected of Oklahoma plumbing contractors. Study areas include bidding and estimating, project management and supervision, contracts, financial management, risk management, payroll and payroll taxes, licensing requirements, Oklahoma plumbing law, Oklahoma plumbing industry regulations, lien law, and Construction Industries Board enforcement topics.

Candidates should prepare by reviewing the listed references and practicing how to locate information quickly. The exam may require understanding Oklahoma plumbing licensing rules, administrative regulations, fine schedules, lien law, and general contractor business principles found in the NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management.

Score reporting is provided after the examination. Candidates who pass receive a successful notification, and candidates who do not pass may receive diagnostic information showing strengths and weaknesses by examination type. Online practice questions can help candidates identify topics that need more review before sitting for the official exam.

Open Book Test

The Oklahoma Plumbing Business and Law Contractor Exam is an open book test. Candidates are responsible for bringing their own approved references to the examination center. Reference materials may be highlighted, underlined, and indexed before the examination session.

References may not be written in before or during the examination. Candidates may use permanent tabs only. Temporary tabs, including Post-it style notes, are not allowed and must be removed before the exam begins. Candidates may not bring additional loose papers with approved references.

Because this is an open book examination, preparation should include reference navigation. Candidates should practice locating information in the Oklahoma Plumbing License Law of 1955, Oklahoma Plumbing Industry Regulations, Oklahoma Construction Industries Board fine schedule, Oklahoma Lien Law, and the NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management. Licensing Steps

Oklahoma plumbing contractor licensing is handled through the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board. Candidates applying for a plumbing contractor license must meet the state’s eligibility requirements, submit the required application materials, receive approval to test, schedule the required examination through PSI, and complete the remaining licensing requirements after passing.

The licensing process begins with determining the correct plumbing license category and confirming that the applicant meets the applicable experience and application requirements. Applicants submit documentation to the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board for review. The Board determines eligibility before the candidate may schedule the required examination.

Once approved, the candidate schedules the PSI examination. Passing the required examination is an important step toward licensure, but contractor licensing also involves compliance responsibilities outside the exam itself. Plumbing contractors must understand licensing rules, renewal obligations, business responsibilities, and requirements connected to state regulations and professional conduct.

After passing the exam, candidates should complete any remaining state requirements before operating as an active plumbing contractor. Active contractors are responsible for maintaining required documentation and operating within the scope of the license. Contractors should keep business records, insurance information, compliance documents, and renewal information current.

Plumbing contractor licensing is not only about passing an exam. It is also about operating legally and professionally in Oklahoma. Business and law preparation helps candidates understand the responsibilities that come with contractor-level authority.
