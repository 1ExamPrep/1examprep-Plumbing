Exam Details

The ICC F24 National Standard Master Plumber examination contains 100 multiple-choice questions and has a 4-hour time limit.

The official F24 content outline divides the examination into seven major areas:

  • Administration General Regulations – 46%
  • Plumbing Fixtures – 14%
  • Water Supply and Distribution – 11%
  • Sanitary Drainage – 11%
  • Plumbing Vent Requirements – 8%
  • Traps, Interceptors, and Separators – 8%
  • Storm Drainage – 2%

Administration General Regulations is the largest portion of the examination at 46%. This category includes general installation requirements, code definitions and terms, plan reading, testing, and materials. Candidates should be comfortable working with the general provisions of the plumbing code and applying those requirements to a wide range of installation conditions.

Plumbing Fixtures represent 14% of the exam. Topics include water heaters, water closets and urinals, specialty fixtures, bathtubs, showers, sinks, lavatories, and related installation requirements. Candidates should understand how fixture type, location, connection, supply, drainage, and installation requirements interact.

Water Supply and Distribution account for 11% of the exam. This area includes service distribution and piping, water supply and pressure, and protection of the potable water supply. Candidates should be prepared to work with pipe sizing, pressure considerations, water-service requirements, distribution systems, valves, backflow protection, and related code provisions.

Sanitary Drainage also represents 11%. This category includes building sanitary drainage, sumps and ejectors, and indirect and special waste systems. Candidates should understand drainage piping, fixture-unit relationships, sizing, slope, cleanouts, special waste conditions, and related drainage requirements.

Plumbing Vent Requirements represent 8% of the examination and include vent connections, grades, and vent terminals. Candidates should understand how vent systems protect trap seals and how different venting methods are regulated by the IPC.

Traps, Interceptors, and Separators represent another 8%. This area includes installation of traps as well as interceptors and separators used for specific waste conditions.

Storm Drainage represents 2% of the exam and includes requirements related to storm drainage systems.

Open Book Test

The ICC F24 National Standard Master Plumber examination is an open book test. Candidates have four hours to complete the 100-question examination and must follow current ICC examination rules regarding permitted reference materials.

Because the exam is open book, reference-navigation skills are an important part of preparation. Candidates should not expect to look up every answer from scratch. Instead, they should become familiar enough with the 2015 International Plumbing Code to recognize the likely chapter, section, or table associated with the question.

The 2015 International Plumbing Code should become a working reference during preparation. Candidates should understand the organization of the code and be able to move efficiently among general regulations, fixtures, water heaters, water supply, sanitary drainage, special waste, vents, traps, interceptors, separators, and storm drainage provisions.

A question involving a plumbing fixture should direct attention toward fixture requirements. A water-supply sizing or pressure question should lead toward the water supply and distribution provisions. A question involving drainage fixture units, slope, or building drains should point toward sanitary drainage requirements.

Venting questions may require candidates to locate provisions governing vent connections, sizing, grades, terminations, or approved venting methods. Questions involving grease, oil, sand, or other special waste conditions may direct the candidate toward interceptors and separators.

Practice questions are most useful when they are treated as reference-navigation exercises. After answering each question, locate the applicable IPC provision and review the surrounding requirements. This helps reinforce both the technical rule and its location in the code.

Licensing Steps

The F24 is an ICC National Standard contractor/trades examination. ICC administers the examination program, while licensing authority remains with participating state and local jurisdictions. Passing the F24 exam does not automatically create a Master Plumber license in every jurisdiction.

  1. Identify the applicable Master Plumber requirement. Determine whether the F24 National Standard Master Plumber examination is required or accepted by the jurisdiction where you intend to work.
  2. Review local licensing requirements. Confirm any additional qualifications established by the applicable state, county, city, or licensing agency.
  3. Prepare with the correct reference. This product uses the 2015 International Plumbing Code.
  4. Study by content area. Organize review around Administration General Regulations, Plumbing Fixtures, Water Supply and Distribution, Sanitary Drainage, Plumbing Vent Requirements, Traps and Interceptors, and Storm Drainage.
  5. Practice plan reading and code navigation. Develop the ability to interpret plumbing information and locate the applicable IPC provision efficiently.
  6. Use the online practice questions. Work through plumbing scenarios and revisit weaker subjects throughout the included access period.
  7. Register for the F24 examination. Follow the applicable ICC contractor/trades registration and scheduling process.
  8. Complete the examination. Answer the 100 multiple-choice questions within the 4-hour testing period.
  9. Complete jurisdiction-specific licensing requirements. Follow any remaining requirements established by the licensing authority where you are seeking a Master Plumber credential.

State Requirements

The ICC F24 National Standard Master Plumber examination is a national contractor/trades examination. ICC provides the examination program, but it is not the licensing agency that grants Master Plumber licenses.

State, county, city, and other local licensing authorities determine whether the F24 examination is accepted or required for a particular plumbing license. Requirements can differ substantially from one jurisdiction to another.

Candidates should therefore separate the examination from the complete licensing process. Passing the F24 may satisfy an examination requirement for a participating jurisdiction, but the licensing authority determines any additional requirements that apply before a license can be issued.

The F24 exam itself focuses on Master Plumber knowledge using the 2015 International Plumbing Code. Its scope includes both broad plumbing regulations and detailed system requirements, making organized preparation important for candidates who need to work across many areas of the code.

Test Information and Study Materials

The online practice-question product includes 3 months of access. The access period gives candidates time to divide the F24 exam into manageable study categories, repeat difficult subjects, and develop familiarity with the organization of the 2015 International Plumbing Code.

Begin with Administration General Regulations because it represents 46% of the examination. Review general installation requirements, approved materials, piping protection, supports, testing, code terminology, and plan reading. Because this category is nearly half of the exam, it should receive substantial study attention.

Code definitions and terms deserve particular attention. Plumbing codes use defined terms to establish precise technical meanings. A question may depend on whether a piping arrangement, fixture, fitting, or system meets the code definition of a particular term.

Plan reading is another important part of the Administration category. Candidates should practice interpreting plumbing layouts, pipe sizes, fixture locations, risers, symbols, and system relationships shown on construction documents. Plan-based questions may require both code knowledge and the ability to extract relevant information from a drawing.

For testing, review the requirements that apply to plumbing systems before they are concealed or placed into service. Candidates should understand where the code addresses required tests and what systems those test provisions apply to.

For Plumbing Fixtures, review fixture installation, water closets, urinals, sinks, lavatories, bathtubs, showers, specialty fixtures, fixture fittings, and water heaters. Study how supply, drainage, clearances, connections, and other installation requirements relate to the fixture being installed.

For Water Supply and Distribution, review water-service piping, distribution piping, sizing, fixture demand, pressure, valves, backflow prevention, and protection of potable water. Candidates should become comfortable using applicable tables and understanding when specific backflow-protection methods are required.

For Sanitary Drainage, study horizontal and vertical drainage, building drains, building sewers, drainage fixture units, pipe sizing, slope, cleanouts, sumps, ejectors, indirect waste, and special waste systems. Many drainage questions require careful attention to both system layout and code tables.

Test Information and Study Materials (continued)

For Plumbing Vent Requirements, review vent connections, vent grades, vent terminals, sizing, and approved venting arrangements. Candidates should understand the purpose of the vent system and how the code protects drainage-system performance and trap seals.

For Traps, Interceptors, and Separators, review trap installation, trap seals, prohibited configurations, grease interceptors, oil separators, and other devices used to control special wastes before they enter the drainage system.

Storm Drainage represents a smaller percentage of the examination but should still be included in the study plan. Review roof drainage and related storm-drainage requirements so that smaller content areas are not overlooked.

Timed practice can help candidates manage the examination more effectively. The F24 exam allows 240 minutes for 100 questions, which averages approximately two minutes and twenty-four seconds per question. Some questions may be answered quickly, while others may require more detailed code navigation or plan interpretation.

Use missed questions as a study tool. If water-supply sizing, drainage fixture units, venting, fixture requirements, or plan reading repeatedly cause difficulty, dedicate additional review sessions to those areas throughout the 3-month access period.
