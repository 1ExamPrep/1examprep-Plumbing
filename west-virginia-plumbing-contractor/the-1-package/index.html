Exam Details

The 1 Package supports preparation for the West Virginia contractor business examination and the Plumbing Contractor trade examination administered through Prov. These examinations cover different areas of knowledge, so candidates should prepare for each portion with a separate but coordinated study plan.

The business preparation portion uses the West Virginia Contractor Licensing Act Study Guide and the included business course. This material supports review of contractor licensing concepts, business responsibilities, and subjects connected with operating within West Virginia’s contractor framework.

The Plumbing Contractor trade portion uses the International Plumbing Code, 2018; International Fuel Gas Code, 2018; and Plumbing Basics for Contractors, 4th Edition. These references support preparation across plumbing systems, fuel-gas work, trade terminology, installation methods, code interpretation, and contractor-level technical knowledge.

Plumbing contractor exam preparation may involve fixtures, water distribution, sanitary drainage, vent systems, storm drainage, traps, interceptors, piping materials, joining methods, testing, inspections, gas piping, appliance connections, combustion air, venting systems, and general installation practices.

The International Plumbing Code contains formal plumbing requirements organized through chapters, sections, definitions, tables, exceptions, and cross-references. Candidates should become familiar with how the code addresses fixtures, water supply, drainage, venting, storm systems, materials, testing, and inspections.

The International Fuel Gas Code supports preparation related to gas piping, appliance installation, combustion air, venting, testing, materials, and related safety provisions. Candidates should practice moving between definitions, chapters, tables, and connected sections.

Plumbing Basics for Contractors supports broad trade review. It helps candidates organize practical plumbing subjects, reinforce terminology, and identify areas that require additional attention before exam day.

The highlighted and tabbed format helps candidates identify major subjects and return to important sections more efficiently. Candidates should still review complete passages, surrounding definitions, related tables, exceptions, and cross-references before selecting an answer.

The online courses and live classes provide structure for working through the material. Candidates can study a topic, locate the related information in the appropriate reference, complete practice-oriented review, and revisit difficult subjects throughout the 1-year access period.

Open Book Test

The West Virginia Plumbing Contractor examination is an open-book test. Candidates may use approved references in accordance with current Prov rules. Open book does not mean that the examination is simple or that every answer can be located quickly without preparation.

Technical plumbing and fuel-gas books contain detailed definitions, chapters, tables, illustrations, exceptions, indexes, and cross-references. Candidates who are unfamiliar with the references may spend too much time searching or may locate related information without finding the complete requirement.

The highlighted and tabbed rental books provide visible navigation points. Tabs help divide the books into recognizable subjects, while highlighting emphasizes selected material for review. These features work best when candidates practice with the books consistently before exam day.

Begin by learning the structure of every reference. Review how the contractor licensing study guide organizes business subjects, how the International Plumbing Code organizes plumbing systems, how the International Fuel Gas Code presents gas-piping and appliance requirements, and how Plumbing Basics for Contractors presents practical trade material.

Practice identifying the type of question before opening a book. Determine whether the question is about contractor business, fixtures, water supply, drainage, venting, gas piping, appliances, combustion air, testing, inspections, or practical plumbing knowledge. Then select the reference most likely to contain the answer.

Do not rely only on highlighted text. A correct answer may depend on surrounding language, a table note, a definition, an exception, an illustration, or a related section. Read enough of the material to understand the complete requirement or trade principle.

The International Plumbing Code often requires candidates to move among definitions, fixture requirements, drainage provisions, venting chapters, water-distribution sections, tables, and exceptions. Repeated navigation practice can improve speed and accuracy.

The International Fuel Gas Code may require candidates to review sizing information, material provisions, appliance requirements, combustion-air rules, venting sections, or testing requirements. Candidates should practice identifying which part of the code applies to the question.
