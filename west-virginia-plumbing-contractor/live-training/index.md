## How does the Plumbing Contractor exam work?

The Plumbing Contractor license comes from the West Virginia State Fire Marshal. The board approves your application first; then you schedule the exam and bring only the references the candidate bulletin allows.

- The West Virginia State Fire Marshal licenses plumbers; plumbing contractors also hold a West Virginia contractor license.
- West Virginia also certifies plumbing inspectors and plans examiners using ICC P1, P2 and P3 exams.
