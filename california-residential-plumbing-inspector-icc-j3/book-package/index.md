**1) Learn the code’s “map” before deep reading**

**2) Train topic recognition before opening the book**
Most time loss comes from uncertainty about where to start. Practice labeling the topic first. A simple label helps you choose a strong starting point and reduces wasted searching.

**3) Practice confirm-and-move**
Open book doesn’t mean you should look up everything from scratch. Narrow down the answer using understanding, confirm the key detail in the code, and move on to protect your pace.

Many residential plumbing questions hinge on exceptions and qualifying conditions. During practice, build the habit of scanning for exception language and “if/when/where” qualifiers before finalizing an answer.
