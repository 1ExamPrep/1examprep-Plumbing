Exam Details

The ICC P2 Commercial Plumbing Inspector exam measures a candidate’s ability to inspect commercial plumbing systems for compliance with applicable plumbing, fuel gas, and accessibility requirements. The exam is intended for individuals responsible for evaluating commercial plumbing installations in the field and determining whether the work meets minimum code standards.

The exam includes 60 multiple-choice questions and has a 2.5-hour time limit. Candidates should be prepared for code-based questions that require reference lookup, careful reading, and application of commercial plumbing inspection judgment to realistic field conditions.

The 2021 International Plumbing Code is the primary reference for commercial plumbing inspection topics. It includes provisions for administration, definitions, general regulations, fixtures, water heaters, water supply and distribution, sanitary drainage, indirect waste, special waste, vents, traps, interceptors, separators, storm drainage, materials, joints, connections, and referenced standards. Candidates should become familiar with the chapter layout, section numbering, definitions, tables, exceptions, and index.

The 2021 International Fuel Gas Code supports fuel gas inspection topics that may appear in commercial plumbing inspection work. It includes requirements for gas piping systems, pipe sizing, appliance installation, combustion air, venting, appliance connections, regulators, shutoff valves, fuel gas equipment, and related safety provisions. Candidates should know when a question belongs in the plumbing code and when it belongs in the fuel gas code.

ICC A117.1-2017 supports accessibility requirements that affect plumbing fixtures and facilities. Commercial plumbing inspectors may need to evaluate accessible water closets, lavatories, sinks, drinking fountains, bathing facilities, toilet compartments, grab bars, clear floor spaces, turning spaces, reach ranges, knee and toe clearances, and fixture mounting requirements.

Commercial plumbing inspection questions may require candidates to determine whether a fixture installation meets code, whether an accessible fixture layout is acceptable, how a drainage or venting rule applies, whether an interceptor is required, whether storm drainage requirements are met, whether a fuel gas installation is compliant, or whether a piping material, joint, or connection is permitted.

The ICC P2 Commercial Plumbing Inspector exam is an open book test. Open book testing allows candidates to use approved reference materials during the exam, but it still requires serious preparation. Candidates must know where information is located, how each reference is organized, and how to apply plumbing, fuel gas, and accessibility code language accurately to inspection questions.

Open book does not mean easy. Commercial plumbing inspection questions often require candidates to move quickly between chapters, tables, definitions, standards, and exceptions. The candidate must understand the topic well enough to know which reference to use and where to search. A strong study routine should include repeated practice with all three listed references.

In an open book exam, time management matters.
