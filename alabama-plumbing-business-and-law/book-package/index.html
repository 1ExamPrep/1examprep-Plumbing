Exam Details

The Alabama Plumbing Business and Law Examination is associated with master-level certification administered through the Alabama Plumbers and Gas Fitters Examining Board.

Beginning January 1, 2026, applicants seeking new Master Plumber or Master Gas Fitter certification must satisfy an additional Business and Law examination requirement alongside the applicable master-level trade examination.

The examination evaluates business management, contracts, Alabama licensing laws, administrative rules, financial responsibilities, and the professional obligations associated with plumbing and gas fitting businesses.

  • Exam Name: Alabama Plumbing Business and Law Examination
  • Licensing Authority: Alabama Plumbers and Gas Fitters Examining Board
  • Examination Provider: Prov
  • Examination Format: Open book
  • Number of Questions: 40
  • Time Allowed: 2 hours
  • Passing Score: 70%, equivalent to 28 correct answers

The examination focuses on contractor business and legal knowledge rather than technical plumbing installations. Candidates should study business organization, contracts, estimating, financial management, employment responsibilities, licensing regulations, and project management.

Alabama-specific subjects are especially important because the examination evaluates the laws and administrative requirements governing plumbing and gas fitting professionals in the state.

State Requirements

The Alabama Plumbers and Gas Fitters Examining Board regulates plumbing and gas fitting certification and establishes requirements governing covered professional work throughout Alabama.

Effective January 1, 2026, applicants pursuing new Master Plumber or Master Gas Fitter certification must successfully complete the applicable master-level trade examination and the Business and Law examination.

The Board's requirements recognize that a master-level professional must understand both technical trade responsibilities and the business and legal obligations associated with contracting.

Applicants generally qualify for the Master Plumber examination after holding journeyman plumber certification for at least one year. The corresponding master-level experience requirement applies to qualifying journeyman gas fitters.

Alabama plumbing and gas fitting laws are contained in Title 34, Chapter 37 of the Code of Alabama. Administrative Code Chapter 720 addresses regulations established by the Board.

These laws and rules govern professional certification, Board authority, regulated activities, and responsibilities associated with plumbing and gas fitting work.

Businesses performing plumbing or gas fitting work in Alabama must also comply with applicable Board registration requirements and the rules governing responsible master-level professionals.

The included Alabama law and administrative references help candidates understand the state-specific responsibilities that accompany professional certification and business operations.
