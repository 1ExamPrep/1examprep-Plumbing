## Exam Details

The **Alabama Plumbing Business and Law Examination** is administered by Prov for the Alabama Plumbers and Gas Fitters Examining Board.

Effective January 1, 2026, applicants pursuing a new Alabama Master Plumber or Master Gas Fitter certification must pass a Business and Law examination in addition to the applicable master-level trade examination.

The Business and Law examination focuses on construction business operations, licensing regulations, contracts, estimating, financial management, and the rules governing plumbing and gas fitting professionals in Alabama.

- **Exam Name:** Alabama Plumbing Business and Law Examination
- **Licensing Authority:** Alabama Plumbers and Gas Fitters Examining Board

- **Examination Format:** Open book
