Exam Details

The Alabama Plumbing Business and Law Examination is administered by Prov for the Alabama Plumbers and Gas Fitters Examining Board.

Effective January 1, 2026, applicants pursuing a new Alabama Master Plumber or Master Gas Fitter certification must pass a Business and Law examination in addition to the applicable master-level trade examination.

The Business and Law examination focuses on construction business operations, licensing regulations, contracts, estimating, financial management, and the rules governing plumbing and gas fitting professionals in Alabama.

  • Exam Name: Alabama Plumbing Business and Law Examination
  • Licensing Authority: Alabama Plumbers and Gas Fitters Examining Board
  • Testing Provider: Prov
  • Examination Format: Open book
  • Number of Questions: 40
  • Time Allowed: 2 hours
  • Passing Score: 70%, equivalent to 28 correct answers

The examination covers 12 subject areas:

  • Business Organization: 3 questions
  • Contracts: 4 questions
  • Estimating: 4 questions
  • Financial Management: 4 questions
  • Labor Laws: 2 questions
  • Licensing Laws: 5 questions
  • Lien Laws: 1 question
  • Project Management: 3 questions
  • Risk Management: 3 questions
  • Safety and Environmental: 2 questions
  • Board Rules: 7 questions
  • Payroll and Employment Taxes: 2 questions

Board Rules is the largest examination category, accounting for seven questions. Licensing Laws is another important subject, with five questions.

Contracts, Estimating, and Financial Management each account for four questions. Candidates should review these business operations topics while developing a strong understanding of Alabama plumbing regulations.

The four included references support preparation across the examination's business, legal, and regulatory subjects.

State Requirements

The Alabama Plumbers and Gas Fitters Examining Board regulates plumbing and gas fitting certification and establishes the requirements governing covered work in Alabama.

Beginning January 1, 2026, individuals seeking new master-level certification must successfully complete both the applicable Master Plumber or Master Gas Fitter examination and the Business and Law examination.

This additional Business and Law examination requirement applies to master-level certification rather than journeyman-level certification.

Alabama plumbing laws are established under Title 34, Chapter 37 of the Code of Alabama. The Board's administrative rules are organized under Chapter 720 of the Alabama Administrative Code.

These laws and regulations address professional certification, Board authority, responsibilities, administrative procedures, and requirements affecting plumbing and gas fitting operations.

Master-level applicants must also satisfy the applicable experience and certification qualifications established by the Board.

Understanding these state-specific requirements is an important part of preparing for the Business and Law examination and managing a compliant plumbing business.

The included statutory materials, administrative rules, and Alabama supplement help candidates study these responsibilities alongside general contractor business management principles.
