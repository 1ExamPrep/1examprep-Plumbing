## Test Information and Study Materials

Effective P2 exam preparation should be organized around the major inspection topics found in the approved references. Candidates should not rely only on job experience or general plumbing knowledge. Field experience is helpful, but ICC certification exams are based on the code books. The correct answer is the one supported by the applicable code section, table, definition, or standard.

A strong study plan begins with code navigation. Candidates should learn how each reference is arranged and where major subjects are located. In the International Plumbing Code, plumbing fixtures, water supply, sanitary drainage, indirect waste, vents, and traps are central study areas. Candidates should practice moving between definitions, installation requirements, sizing tables, material provisions, and exceptions. Many exam questions require more than finding a keyword. They require understanding how the section applies to the condition described.

Plumbing fixture study should include fixture requirements, fixture fittings, fixture installation, minimum clearances, public and employee facilities, drinking fountains, service sinks, lavatories, water closets, urinals, showers, bathtubs, and special fixture requirements. Candidates should also understand how accessibility standards may affect plumbing fixture placement and usability. When a question involves an accessible toilet room, lavatory, sink, shower, or drinking fountain, ICC A117.1 may be needed to answer dimensional or usability requirements.

Water supply and distribution study should include potable water protection, approved materials, pipe sizing, pressure, volume, valves, service piping, distribution piping, hot water delivery, backflow prevention, water hammer, thermal expansion, and protection from contamination. Inspectors need to understand how water distribution systems are installed and how code requirements protect the potable water supply.

Sanitary drainage is another major exam area. Candidates should review drainage pipe materials, fittings, slope, horizontal and vertical drainage piping, building drains, building sewers, cleanouts, traps, fixture units, sumps, ejectors, indirect waste, and special waste. Drainage questions often involve tables and specific installation requirements, so candidates should practice locating information quickly and reading all applicable notes.

Storm drainage and roof drainage topics may also appear in commercial plumbing inspection preparation. Candidates should study storm drainage materials, sizing methods, secondary drainage, roof drains, leaders, conductors, scuppers, and disposal. Commercial buildings often require careful drainage design, and inspectors may need to verify that roof drainage systems meet the applicable requirements.

The International Fuel Gas Code should be studied as a separate reference, not as an afterthought. ICC A117.1-2017 should be reviewed with plumbing fixtures in mind. Candidates should pay attention to accessible routes, clear floor space, turning space, reach ranges, operable parts, toilet compartments, water closets, grab bars, lavatories, sinks, mirrors, bathtubs, showers, washing machines, drinking fountains, and related dimensional provisions. Accessibility questions often depend on exact measurements, so candidates should practice finding the correct figure, table, or section.

Timed study is important for the P2 exam. A 60-question exam with a 2.5-hour time limit allows an average of two and a half minutes per question. Some questions may be answered quickly, while others may require code research. Candidates should practice deciding when to look up a section immediately, when to answer from known code knowledge, and when to mark a question for review. The goal is to develop accuracy without losing control of the clock.
