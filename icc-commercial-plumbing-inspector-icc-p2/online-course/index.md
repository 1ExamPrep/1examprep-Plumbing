## Open Book Test

The ICC Commercial Plumbing Inspector P2 exam is an open book test. Candidates may use the approved references during the exam, but they must know how to use those references efficiently. Open book testing rewards candidates who are familiar with the books, organized in their study habits, and comfortable locating code requirements under timed conditions.

For this exam, candidates should become comfortable using the International Plumbing Code, 2021; International Fuel Gas Code, 2021; and ICC A117.1-2017. A strong open book strategy begins with identifying the subject of the question. If the question involves fixtures, water heaters, water distribution, sanitary drainage, storm drainage, interceptors, separators, indirect waste, cleanouts, traps, vents, or commercial plumbing installation rules, the International Plumbing Code is usually the primary reference. If the question involves fuel gas piping, gas-fired appliances, regulators, shutoff valves, combustion air, chimneys, vents, vent connectors, or fuel gas installation, the International Fuel Gas Code is usually the primary reference. If the question involves accessible plumbing fixtures, clear floor spaces, grab bars, lavatory clearances, water closet clearances, drinking fountains, bathing fixtures, or accessibility dimensions, ICC A117.1-2017 may be the primary reference.

Open book does not mean the answer will be obvious. Commercial plumbing inspection questions may turn on a specific fixture type, occupancy condition, accessibility dimension, pipe size, fitting, joint, material, slope, developed length, venting method, connection point, water heater safety requirement, cleanout location, gas appliance condition, or code exception. Candidates should read the full question, identify the key condition, locate the correct reference, and review the complete requirement before choosing an answer.
