How does the Commercial Plumbing Inspector (ICC P2) exam work?

The Commercial Plumbing Inspector (ICC P2) license comes from the International Code Council (ICC), for the city or state that accepts the exam. Pearson VUE gives the exam. The board approves your application first; then you schedule the exam and bring only the references the candidate bulletin allows.
