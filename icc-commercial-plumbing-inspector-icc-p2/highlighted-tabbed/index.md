The ICC Commercial Plumbing Inspector - P2 exam is a certification exam focused on commercial plumbing inspection knowledge. The 2021 Commercial Plumbing Inspector - P2 exam is commonly listed as a 60-question exam with a 2.5-hour time limit. Candidates should prepare for a timed, code-based exam that requires efficient use of the approved references. The exam tests plumbing code knowledge, fuel gas code knowledge, accessibility awareness, and the ability to evaluate installed systems for code compliance.

Major study areas include general plumbing requirements, fixtures, water heaters, water supply and distribution, sanitary drainage, indirect waste, vents, traps, storm drainage, special waste, special piping systems, fuel gas provisions, and accessibility requirements related to plumbing fixtures and facilities. Candidates should understand how inspection differs from plan review. A plans examiner evaluates proposed design documents before installation, while an inspector verifies installed work in the field.

## Test Information and Study Materials

Effective preparation for the ICC Commercial Plumbing Inspector - P2 exam should begin with reference navigation. Candidates should learn the structure of each reference, including the table of contents, index, definitions, chapter organization, tables, figures, and exceptions. General plumbing requirements are a major part of preparation. Candidates should understand how the code addresses materials, equipment, inspections, testing, protection of piping, installation requirements, permits, approval, and unsafe conditions. Commercial plumbing inspectors must evaluate whether installed work meets the code and whether required tests or protections have been completed.

Fixture installation should receive careful attention. Candidates should review minimum plumbing facilities, fixture requirements, fixture fittings, water closets, urinals, lavatories, sinks, bathtubs, showers, drinking fountains, service sinks, employee facilities, public facilities, and accessible fixture requirements. Accessibility standards may affect fixture location and usability, so candidates should be ready to use both the International Plumbing Code and ICC A117.1 when reviewing installed plumbing facilities.

Water supply and distribution study should include potable water protection, pipe sizing, pressure, volume, valves, service piping, distribution piping, hot water delivery, backflow prevention, water hammer, thermal expansion, water heaters, and protection from contamination. Inspection questions may require checking whether a system is properly installed and whether the correct protection is provided.

Sanitary drainage is another central study area. Candidates should review drainage fixture units, building drains, building sewers, horizontal branches, stacks, offsets, pipe slope, cleanouts, traps, interceptors, sumps, ejectors, indirect waste, and special waste. Drainage questions often involve tables, diagrams, and detailed code conditions. Candidates should practice locating the correct sizing table and reading all notes before selecting an answer.

Venting system inspection should be studied in depth. Candidates should review vent sizing, vent connections, dry vents, wet vents, common vents, circuit vents, combination waste and vent systems, vent terminals, developed length, trap arms, and vent grades. Venting questions can be challenging because the correct answer may depend on fixture arrangement, pipe size, distance, vent type, or permitted method.
