Exam Details

The ICC Commercial Plumbing Inspector certification is Exam P2. ICC's current national P2 examination listing identifies the exam as containing 60 questions with a 2-1/2-hour testing period.

The examination evaluates knowledge associated with the inspection of commercial plumbing installations. Candidates should be prepared to evaluate piping systems, plumbing fixtures, water heaters, potable and nonpotable water systems, sanitary drainage, venting, special waste systems, storm drainage, and other regulated plumbing installations.

Important Commercial Plumbing Inspector preparation areas include:

  • General plumbing requirements
  • Piping protection and installation
  • Pipe supports and penetrations
  • Plumbing fixtures and fixture fittings
  • Accessibility requirements
  • Water heaters and related safety devices
  • Fuel-gas piping associated with equipment
  • Combustion air and venting
  • Water supply and distribution
  • Backflow protection
  • Sanitary drainage systems
  • Drainage fixture units and pipe sizing
  • Vent systems
  • Traps, interceptors, and separators
  • Special waste and special piping
  • Storm drainage systems
  • Health-care plumbing considerations
  • Testing and inspection requirements

Candidates should be able to inspect plumbing systems not only for correct pipe sizing but also for proper materials, support, slope, protection, clearances, connections, accessibility, testing, and installation methods.

Water Supply and Distribution is an especially important study area. Commercial Plumbing Inspectors may need to review water-service sizing, distribution piping, pressure requirements, fixture demand, backflow prevention, hot-water systems, and protection of the potable water supply.

Sanitary Drainage and Venting are also major areas of preparation. Candidates should be comfortable determining whether drainage piping is properly sized, sloped, connected, supported, and vented and whether traps and vent configurations meet applicable code requirements.

The exam also requires familiarity with fuel-gas provisions associated with plumbing equipment. Water-heater installations may involve gas-pipe sizing, appliance connections, combustion air, venting, clearances, temperature and pressure relief protection, and other requirements that may direct candidates to the International Fuel Gas Code.

Open Book Test

The ICC Commercial Plumbing Inspector P2 examination is an open book test. ICC's examination rules allow permitted references for qualifying open-book certification exams, and candidates should follow the current exam catalog requirements for the code cycle and testing method selected.

Open-book testing makes code navigation a critical part of preparation. A candidate who knows where information is located can use the references much more efficiently than someone who attempts to search every chapter from the beginning.

The 2021 International Plumbing Code should be the primary focus of study. Candidates should become comfortable navigating definitions, general regulations, fixtures, water heaters, water supply, sanitary drainage, indirect and special waste, vents, traps, interceptors, storm drainage, and special piping provisions.

The 2021 International Fuel Gas Code supports inspection topics involving gas-fired water heaters and other fuel-burning equipment. Candidates should understand where to find provisions covering fuel-gas piping, equipment connections, combustion air, venting, clearances, and related installation requirements.

The ICC A117.1-2017 Standard for Accessible and Usable Buildings and Facilities supports preparation involving accessible plumbing fixtures and spaces. Candidates may need to evaluate fixture locations, clear floor spaces, dimensions, heights, approach conditions, and other accessibility features associated with plumbing facilities.

A productive study approach is to identify the subject before opening a reference. A question about sanitary drainage should immediately direct attention toward drainage provisions. A question involving an accessible water closet or lavatory should suggest the accessibility standard. A question involving combustion air for a fuel-fired water heater should point toward the fuel-gas code.

Practice questions are most useful when they become reference-navigation exercises. After answering, locate the applicable provision and read enough surrounding material to understand how the rule applies and whether exceptions or related requirements affect the situation.

Licensing Steps

The ICC Commercial Plumbing Inspector P2 is a professional certification rather than a universal state plumbing license. Requirements to work as an official plumbing inspector vary by state, county, municipality, employer, and authority having jurisdiction.

  1. Identify the P2 certification. Confirm that the ICC Commercial Plumbing Inspector credential matches your professional, employer, or jurisdictional requirement.
  2. Select the correct code cycle. This preparation product is built around the 2021 International Plumbing Code, 2021 International Fuel Gas Code, and ICC A117.1-2017.
  3. Review the exam scope. Organize study around fixtures, water heaters, water supply, drainage, venting, storm drainage, special piping, accessibility, and general inspection requirements.
  4. Study the supplied references. Learn when to use the IPC, IFGC, and accessibility standard.
  5. Use the online practice questions. Practice locating and applying plumbing requirements rather than relying only on memorization.
  6. Register for the ICC exam. Purchase and schedule the appropriate P2 examination and code cycle through ICC's credentialing process.
  7. Complete the examination. Take the Commercial Plumbing Inspector examination within the testing period assigned to the selected exam.
  8. Complete local requirements when applicable. Follow any additional state or local requirements for appointment, employment, certification, registration, or continuing education.

State Requirements

The ICC Commercial Plumbing Inspector P2 credential is a national ICC certification. It is not tied to a single state licensing program, and earning the credential does not automatically authorize a person to perform official plumbing inspections in every jurisdiction.

States, cities, counties, municipalities, and other authorities having jurisdiction may establish their own requirements for plumbing inspectors. Those requirements may include ICC certification, state certification, trade experience, education, continuing education, registration, or employment by a governmental or approved inspection agency.

Candidates should therefore distinguish between earning the ICC P2 credential and meeting the requirements of the jurisdiction where they intend to work. The ICC examination evaluates commercial plumbing inspection knowledge, while each jurisdiction determines who may exercise official inspection authority.

Commercial Plumbing Inspectors may be responsible for verifying approved materials, pipe sizing, system testing, fixture installations, water heaters, backflow protection, drainage and vent systems, storm drainage, accessibility, and special plumbing systems. The exact scope of official authority depends on the applicable jurisdiction.

Test Information and Study Materials

The online practice-question product includes 3 months of access. The access period allows candidates to divide preparation into manageable subjects and revisit difficult plumbing-code topics throughout the study period.

Begin with the International Plumbing Code and become familiar with the organization of the major plumbing-system chapters. Practice locating requirements for piping materials, supports, protection, fixtures, water heaters, water distribution, sanitary drainage, vents, traps, storm drainage, and special piping.

For fixtures, review material approvals, installation, clearances, access, fixture fittings, flow rates, waste receptors, and related requirements. Accessibility should be studied alongside fixture requirements because a technically correct plumbing fixture installation can still require specific accessible dimensions and clearances.

For water heaters, review equipment approval, location, clearances, pans, temperature and pressure relief valves, discharge piping, expansion control, fuel connections, combustion air, and venting. These topics may require candidates to move between the IPC and IFGC.

For water supply and distribution, study water-service and distribution piping, pressure, fixture-unit calculations, sizing, shutoff valves, backflow prevention, nonpotable systems, and hot-water requirements.

For sanitary drainage, review pipe sizing, slope, drainage fixture units, cleanouts, horizontal and vertical drainage, building drains, building sewers, sumps, ejectors, and related installation rules.

Venting preparation should include vent sizing, vent connections, vent terminals, wet venting where applicable, common vent arrangements, circuit venting, and other approved vent-system configurations.

Traps, interceptors, and separators require familiarity with trap seals, prohibited configurations, grease interceptors, oil separators, and other installations associated with special waste streams.

Storm drainage should also be included in the study plan. Candidates may encounter questions involving roof drains, storm piping, sizing, secondary drainage, conductors, leaders, and system discharge.

Timed practice helps candidates become more efficient. The current national P2 listing identifies 60 questions in a 2-1/2-hour testing period. That means candidates should develop a steady pace while preserving enough time to locate code provisions for more detailed questions.
