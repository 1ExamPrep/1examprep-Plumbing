## State Requirements

Arizona uses contractor license classifications to define the type of work a contractor may legally contract for and perform. The CR-77 Plumbing, Including Solar classification allows the scopes of work permitted by the commercial C-77 and residential R-77 Plumbing, Including Solar classifications.

The plumbing including solar scope allows installation, alteration, and repair of all plumbing, including solar, when performed solely within property lines and not on public easements or rights-of-way except as allowed by the classification. The scope includes piping, fixtures, and appliances related to water supply, including pressure vessels and tanks, except municipal or related water supply systems.

The classification also includes venting and sanitary drainage systems, fuel gas piping, water conditioning equipment, water heaters, solar water heating applications, hydronic-related plumbing work, sprinkler-related plumbing, sewage treatment systems, and equipment common to the plumbing industry when performed within the allowed scope.

Contractors should stay within the scope of the license issued to them. Plumbing including solar work can overlap with mechanical, fire protection, electrical, solar energy, excavation, utility, boiler, and general contracting activities. Contractors should understand the limits of the CR-77 classification before bidding or performing work and should use properly licensed subcontractors when work falls outside the classification scope.

Residential plumbing including solar projects may involve dwelling units, water heaters, solar water heating components, fixtures, water supply piping, drainage and vent systems, fuel gas piping, sprinkler systems covered by residential standards, and on-site wastewater provisions. Commercial projects may involve larger plumbing systems, commercial fixtures, equipment rooms, sprinkler coordination, hydronic systems, boilers, backflow prevention, utility coordination, and more complex code requirements.
