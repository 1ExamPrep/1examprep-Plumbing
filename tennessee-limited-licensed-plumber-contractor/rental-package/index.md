## State Requirements

The Tennessee Limited Licensed Plumber license is regulated through the Tennessee Board for Licensing Contractors. The license is issued to individuals rather than businesses or companies.

The LLP license applies to covered plumbing work costing less than $25,000 per project in municipalities that use the Tennessee Division of Fire Prevention for permits or inspections. Tennessee also identifies general maintenance work costing less than $500 as exempt from the LLP license requirement.

Covered LLP plumbing work can involve piping, fixtures, and appliances within residential or commercial buildings for supplying gas, water, or other liquids and disposing of wastewater or sewage. Plumbing work outside residential or commercial buildings, such as certain utility connections or irrigation systems, is outside the stated LLP scope.

The Limited Licensed Plumber credential is different from a Tennessee contractor license. Plumbers who intend to perform projects requiring contractor licensure need to obtain the appropriate contractor classification for the scope and value of the work.

Local requirements should always be considered. Tennessee directs LLP licensees and applicants to check with the local government before beginning work because municipalities may maintain separate plumbing licensing, permit, and inspection requirements.
