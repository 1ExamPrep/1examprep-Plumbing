## State Requirements

The Tennessee Board for Licensing Contractors regulates the Limited Licensed Plumber program. The LLP license is an individual credential for qualifying plumbing work under Tennessee's limited plumbing licensing framework.

The license applies to plumbing work with a total cost of less than $25,000 per project in municipalities that utilize the Tennessee Division of Fire Prevention for permits or inspections. Tennessee also states that general maintenance work costing less than $500 is exempt from the LLP licensing requirement.

The LLP license covers qualifying plumbing work within residential or commercial buildings involving piping, fixtures, and appliances for supplying gas, water, or other liquids and for disposing of wastewater or sewage. Tennessee's LLP framework does not extend the license to plumbing work outside residential or commercial buildings such as utility connections or irrigation systems.

Local requirements still apply. Tennessee advises plumbers to check with the local government where work will be performed because municipalities may maintain their own plumbing licensing, permit, and inspection requirements.

Plumbers planning to perform projects at or above the LLP monetary limit should also understand the difference between the Limited Licensed Plumber license and Tennessee's contractor-level plumbing classifications. The appropriate credential depends on the scope, project value, location, and nature of the work.
