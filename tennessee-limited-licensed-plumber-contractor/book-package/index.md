## State Requirements

The Tennessee Board for Licensing Contractors regulates the Limited Licensed Plumber program. The LLP license is currently required for plumbers performing covered work in municipalities using the Division of Fire Prevention for permits or inspections and applies to plumbing projects costing less than $25,000.

Tennessee's current LLP guidance states that general maintenance work costing less than $500 is exempt from the LLP license requirement. Other exemptions and local requirements may apply depending on the individual, employer, location, and nature of the work.

The LLP license differs from Tennessee's contractor-level plumbing classifications. Candidates who plan to undertake plumbing projects at or above the contractor licensing threshold should understand whether a different Tennessee contractor classification is required for their intended work.

Local government requirements remain important even after a state LLP license is issued. Municipalities or counties may impose additional licensing, permit, inspection, or code requirements. Candidates preparing for the LLP exam should consider the state license one part of the broader process of performing plumbing work legally and professionally.
