About this Limited Licensed Plumber Contractor package

Prepare for the Tennessee Limited Licensed Plumber (LLP) examination with a focused collection of plumbing code, trade mathematics, practical plumbing, fuel gas, and construction safety references. The Tennessee Limited Licensed Plumber Contractor Exam Book Package brings together five technical resources to support candidates preparing for the Prov-administered Tennessee LLP trade examination and building stronger plumbing knowledge for professional work.

The Tennessee Limited Licensed Plumber license is designed for qualifying plumbing work performed under the state's limited plumbing licensing framework. Tennessee currently requires the LLP license for plumbers performing covered work in municipalities that use the Division of Fire Prevention for permits or inspections when the total plumbing project is less than $25,000. Local requirements can vary, so plumbers should also understand the licensing and permitting rules where they intend to work.

The Tennessee LLP exam is open book, making reference navigation an important part of preparation. Simply owning the references is not enough; effective preparation requires repeated use of the books before exam day.

Exam Details

The Tennessee Limited Licensed Plumber examination is administered by Prov for the Tennessee Board for Licensing Contractors. The current LLP examination contains 40 questions and allows candidates 2 hours to complete the test.

The current examination outline is divided among the following subject areas:

  • General Knowledge: 3 questions
  • Plumbing Math: 2 questions
  • Code Requirements: 4 questions
  • Water Supply and Distribution: 4 questions
  • Water Heaters: 2 questions
  • Drain, Waste, and Vent Systems: 10 questions
  • Fixtures & Fittings: 2 questions
  • Isometrics: 7 questions
  • Fuel Gas Systems: 4 questions
  • OSHA Safety: 2 questions

Drain, Waste, and Vent Systems is the largest single category on the current exam, accounting for 10 of the 40 questions. Isometrics follows with 7 questions. Code Requirements, Water Supply and Distribution, and Fuel Gas Systems each account for 4 questions. Candidates should therefore spend significant study time on drainage, waste and vent systems, plumbing drawings, plumbing-code navigation, water systems, and fuel gas concepts.

Plumbing Math, Water Heaters, Fixtures & Fittings, OSHA Safety, and General Knowledge make up the rest of the examination. Although some categories contain fewer questions, they should not be ignored. A balanced preparation plan gives candidates the opportunity to strengthen both their code knowledge and practical trade skills across the complete examination outline.

Licensing Steps

Determine whether the Tennessee LLP license applies to your work. The Limited Licensed Plumber license applies to qualifying plumbing work under the state's LLP requirements. Tennessee advises plumbers to check with the local government where they intend to work because municipalities may have their own licensing, permitting, or inspection requirements.

Prepare for the LLP trade exam. The Tennessee LLP license is issued to individuals who meet the applicable requirements and pass the LLP trade examination. Candidates should use the current exam outline to guide preparation and become familiar with the approved reference materials before test day.

Complete the examination requirement. The LLP trade examination is administered through Prov. Candidates should register for the correct Limited Licensed Plumber examination and follow the current provider procedures for scheduling and testing.

Prepare the state application information. Tennessee's current LLP licensing requirements include identification information and completion of the Initial Limited Licensed Plumber application. Applicants should make sure all required information is accurate before submission.

Submit the application and required fee. The Tennessee Board for Licensing Contractors processes LLP applications and determines whether applicants meet the requirements for issuance of the license.

Confirm local requirements before beginning work. An LLP license does not automatically replace every local plumbing license, permit, inspection, or code requirement. Plumbers should check with the appropriate municipality or local authority before performing work.

State Requirements

The Tennessee Board for Licensing Contractors regulates the Limited Licensed Plumber program. The LLP license is currently required for plumbers performing covered work in municipalities using the Division of Fire Prevention for permits or inspections and applies to plumbing projects costing less than $25,000.

Tennessee's current LLP guidance states that general maintenance work costing less than $500 is exempt from the LLP license requirement. Other exemptions and local requirements may apply depending on the individual, employer, location, and nature of the work.

The LLP license differs from Tennessee's contractor-level plumbing classifications. Candidates who plan to undertake plumbing projects at or above the contractor licensing threshold should understand whether a different Tennessee contractor classification is required for their intended work.

Local government requirements remain important even after a state LLP license is issued. Municipalities or counties may impose additional licensing, permit, inspection, or code requirements. Candidates preparing for the LLP exam should consider the state license one part of the broader process of performing plumbing work legally and professionally.

Test Information and Study Materials

A productive LLP study plan should begin with the largest areas of the current examination. Drain, Waste, and Vent Systems accounts for one-quarter of the 40-question exam. Candidates should become familiar with sanitary drainage, drainage fittings, vent systems, traps, cleanouts, materials, sizing concepts, and the sections of the International Plumbing Code where these requirements are located.

Isometrics accounts for 7 questions and deserves dedicated practice. Plumbing isometric questions can require candidates to visualize piping systems and interpret relationships among fixtures, drainage piping, vents, and other components. Practice reading plumbing drawings and following piping paths rather than relying exclusively on text-based study.

Water Supply and Distribution and Code Requirements each contain 4 questions. The International Plumbing Code, 2021 should be used regularly during preparation for these subjects. Candidates should learn how chapters are organized and practice using code tables, definitions, exceptions, and index terms.

Fuel Gas Systems also accounts for 4 questions. The International Fuel Gas Code, 2021 provides additional study material involving fuel-gas piping, sizing, materials, appliances, combustion air, vents, and related system requirements. Candidates can use this reference to strengthen technical understanding while preparing for fuel-gas questions.

Mathematics for Plumbers and Pipefitters, 8th Edition supports the Plumbing Math portion of the examination. Candidates should actively solve problems involving measurements and plumbing-related calculations instead of only reading worked examples. Repetition helps build speed and reduces the likelihood of basic calculation errors under testing conditions.

OSHA Safety accounts for 2 questions. Code of Federal Regulations - 29 CFR Part 1926 gives candidates a broader construction-safety resource for studying hazards and safe work practices relevant to plumbing and construction jobsites.

The remaining exam areas include General Knowledge, Water Heaters, and Fixtures & Fittings. These sections should be incorporated into a complete study routine. Candidates should review terminology, installation principles, fixture concepts, water-heater requirements, and other practical plumbing knowledge connected to these subjects.
