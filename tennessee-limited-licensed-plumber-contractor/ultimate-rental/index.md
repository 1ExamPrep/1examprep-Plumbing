## State Requirements

The Tennessee Limited Licensed Plumber license applies to plumbing projects with a total cost of less than $25,000. Tennessee's current rules also require plumbers to comply with applicable state and local municipality requirements, including permit and inspection requirements.

The LLP credential is different from a Tennessee contractor license. Current Tennessee renewal materials specifically state that an LLP is not authorized to use the designation "contractor" unless the individual also holds a valid contractor license. For plumbing projects of $25,000 or more, Tennessee requires the appropriate contractor license with a qualifying mechanical or plumbing classification.

The LLP scope includes qualifying plumbing work involving piping, fixtures, and appliances within residential or commercial buildings for supplying gas, water, or other liquids and disposing of wastewater or sewage. The LLP definition does not extend to plumbing work performed outside residential or commercial buildings, including utility connections or irrigation systems.

Candidates should also consider local requirements before planning work. The state LLP credential operates alongside local licensing, permitting, and inspection requirements, and the rules can differ depending on the municipality where the plumbing project is located.
