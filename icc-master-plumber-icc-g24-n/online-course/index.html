How does the Master Plumber (ICC G24-N) exam work?

The Master Plumber (ICC G24-N) license comes from the International Code Council (ICC), for the city or state that accepts the exam. Pearson VUE gives the exam. The board approves your application first; then you schedule the exam and bring only the references the candidate bulletin allows.

  • ICC national standard plumber exams (G24, G25, G27, G28 and the F series) are used by many cities and counties that license plumbers.
  • ICC inspector and plans examiner certifications (P1, P2, P3) are open book on the code editions listed in the exam bulletin.
