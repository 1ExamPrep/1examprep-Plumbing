## Open Book Test

The Idaho Plumbing Specialty Contractor exam is an **open book** exam. Your books are allowed in the testing room—but only the approved references, in an acceptable format, and within the published rules.

DOPL’s plumbing exam bulletin allows candidates to **highlight** and **tab** reference materials and to leave notes in the reference material; however, **loose paper is not allowed**. ## Test Information and Study Materials

Because the Plumbing Specialty Contractor exam is open book, the most important skill to build is efficient reference use. Here’s how to prepare using the Contractor’s Business and Law Reference Manual as your foundation:

- **Study in “lookup mode.”** Instead of reading cover-to-cover, practice locating key items: definitions, requirements, exceptions, responsibilities, and procedures. You’re training speed and accuracy together.
- **Create a simple, compliant tab system.** Tabs are helpful when they direct you to major sections quickly. Avoid over-tabbing—too many tabs can slow you down and make sections harder to find.
- **Highlight with restraint.** Highlight keywords, thresholds, and exceptions. Heavy highlighting makes scanning slower because everything looks important.
- **Practice under a timer.** Do timed practice sets so you learn how long a “reasonable” search should take. If you’re searching too long, your strategy should be to make the best choice available and move forward.
- **Train your question-recognition skill.** Most business-and-law exam questions fall into common categories: definitions, compliance requirements, contract responsibility, recordkeeping expectations, and scenario-based judgment questions. If you can identify the category quickly, you’ll know where to look.
- **Build a repeatable routine.** Use the same process every time: read → identify the topic → locate the section → confirm the detail → answer → move on.

**A simple weekly prep rhythm:**

- **Session 1:** Review a major section and build your index/tabs for that area
- **Session 2:** Practice a timed set focused on that section
- **Session 3:** Review missed questions and confirm the correct rule location
- **Session 4:** Mixed timed set to build pacing and endurance

This approach keeps your prep practical and exam-aligned. The goal is not just learning information—it’s becoming fast and confident with your reference manual.
