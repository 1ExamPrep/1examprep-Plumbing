Exam Details

The 557 Kansas Master Plumber with Gas examination is part of the International Code Council contractor and trade examination program. The examination contains 100 multiple-choice questions and provides a 4-hour time limit.

The published examination outline divides the test into the following content areas:

  • Administration: 4%
  • General Regulations: 8%
  • Fixtures: 5%
  • Water Heaters: 4%
  • Water Supply and Distribution: 11%
  • Sanitary Drainage: 9%
  • Vents: 13%
  • Traps, Interceptors and Separators: 4%
  • Storm Drainage: 2%
  • Plan Reading: 21%
  • Gas: 19%

Plan Reading is the largest published content category at 21 percent of the examination. This makes the ability to interpret plumbing and gas-system layouts especially important for candidates preparing for the master-level test. Candidates should be comfortable identifying system components, following piping arrangements, recognizing applicable code requirements, and applying technical information to drawings.

Gas represents another significant portion of the examination at 19 percent. Candidates should devote meaningful preparation time to the 2015 International Fuel Gas Code and become comfortable working with requirements affecting fuel gas piping, appliances, combustion air, venting, connectors, and related system provisions.

Vents account for 13 percent of the examination, while Water Supply and Distribution accounts for 11 percent and Sanitary Drainage accounts for 9 percent. Together, these subjects make up an important portion of the test and require candidates to understand how plumbing systems function as a complete installation.

The master examination is intended to evaluate broad technical knowledge. Candidates should prepare to interpret code provisions rather than merely memorize isolated facts. Practice should emphasize understanding how a requirement applies to a particular installation condition.

Open Book Test

The Kansas Master Plumber with Gas ICC 557-KS examination is an open book test. Candidates are permitted to use approved references during the examination in accordance with ICC testing requirements.

The approved code references for this product are the 2015 International Plumbing Code and the 2015 International Fuel Gas Code.

Although the exam is open book, preparation is still essential. A candidate has four hours to answer 100 questions. If time were divided evenly, that would provide an average of approximately 2.4 minutes for each question. Searching through the code for every response can consume time quickly.

Candidates should therefore learn how both references are organized. Become familiar with chapter titles, definitions, tables, common terminology, and the general location of important plumbing and fuel gas subjects. The goal is not to memorize every code section but to recognize where information is likely to be found.

Practice questions are particularly useful for open-book preparation because they help candidates develop a repeatable lookup process. Read the question carefully, identify the system involved, determine which reference applies, narrow the subject to the appropriate chapter, and then confirm the applicable provision.

Master-level candidates should also practice questions without immediately opening a book. Attempt the problem first, then use the reference to confirm the answer. This can help build stronger code knowledge while still developing efficient reference-navigation habits.

Licensing Steps

Kansas plumbing licenses are administered through cities and counties under the state's statutory framework. Candidates should determine the requirements of the specific Kansas jurisdiction where they intend to obtain a master plumbing credential.

  1. Identify the issuing jurisdiction. Determine which Kansas city or county administers the master plumbing license for the area where you intend to work.
  2. Confirm the accepted examination. Verify that the jurisdiction uses or accepts the ICC 557 Kansas Master Plumber with Gas examination for the applicable master credential.
  3. Meet the experience requirement. Kansas law requires applicants for a master certificate to document qualifying journeyman-level or field experience before the certificate is issued.
  4. Prepare using the approved references. Study the 2015 International Plumbing Code and 2015 International Fuel Gas Code while practicing the subjects in the ICC exam outline.
  5. Register for the ICC examination. Complete the applicable ICC examination registration and scheduling requirements.
  6. Take the examination. The ICC 557 Kansas Master Plumber with Gas exam contains 100 multiple-choice questions with a four-hour time limit.
  7. Complete the local application process. Submit the examination results, experience documentation, application, and other items required by the applicable Kansas city or county.
  8. Maintain the credential. Follow the renewal and continuing-education requirements associated with the license issued by the applicable jurisdiction.

State Requirements

Kansas statutes designate standard plumbing competency examinations administered by organizations including the International Code Council for determining qualifications for plumbing contractor, master, and journeyman licenses under the state's local licensing framework.

Before issuing a master plumber certificate, a Kansas issuing jurisdiction must verify qualifying experience. State law provides two experience pathways: an applicant may document possession of a valid journeyman certificate for a minimum of two years, or document a minimum of four years of field experience.

This requirement reflects the advanced responsibility associated with master-level plumbing work. Candidates are expected to bring substantial trade experience to the examination and then demonstrate knowledge of code requirements, system design considerations, plan interpretation, and the technical principles governing plumbing and fuel gas installations.

Kansas plumbing licensing has historically been administered through cities and counties. Applicants should therefore confirm local requirements in addition to studying for the ICC examination. A jurisdiction may require specific applications, proof of experience, examination documentation, fees, continuing education, and other administrative materials.

The examination itself should not be confused with the license. Passing an ICC test demonstrates examination performance, while the applicable city or county determines whether the applicant has satisfied all requirements for issuance of the local credential.

Candidates who perform specialized work such as medical gas piping may also be subject to additional qualification requirements beyond a general plumbing license. The ICC 557 examination should therefore be viewed as one component of professional qualification rather than authorization for every specialized piping activity.

Test Information and Study Materials

Effective preparation for the Kansas Master Plumber with Gas examination should be organized around the published content outline. Because the examination contains 100 questions across numerous plumbing and gas categories, candidates benefit from studying individual subjects and then combining them through comprehensive practice.

Give plan reading substantial attention. Plan Reading accounts for 21 percent of the examination, making it the largest published category. Practice interpreting piping layouts, fixture locations, system relationships, and other information presented graphically. The ability to connect a drawing with an applicable code requirement is an important master-level skill.

Build strong fuel gas knowledge. Gas accounts for 19 percent of the exam. Become familiar with the organization of the 2015 International Fuel Gas Code and practice locating requirements associated with piping, appliances, combustion air, venting, connectors, and system installation.

Review vent systems carefully. Vents represent 13 percent of the examination. Study vent-system principles as well as code requirements affecting sizing, connections, terminations, and different venting arrangements.

Strengthen water supply and distribution knowledge. This category represents 11 percent of the exam. Review potable water protection, water-service and distribution systems, pressure considerations, piping requirements, sizing provisions, and related installation requirements.

Practice sanitary drainage. Sanitary Drainage accounts for 9 percent of the published content. Candidates should understand drainage-system layout, sizing, installation, indirect waste, sumps, ejectors, and other related provisions.

Review administration and general regulations. Together these areas represent 12 percent of the examination. Master candidates should understand general code responsibilities, installation requirements, definitions, materials, approvals, and other rules that affect the entire plumbing system.

Include fixtures and water heaters in regular review. Fixture requirements account for 5 percent of the examination and water heaters account for 4 percent. Study installation requirements, fixture-related provisions, water-heating connections, protection, and other applicable code subjects.

Do not overlook traps, interceptors, separators, and storm drainage. These categories represent smaller portions of the examination, but every question contributes to the final result. A complete study plan should include all published content areas.

Test Information and Study Materials (continued)

Practice reference navigation. Because the examination is open book, speed with the code matters. Practice identifying which book applies before beginning a lookup. Then locate the appropriate chapter, section, or table instead of searching the entire book.

Use timed question sets. The four-hour examination requires candidates to manage 100 questions efficiently. Timed practice can help you develop a steady pace and identify whether code searches are consuming too much time.

Analyze missed questions. When an answer is incorrect, find the applicable provision and determine why the selected response did not apply. This method helps transform individual mistakes into lasting code knowledge rather than simply memorizing an answer key.
