## How does the Journeyman Plumber exam work?
The Journeyman Plumber license comes from the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board (CIB). PSI gives the exam. The board approves your application first; then you schedule the exam and bring only the references the candidate bulletin allows.

  - The Oklahoma Construction Industries Board licenses plumbing and natural gas contractors and journeymen, including residential classes.
  - PSI gives the Oklahoma plumbing exams, and contractors also take a plumbing business and law exam.
