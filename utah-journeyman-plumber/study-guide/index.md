## How does the Journeyman Plumber exam work?

The Journeyman Plumber license comes from the Utah Division of Professional Licensing (DOPL). The board approves your application first; then you schedule the exam and bring only the references the candidate bulletin allows.

- Utah DOPL licenses journeyman, residential journeyman and master plumbers, and master plumbers take a law and rule exam.
- Utah apprentices can test out of training levels 1A to 2B with their own exams.
