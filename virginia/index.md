## How does plumber licensing work in Virginia?

The Virginia Board for Contractors (DPOR) licenses plumbers here. The board approves your application first; then you schedule the exam and bring only the references the candidate bulletin allows.

- Virginia licenses journeyman, master and residential plumbing tradesmen through DPOR; PSI gives the exams.
- Virginia also certifies plumbing inspectors and plans examiners using ICC P1, P2 and P3 exams.
