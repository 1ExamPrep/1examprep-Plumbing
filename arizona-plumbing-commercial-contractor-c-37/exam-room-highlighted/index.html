Exam Details

Preparation for the Arizona Plumbing Commercial Contractor (C-37) examination should focus on the technical subjects represented in the approved exam-room reference set. Candidates should build familiarity with plumbing systems, fuel-gas installations, mechanical systems, sprinkler requirements, wastewater provisions, and construction safety.

The International Plumbing Code, 2018 should be a major part of preparation. Candidates should become comfortable locating information involving water distribution, drainage, venting, fixtures, traps, indirect waste, sanitary drainage, storm drainage, and related plumbing-system requirements.

The International Fuel Gas Code, 2018 supports study involving fuel-gas piping, materials, sizing, appliances, combustion air, venting, testing, and other installation concepts. Candidates should practice distinguishing fuel-gas questions from general plumbing or mechanical questions so they can move quickly to the correct reference.

The International Mechanical Code, 2018 adds technical material involving mechanical systems, equipment, ventilation, and other building-service requirements. Commercial plumbing can overlap with mechanical and fuel-gas systems, making it important to recognize which code applies to a specific subject.

Fire-sprinkler preparation is represented through NFPA 13, NFPA 13D, and NFPA 13R. These standards apply to different sprinkler-system situations, so candidates should learn the role and scope of each reference rather than treating the three books as interchangeable.

The Arizona wastewater reference supports preparation involving on-site wastewater treatment facility provisions represented in state water-quality rules. OSHA 29 CFR Part 1926 adds federal construction-safety requirements relevant to excavation, equipment, personal protective equipment, material handling, ladders, scaffolds, and other jobsite activities.

Having books available does not eliminate the need to study. Plumbing codes, NFPA standards, safety regulations, and wastewater provisions contain large amounts of technical information. Candidates who are unfamiliar with the organization of the books may lose valuable time searching through the wrong reference.

The highlighted and tabbed books included in this package are designed to support a more organized preparation process. Tabs can help separate major sections, while highlighted passages can make important information easier to identify during repeated study.

For plumbing-system questions, the International Plumbing Code should be an important starting point. Fuel-gas questions may lead to the International Fuel Gas Code, while mechanical-system topics may require the International Mechanical Code.

Sprinkler questions should be matched carefully to NFPA 13, NFPA 13D, or NFPA 13R. Wastewater subjects may require the Arizona Administrative Code reference, while safety-related questions can be studied through OSHA 29 CFR Part 1926.
