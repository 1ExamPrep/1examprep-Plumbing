  3. Complete required examinations. Complete the examinations required for the applicable license classification unless an approved waiver applies.

Test Information and Study Materials

Studying for an exam with multiple reference books requires a different approach from studying for a closed-book test. Instead of trying to memorize every technical requirement, candidates should work on understanding the major concepts while learning where detailed information can be found.

Begin by becoming familiar with the purpose of each book. The International Plumbing Code should become a natural starting point for core plumbing subjects. The International Fuel Gas Code covers fuel gas-related material, while the International Mechanical Code addresses mechanical systems and related requirements. Construction safety subjects should direct you toward 29 CFR Part 1926.

The NFPA sprinkler standards should also be studied as distinct references. Become familiar with the scope and organization of NFPA 13, NFPA 13D, and NFPA 13R so you can recognize which publication applies to a particular sprinkler-related subject. The Arizona wastewater reference should receive dedicated review because state-specific provisions may require a different navigation approach from the international codes.

Use your highlighted and tabbed books throughout your course preparation. Rather than saving them for the end of your study period, work with the references from the beginning. The more frequently you locate code sections and technical provisions, the more familiar the books become.

Practice questions are most useful when you use them to build both knowledge and navigation skills. When you answer a question incorrectly, do not simply memorize the correct choice. Locate the supporting material in the reference book and understand why the answer is correct. This process can improve your ability to recognize similar concepts later.

Timed study sessions can also help you develop a more efficient routine. Choose a group of questions, set a reasonable practice period, and work through them while using the reference books. Pay attention to which types of questions take the longest to locate. Those areas may need additional review.
