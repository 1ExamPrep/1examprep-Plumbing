## Open Book Test

The Arizona Plumbing Commercial Contractor (C-37) exam should be prepared for as an **open book test**. Open-book preparation requires both technical knowledge and efficient reference navigation.

Having access to books does not eliminate the need to study. Plumbing codes, NFPA standards, wastewater provisions, mechanical references, and trade manuals contain large amounts of information. Candidates who are not familiar with the books may spend too much time searching for answers during testing.

A stronger approach is to learn the purpose of each reference before focusing on detailed questions. If a question involves drainage, venting, fixtures, or water distribution, the International Plumbing Code may be the best starting point. Fuel-gas topics may lead to the International Fuel Gas Code, while mechanical-system topics may require the International Mechanical Code.

Sprinkler questions require careful attention to the correct standard. NFPA 13, NFPA 13D, and NFPA 13R should not be treated as interchangeable. Candidates should understand the basic scope of each book and practice choosing the correct reference before searching for the answer.

Safety questions may lead to OSHA 29 CFR Part 1926. Underground utility topics can be reviewed through the Arizona 811 - Arizona Blue Stake Brochure. Wastewater questions may point toward the Arizona Administrative Code reference, while boiler and hydronic-heating topics can be studied through their dedicated books.

During practice sessions, identify the subject first. Then choose the reference most likely to contain the answer. Use the table of contents, index, definitions, chapter headings, formulas, technical tables, and section organization to locate the supporting material efficiently.
