## Exam Details

Preparation for the Arizona Plumbing Commercial Contractor (C-37) examination should focus on the technical knowledge involved in commercial plumbing systems and the related construction disciplines represented in the reference set. Candidates should develop familiarity with plumbing codes, fuel-gas systems, mechanical requirements, fire-sprinkler standards, wastewater provisions, plumbing mathematics, pipe-trade information, boiler systems, hydronic heating, construction safety, and underground utility awareness.

The **International Plumbing Code, 2018** should be a major part of preparation. Candidates should practice locating requirements involving water distribution, sanitary drainage, venting, fixtures, traps, indirect waste, storm drainage, and related plumbing-system components.

The **International Fuel Gas Code, 2018** supports study involving gas piping, sizing, materials, appliances, combustion air, venting, testing, and installation concepts. Candidates should become comfortable recognizing when a technical question belongs in the fuel-gas code rather than the plumbing or mechanical code.

The **International Mechanical Code, 2018** provides additional preparation involving mechanical systems, equipment, ventilation, and related building-service requirements. Commercial plumbing work can overlap with mechanical systems, making familiarity with this reference important.

Fire-sprinkler preparation is represented through NFPA 13, NFPA 13D, and NFPA 13R. These references address different sprinkler-system applications. Candidates should understand the purpose of each standard and practice identifying which book applies before searching for a specific requirement.

Trade mathematics should also be included in regular study. Plumbing and pipefitting can involve measurements, pipe dimensions, areas, volumes, slopes, offsets, and other calculations. Mathematics for Plumbers and Pipefitters provides focused preparation for these types of problems.

IPT’s Pipe Trades Handbook adds practical pipe-trade data and terminology, while Boiler Operator’s Guide and Modern Hydronic Heating support preparation involving boilers, hydronic systems, circulation, piping arrangements, equipment, and controls.
