Exam Details

Preparation for the Arizona Plumbing Commercial Contractor (C-37) examination should focus on commercial plumbing and the related systems represented in the reference set. Candidates should develop familiarity with plumbing-code requirements, fuel-gas installations, mechanical systems, sprinkler standards, wastewater provisions, trade mathematics, pipe-trade information, boiler systems, hydronic heating, construction safety, and underground utility awareness.

The International Plumbing Code, 2018 should be a major part of preparation. Candidates should practice locating requirements involving water distribution, sanitary drainage, venting, fixtures, traps, indirect waste, storm drainage, and related plumbing-system components.

The International Fuel Gas Code, 2018 supports study involving fuel-gas piping, materials, sizing, appliances, combustion air, venting, testing, and related installation requirements. Candidates should become comfortable identifying when a question belongs in the fuel-gas code rather than the plumbing or mechanical code.

The International Mechanical Code, 2018 provides additional material involving mechanical systems, equipment, ventilation, and related construction requirements. Commercial plumbing can overlap with mechanical work, making familiarity with this code useful during preparation.

Fire-sprinkler preparation is represented through NFPA 13, NFPA 13D, and NFPA 13R. These standards apply to different sprinkler-system situations, so candidates should learn the purpose of each reference and practice selecting the correct book before looking for detailed information.

Mathematics should also receive regular attention. Plumbing and pipefitting work can involve measurements, pipe dimensions, slopes, offsets, areas, volumes, and other calculations. Mathematics for Plumbers and Pipefitters provides focused material for strengthening these trade skills.

IPT’s Pipe Trades Handbook adds practical information involving pipe materials, fittings, dimensions, formulas, and field-reference data. Boiler Operator’s Guide and Modern Hydronic Heating provide additional preparation involving boiler systems, hydronic circulation, piping arrangements, system components, and controls.

Licensing Steps

Preparing for the C-37 trade exam is one part of pursuing an Arizona contractor license. Candidates should also organize the administrative, business, and qualification responsibilities associated with the licensing process.

  1. Determine the license applicant. Decide whether the contractor license will be pursued by an individual or through an eligible business entity.
  2. Establish the business entity when applicable. Organize the LLC or corporation and keep the legal business information consistent throughout the licensing process.
  3. Select the appropriate classification. Identify the commercial plumbing classification associated with the work the business intends to perform.
  4. Complete the contractor license application. Provide the legal applicant name, business details, contact information, and requested classification.
  5. Identify the qualifying individual. Make sure the person responsible for satisfying the trade qualification is properly associated with the applicant.
  6. Complete applicable examination requirements. The qualifying individual completes the testing associated with the contractor licensing process.
  7. Provide required business documentation. Organize entity documents and other records requested as part of the application.
  8. Complete applicable financial or bonding requirements. Prepare the documentation required through the contractor licensing process.
  9. Resolve remaining application items. Submit missing documentation and address outstanding requirements before license issuance.

Test Information and Study Materials

A productive Arizona Plumbing Commercial Contractor study plan should combine course lessons, direct reference review, code navigation, trade calculations, and practice-oriented questions. Begin by learning the purpose of each reference before focusing on detailed information.

Use the International Plumbing Code as a foundation for plumbing-system study. Practice locating water-supply, drainage, venting, fixture, trap, sanitary-system, and storm-drainage requirements through the code's organization.

Review the International Fuel Gas Code and International Mechanical Code alongside the plumbing code. Practice determining which code applies when questions involve overlapping plumbing, fuel-gas, and mechanical responsibilities.

Study NFPA 13, NFPA 13D, and NFPA 13R together so you understand the different sprinkler applications represented by each standard. Learning which reference applies can make technical searching more efficient.
