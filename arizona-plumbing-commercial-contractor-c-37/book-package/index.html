Exam Details

Preparation for the Arizona Plumbing Commercial Contractor (C-37) examination should focus on commercial plumbing systems and the construction knowledge associated with installing, maintaining, and understanding those systems. Candidates should become familiar with plumbing codes, fuel-gas requirements, mechanical systems, sprinkler standards, wastewater rules, pipe calculations, boilers, hydronic heating, and construction safety.

The International Plumbing Code, 2018 should be a major part of preparation. Candidates should practice locating information involving water distribution, fixtures, drainage, venting, traps, sanitary systems, indirect waste, storm drainage, and other plumbing-system requirements.

The International Fuel Gas Code, 2018 adds fuel-gas subjects such as piping materials, sizing, installation, appliances, combustion air, venting, testing, and related system requirements. Commercial plumbing work can overlap with fuel-gas installation, making this reference important for broader trade preparation.

The International Mechanical Code, 2018 supports study of mechanical systems, equipment, ventilation, piping-related concepts, and the relationship between plumbing and mechanical construction. Candidates should understand how to navigate this code and recognize when a question belongs in the mechanical reference rather than the plumbing or fuel-gas code.

Fire-sprinkler preparation is represented through NFPA 13, NFPA 13D, and NFPA 13R. These standards address different sprinkler-system applications, so candidates should learn the purpose and scope of each reference instead of treating them as interchangeable.

Trade mathematics should also receive dedicated review. Mathematics for Plumbers and Pipefitters, 8th Edition supports calculations involving measurements, areas, volumes, pipe dimensions, slopes, offsets, and other mathematical concepts used in plumbing and pipefitting work.

IPT’s Pipe Trades Handbook adds practical field information involving pipe materials, fittings, dimensions, formulas, terminology, and common pipe-trade data. This type of reference can be especially useful when studying practical construction and calculation concepts.

The package also includes references for boilers, hydronic heating, wastewater treatment, underground utilities, and construction safety, giving candidates a broad base for commercial plumbing preparation.

Open Book Test

The Arizona Plumbing Commercial Contractor (C-37) exam should be prepared for as an open book test. Open-book testing requires candidates to combine technical knowledge with efficient reference navigation.

Having the books available does not replace studying. The International Codes, NFPA standards, plumbing mathematics books, boiler references, hydronic-heating texts, and state regulatory materials contain substantial amounts of technical information. Candidates should become familiar with the books before exam day rather than relying on searching unfamiliar references under time pressure.

For plumbing-system questions, the International Plumbing Code should be one of the first references considered. Fuel-gas questions may point toward the International Fuel Gas Code, while mechanical-system questions may require the International Mechanical Code.

Fire-sprinkler questions require careful identification of the applicable standard. NFPA 13, NFPA 13D, and NFPA 13R address different system applications, so candidates should understand which reference is likely to contain the answer before beginning a search.

Wastewater subjects can be reviewed through the Arizona Administrative Code material included in the package. Safety questions can be studied through OSHA 29 CFR Part 1926, while underground utility awareness can be reinforced through the Arizona 811 - Arizona Blue Stake Brochure.

Mathematical questions can be studied with Mathematics for Plumbers and Pipefitters, while IPT’s Pipe Trades Handbook can support practical pipe data and trade-reference topics. Boiler and hydronic questions can be studied through Boiler Operator’s Guide and Modern Hydronic Heating for Residential and Light Commercial Buildings.

During practice sessions, identify the subject first, then select the reference most likely to contain the information. Use the table of contents, index, chapter headings, definitions, technical tables, and section organization to improve navigation speed.

Licensing Steps

Preparing for the C-37 trade examination is one part of pursuing an Arizona contractor license. Candidates should also keep the administrative, business, qualifying, and documentation requirements associated with contractor licensing organized.

  1. Determine the license applicant. Decide whether the contractor license will be pursued by an individual or through an eligible business entity.
  2. Select the appropriate contractor classification. Identify the commercial plumbing classification associated with the work the business intends to perform.
  3. Complete the contractor license application. Provide the legal applicant name, contact information, business details, and requested classification.
  4. Identify the qualifying individual. Make sure the person responsible for satisfying the trade qualification is properly associated with the applicant.
  5. Complete applicable examinations. The qualifying individual completes the required trade and business-related examinations associated with the licensing process.
  6. Provide required business documentation. Business entities should organize documentation applicable to their legal structure.
  7. Complete applicable bonding or financial requirements. Prepare the documentation required as part of the contractor licensing process.
  8. Submit remaining application materials. Complete outstanding documentation and correct any deficiencies identified during application review.

Candidates should coordinate exam preparation with the licensing process. Keeping study responsibilities and application requirements organized at the same time can help create a smoother path toward completing the contractor licensing process.

Test Information and Study Materials

A productive C-37 study plan should combine direct reference review, code navigation, practice-oriented questions, and plumbing calculations. Start by learning what each reference covers so you know where to look when a technical subject appears.

Use the International Plumbing Code as a foundation for plumbing-system preparation. Practice locating water-supply, drainage, venting, fixture, trap, sanitary, and storm-drainage requirements through chapter organization and index searches.

Review the International Fuel Gas Code and International Mechanical Code next. Pay attention to where these systems intersect with plumbing work and practice deciding which reference is most appropriate for a particular question.

Study NFPA 13, NFPA 13D, and NFPA 13R together. The goal is not simply to recognize the book titles but to understand the application of each sprinkler standard and become familiar with where key system requirements are organized.

Use Mathematics for Plumbers and Pipefitters for regular calculation practice. Work through the formulas and processes rather than relying only on memorization. IPT’s Pipe Trades Handbook can be used alongside the mathematics reference to reinforce practical pipe dimensions, field information, and formulas.

Review Boiler Operator’s Guide and Modern Hydronic Heating for equipment, circulation, piping, control, and heating-system concepts. These references expand preparation beyond basic plumbing codes into related mechanical systems.

Use the Arizona Administrative Code material for wastewater-treatment topics and Arizona 811 for underground utility awareness. OSHA 29 CFR Part 1926 should remain part of the study rotation for construction safety.

When answering practice questions, identify the topic before opening a reference. Decide whether the question involves plumbing, fuel gas, mechanical systems, sprinklers, wastewater, mathematics, pipe trades, boilers, hydronic heating, safety, or underground utilities. Then choose the reference most likely to contain the answer.

If a practice question is answered incorrectly, return to the supporting reference and review the surrounding section. Understanding why the answer applies can help strengthen knowledge for similar questions later.
