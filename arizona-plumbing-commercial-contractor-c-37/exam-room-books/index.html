Exam Details

Preparation for the Arizona Plumbing Commercial Contractor (C-37) exam should focus on the technical subjects represented in the allowed reference set. Candidates should study commercial plumbing systems, fuel-gas installations, mechanical requirements, sprinkler systems, wastewater provisions, and construction safety.

The International Plumbing Code, 2018 should be one of the primary references during study. Candidates should become familiar with water distribution, drainage, venting, fixtures, traps, sanitary systems, indirect waste, storm drainage, and related plumbing-system topics. Rather than reading the code only from beginning to end, candidates should practice finding specific subjects through the index, table of contents, chapter structure, and definitions.

The International Fuel Gas Code, 2018 supports preparation involving fuel-gas piping, system materials, sizing, appliance connections, combustion air, venting, testing, and related installation concepts. Candidates should practice recognizing when a question belongs in the fuel-gas code instead of the plumbing or mechanical code.

The International Mechanical Code, 2018 provides additional technical material involving mechanical systems, ventilation, equipment, and related building-service requirements. Commercial plumbing work can overlap with mechanical systems, so candidates should understand how this reference differs from the plumbing and fuel-gas codes.

Fire-sprinkler preparation is represented through NFPA 13, NFPA 13D, and NFPA 13R. These standards apply to different sprinkler-system situations. Candidates should understand the purpose of each reference and practice determining which standard applies before searching for a specific requirement.

The On-site Wastewater Treatment Facility Provisions in Unified Water Quality Permit Rule, Arizona Administrative Code Title 18, Chapter 9, 2006 provides the Arizona-specific wastewater material included in this package. Candidates should become familiar with its structure, terminology, and subject organization.

Construction safety should also remain part of exam preparation. Code of Federal Regulations - 29 CFR Part 1926 (OSHA) supports study involving excavation, ladders, scaffolds, personal protective equipment, tools, equipment, material handling, and other jobsite safety responsibilities.

Open Book Test

The Arizona Plumbing Commercial Contractor (C-37) exam should be prepared for as an open book test. Open-book preparation requires more than bringing the correct references into the exam room. Candidates should know how each book is organized and which reference is most likely to contain the answer to a particular question.

Having multiple books available can become a disadvantage if a candidate does not know where to look. The goal of study should be to connect subject areas with the correct reference. Plumbing-system questions may point toward the International Plumbing Code. Fuel-gas questions may require the International Fuel Gas Code. Mechanical-system topics may lead to the International Mechanical Code.

Sprinkler questions require careful selection among NFPA 13, NFPA 13D, and NFPA 13R. Candidates should understand the general purpose of each standard so they do not waste time searching through the wrong book.

Wastewater questions can be studied through the Arizona Administrative Code material included in the package, while construction-safety subjects can be reviewed through OSHA 29 CFR Part 1926.

During study sessions, candidates should practice a repeatable process. First, identify the subject of the question. Second, select the reference most likely to contain the answer. Third, use the index, table of contents, chapter headings, definitions, and technical tables to locate the applicable material.

This kind of repeated navigation practice can improve familiarity with the books and help candidates approach open-book testing with a more organized strategy.
