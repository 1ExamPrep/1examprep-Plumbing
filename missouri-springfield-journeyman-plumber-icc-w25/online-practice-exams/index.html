Exam Details

The Missouri (Springfield) Journeyman Plumber W25-MO-S examination is part of the International Code Council (ICC) Contractor/Trades examination program. Testing is delivered through Pearson VUE.

The examination evaluates technical knowledge involving plumbing installation requirements, fixtures, water distribution, drainage, venting, traps, and storm drainage.

  • Exam Name: Missouri (Springfield) Journeyman Plumber
  • Exam Code: W25-MO-S
  • Examination Organization: International Code Council (ICC)
  • Testing Provider: Pearson VUE
  • Examination Format: Multiple-choice questions
  • Number of Questions: 70
  • Time Limit: 180 minutes (3 hours)
  • Exam Type: Open book
  • Approved Reference: International Plumbing Code, 2018 Edition
  • Online Practice Access: 3 months

The official ICC examination outline identifies seven principal subject categories:

  • Administration and General Regulations – 40%: General installation requirements, plumbing terminology, testing, and materials.
  • Plumbing Fixtures – 17%: Water heaters, water closets, urinals, specialty fixtures, and related installation requirements.
  • Water Supply and Distribution – 14%: Water service piping, distribution systems, pressure requirements, and potable water protection.
  • Sanitary Drainage – 13%: Building drainage, sumps, ejectors, and indirect or special waste systems.
  • Plumbing Vent Requirements – 7%: Vent connections, grading, and vent terminals.
  • Traps, Interceptors, and Separators – 7%: Trap installation and equipment used to separate materials from wastewater.
  • Storm Drainage – 2%: Stormwater drainage systems and related plumbing requirements.

Administration and General Regulations is the largest category, accounting for 40% of the examination. Plumbing Fixtures follows at 17%, while Water Supply and Distribution represents 14%.

Together, these three categories represent 71% of the examination. Understanding their importance helps candidates organize study time while maintaining familiarity with all seven subjects.

The three-hour examination provides approximately 2.6 minutes per question. Efficient code navigation and strong technical understanding are valuable examination preparation skills.
