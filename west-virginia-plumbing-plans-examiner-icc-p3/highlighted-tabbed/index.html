Exam Details

The ICC P3 Plumbing Plans Examiner exam measures a candidate’s ability to review plumbing plans and construction documents for compliance with applicable codes. The exam is intended for individuals who evaluate submitted plans before permits are issued or before plumbing work is approved for construction. Unlike a field inspection exam, a plans examiner exam places strong emphasis on document review, code interpretation, system layout, sizing, fixture requirements, and coordination between drawings, specifications, and code provisions.

The P3 exam is connected to the job tasks of a plumbing plans examiner. Candidates should be prepared to review plumbing system designs and determine whether the submitted documents show compliance with code requirements. This may include identifying missing information, reviewing fixture calculations, checking pipe sizing, evaluating drainage and vent arrangements, reviewing water distribution provisions, applying fuel gas requirements, and recognizing accessibility requirements for plumbing fixtures and facilities.

Important study areas include code administration, plan review procedures, definitions, plumbing fixtures, fixture fittings, minimum fixture counts, accessible plumbing facilities, water supply and distribution, sanitary drainage, indirect waste, special waste, vents, traps, interceptors, separators, storm drainage, materials, joints, connections, fuel gas piping, combustion air, appliances, and related installation requirements.

Candidates should also understand how plans examiners use the code differently from field inspectors. A plans examiner may not be looking at an installed system. Instead, the plans examiner evaluates drawings, specifications, schedules, riser diagrams, fixture counts, equipment information, and supporting documents to determine whether the proposed work meets code requirements. This requires the ability to connect plan information with code language.

The ICC P3 exam is code-based and requires strong reference navigation. A question may ask about the required number of fixtures, the proper application of a sizing table, the code section governing a venting arrangement, a fuel gas provision, or an accessibility requirement for a plumbing fixture. Candidates should be prepared to move between the International Plumbing Code, International Fuel Gas Code, and ICC A117.1 standard during study.
