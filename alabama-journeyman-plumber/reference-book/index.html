Exam Details

The Alabama Journeyman Plumber examination is administered by Prov for the Alabama Plumbers and Gas Fitters Examining Board. The exam contains 70 questions, and candidates are allowed approximately 2.92 hours, or about 175 minutes, to complete the test.

The examination content outline is divided among the following areas:

  • General Knowledge: 10 questions
  • Drawings and Plan Reading: 5 questions
  • Trade Math: 6 questions
  • Tools: 5 questions
  • Sizing and Design: 9 questions
  • Fixtures and Fittings: 6 questions
  • Drain Waste and Vents: 6 questions
  • Roof Drain Systems: 5 questions
  • System Installation: 7 questions
  • Water Heaters: 6 questions
  • Safety: 5 questions

General Knowledge is the largest individual category with 10 questions. Sizing and Design follows with 9 questions, while System Installation contains 7 questions. Trade Math, Fixtures and Fittings, Drain Waste and Vents, and Water Heaters each make up important portions of the examination.

The content distribution means candidates should prepare across the complete plumbing trade rather than relying on experience in only one specialty. A single exam session can move from a sizing calculation to a fixture requirement, roof drain problem, water-heater question, plan-reading item, or practical installation scenario.

Open Book Test

The Alabama Journeyman Plumber examination is an open book test. Candidates may use authorized references in accordance with Prov's testing rules.

Open-book testing does not eliminate the need to study. With 70 questions and approximately 175 minutes available, candidates have an average of about two and a half minutes per question. That time must cover reading and interpreting the problem, completing any necessary calculations, locating information in a reference when needed, and selecting an answer.

Reference-navigation practice should therefore be a regular part of preparation. Candidates should know how the International Plumbing Code is organized and become comfortable using chapter headings, tables, definitions, indexes, and code sections. Mathematics for Plumbers and Pipefitters can be used to strengthen calculation skills, while Modern Plumbing can support practical understanding of system components and installation techniques.

The Alabama law and administrative-rule references should also be reviewed so candidates understand the state framework governing plumbers and gas fitters. Even when a particular exam question is primarily technical, familiarity with the broader certification requirements is useful during the licensing process.

A strong practice routine involves switching among the books. Candidates can locate a plumbing code requirement, work a mathematics problem, review a practical installation concept, and then return to the code for a drainage or water-heater question. This develops the ability to recognize which reference is most useful for a specific problem.

Licensing Steps

The Alabama Plumbers and Gas Fitters Examining Board regulates Journeyman Plumber certification in Alabama. Candidates must satisfy the Board's experience, examination, and certification requirements.

  1. Complete qualifying plumbing experience. The standard Journeyman Plumber pathway requires a minimum of two years as an apprentice or successful completion of a Board-approved apprentice training program combined with registered apprentice experience equal to two years.
  2. Maintain appropriate apprentice registration. Apprentice plumbers work under the required supervision while gaining practical experience in plumbing installation, repair, and replacement.
  3. Complete the Journeyman examination application. Candidates must submit the required examination application and supporting materials to the Alabama Plumbers and Gas Fitters Examining Board.
  4. Receive eligibility to test. After the required application process is completed, eligible candidates can schedule the examination through Prov.
  5. Take the Journeyman Plumber examination. Candidates complete the computerized open-book examination administered by Prov.
  6. Pass the prescribed examination. Successful examination completion satisfies the testing requirement for Journeyman Plumber certification.
  7. Complete initial certification. Candidates who pass the Journeyman examination have 90 calendar days from the date they pass to complete the required certification process.
  8. Maintain a current annual certificate. A certified Journeyman Plumber must maintain the valid annual certificate required by the Board.

State Requirements

The Alabama Plumbers and Gas Fitters Examining Board regulates plumbing and gas fitting in Alabama. A Journeyman Plumber is a person who works at the actual installation, repair, or replacement of plumbing systems, has fulfilled the examination requirements established by the Board, has been issued a Journeyman Plumber certificate, and possesses a valid current annual certificate.

The standard journeyman pathway requires at least two years as an apprentice or a Board-approved combination of apprentice training and registered apprenticeship equal to two years. Applicants may also request consideration of verifiable experience obtained through other means according to the Board's procedures.

The Board requires candidates who pass the Journeyman Plumber examination to complete certification within 90 calendar days of the passing date. Candidates who do not complete certification within that period must re-test.

Alabama also distinguishes between plumbing and gas-fitting credentials. A Journeyman Plumber who intends to perform gas-fitting work must hold the appropriate gas-fitting credential required by the Board.

Journeyman certification is separate from Master Plumber certification. Individuals who later want to advance to the master level must satisfy the additional eligibility and examination requirements established for that classification.

Test Information and Study Materials

Preparation for the Alabama Journeyman Plumber exam should follow the official subject distribution and include both technical study and active reference practice.

General Knowledge contains 10 questions. Candidates should review plumbing terminology, system principles, materials, installation practices, and common trade concepts. Modern Plumbing can help reinforce this broad knowledge while the International Plumbing Code provides specific technical requirements.

Sizing and Design contains 9 questions. Candidates should practice using plumbing code tables and solving sizing problems. Knowing which table applies can be just as important as completing the calculation correctly.

System Installation contributes 7 questions. Preparation should include practical plumbing methods and the technical requirements governing how system components are installed and connected.

Trade Math contains 6 questions. Candidates should practice calculations involving dimensions, measurements, geometry, piping, conversions, and other mathematics used in plumbing work. Mathematics for Plumbers and Pipefitters provides a focused resource for this part of preparation.

Fixtures and Fittings, Drain Waste and Vents, and Water Heaters each account for 6 questions. Candidates should be comfortable navigating the relevant International Plumbing Code chapters and understanding the practical principles behind these systems.

Drawings and Plan Reading contains 5 questions. Candidates should practice interpreting plumbing drawings, isometric sketches, layouts, dimensions, system routes, and other information presented graphically.

Tools, Roof Drain Systems, and Safety each contribute 5 questions. These categories reinforce the importance of broad preparation. Candidates should recognize common tools, understand roof-drainage concepts and sizing, and follow safe plumbing work practices.

The Alabama Plumbers and Gas Fitters Examining Board also provides preparation materials for Journeyman Plumber candidates that include threaded-fitting allowances, reading isometric sketches, and plumbing practice questions. These study areas demonstrate the importance of combining mathematical ability, plan-reading skills, practical plumbing knowledge, and code research.

Timed practice is especially useful. Candidates can work through questions while limiting how long they spend searching the books. This helps develop judgment about when an answer can be found quickly and when it may be better to move forward and return later.
