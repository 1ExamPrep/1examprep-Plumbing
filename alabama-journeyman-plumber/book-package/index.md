## Licensing Steps

The Alabama Plumbers and Gas Fitters Examining Board regulates plumber certification in Alabama. Individuals pursuing Journeyman Plumber certification must complete the experience, examination, and certification requirements established by the Board.

1. **Gain qualifying plumbing experience.** Individuals seeking Journeyman Plumber certification must complete a minimum of two years as an apprentice or complete a Board-approved apprentice training program combined with registered apprentice experience equal to two years.
2. **Maintain apprentice registration while gaining experience.** Apprentice plumbers work under the appropriate supervision while gaining practical field experience in plumbing installation, repair, and replacement.
3. **Complete the Journeyman examination application.** Candidates must submit the application and required documentation to the Alabama Plumbers and Gas Fitters Examining Board.
4. **Receive eligibility for testing.** After the Board processes and approves the examination application, eligible candidates can schedule the Journeyman Plumber examination through Prov.
5. **Take the Alabama Journeyman Plumber examination.** Candidates complete the computerized, open-book examination administered through Prov.
6. **Complete certification after passing.** Candidates who pass the prescribed examination have 90 calendar days from the passing date to complete the certification process with the Board.
7. **Maintain a current annual certificate.** A Journeyman Plumber must possess a valid and current annual certificate while performing work within the scope of the certification.

The Board also provides a process for applicants who have qualifying experience obtained through other means. Such applicants may submit verifiable experience documentation for Board consideration according to the Board's procedures.

## State Requirements

Alabama law regulates plumbing through the Alabama Plumbers and Gas Fitters Examining Board. A Journeyman Plumber is a person who engages in or works at the actual installation, repair, or replacement of plumbing systems, has successfully fulfilled the Board's examination requirements, has been issued a Journeyman Plumber certificate, and possesses a valid and current annual certificate.

The standard Alabama journeyman pathway requires a minimum of two years as an apprentice or successful completion of a Board-approved apprentice training program combined with registered apprentice experience equal to two years. The Board may also consider verifiable experience through its established review process.

Once an individual passes the Journeyman Plumber examination, the Board requires certification to be completed within **90 calendar days** of the passing date. A candidate who does not obtain certification within that period must re-test.

Alabama also distinguishes plumbing work from gas-fitting work. A person who passes a Journeyman or Master Plumber examination and wants to continue performing gas fitting must hold at least the appropriate apprentice-level gas-fitting certification unless the individual also possesses the applicable gas fitter credential.

Journeyman Plumber certification is separate from Master Plumber certification. The journeyman classification allows an individual to perform plumbing installation, repair, and replacement within the scope of the certification. Progression to Master Plumber requires additional qualification and examination requirements established by the Board.
