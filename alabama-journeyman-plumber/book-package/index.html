Exam Details

The Alabama Journeyman Plumber examination is administered by Prov for the Alabama Plumbers and Gas Fitters Examining Board. The examination is computerized and contains 70 questions. Candidates are allowed 2.92 hours, which is approximately 175 minutes, to complete the test.

The examination outline divides the 70 questions among the following subject areas:

  • General Knowledge: 10 questions
  • Drawings and Plan Reading: 5 questions
  • Trade Math: 6 questions
  • Tools: 5 questions
  • Sizing and Design: 9 questions
  • Fixtures and Fittings: 6 questions
  • Drain Waste and Vents: 6 questions
  • Roof Drain Systems: 5 questions
  • System Installation: 7 questions
  • Water Heaters: 6 questions
  • Safety: 5 questions

General Knowledge is the largest individual category with 10 questions, followed by Sizing and Design with 9 questions and System Installation with 7 questions. Trade Math, Fixtures and Fittings, Drain Waste and Vents, and Water Heaters each make up meaningful portions of the exam as well. Drawings and Plan Reading, Tools, Roof Drain Systems, and Safety complete the content outline.

This distribution means candidates should prepare across the complete plumbing trade instead of concentrating on only one code chapter or system. A test session can move from a mathematical calculation to a drainage requirement, fixture question, roof drain problem, water-heater requirement, plan-reading item, or installation scenario.

Open Book Test

The Alabama Journeyman Plumber examination is an open book test. Prov's Alabama candidate information identifies the examination program as open book, and candidates are permitted to use authorized reference materials during testing according to Prov's examination rules.

The open-book format makes reference navigation an important part of preparation. With 70 questions and approximately 175 minutes, candidates have an average of about two and a half minutes per question. That time must cover reading the question, understanding the problem, performing any calculations, locating information when needed, and selecting an answer.

Candidates are encouraged to become familiar with their reference materials before testing. Prov allows authorized materials to be prepared with highlighting and permanent tabs according to its reference rules. The purpose of book preparation is to make important information easier to locate, but candidates should still practice with the references repeatedly before exam day.

The International Plumbing Code is especially important for subjects such as fixtures, sanitary drainage, vents, water supply and distribution, water heaters, and system installation. Mathematics for Plumbers and Pipefitters can support trade calculations, while Modern Plumbing reinforces practical system knowledge. The International Fuel Gas Code can support topics that involve fuel-gas connections and appliances within plumbing work.

A useful study method is to practice switching between books. Locate a plumbing code requirement, then work a trade-math problem, review a practical installation concept, and return to a code table. This type of practice develops the ability to identify the correct source without losing excessive time searching.

Licensing Steps

The Alabama Plumbers and Gas Fitters Examining Board regulates plumber certification in Alabama. Individuals pursuing Journeyman Plumber certification must complete the experience, examination, and certification requirements established by the Board.

  1. Gain qualifying plumbing experience. Individuals seeking Journeyman Plumber certification must complete a minimum of two years as an apprentice or complete a Board-approved apprentice training program combined with registered apprentice experience equal to two years.
  2. Maintain apprentice registration while gaining experience. Apprentice plumbers work under the appropriate supervision while gaining practical field experience in plumbing installation, repair, and replacement.
  3. Complete the Journeyman examination application. Candidates must submit the application and required documentation to the Alabama Plumbers and Gas Fitters Examining Board.
  4. Receive eligibility for testing. After the Board processes and approves the examination application, eligible candidates can schedule the Journeyman Plumber examination through Prov.
  5. Take the Alabama Journeyman Plumber examination. Candidates complete the computerized, open-book examination administered through Prov.
  6. Complete certification after passing. Candidates who pass the prescribed examination have 90 calendar days from the passing date to complete the certification process with the Board.
  7. Maintain a current annual certificate. A Journeyman Plumber must possess a valid and current annual certificate while performing work within the scope of the certification.

The Board also provides a process for applicants who have qualifying experience obtained through other means. Such applicants may submit verifiable experience documentation for Board consideration according to the Board's procedures.

State Requirements

Alabama law regulates plumbing through the Alabama Plumbers and Gas Fitters Examining Board. A Journeyman Plumber is a person who engages in or works at the actual installation, repair, or replacement of plumbing systems, has successfully fulfilled the Board's examination requirements, has been issued a Journeyman Plumber certificate, and possesses a valid and current annual certificate.

The standard Alabama journeyman pathway requires a minimum of two years as an apprentice or successful completion of a Board-approved apprentice training program combined with registered apprentice experience equal to two years. The Board may also consider verifiable experience through its established review process.

Once an individual passes the Journeyman Plumber examination, the Board requires certification to be completed within 90 calendar days of the passing date. A candidate who does not obtain certification within that period must re-test.

Alabama also distinguishes plumbing work from gas-fitting work. A person who passes a Journeyman or Master Plumber examination and wants to continue performing gas fitting must hold at least the appropriate apprentice-level gas-fitting certification unless the individual also possesses the applicable gas fitter credential.

Journeyman Plumber certification is separate from Master Plumber certification. The journeyman classification allows an individual to perform plumbing installation, repair, and replacement within the scope of the certification. Progression to Master Plumber requires additional qualification and examination requirements established by the Board.

Reference Books

  • 2022 Code of Alabama, Title 34, Chapter 37, Plumbers and Gas Fitters
    The Alabama-specific legal reference addresses the statutory framework governing plumbers and gas fitters. It covers Board authority, certification, registration, examinations, classifications, enforcement, and other state requirements affecting the trade. It is useful for understanding the legal structure under which Alabama plumbers operate.
  • Administrative Code Chapter 720, 2018
    This Alabama regulatory reference contains administrative rules associated with the Plumbers and Gas Fitters Examining Board. It complements the statutory provisions found in Title 34, Chapter 37 and provides additional information concerning certification and Board procedures.
  • International Fuel Gas Code, 2021
    The 2021 International Fuel Gas Code addresses fuel-gas piping, appliances, combustion air, venting, materials, installation, and related requirements. It provides supporting code knowledge for plumbers who encounter fuel-gas systems and appliances as part of plumbing work.
  • International Plumbing Code, 2021
    The 2021 International Plumbing Code is a primary technical reference for Alabama plumbing examinations. It addresses fixtures, water supply and distribution, sanitary drainage, vents, traps, interceptors, water heaters, storm drainage, materials, system design, installation requirements, definitions, and other plumbing provisions. Candidates should become comfortable navigating its chapters, tables, and index.
  • Mathematics for Plumbers and Pipefitters, 8th Edition
    This trade mathematics reference is designed around calculations used in plumbing and pipefitting. It supports preparation for measurement, geometry, pipe calculations, conversions, offsets, and other mathematical skills relevant to the Trade Math and Sizing and Design portions of the examination.
  • Modern Plumbing, 8th Edition
    This practical plumbing reference covers plumbing systems, materials, tools, fixtures, piping, installation techniques, drainage, water systems, and related trade knowledge. It complements the code books by providing broader explanations of field practices and plumbing principles.

Test Information and Study Materials

A productive Alabama Journeyman Plumber study plan should follow the official examination categories and combine technical review with active reference navigation.

General Knowledge represents 10 questions and requires broad familiarity with plumbing terminology, materials, systems, installation principles, and trade practices. Modern Plumbing can help reinforce these concepts while the International Plumbing Code provides specific regulatory requirements.

Sizing and Design accounts for 9 questions. Candidates should practice working with code tables and sizing methods used for water distribution, drainage systems, vents, and other plumbing components. Understanding how to identify the correct table is as important as performing the calculation correctly.

System Installation contributes 7 questions. Candidates should review practical installation requirements and understand how different plumbing components work together within a complete system.

Trade Math accounts for 6 questions. Mathematics for Plumbers and Pipefitters can support preparation for trade calculations involving measurements, dimensions, piping, geometry, conversions, and other mathematical operations used in plumbing work.

Fixtures and Fittings, Drain Waste and Vents, and Water Heaters each account for 6 questions. Candidates should become comfortable locating relevant chapters and tables in the International Plumbing Code while also understanding the practical concepts described in Modern Plumbing.

Drawings and Plan Reading represents 5 questions. The Alabama Plumbers and Gas Fitters Examining Board provides preparation material involving reading isometric sketches, reinforcing the importance of understanding plumbing drawings and system layouts.

Tools also accounts for 5 questions. Candidates should recognize common plumbing tools and understand their applications in installation, repair, cutting, joining, and measurement work.

Roof Drain Systems contribute 5 questions. Candidates should review storm drainage provisions, roof drainage concepts, sizing, and system components. This area often requires candidates to combine code navigation with basic calculations.

Safety accounts for 5 questions. Practical plumbing work involves tools, equipment, materials, confined work areas, ladders, cutting operations, and other jobsite conditions that require safe work practices.
