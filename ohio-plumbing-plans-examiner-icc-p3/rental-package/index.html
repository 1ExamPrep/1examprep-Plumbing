Exam Details

The ICC Plumbing Plans Examiner P3 exam is an ICC certification exam focused on plumbing plan review and code compliance. The exam includes 60 multiple-choice questions and has a 2.5-hour time limit. Students preparing for the 2021 code version should study the 2021 International Plumbing Code and the 2021 International Fuel Gas Code, along with the accessibility standard listed in this package.

The P3 exam measures a student’s ability to review plumbing construction documents and determine whether the proposed work complies with applicable code requirements. Questions may require direct code lookup, interpretation of code language, use of tables, review of fixture counts, evaluation of water supply sizing, drainage review, venting review, storm drainage review, special system review, and understanding of plan documentation requirements.

Major study areas include general requirements, fixtures, water heaters, water supply and distribution, sanitary drainage, vents, traps, interceptors, separators, special piping, storage systems, storm drainage, and health care plumbing. Students should be prepared to connect plan-review questions to the proper chapter, section, table, exception, or definition.

The 2021 International Plumbing Code is the primary reference for plumbing plan review. It includes requirements for plumbing fixtures, fixture fittings, water supply systems, sanitary drainage, indirect waste, vents, traps, interceptors, separators, storm drainage, special piping systems, and health care plumbing systems. Plans examiner questions often test whether the student can use these provisions to evaluate design information before installation occurs.

The 2021 International Fuel Gas Code supports preparation for fuel gas-related plan review. Students should understand how fuel gas provisions may affect piping layout, equipment connections, appliance requirements, combustion air, venting, and related code coordination. The ICC A117.1-2017 standard supports review of accessible and usable building features, including plumbing-related accessibility requirements that may affect fixtures, clearances, approach, reach ranges, and usability.

Open Book Test

The ICC P3 Plumbing Plans Examiner exam is an open book test. Open book testing allows students to use approved references, but it still requires serious preparation. Students must know how the books are organized, how to locate requirements quickly, and how to apply the correct section to a plan-review scenario.

Open book does not mean the exam is simple. With 60 questions and a 2.5-hour time limit, students need to move efficiently. A strong preparation routine should include repeated practice with the 2021 International Plumbing Code, 2021 International Fuel Gas Code, and ICC A117.1-2017. Students should become comfortable with tables, definitions, indexes, chapter organization, and common plan-review topics before exam day.

For plumbing plans examiner preparation, students should practice identifying what the question is asking before searching the book. Keywords such as fixture unit, drainage, vent, storm drainage, water supply, developed length, trap, interceptor, health care plumbing, accessible fixture, gas piping, or construction documents can point toward the correct reference and section. The more familiar students become with these terms, the more efficient their open-book exam process becomes.

Test Information and Study Materials

The study materials in this package are designed to help students prepare with structure. Plumbing plans examination covers a wide range of code topics, and students need to understand how to review proposed plumbing systems before installation. This requires careful reading, code navigation, plan-review judgment, and the ability to apply requirements to drawings and design information.

Students should begin with general plan review procedures and construction document requirements. A plumbing plans examiner must understand whether submitted documents contain enough information to evaluate compliance. This can include fixture schedules, pipe sizing, drainage layouts, venting diagrams, water supply calculations, storm drainage design, gas piping information, and accessibility-related fixture details.

Fixture review is an important part of preparation. Students should study fixture requirements, minimum fixture counts, fixture fittings, accessibility coordination, public and employee facilities, drinking fountains, service sinks, toilet facilities, and related code provisions. Some questions may require students to connect plumbing code requirements with accessibility provisions from ICC A117.1-2017.

Water supply and distribution should receive focused study time. Students should review pipe sizing concepts, fixture units, pressure requirements, backflow prevention, water service, distribution piping, valves, protection of potable water, hot water supply, and related system design issues. Plans examiner questions may require the student to identify whether a proposed design provides the required capacity, protection, or layout.

Sanitary drainage and venting are major study areas for the P3 exam. Students should review drainage fixture units, building drains, branch drains, stacks, horizontal drainage piping, cleanouts, indirect waste, vent sizing, vent connections, vent terminals, wet venting, circuit venting, and other venting provisions. These topics often require table use and careful reading of design conditions.

Traps, interceptors, separators, special piping, and storage systems should also be included in the study schedule. Students should understand where these provisions are located and how they apply to commercial plumbing designs. Storm drainage is another important area, including roof drainage, secondary drainage, conductor sizing, storm drains, and related plan review considerations.

Health care plumbing should not be ignored. Plumbing plans for health care facilities can involve specialized requirements, system separation, fixture and equipment considerations, and provisions that differ from ordinary commercial layouts. Students should become familiar with where these requirements appear in the 2021 International Plumbing Code.

The best study strategy is to combine course review with repeated code navigation. Students should practice locating sections quickly, reading tables accurately, and identifying when an exception applies. Timed practice is especially useful because the exam requires steady pacing. The goal is to become comfortable using the books as working tools rather than unfamiliar references.
