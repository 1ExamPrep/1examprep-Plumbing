Exam Details

The ICC Plumbing Plans Examiner (P3) exam is a certification exam for candidates who review plumbing construction documents for code compliance. The exam measures the ability to apply the International Plumbing Code, International Fuel Gas Code, and accessibility-related requirements to submitted plumbing plans and design conditions.

The exam commonly includes 60 multiple-choice questions with a 2.5-hour time limit. The exam is open book and requires candidates to locate code provisions quickly and accurately. Candidates preparing for the 2021 exam version should study from the International Plumbing Code, 2021, International Fuel Gas Code, 2021, and ICC A117.1-2017 Standard for Accessible and Usable Buildings and Facilities.

Major study areas include general administration, plumbing fixtures, fixture count requirements, accessible plumbing facilities, water supply and distribution, sanitary drainage, venting, traps, interceptors, indirect waste, storm drainage, special piping and storage systems, fuel gas piping, gas appliances, combustion air, appliance venting, and plan review documentation.

Administrative questions may involve construction documents, permit-related information, inspections, approved materials, alternative materials and methods, unsafe conditions, code enforcement responsibilities, and plan review procedures. Plumbing plans examiners should understand how submitted documents support approval and how incomplete or unclear information may affect plan review.

Plumbing fixture questions may involve minimum fixture counts, fixture types, separate facilities, public toilet rooms, drinking fountains, service sinks, employee facilities, required distribution, and accessibility-related plumbing features. Candidates should be prepared to use tables carefully and evaluate fixture requirements based on occupancy, occupant load, use, and space conditions described in the question.

Accessibility-related questions may involve water closet clearances, lavatory clearances, urinal requirements, drinking fountain accessibility, toilet compartment dimensions, grab bar locations, reach ranges, knee and toe clearance, accessible routes, turning space, and usable plumbing facilities. ICC A117.1-2017 is important for these topics because plans examiners may need to evaluate whether plumbing elements and rooms are designed for accessibility.

Water supply and distribution questions may involve pipe sizing, water pressure, fixture supply, hot water distribution, protection of potable water, backflow prevention, water heaters, temperature control, shutoff valves, and supply system design. Candidates should study how the plumbing code organizes water distribution requirements and how plan details show compliance.

Sanitary drainage and venting questions may involve drainage fixture units, pipe sizing, slope, cleanouts, traps, trap arms, vent connections, vent sizing, wet venting, circuit venting, stack venting, vent terminals, and drainage system layout. These topics are central to plumbing plan review because the plans examiner must determine whether the proposed system can safely convey waste and protect trap seals.

Storm drainage questions may involve roof drainage, secondary drainage, scuppers, leaders, storm drainage sizing, rainfall rates where applicable, roof drain locations, and overflow provisions. Candidates should practice using applicable tables and understanding how storm drainage requirements are shown on plans.

Fuel gas questions may involve gas pipe sizing, pipe materials, appliance connectors, shutoff valves, sediment traps, pressure testing, appliance installation, combustion air, chimneys, vents, and fuel gas safety provisions. The International Fuel Gas Code, 2021 is an important part of P3 preparation because plumbing plan review can include fuel gas system evaluation.

For the 2021 exam version, candidates should prepare with the International Plumbing Code, 2021, International Fuel Gas Code, 2021, and ICC A117.1-2017 Standard for Accessible and Usable Buildings and Facilities.
