Test Information and Study Materials

The most effective way to prepare for ICC 3P is to practice like a plans examiner: identify scope, locate controlling language, confirm conditions, and make a clear decision. This package supports that approach by combining your references with a navigation-friendly format (highlighting and tabs) that helps you build speed through repetition.

1) Train “right book first” habits. Most wasted time comes from opening the wrong book first. Build a simple decision rule you repeat every practice question:

  • IPC first when the scenario is about plumbing systems, fixtures, piping, materials, or plumbing installation requirements in plan review context.
  • IFGC first when the scenario is about fuel gas installation, safety logic, or fuel-gas-specific conditions in plan review context.
  • A117.1 first when the scenario is clearly about accessibility/usability and the decision hinges on accessible design standards.

2) Practice the “scope → requirement → condition” loop. Many questions are testing applicability. Train this loop until it becomes automatic:

  • Scope: which reference controls the scenario?
  • Requirement: what is the controlling section or rule that answers the question?
  • Condition: what detail in the scenario changes how the requirement applies?

3) Use time-boxed navigation drills. Set a short timer during study and practice locating the controlling section quickly. If you miss, reset and repeat. Your goal is calm, reliable pace—not frantic speed.

4) Learn to spot scenario “trigger details.” Fuel gas questions often include a few words that tell you IFGC should be opened first. Accessibility questions often include usability/clearance-style cues that point you toward A117.1. Underline those trigger details during practice and make your first move based on them.

5) Use active recall to build retention. After each study session:

  • Write a short summary from memory of what you practiced.
  • Do a “find it again” drill for three sections you used today.
  • Note the “trigger words” that helped you choose the correct book first.

How does the Plumbing Plans Examiner (ICC P3) exam work?

The Plumbing Plans Examiner (ICC P3) license comes from the Florida Construction Industry Licensing Board (CILB). Pearson VUE gives the exam. The board approves your application first; then you schedule the exam and bring only the references the candidate bulletin allows.

  • The Florida Construction Industry Licensing Board certifies plumbing contractors statewide; Pearson VUE gives the state trade exam.
  • Florida journeyman and master plumber cards are issued by counties, which use Prov or GITS exams.
  • Florida also certifies plumbing inspectors and plans examiners using ICC exams (1P, 2P and 3P).
