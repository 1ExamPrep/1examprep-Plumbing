Exam Details

The ICC Plumbing Plans Examiner exam evaluates your ability to apply plumbing and fuel gas code requirements to plan review scenarios using approved references. Questions are designed to confirm that you can interpret installation conditions, verify compliance requirements, and navigate efficiently through the IPC and IFGC to find the correct answer.

  • Exam focus: Plumbing plan review and code application
  • Primary references: International Plumbing Code (IPC) 2021 and International Fuel Gas Code (IFGC) 2021
  • Accessibility reference: ICC A117.1-2017 (as included)
  • Question type: Multiple-choice

Plumbing plan review questions often require you to confirm details tied to fixtures, supply and distribution, drainage, venting, materials, and sizing-related requirements. Because the exam is open book, navigation speed and accuracy—especially with tables, notes, and exceptions—plays a major role in performance.

Open Book Test

The ICC Plumbing Plans Examiner exam is an open book exam. Open book rewards candidates who develop a fast, repeatable approach to finding answers in the IPC and IFGC. The advantage isn’t simply having the books—it’s being able to use them efficiently under time pressure.

High-impact open-book prep habits include:

  • Fast topic recognition: Learn where major system topics live so you can jump to the correct chapter quickly.
  • Table and note discipline: Many plumbing answers depend on table values, footnotes, and notes that are easy to miss.
  • Exception awareness: Exceptions can change compliance requirements completely—practice checking them every time.
  • Timed practice sets: Build pacing so you can answer steadily and still have time to review flagged questions.

When these habits become routine, open-book testing becomes far less stressful because you’re relying on a consistent process rather than memory alone.

Test Information and Study Materials

Plumbing plans examiner prep is most effective when you study the way plans examiners work: verify compliance by locating the correct IPC/IFGC requirement and confirming every detail that affects the outcome. Rather than reading straight through, practice scenario-based lookups that build speed and accuracy.

Many candidates build confidence by organizing study into plan-review buckets and drilling them with timed lookups:

  • Sanitary drainage plan review: Practice your lookup routine for drainage layouts, system components, cleanouts, indirect wastes concepts, and common requirements that appear in exam-style plan review scenarios.
  • Venting plan review: Build a repeatable workflow for locating vent requirements, confirming sizing/methods, and checking exceptions and table notes.
  • Water supply and distribution: Strengthen navigation for materials, joints and connections, protection of potable water, and system installation provisions.
  • Fixtures and accessibility awareness: Use ICC A117.1-2017 and Florida accessibility references to reinforce accessibility-related requirements that intersect with fixture placement and plumbing design considerations.
  • Fuel gas crossover topics: Use IFGC 2021 to strengthen confidence with fuel gas piping, appliance connections, combustion air, and venting provisions that can appear in plan review scenarios.
  • Definitions and trigger terms: Train yourself to check defined terms early so you don’t lose time or misinterpret what the scenario is actually testing.

Timed practice sets are one of the fastest ways to improve open-book performance because they build pacing and reduce exam-day stress. The goal is a consistent process: identify, locate, confirm, answer.
