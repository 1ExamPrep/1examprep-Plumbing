About this Journeyman Plumber package

Prepare for the Texas Journeyman Plumber examination with a focused collection of plumbing and fuel-gas references selected for Texas exam study. This package includes the 2018 International Plumbing Code, 2018 International Fuel Gas Code, and 2018 Uniform Plumbing Code, giving candidates strong resources for reviewing commercial and residential plumbing systems, drainage, venting, water distribution, fixtures, water heaters, fuel-gas piping, materials, and installation requirements.

The Texas Journeyman Plumber license is administered by the Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners. A licensed Journeyman Plumber may install, change, repair, service, or renovate plumbing while working under the supervision of a Responsible Master Plumber. A Journeyman Plumber may also directly supervise a Plumber's Apprentice on plumbing job sites and may supervise a Tradesman Plumber-Limited on structures other than one- and two-family dwellings.

The Journeyman level represents a broader plumbing scope than the Tradesman Plumber-Limited credential. Candidates are expected to understand both residential and commercial plumbing systems and demonstrate the practical knowledge developed through substantial supervised experience in the plumbing trade.

The examination process includes a computer-based written examination and, for applicants advancing from the Plumber's Apprentice level, a practical hands-on examination. Candidates moving from an existing Texas Tradesman Plumber-Limited license to Journeyman are not required to repeat the practical portion after passing the Journeyman written examination.

Exam preparation should combine code knowledge with practical plumbing understanding. Candidates should study water supply and distribution, sanitary drainage, vent systems, traps, fixtures, water heaters, fuel-gas systems, piping materials, installation methods, plumbing calculations, code terminology, and system design. Hands-on experience with commonly used plumbing materials is particularly important for candidates who must complete the practical examination.

Exam Details

The Texas Journeyman Plumber examination is administered through Pearson VUE for the Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners.

  • License: Texas Journeyman Plumber
  • Licensing Agency: Texas State Board of Plumbing Examiners
  • Written Testing Provider: Pearson VUE
  • Written Examination Questions: 100
  • Written Examination Time: 120 minutes
  • Practical Examination Items: 6
  • Practical Examination Time: 240 minutes
  • Written Exam Format: Computer based

The written examination focuses on the knowledge and judgment expected of a Texas Journeyman Plumber. Candidates are tested on proper installation of commercial and residential plumbing systems and on decisions commonly made while performing journeyman-level plumbing work.

The practical portion evaluates hands-on plumbing skills. Current practical testing includes measuring, cutting, assembling, and fitting commonly used piping materials. Candidates may work with materials such as solvent-cemented PVC, soldered copper, brazed copper, threaded steel, and compression-band cast iron.

The practical examination also includes identifying proper and improper water-heater installations and designing a two-story sanitary drainage and vent system using common plumbing fixtures. The candidate must apply the design to a scaled plumbing model using miniature pipe and fittings.

Applicants moving from Plumber's Apprentice status to the Journeyman license must complete the practical examination after passing the computer-based written test. Applicants who already hold a Texas Tradesman Plumber-Limited license and are advancing to Journeyman are not required to retake the practical portion.

Closed Book Test

The Texas Journeyman Plumber computer-based examination is a closed book test. Personal plumbing codes, study guides, notes, papers, and other reference materials are not permitted in the Pearson VUE testing room.

The books in this package are therefore intended for preparation before the examination. Candidates should use the references to learn the rules, understand how plumbing systems are designed and installed, and build enough familiarity with common code requirements to answer questions without consulting the books during the test.

Closed-book preparation should include active recall. After reviewing a chapter or topic, practice explaining important requirements without looking at the code. Candidates can then return to the book to check their understanding and correct weak areas.

The Texas examination is not limited to one single plumbing code. Candidates are advised to study Texas plumbing law and rules along with either the 2018 International Plumbing Code and 2018 International Fuel Gas Code or the 2018 Uniform Plumbing Code. This package includes all three technical references, allowing candidates to build a broader plumbing-code library for exam preparation and professional reference.

Licensing Steps

  1. Hold an eligible Texas registration or license. Applicants may qualify while registered as a Texas Plumber's Apprentice, while licensed as a Texas Tradesman Plumber-Limited, or through certain qualifying out-of-state Journeyman or Master Plumber credentials.
  2. Accumulate qualifying plumbing experience. The Journeyman Plumber pathway requires at least 8,000 hours of experience working in the plumbing trade.
  3. Complete required training. Applicants generally must complete a 48-hour TSBPE-approved training program unless an approved exemption or apprenticeship pathway applies.
  4. Complete fingerprint requirements. Texas plumbing examination applicants must have the required fingerprint information submitted and reviewed as part of the licensing process.
  5. Submit the Journeyman Plumber examination application. Provide the required application and supporting experience documentation to TSBPE.
  6. Receive Authorization to Test. After the application has been approved, the candidate receives authorization to schedule the computer-based examination.
  7. Schedule through Pearson VUE. The written examination is taken through Pearson VUE testing centers.
  8. Pass the written examination. Complete the 100-question computer-based examination within the allotted 120 minutes.
  9. Complete the practical examination when required. Applicants advancing from Plumber's Apprentice status must complete the practical hands-on examination after passing the written test.
  10. Complete license issuance requirements. After satisfying all applicable testing requirements, complete the remaining TSBPE licensing steps for the Journeyman Plumber credential.

State Requirements

Applicants for the Texas Journeyman Plumber examination must have at least 8,000 hours of experience working in the plumbing trade. The required experience must be documented as part of the examination application process.

An applicant may qualify while currently registered as a Texas Plumber's Apprentice or while holding a Texas Tradesman Plumber-Limited license. Texas also recognizes certain applicants who hold current Journeyman or Master Plumber licenses from another state.

Journeyman applicants generally must complete a 48-hour TSBPE-approved training program. The training structure includes continuing professional education, OSHA construction-industry safety training, plumbing-code instruction, and commercial plumbing-code training.

Applicants enrolled in, or having completed, a training program approved by the United States Department of Labor Office of Apprenticeship may qualify under an alternative training pathway. Applicants holding qualifying out-of-state Journeyman or Master Plumber licenses may also be exempt from the 48-hour training requirement.

A licensed Texas Journeyman Plumber may install, change, repair, service, or renovate plumbing under the supervision of a Responsible Master Plumber.

A Journeyman Plumber may directly supervise a Plumber's Apprentice on plumbing job sites. The license also allows the Journeyman to supervise a Tradesman Plumber-Limited when the work involves structures other than one- and two-family dwellings.

Reference Books

  • International Plumbing Code, 2018
    A comprehensive plumbing-code reference covering plumbing fixtures, water supply and distribution, sanitary drainage, venting, traps, interceptors, indirect waste, storm drainage, piping materials, testing, sizing, and installation requirements for residential and commercial plumbing systems.
  • International Fuel Gas Code, 2018
    Covers fuel-gas piping, pipe sizing, appliance connections, shutoff valves, regulators, pressure testing, combustion air, venting, gas-fired equipment, and other fuel-gas installation and safety requirements important to journeyman plumbing work.
  • Uniform Plumbing Code, 2018
    A broad plumbing-code reference addressing plumbing fixtures, potable water, sanitary drainage, venting, traps, interceptors, water heaters, fuel-gas piping, materials, sizing, testing, and installation practices used throughout the plumbing trade.

Test Information and Study Materials

Sanitary Drainage: Review approved drainage materials, pipe sizing, drainage fixture units, slope, changes in direction, cleanouts, building drains, building sewers, testing requirements, and proper installation methods.

Venting: Understand the purpose of the vent system and how it protects fixture trap seals. Review individual vents, common vents, wet venting, stack venting, vent sizing, vent connections, developed length, and termination requirements.

Traps: Study fixture traps, trap seals, prohibited traps, trap-arm requirements, protection against siphonage and backpressure, and proper fixture connections.

Water Supply and Distribution: Review water-service piping, distribution systems, fixture supply requirements, pipe sizing, pressure, valves, backflow protection, cross-connection control, and approved materials.

Plumbing Fixtures: Study proper installation and connection requirements for water closets, lavatories, sinks, showers, bathtubs, commercial fixtures, floor drains, and other plumbing fixtures.

Water Heaters: Water-heater installations are important on both the knowledge and practical sides of Journeyman preparation. Review temperature and pressure relief valves, discharge piping, shutoff valves, drain pans, combustion air, venting, location requirements, and prohibited installation practices.

Fuel Gas: Use the International Fuel Gas Code and applicable Uniform Plumbing Code provisions to review fuel-gas piping, sizing, regulators, appliance connections, shutoff valves, pressure tests, sediment traps, combustion air, and venting.

Piping Materials: Become familiar with common plumbing materials and approved joining methods. Journeyman candidates should understand PVC, copper, cast iron, steel, and other commonly encountered plumbing piping systems.

Pipe Joining: Practical preparation should include measuring and cutting piping and making proper solvent-cemented PVC, soldered copper, brazed copper, threaded steel, and compression-band cast-iron joints.

Commercial Plumbing: The Journeyman license extends beyond one- and two-family residential work. Study commercial fixture arrangements, commercial drainage and vent systems, larger water-distribution systems, indirect waste, interceptors, and code requirements encountered in commercial buildings.

Plumbing Calculations: Practice drainage fixture-unit calculations, water-supply fixture-unit calculations, pipe sizing, developed-length calculations, vent sizing, and other code-based numerical problems.

System Design: The practical examination requires system-design ability. Candidates should practice laying out sanitary drainage and vent systems for multiple fixtures and understanding how horizontal branches, stacks, fixture drains, vents, and building drains work together.

Test Information and Study Materials (continued)

Water-Heater Inspection: Candidates should be able to recognize proper and improper water-heater installations. Review common violations involving relief-valve discharge, gas supply, shutoff valves, vent connectors, combustion air, clearances, and water piping.

Code Definitions: Learn essential plumbing terminology, including building drain, building sewer, fixture drain, drainage fixture unit, developed length, trap arm, vent stack, stack vent, water service, branch, riser, and other definitions used throughout the examination.

Texas Plumbing Law and Rules: Technical codes are only one part of Texas preparation. Candidates should also understand the licensing structure, supervision requirements, responsibilities of licensed plumbers, and regulatory framework administered by TSBPE.

Closed-Book Practice: The written examination does not permit personal reference books. Use the books while learning a topic, then answer practice questions without looking at them. Repeating this cycle helps strengthen recall and identify topics that need additional review.

Time Management: The written examination allows 120 minutes for 100 questions. Candidates should practice working at a steady pace and avoid spending excessive time on a single difficult question.

Hands-On Preparation: Applicants who must take the practical examination should spend time working with actual plumbing tools, piping materials, fittings, measurement methods, and assembly techniques. Practical familiarity is difficult to replace with reading alone.
