## Licensing Steps

1. **Confirm the appropriate classification.** Determine that the Mississippi Plumbing classification matches the plumbing services the business intends to provide.
2. **Select the qualifying party.** Identify the person who will qualify the business and complete the required examinations.
3. **Document relevant experience.** Organize project information involving water distribution, sanitary drainage, venting, fixtures, piping, installation, supervision, or project management.
4. **Choose the applicant structure.** Determine whether the application will be submitted by an individual, corporation, limited liability company, partnership, or another accepted business entity.
5. **Prepare the contractor application.** Gather the requested business, ownership, qualifying-party, experience, financial, insurance, reference, and supporting information.
6. **Submit the application.** Send the completed filing to the Mississippi State Board of Contractors for review.
7. **Respond to application requests.** Provide missing documents, corrections, explanations, or additional information requested during the review process.
8. **Receive examination eligibility.** Follow the examination registration instructions after authorization to test is issued.
9. **Schedule the required examinations.** Select an available examination date and approved testing location.
10. **Prepare with the reference books.** Review plumbing code, construction safety, trade mathematics, and open-book navigation.
11. **Complete timed practice.** Work through plumbing questions, calculations, and book-lookup exercises under structured conditions.
12. **Prepare for Business and Law.** Obtain the separate Mississippi Business and Law reference and complete the required preparation.
13. **Take the examinations.** Bring acceptable identification and only the references and materials permitted under current testing rules.
14. **Complete outstanding requirements.** Submit any remaining documents or corrections requested during final application review.
15. **Receive final approval.** Passing the examinations does not automatically issue a contractor license. The application and supporting documentation must also be approved.
16. **Maintain the license.** After issuance, contractors are responsible for renewals, insurance, business records, taxes, permits, safety obligations, and ongoing compliance.
