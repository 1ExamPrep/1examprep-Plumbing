## Exam Details

The Mississippi Plumbing contractor examination evaluates the candidate’s understanding of plumbing systems, installation requirements, code navigation, construction safety, and practical trade mathematics. Candidates should combine field experience with repeated study of the listed references.

The International Plumbing Code, 2021 Edition, is the primary technical code used with this course. It addresses plumbing fixtures, water-distribution systems, sanitary drainage, venting, traps, interceptors, piping materials, joints, testing, inspections, storm drainage, and related installation requirements.

Water-distribution preparation may involve service piping, distribution piping, pipe sizing, water demand, pressure, materials, valves, backflow protection, joints, supports, testing, and fixture supply. Candidates should learn how to locate applicable tables and understand how the code organizes general and system-specific requirements.

Sanitary-drainage preparation may involve fixture units, horizontal branches, stacks, building drains, building sewers, cleanouts, slopes, pipe sizing, materials, joints, supports, and testing. Questions may require direct code lookup, interpretation of a table, or application of practical plumbing knowledge.

Venting subjects may include vent sizing, developed length, vent connections, wet venting, common venting, circuit venting, stack venting, vent terminals, and prohibited arrangements. Candidates should understand how vent systems protect trap seals and support proper drainage-system performance.

Fixture-related preparation may include required fixture quantities, installation, clearances, supports, water-supply connections, drainage connections, accessibility considerations, materials, and testing. Additional subjects may involve traps, interceptors, separators, indirect waste, special plumbing systems, and storm drainage.

The Code of Federal Regulations, 29 CFR Part 1926, supports preparation involving federal construction-safety requirements. Plumbing professionals may need to review excavation, trench protection, ladders, scaffolds, fall protection, personal protective equipment, tools, equipment, electrical hazards, and material handling.

Plumbing work can involve trenches, heavy piping, elevated work areas, temporary power, lifting equipment, confined spaces, and interaction with other trades. Candidates should connect written safety requirements with the practical hazards encountered on construction projects.

*Mathematics for Plumbers and Pipefitters*, 8th Edition, supports preparation involving measurements, fractions, decimals, percentages, ratios, geometry, areas, volumes, pressures, pipe calculations, offsets, and unit conversions.

## Open Book Test

The Mississippi Plumbing contractor examination is open book. Candidates are responsible for bringing the permitted references and following current examination rules for approved titles, editions, highlighting, tabs, markings, notes, and supplemental materials.

An open-book examination still requires detailed preparation. Having the correct books available does not help when a candidate cannot identify the relevant reference, locate an effective index term, interpret a technical table, recognize an exception, or complete a calculation within the available time.

The course helps candidates develop a repeatable lookup process. Begin by identifying the main subject of the question. Determine which reference is most likely to contain the answer. Search the index, table of contents, chapter heading, definition, table, or formula. Read enough surrounding material to confirm that the provision applies.

Questions involving fixtures, water distribution, sanitary drainage, venting, traps, piping materials, testing, or storm drainage may lead to the International Plumbing Code. Candidates should practice searching by system, component, fixture type, chapter, table, and index term.

Construction-safety questions may require 29 CFR Part 1926. Candidates should become familiar with the sections addressing excavation, trench protection, access and egress, ladders, scaffolds, personal protective equipment, tools, equipment, electrical hazards, and material handling.

Calculation questions may require *Mathematics for Plumbers and Pipefitters*. Candidates should practice working with measurements, fractions, decimals, ratios, geometry, areas, volumes, offsets, pipe lengths, pressure, and conversions.

Effective search terminology is important. A drainage question may require searches involving fixture units, horizontal branches, stacks, building drains, cleanouts, slopes, or building sewers. A water-supply question may involve demand, pressure, service piping, distribution piping, valves, backflow protection, or fixture supply.

A venting question may require searches involving developed length, trap seals, wet venting, common venting, stack vents, or vent terminals. Learning related terms can help candidates find the correct section when examination wording differs from the terminology used in the code.

## Licensing Steps

1. **Confirm the appropriate classification.** Determine that the Mississippi Plumbing classification matches the plumbing services the business intends to provide.
2. **Select the qualifying party.** Identify the person who will qualify the business and complete the required examinations.
3. **Document relevant experience.** Organize project information involving water distribution, sanitary drainage, venting, fixtures, piping, installation, supervision, or project management.
4. **Choose the applicant structure.** Determine whether the application will be submitted by an individual, corporation, limited liability company, partnership, or another accepted business entity.
5. **Prepare the contractor application.** Gather the requested business, ownership, qualifying-party, experience, financial, insurance, reference, and supporting information.
6. **Submit the application.** Send the completed filing to the Mississippi State Board of Contractors for review.
7. **Respond to application requests.** Provide missing documents, corrections, explanations, or additional information requested during the review process.
8. **Receive examination eligibility.** Follow the examination registration instructions after authorization to test is issued.
9. **Schedule the required examinations.** Select an available examination date and authorized testing location.
10. **Obtain the required references.** Secure the listed books and applicable editions before completing the reference-navigation portions of the course.
11. **Prepare with the online course.** Review plumbing code, construction safety, trade mathematics, technical terminology, and open-book strategies.
12. **Complete timed practice.** Work through code lookups, calculation exercises, and practice-oriented questions under structured conditions.
13. **Prepare for Business and Law.** Obtain separate preparation for applicable Mississippi contractor business and law requirements.
14. **Take the examinations.** Bring acceptable identification and only the references and materials permitted under current testing rules.
15. **Complete outstanding requirements.** Submit any remaining records, corrections, or supporting documents requested during final review.
16. **Receive final approval.** Passing the examinations does not automatically issue a contractor license. The completed application and supporting documentation must also be approved.
