Mississippi contractor examinations administered through PSI are open book. Candidates are responsible for bringing the permitted references to the testing center and following current rules for approved titles, editions, highlighting, tabs, markings, notes, and supplemental materials.

An open-book format does not remove the need for preparation. Candidates may have the correct books available but still lose valuable time by selecting the wrong reference, using an ineffective index term, misreading a table, overlooking an exception, or applying a rule outside its intended scope.
