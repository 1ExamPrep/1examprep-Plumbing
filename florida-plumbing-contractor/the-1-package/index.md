## Exam Details

Florida Certified Plumbing Contractor applicants must prepare for two main examination components: **Plumbing Trade Knowledge** and **Business and Finance**.

The examinations are part of Florida's contractor certification program overseen by the Department of Business and Professional Regulation (DBPR) and the Construction Industry Licensing Board.

- **License Classification:** Florida Certified Plumbing Contractor
- **Trade Examination:** Plumbing Contractor Trade Knowledge
- **Additional Examination:** Business and Finance
- **Examination Format:** Open book
- **Question Format:** Multiple choice
- **Plumbing Trade Delivery:** Scheduled paper-and-pencil testing
- **Business and Finance Delivery:** Computer-based testing
- **Examination Registration:** Professional Testing, Inc.
- **Computer-Based Testing Provider:** Pearson VUE

The Plumbing Trade Knowledge examination addresses technical plumbing principles, piping systems, construction requirements, materials, calculations, and professional installation practices.

The Business and Finance examination evaluates knowledge relating to contractor business operations, financial management, contracts, project responsibilities, and professional administration.

The 1 Package includes separate online courses and rental references for these two examination areas, helping candidates organize their preparation across both technical and business subjects.

## Open Book Test

Florida's Plumbing Contractor Trade Knowledge and Business and Finance examinations are **open-book tests**. Candidates may consult reference publications specifically authorized for their examination category and testing period.

Open-book preparation requires understanding how technical publications are organized. Candidates must recognize the subject of a question, identify the appropriate book, locate the relevant information, and apply it correctly.

For plumbing questions, that may involve researching drainage requirements, water distribution, fuel gas piping, accessibility provisions, or mathematical formulas. Business questions may involve contractor accounting, construction contracts, or professional responsibilities.

## State Requirements

Florida regulates Plumbing Contractors under **Chapter 489 of the Florida Statutes**.

The Certified Plumbing Contractor classification covers extensive plumbing activities, including water supply, sanitary and storm drainage, venting, septic systems, irrigation, swimming pool piping, and solar water heating.

The authorized scope also includes specified natural gas, liquefied petroleum gas, medical gas, pressure piping, and related installations.

Contractors must operate within their licensed classification and comply with applicable building codes, permits, safety requirements, and professional regulations.

Certification involves meeting the applicable examination, experience, financial responsibility, insurance, and administrative qualifications.

Establishing a business entity does not replace the contractor certification process. The 1 Package addresses both areas through its included Application Service and business setup support.

## How does the Plumbing Contractor exam work?

The Plumbing Contractor license comes from the Florida Construction Industry Licensing Board (CILB). Pearson VUE gives the exam. The board approves your application first; then you schedule the exam and bring only the references the candidate bulletin allows.

- The Florida Construction Industry Licensing Board certifies plumbing contractors statewide; Pearson VUE gives the state trade exam.
- Florida journeyman and master plumber cards are issued by counties, which use Prov or GITS exams.
- Florida also certifies plumbing inspectors and plans examiners using ICC exams (1P, 2P and 3P).
