Exam Details

The Florida Plumbing Contractor Trade Knowledge Examination evaluates technical knowledge associated with plumbing construction, piping systems, installation methods, calculations, applicable codes, and professional trade practices.

The examination is part of Florida's contractor certification program overseen by the Department of Business and Professional Regulation and the Construction Industry Licensing Board.

  • Examination: Florida Plumbing Contractor General Trade Knowledge
  • Licensing Authority: Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation
  • Licensing Board: Construction Industry Licensing Board
  • Testing Format: Paper-and-pencil examination
  • Examination Type: Open book
  • Examination Registration: Professional Testing, Inc.
  • Principal Study Subjects: Plumbing systems, pipefitting mathematics, installation requirements, construction codes, and safety
  • Additional Certification Examination: Business and Finance

The Florida Plumbing Contractor trade examination includes technical plumbing knowledge and an isometric drawing component. Therefore, complete examination preparation involves more than answering multiple-choice questions.

Online practice questions are particularly useful for reinforcing code-related knowledge, calculations, terminology, material selection, and installation principles. Candidates should also study the drawing-related skills required for the official trade examination.

The Business and Finance examination is a separate component of Florida contractor certification. This product focuses on plumbing trade knowledge rather than business examination preparation.

Open Book Test

The Florida Plumbing Contractor Trade Knowledge Examination is an open-book test. Candidates may use authorized reference materials during the examination, subject to Florida's official testing rules.

Open-book testing requires more than knowing how to find a particular code section. Candidates must understand the question, select the appropriate technical publication, locate the relevant information, and apply it correctly.

For example, a question about water distribution or sanitary drainage may require familiarity with the Florida Plumbing Code. Questions concerning accessibility may involve the Florida Building Code - Accessibility, while fuel gas installation questions may involve the Fuel Gas Code.

Mathematical questions may require measurements, formulas, conversions, or piping calculations. Specialized questions may involve medical gas systems, standpipes, solar thermal installations, or onsite sewage treatment.

Florida publishes official reference lists identifying the publications authorized for its Plumbing Contractor examinations. Candidates may bring only materials permitted for their scheduled examination.

Studying with relevant reference materials while working through online practice questions helps develop familiarity with indexes, definitions, diagrams, technical tables, and installation requirements.

A productive practice routine involves understanding the technical problem first, researching the applicable information, and reviewing the reasoning behind the selected answer.

State Requirements

Florida regulates Plumbing Contractors under Chapter 489 of the Florida Statutes.

The Plumbing Contractor classification covers a broad range of authorized plumbing activities, including installation, maintenance, repair, alteration, extension, and certain design activities when permitted by law.

The authorized scope includes sanitary drainage, storm drainage, venting, public and private water supply systems, septic tanks, drainage and supply wells, swimming pool piping, irrigation, and solar water heating systems.

Florida Plumbing Contractors may also perform specified work involving water, natural gas, liquefied petroleum gas, medical gas systems, pressure piping, and other specialized systems within their authorized classification.

Contractors must comply with applicable construction codes, approved plans, specifications, safety requirements, and professional regulations.

State certification also involves meeting the applicable examination, experience, financial responsibility, insurance, and administrative requirements.

Reference Books

The Florida Plumbing Contractor Online Practice Questions focus on technical subjects associated with the following 12 study references:

  • Chapter 64E-6, Florida Administrative Code, Standards for Onsite Sewage Treatment and Disposal Systems, July 16, 2018
    A regulatory reference addressing onsite sewage treatment and disposal systems. It supports review of septic systems, wastewater treatment concepts, and installation-related requirements.
  • Code of Federal Regulations - 29 CFR Part 1926 (OSHA)
    Contains federal construction safety requirements involving excavation, trenching, protective equipment, electrical hazards, tools, machinery, ladders, and scaffolding.
  • Mathematics for Plumbers and Pipefitters, 8th Edition
    A trade mathematics publication covering measurements, fractions, geometry, conversions, calculations, and practical mathematical applications used in plumbing and pipefitting.
  • 2024 NFPA 14 Standard for the Installation of Standpipe and Hose Systems
    Addresses requirements for standpipe and hose system installations, including system components, arrangements, and water supply considerations.
  • NFPA 99 Health Care Facilities, (Chapter 3 & 5, Annex A & C), 2024
    Provides specialized healthcare facility information, including terminology and technical provisions associated with medical gas and vacuum systems.
  • Plumber's Handbook Revised, 2018, 5th Ed.
    A practical plumbing resource covering piping materials, fittings, installation methods, technical information, calculations, and plumbing system principles.
  • Solar Thermal Manual: Solar Water & Pool Heating Manual, 2006
    Provides information concerning solar water heating, solar pool heating, equipment components, installation concepts, and system applications.
  • Florida Building Code - Mechanical, 2026
    Addresses mechanical construction provisions involving equipment installation, ventilation, mechanical systems, and related building requirements.
  • Florida Building Code - Accessibility, 2026
    Covers accessibility requirements affecting plumbing fixtures, sanitary facilities, clearances, and accessible building elements.
  • Florida Building Code - Plumbing, 2026
    Addresses water distribution, drainage, venting, plumbing fixtures, piping materials, system components, and installation requirements.
  • Florida Building Code - Residential, 2026
    Provides residential construction requirements involving plumbing installations, water supply, drainage, fixtures, and associated systems.
  • Florida Building Code – Fuel Gas, 2026
    Addresses fuel gas piping, appliance installation, combustion air, venting, and related system safety provisions.

Physical reference books are not included with this online practice-question product. The listed references identify the technical publications supporting the preparation subjects.
