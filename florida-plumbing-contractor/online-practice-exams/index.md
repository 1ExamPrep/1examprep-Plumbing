## State Requirements

Florida regulates Plumbing Contractors under **Chapter 489 of the Florida Statutes**.

The Plumbing Contractor classification covers a broad range of authorized plumbing activities, including installation, maintenance, repair, alteration, extension, and certain design activities when permitted by law.

The authorized scope includes sanitary drainage, storm drainage, venting, public and private water supply systems, septic tanks, drainage and supply wells, swimming pool piping, irrigation, and solar water heating systems.

Florida Plumbing Contractors may also perform specified work involving water, natural gas, liquefied petroleum gas, medical gas systems, pressure piping, and other specialized systems within their authorized classification.

Contractors must comply with applicable construction codes, approved plans, specifications, safety requirements, and professional regulations.

State certification also involves meeting the applicable examination, experience, financial responsibility, insurance, and administrative requirements.
