## State Requirements

Florida regulates Plumbing Contractors under Chapter 489 of the Florida Statutes.

The Plumbing Contractor classification includes installing, maintaining, repairing, altering, extending, and, where legally permitted, designing plumbing systems.

The authorized scope includes sanitary and storm drainage, venting, water supply systems, septic tanks, drainage and supply wells, swimming pool piping, irrigation, and solar water heating systems.

Plumbing contractors may also perform specified work involving natural gas, liquefied petroleum gas, medical gas systems, pressure piping, and related installations within the authorized classification.

Contractors must comply with applicable codes, approved plans, specifications, safety requirements, and professional regulations.

Certification also requires meeting Florida's applicable examination, experience, financial responsibility, insurance, and administrative qualifications.
