Exam Details

The Florida Plumbing Contractor Trade Knowledge Examination evaluates knowledge associated with plumbing installation, repair, materials, piping systems, codes, and construction practices.

Florida contractor certification is overseen by the Department of Business and Professional Regulation (DBPR) and the Construction Industry Licensing Board.

  • Examination: Florida Plumbing Contractor Trade Knowledge
  • Licensing Authority: Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation
  • Licensing Board: Construction Industry Licensing Board
  • Examination Format: Open book
  • Question Type: Multiple choice
  • Trade Examination Delivery: Paper-and-pencil testing
  • Examination Registration: Professional Testing, Inc.
  • Additional Examination: Business and Finance

Unlike Florida's computer-based construction trade examinations, the Plumbing Contractor Trade Knowledge Examination is administered in scheduled paper-and-pencil sessions.

Preparation should include familiarity with residential and commercial plumbing, construction drawings, plumbing calculations, water supply, drainage systems, venting, and specialized piping applications.

Technical questions can require candidates to combine field experience with published plumbing requirements, installation principles, and mathematical problem-solving.

State Requirements

Florida regulates Plumbing Contractors under Chapter 489 of the Florida Statutes.

The plumbing contractor classification covers the installation, maintenance, repair, alteration, extension, and legally authorized design of plumbing systems.

The statutory scope includes sanitary and storm drainage, venting systems, public and private water supplies, water and sewer facilities, septic tanks, drainage and supply wells, swimming pool piping, irrigation, and solar water heating systems.

Florida Plumbing Contractors may also perform specified work involving water, natural gas, liquefied petroleum gas, medical gas systems, boilers, pressure piping, and related systems within their authorized scope.

Plumbing contractors must comply with applicable plans, specifications, construction codes, laws, and regulations.

The classification also includes certain excavation activities incidental to plumbing work. Work outside the plumbing classification must be performed by appropriately qualified contractors when required.

Applicants pursuing state certification must satisfy applicable examination, experience, financial responsibility, insurance, and administrative requirements.
