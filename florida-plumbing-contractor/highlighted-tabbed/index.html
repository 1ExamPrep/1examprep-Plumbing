Exam Details

The Florida Plumbing Contractor Trade Knowledge Examination evaluates technical knowledge associated with plumbing systems, construction requirements, materials, installation practices, and related calculations.

Florida's construction contractor licensing program is overseen by the Department of Business and Professional Regulation and the Construction Industry Licensing Board.

  • Examination: Florida Plumbing Contractor Trade Knowledge
  • Licensing Authority: Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation
  • Licensing Board: Construction Industry Licensing Board
  • Examination Format: Open book
  • Question Type: Multiple choice
  • Trade Examination Delivery: Scheduled paper-and-pencil testing
  • Examination Registration: Professional Testing, Inc.
  • Additional Examination Component: Business and Finance

The examination involves practical understanding of plumbing construction and the ability to work with technical standards. Candidates should study water supply systems, drainage and venting, piping arrangements, plumbing fixtures, fuel gas, accessibility, and construction safety.

Plumbing mathematics is another important preparation area. Candidates should be comfortable working with measurements, conversions, dimensional relationships, and calculations relevant to piping installations.

This highlighted and tabbed book set supports familiarity with the technical publications used to research these subjects.

Open Book Test

The Florida Plumbing Contractor Trade Knowledge Examination is an open-book test. Candidates may consult materials authorized for the Plumbing Contractor examination and their scheduled testing period.

Open-book testing requires more than access to technical publications. Candidates must identify the subject of a question, determine the appropriate reference, locate the relevant provision, and apply the information accurately.

For example, water distribution and drainage questions may involve the Florida Plumbing Code. Accessibility questions may require a different code volume, while mathematical problems may call for calculations using pipefitting principles.

Highlighted content and organized tabs support practice locating information during study. Candidates can become familiar with technical indexes, sizing tables, illustrations, definitions, and installation requirements.

Florida's examination rules permit highlighting in approved references and restrict handwritten notes, inserted materials, and movable tabs. Examination materials must satisfy the applicable testing-room requirements.

Only references appearing on the official approved list for the scheduled Plumbing examination may enter the testing room. The 12-reference package is a trade study collection, not a declaration that every listed edition is approved for every examination date.

Regular practice with the references helps candidates develop a systematic approach to finding information under examination conditions.

State Requirements

Florida regulates Plumbing Contractors under Chapter 489 of the Florida Statutes.

The Plumbing Contractor classification covers a broad range of plumbing work, including sanitary and storm drainage, venting, water supply systems, septic tanks, swimming pool piping, irrigation, and solar water heating.

The authorized scope also includes certain natural gas and liquefied petroleum gas installations, medical gas systems, pressure piping, and related systems, subject to applicable laws and qualifications.

Florida Plumbing Contractors must comply with construction codes, project specifications, safety requirements, and the boundaries of their authorized classification.

Applicants must also meet the licensing board's applicable examination, experience, financial responsibility, insurance, and administrative requirements.

    A regulatory publication addressing onsite sewage treatment and disposal. It supports study of wastewater systems, installation terminology, and requirements associated with septic installations.
  • Code of Federal Regulations - 29 CFR Part 1926 (OSHA)
    Contains federal construction safety regulations covering excavation, trenching, ladders, scaffolding, tools, electrical hazards, protective equipment, and workplace conditions.
  • Mathematics for Plumbers and Pipefitters, 8th Edition
    A practical mathematics reference addressing measurements, fractions, geometry, conversions, formulas, and calculations used in plumbing and pipefitting.
  • 2024 NFPA 14 Standard for the Installation of Standpipe and Hose Systems
    Addresses installation requirements for standpipe and hose systems, including components, arrangements, and water supply considerations.
  • NFPA 99 Health Care Facilities, (Chapter 3 & 5, Annex A & C), 2024
    Provides technical provisions concerning healthcare facility systems, including medical gas and vacuum systems and related terminology.
  • Plumber's Handbook Revised, 2018, 5th Ed.
    A plumbing trade reference covering piping, fittings, materials, calculations, installation methods, and technical information used in professional plumbing work.
  • Solar Thermal Manual: Solar Water & Pool Heating Manual, 2006
    Addresses solar water heating, solar pool heating, equipment components, installation principles, and solar thermal system applications.
  • Florida Building Code - Mechanical, 2026
    Covers mechanical construction requirements affecting equipment, ventilation, system installation, and related building services.
  • Florida Building Code - Accessibility, 2026
    Addresses accessibility requirements affecting plumbing fixtures, sanitary facilities, clearances, and accessible building elements.
  • Florida Building Code - Plumbing, 2026
    Provides requirements covering water distribution, drainage, venting, fixtures, piping systems, plumbing materials, and installations.
  • Florida Building Code - Residential, 2026
    Addresses residential construction requirements, including plumbing systems, fixtures, water supply, drainage, and related installations.
  • Florida Building Code – Fuel Gas, 2026
    Contains requirements involving fuel gas piping, appliances, combustion air, venting, system installation, and related safety considerations.
