How does the Plumbing Contractor exam work?

The Plumbing Contractor license comes from the Florida Construction Industry Licensing Board (CILB). Pearson VUE gives the exam. The board approves your application first; then you schedule the exam and bring only the references the candidate bulletin allows.

  • The Florida Construction Industry Licensing Board certifies plumbing contractors statewide; Pearson VUE gives the state trade exam.
  • Florida journeyman and master plumber cards are issued by counties, which use Prov or GITS exams.
  • Florida also certifies plumbing inspectors and plans examiners using ICC exams (1P, 2P and 3P).

Is the Plumbing Contractor exam open book?

Yes, for most exams here. The trade exam allows the references listed in the candidate bulletin, so highlighted and tabbed books save the most time.

Who issues the Plumbing Contractor license in Florida?

It comes from the Florida Construction Industry Licensing Board (CILB). Buying a package here does not register you or make you eligible; the board approves you first, and the exam vendor schedules the test.

Does the Plumbing Contractor exam use the IPC or the UPC?

Each package names its code in the title. The 2026 journeyman study guides say "Based on 2024 IPC" or "Based on 2024 UPC"; book packages list the exact editions. Match it to the editions in your candidate bulletin.
