## State Requirements

Florida regulates Plumbing Contractors under **Chapter 489 of the Florida Statutes**.

The Plumbing Contractor classification covers installing, maintaining, repairing, altering, extending, and, when permitted by law, designing plumbing systems.

The authorized scope includes sanitary and storm drainage, venting, water supply systems, septic tanks, drainage and supply wells, swimming pool piping, irrigation, and solar water heating systems.

Florida Plumbing Contractors may also perform specified work involving natural gas, liquefied petroleum gas, medical gas systems, pressure piping, and related installations within their authorized scope.

Other authorized activities include certain standpipe and fire sprinkler work when permitted by law, specialized piping systems, and excavation incidental to plumbing construction.

Contractors must follow applicable construction codes, approved plans, specifications, safety requirements, and professional regulations.

Certification also involves satisfying applicable experience, examination, financial responsibility, insurance, and administrative requirements.
