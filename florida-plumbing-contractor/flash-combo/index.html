Exam Details

Florida Plumbing Contractor certification is regulated by the Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation (DBPR) through the Construction Industry Licensing Board (CILB).

Applicants generally must pass the Plumbing General Trade Knowledge examination and the Construction Business & Finance examination, unless an applicable exemption applies.

Florida Plumbing Contractor Trade Knowledge Exam

  • Exam Name: Plumbing General Trade Knowledge
  • Exam Format: Paper-and-pencil examination
  • Multiple-Choice Questions: 110
  • Isometric Drawings: 5
  • Maximum Score: 160 points
  • Passing Score: 112 points
  • Testing Sessions: Morning and afternoon sessions of 4 hours and 30 minutes each
  • Exam Type: Open book

Unlike most Florida construction trade examinations, the Plumbing General Trade Knowledge examination is administered in paper-and-pencil format on scheduled examination dates.

The examination includes multiple-choice trade questions and five plumbing isometric drawings. Each drawing is worth up to 10 points and evaluates the candidate's ability to represent plumbing systems accurately.

Technical knowledge and drawing practice are important components of Florida Plumbing Contractor exam preparation.

Florida Business &amp; Finance Exam

  • Exam Name: Construction Business & Finance
  • Exam Format: Computer-based, multiple-choice examination
  • Number of Scored Questions: 120
  • Time Allowed: 6 hours and 30 minutes
  • Exam Type: Open book
  • Testing Provider: Pearson VUE

The Business & Finance examination evaluates business administration, financial management, accounting, estimating, contracts, employee management, and regulations applicable to contracting businesses.

The Florida Plumbing Contractor + Business & Finance Flash Card Combo supports preparation across these technical and administrative knowledge areas.

The Florida Plumbing General Trade Knowledge and Construction Business & Finance examinations are open-book examinations. Candidates may consult approved reference materials during testing in accordance with the applicable examination rules.

The Plumbing General Trade Knowledge examination also requires candidates to complete isometric drawings. Drawing proficiency involves technical application and cannot be developed through reference searching alone.

Licensing Steps

Obtaining a Florida Certified Plumbing Contractor license involves completing the required examinations and meeting the qualifications established by the Construction Industry Licensing Board.

  1. Choose the Plumbing Contractor Classification: Review Florida's Plumbing Contractor scope of work and determine whether it matches your professional goals.
  2. Review Licensing Qualifications: Examine applicable plumbing experience, education, financial responsibility, insurance, and background requirements.
  3. Register for the Examinations: Apply for the required construction examinations through Professional Testing, Inc.
  4. Prepare for Both Examinations: Study Plumbing General Trade Knowledge and Business & Finance using appropriate preparation resources.
  5. Schedule Your Examinations: Follow the scheduled paper-and-pencil plumbing examination process and arrange the computer-based Business & Finance examination through Pearson VUE after approval.
  6. Complete the Required Examinations: Pass the examination components applicable to your certification.
  7. Submit Your Licensing Application: Apply to the Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation using the appropriate contractor certification application.
  8. Meet Remaining State Requirements: Complete applicable licensing conditions before performing regulated plumbing contracting work.

Examination approval and contractor licensing approval are separate processes. Passing the required examinations is an important step toward certification but does not independently authorize someone to operate as a licensed Plumbing Contractor.

State Requirements

Florida Plumbing Contractors are regulated under Chapter 489 of the Florida Statutes. The Plumbing Contractor classification belongs to Division II of the state's construction contractor licensing system.

Florida Plumbing Contractor Scope of Work

Florida Plumbing Contractors have broad authority within the plumbing trade, including the installation, maintenance, repair, alteration, and extension of qualifying plumbing systems.

The statutory scope includes numerous activities associated with residential, commercial, and specialized plumbing installations.

  • Sanitary drainage and storm drainage systems.
  • Water distribution and supply systems.
  • Drainage and venting systems.
  • Natural gas and related piping systems.
  • Medical gas piping and related systems.
  • Water and sewer lines.
  • Swimming pool piping and irrigation systems.
  • Solar water-heating systems.
  • Qualifying industrial and process piping.

The classification also includes certain additional piping, equipment, and related activities under Florida law. Contractors must perform authorized work in accordance with applicable codes, regulations, and professional requirements.

Certified vs. Registered Plumbing Contractor

Florida distinguishes between certified and registered contractors.

A state-certified Plumbing Contractor may perform authorized plumbing contracting activities throughout Florida, subject to applicable laws, permitting requirements, and local regulations.

A registered Plumbing Contractor's authority is associated with the local jurisdiction or jurisdictions for which the registration is valid.

This distinction is important when selecting a licensing pathway and planning future business activities.

Experience and Education Requirements

Florida Plumbing Contractor applicants must establish qualifying experience or an accepted combination of education and experience under applicable state licensing rules.

Relevant experience may include plumbing installation, drainage systems, water distribution, piping work, or qualifying supervisory responsibilities.

Applicants must provide the documentation required by their licensing application.

Financial Responsibility and Insurance

Florida Plumbing Contractor applicants must satisfy applicable financial responsibility, insurance, and other licensing requirements.

These obligations are separate from the knowledge evaluated during the Business & Finance examination and represent important responsibilities of professional contracting.

Plumbing Trade Knowledge Study Topics

The Florida Plumbing General Trade Knowledge examination covers several major technical content areas.

  • Drainage (20%): Review sanitary drainage, drainage components, venting relationships, and plumbing system principles.
  • Water Distribution (20%): Study water supply systems, distribution piping, pressure considerations, and installation principles.
  • Natural Gas Piping (15%): Review gas piping components, distribution concepts, sizing principles, and applicable safety considerations.
  • Medical Gas Piping (20%): Understand piping systems associated with medical gases and related installation requirements.
  • Industrial Piping (10%): Review specialized piping systems and industrial piping applications.
  • Swimming Pools, Wells, and Irrigation (5%): Study piping principles associated with water wells, pools, and irrigation systems.
  • Solar (5%): Review plumbing applications involving solar water-heating systems.
  • Fire Protection (5%): Reinforce knowledge of piping applications associated with qualifying fire protection systems.

These content areas provide a foundation for organizing technical plumbing study sessions.

Plumbing Isometric Drawing Preparation

One distinguishing feature of the Florida Plumbing Contractor trade examination is its isometric drawing component.

Candidates must prepare five plumbing isometric drawings demonstrating their understanding of plumbing system layouts and piping relationships.

Important drawing principles include:

  • Accurate representation of plumbing fixtures and piping.
  • Proper orientation of system components.
  • Clear identification of drainage and venting relationships.
  • Correct representation of piping connections.
  • Appropriate isometric drawing angles.
  • Clear labeling and legible presentation.

Flash cards can reinforce terminology and system knowledge, but successful drawing preparation also requires hands-on practice creating and interpreting plumbing isometric drawings.

Business &amp; Finance Study Topics

The Florida Construction Business & Finance examination evaluates the financial and administrative responsibilities associated with professional contracting.

  • Business Organization: Understand business structures, administration, and operational responsibilities.
  • Accounting: Review financial statements, accounting principles, and recordkeeping.
  • Financial Management: Study cash flow, budgeting, and business financial planning.
  • Estimating and Bidding: Review project pricing, job costs, proposals, and estimating concepts.
  • Contracts: Understand construction agreements, contractual obligations, and administration.
  • Employee Management: Review payroll, personnel responsibilities, and business management practices.
  • Government Regulations: Reinforce knowledge of applicable contracting laws and regulatory responsibilities.

These subjects help candidates prepare for the business-related responsibilities associated with managing a plumbing contracting operation.
