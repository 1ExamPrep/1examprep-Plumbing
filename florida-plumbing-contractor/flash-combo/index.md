## Exam Details

Florida Plumbing Contractor certification is regulated by the Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation (DBPR) through the Construction Industry Licensing Board (CILB).

Applicants generally must pass the Plumbing General Trade Knowledge examination and the Construction Business & Finance examination, unless an applicable exemption applies.

## Florida Plumbing Contractor Trade Knowledge Exam

- **Exam Name:** Plumbing General Trade Knowledge
- **Exam Format:** Paper-and-pencil examination
- **Multiple-Choice Questions:** 110
- **Isometric Drawings:** 5
- **Maximum Score:** 160 points
- **Passing Score:** 112 points
- **Testing Sessions:** Morning and afternoon sessions of 4 hours and 30 minutes each
- **Exam Type:** Open book

Unlike most Florida construction trade examinations, the Plumbing General Trade Knowledge examination is administered in paper-and-pencil format on scheduled examination dates.

The examination includes multiple-choice trade questions and five plumbing isometric drawings. Each drawing is worth up to 10 points and evaluates the candidate's ability to represent plumbing systems accurately.

Technical knowledge and drawing practice are important components of Florida Plumbing Contractor exam preparation.

## Florida Business &amp; Finance Exam

- **Exam Name:** Construction Business & Finance
- **Exam Format:** Computer-based, multiple-choice examination
- **Number of Scored Questions:** 120
- **Time Allowed:** 6 hours and 30 minutes
- **Exam Type:** Open book
- **Testing Provider:** Pearson VUE

The Business & Finance examination evaluates business administration, financial management, accounting, estimating, contracts, employee management, and regulations applicable to contracting businesses.

### State Requirements

Florida Plumbing Contractors are regulated under Chapter 489 of the Florida Statutes. The Plumbing Contractor classification belongs to Division II of the state's construction contractor licensing system.

## Florida Plumbing Contractor Scope of Work

Florida Plumbing Contractors have broad authority within the plumbing trade, including the installation, maintenance, repair, alteration, and extension of qualifying plumbing systems.

The statutory scope includes numerous activities associated with residential, commercial, and specialized plumbing installations.

- Sanitary drainage and storm drainage systems.
- Water distribution and supply systems.
- Drainage and venting systems.
- Natural gas and related piping systems.
- Medical gas piping and related systems.
- Water and sewer lines.
- Swimming pool piping and irrigation systems.
- Solar water-heating systems.
- Qualifying industrial and process piping.

The classification also includes certain additional piping, equipment, and related activities under Florida law. Contractors must perform authorized work in accordance with applicable codes, regulations, and professional requirements.

## Certified vs. Registered Plumbing Contractor

Florida distinguishes between certified and registered contractors.

A state-certified Plumbing Contractor may perform authorized plumbing contracting activities throughout Florida, subject to applicable laws, permitting requirements, and local regulations.

A registered Plumbing Contractor's authority is associated with the local jurisdiction or jurisdictions for which the registration is valid.

This distinction is important when selecting a licensing pathway and planning future business activities.

## Plumbing Isometric Drawing Preparation

One distinguishing feature of the Florida Plumbing Contractor trade examination is its isometric drawing component.

Candidates must prepare five plumbing isometric drawings demonstrating their understanding of plumbing system layouts and piping relationships.

Important drawing principles include:

- Accurate representation of plumbing fixtures and piping.
- Proper orientation of system components.
- Clear identification of drainage and venting relationships.
- Correct representation of piping connections.
- Appropriate isometric drawing angles.
- Clear labeling and legible presentation.

Flash cards can reinforce terminology and system knowledge, but successful drawing preparation also requires hands-on practice creating and interpreting plumbing isometric drawings.
