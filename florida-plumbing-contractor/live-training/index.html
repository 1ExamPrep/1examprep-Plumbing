Exam Details

The Florida Certified Plumbing Contractor General Trade Knowledge Examination is part of the contractor licensing program regulated by the Florida Construction Industry Licensing Board through the Department of Business and Professional Regulation.

Florida administers the plumbing trade examination in a paper-and-pencil format over two examination sessions.

  • Examination: Florida Plumbing Contractor General Trade Knowledge
  • Licensing Authority: Florida Construction Industry Licensing Board
  • Regulatory Department: Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation
  • Examination Format: Open book, paper and pencil
  • Morning Session: 45 questions and 5 isometric drawings
  • Isometric Drawings: 10 points each
  • Afternoon Session: 65 questions
  • Scoring: Morning and afternoon results are combined

The examination evaluates knowledge of plumbing systems, code interpretation, mathematics, installation methods, maintenance, workplace safety, and isometric drawings.

Its published content outline includes drainage, water distribution, natural gas piping, medical gas piping, and industrial piping. Other practical knowledge areas include reading plans, interpreting specifications, selecting equipment, and understanding installation techniques.

The examination requires candidates to connect field knowledge with mathematical calculations and technical references. A balanced preparation plan should address both conceptual questions and the practical skills used to interpret piping systems.

Florida contractor certification also includes a separate Business and Finance examination component. This program focuses on plumbing trade knowledge.

Open Book Test

The Florida Plumbing Contractor trade examination is an open book test. Candidates may use authorized reference publications in accordance with the state's testing requirements.

Open-book examinations require familiarity with technical information and efficient reference navigation. Students must understand the question, identify the relevant code or technical reference, locate the material, and apply it correctly.

The Florida Building Code - Plumbing supports study of drainage, water supply, fixtures, and plumbing installations. The Fuel Gas Code addresses gas piping and related installation requirements. NFPA 99 provides technical requirements relevant to medical gas systems.

Mathematics for Plumbers and Pipefitters supports calculation practice, while OSHA 29 CFR Part 1926 addresses construction safety.

The 2026 Florida Building Code editions listed for this course belong to the Ninth Edition code cycle, scheduled to take effect December 31, 2026.

For the plumbing trade examination, the published reference schedule identifies July 1, 2027, as the date when the 2026 Florida Building Code editions become the examination basis. The 2024 editions of NFPA 14 and NFPA 99 also become the examination basis on July 1, 2027.

Until that examination transition, the official reference list identifies the earlier code and NFPA editions as the examination basis. Candidates should prepare using the editions applicable to their examination date.

Florida's approved reference rules also address permitted bindings, notes, highlighting, and tabs. Familiarity with the authorized books and examination procedures is an important part of preparation.

State Requirements

The Florida Construction Industry Licensing Board, operating through the Department of Business and Professional Regulation, regulates certified plumbing contractors.

Florida plumbing contractors perform authorized work involving plumbing systems within the scope established by state law. Professional responsibilities can involve water distribution, sanitary drainage, venting, fixtures, piping connections, and related installations.

Plumbing work may also involve specialized systems such as fuel gas piping, medical gas, and other mechanical or water-related installations subject to applicable licensing and regulatory requirements.

Contractors must understand building codes, construction safety responsibilities, permits, professional qualifications, and the requirements associated with their authorized scope of work.

The Florida Building Code provides requirements for plumbing, mechanical, accessibility, residential, and fuel gas systems. These publications help establish technical expectations for construction under the applicable code cycle.

The program's listed 2026 code references support familiarity with the Ninth Edition Florida Building Code and its plumbing-related requirements.

    Addresses health care facility safety requirements and medical gas and vacuum system provisions relevant to plumbing trade preparation.

  • Florida Building Code - Residential, 2026
    Covers residential construction requirements, including provisions affecting plumbing systems in applicable residential buildings.
  • Florida Building Code - Fuel Gas, 2026
