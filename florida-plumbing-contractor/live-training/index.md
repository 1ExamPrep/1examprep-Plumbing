## Exam Details

The **Florida Certified Plumbing Contractor General Trade Knowledge Examination** is part of the contractor licensing program regulated by the Florida Construction Industry Licensing Board through the Department of Business and Professional Regulation.

Florida administers the plumbing trade examination in a paper-and-pencil format over two examination sessions.

- **Examination:** Florida Plumbing Contractor General Trade Knowledge
- **Licensing Authority:** Florida Construction Industry Licensing Board
- **Regulatory Department:** Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation
- **Examination Format:** Open book, paper and pencil
- **Morning Session:** 45 questions and 5 isometric drawings
- **Isometric Drawings:** 10 points each
- **Afternoon Session:** 65 questions
- **Scoring:** Morning and afternoon results are combined

The examination evaluates knowledge of plumbing systems, code interpretation, mathematics, installation methods, maintenance, workplace safety, and isometric drawings.

Its published content outline includes drainage, water distribution, natural gas piping, medical gas piping, and industrial piping. Other practical knowledge areas include reading plans, interpreting specifications, selecting equipment, and understanding installation techniques.

The examination requires candidates to connect field knowledge with mathematical calculations and technical references. A balanced preparation plan should address both conceptual questions and the practical skills used to interpret piping systems.

Florida contractor certification also includes a separate Business and Finance examination component. This program focuses on plumbing trade knowledge.
