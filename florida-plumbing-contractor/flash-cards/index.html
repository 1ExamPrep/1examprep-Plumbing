Exam Details

The Florida Plumbing Contractor General Trade Knowledge Examination evaluates technical knowledge associated with plumbing construction, system installation, piping materials, calculations, and specialized plumbing applications.

Florida's contractor certification program is overseen by the Department of Business and Professional Regulation and the Construction Industry Licensing Board.

  • Examination: Florida Plumbing Contractor General Trade Knowledge
  • Licensing Authority: Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation
  • Licensing Board: Construction Industry Licensing Board
  • Testing Format: Paper-and-pencil
  • Reference Format: Open book
  • Examination Components: Multiple-choice questions and plumbing isometric drawings
  • Additional Certification Examination: Business and Finance

The Plumbing General Trade Knowledge Examination includes 110 multiple-choice questions and five isometric drawings, with 160 possible points. Each drawing is worth up to ten points.

The published examination content outline covers drainage, water distribution, natural gas piping, medical gas piping, industrial piping, swimming pools, wells, irrigation, solar applications, and fire protection.

Flash cards support preparation for technical terminology and knowledge-based questions. Candidates must also develop their plumbing isometric drawing skills separately.

Open Book Test

The Florida Plumbing Contractor General Trade Knowledge Examination is an open-book test. Candidates may consult the reference publications authorized for their examination and scheduled testing period.

Open-book testing requires a combination of knowledge and effective reference navigation. Candidates need to recognize the topic, determine which publication applies, locate technical information, and use it correctly.

Flash cards help build the foundational knowledge that makes this process more efficient. Recognizing terms such as sanitary drainage, venting, water distribution, combustion air, and medical gas piping helps candidates identify the correct area for further research.

For example, questions about plumbing fixtures and drainage may involve the Florida Plumbing Code. Fuel gas questions may involve the Fuel Gas Code, while specialized healthcare systems may involve NFPA 99.

Students can combine flash-card review with reference-book study by answering a question from memory and then locating the related information in the appropriate publication.

Florida maintains official approved-reference lists for contractor examinations. Only authorized publications may be brought into the examination room.

State Requirements

Florida regulates Plumbing Contractors under Chapter 489 of the Florida Statutes.

The Plumbing Contractor classification includes installing, maintaining, repairing, altering, extending, and, where legally permitted, designing plumbing systems.

Authorized activities include sanitary and storm drainage, venting systems, water supply, septic tanks, irrigation, swimming pool piping, solar water heating, and related installations.

The classification also covers specified work involving natural gas, liquefied petroleum gas, medical gas, pressure piping, and specialized piping systems.

Contractors must work within their authorized scope and comply with applicable construction codes, safety requirements, and professional regulations.

State certification also requires satisfying applicable experience, examination, financial responsibility, insurance, and administrative qualifications.
