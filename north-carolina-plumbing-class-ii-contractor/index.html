Is the Plumbing Class II Contractor exam open book?

It depends on the exam. Many plumbing trade exams allow the code book, but some are closed book. Your candidate bulletin lists the references allowed; if books are allowed, highlighted and tabbed sets save the most time.

Who issues the Plumbing Class II Contractor license in North Carolina?

It comes from the North Carolina State Board of Examiners of Plumbing, Heating and Fire Sprinkler Contractors. Buying a package here does not register you or make you eligible; the board approves you first, and the exam vendor schedules the test.

Does the Plumbing Class II Contractor exam use the IPC or the UPC?

Each package names its code in the title. The 2026 journeyman study guides say "Based on 2024 IPC" or "Based on 2024 UPC"; book packages list the exact editions. Match it to the editions in your candidate bulletin.
