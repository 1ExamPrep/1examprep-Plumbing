## How does the Journeyman Plumber (County, GITS PBE1B) exam work?

The Journeyman Plumber (County, GITS PBE1B) license comes from the Florida Construction Industry Licensing Board (CILB). Pearson VUE gives the exam. The board approves your application first; then you schedule the exam and bring only the references the candidate bulletin allows.

- The Florida Construction Industry Licensing Board certifies plumbing contractors statewide; Pearson VUE gives the state trade exam.
- Florida journeyman and master plumber cards are issued by counties, which use Prov or GITS exams.
- Florida also certifies plumbing inspectors and plans examiners using ICC exams (1P, 2P and 3P).
