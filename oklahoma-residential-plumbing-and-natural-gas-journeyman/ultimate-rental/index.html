Exam Details

The Oklahoma Residential Journeyman Plumbing and Natural Gas examination evaluates knowledge of residential plumbing installations, fuel gas systems, piping, trade calculations, and construction safety.

The Oklahoma Construction Industries Board administers plumbing licensing, while PSI provides the examination.

  • Exam Name: Oklahoma Residential Journeyman Plumbing and Natural Gas
  • Licensing Authority: Oklahoma Construction Industries Board
  • Testing Provider: PSI
  • Number of Scored Questions: 85
  • Time Allowed: 180 minutes
  • Passing Score: 75%
  • Minimum Correct Answers: 64
  • Examination Format: Open book

The examination covers ten major technical categories:

  • General Plumbing Knowledge and Regulations: 10 questions.
  • Piping, Valves, and Control Valves: 8 questions.
  • Fixtures and Equipment: 8 questions.
  • Water Supply: 10 questions.
  • Drains and Sewers: 10 questions.
  • Vents: 13 questions.
  • Traps, Interceptors, Indirect, and Special Waste: 5 questions.
  • Isometric Analysis: 7 questions.
  • Fuel Gas: 10 questions.
  • Safety: 4 questions.

Venting is the largest individual examination category. General plumbing knowledge, water supply, drains and sewers, and fuel gas also represent important portions of the test.

Candidates should understand how residential plumbing systems operate, recognize common installation requirements, and practice interpreting plumbing diagrams, mathematical formulas, and technical code tables.

With 180 minutes available for 85 scored questions, candidates have approximately two minutes and seven seconds per question. Familiarity with technical references and regular examination preparation can help support efficient time management.

Open Book Test

The Oklahoma Residential Journeyman Plumbing and Natural Gas examination is an open-book test. Candidates may consult approved examination references according to PSI testing requirements.

Open-book examinations require both technical understanding and effective reference navigation. Candidates must recognize the subject being tested, locate the appropriate information, and interpret applicable requirements within the examination time limit.

The International Residential Code addresses residential plumbing and fuel gas systems, including water distribution, sanitary drainage, venting, fixtures, piping materials, and natural gas installations.

Mathematics for Plumbers and Pipefitters supports preparation involving measurements, geometry, pipe offsets, and practical plumbing calculations. OSHA construction regulations provide important information involving workplace safety requirements.

Oklahoma adopted the 2024 International Residential Code with state amendments effective September 14, 2026. The Oklahoma Construction Industries Board established an examination transition period from September 14 through December 14, 2026.

During this transition period, candidates taking affected examinations may test using either the previously adopted or newly adopted code. Beginning December 15, 2026, affected examinations are based exclusively on the newly adopted codes.

Approved examination references may be highlighted, underlined, and indexed according to PSI rules. Permanent reference tabs are permitted, while temporary removable tabs and unauthorized additional papers are not allowed.

State Requirements

Residential plumbing and natural gas work in Oklahoma is regulated under the state's plumbing licensing laws and administrative regulations. The Oklahoma Construction Industries Board oversees licensing qualifications and related professional requirements.

Residential plumbing work involves installation, maintenance, repair, and alteration of plumbing systems within the scope of the applicable journeyman license.

Important technical responsibilities include water distribution, sanitary drainage, venting, plumbing fixtures, piping materials, fuel gas systems, and appropriate installation methods.

The International Residential Code provides technical requirements for qualifying residential buildings, including one- and two-family dwellings and townhouses within its scope.

Oklahoma applies state amendments to its adopted construction codes. Familiarity with the relevant code requirements supports professional understanding of residential plumbing installations.

Journeyman licensing differs from contractor licensing. A residential journeyman performs authorized plumbing work within Oklahoma's licensing framework, while independently operating a plumbing contracting business requires the appropriate contractor authorization.

Construction safety is another important professional responsibility. Plumbing professionals may encounter excavation hazards, ladders, power tools, equipment, and other workplace conditions covered by OSHA construction safety regulations.
