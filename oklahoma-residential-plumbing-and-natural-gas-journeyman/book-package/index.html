Exam Details

The Oklahoma Residential Journeyman Plumbing and Natural Gas and Residential Contractor Plumbing and Natural Gas examinations address technical knowledge associated with residential plumbing installations and natural gas systems.

The Oklahoma Construction Industries Board administers plumbing licensing, while PSI provides the applicable licensing examinations.

These are separate examination classifications. Journeyman licensing concerns qualified trade work under an appropriately licensed contractor, while contractor licensing involves additional responsibilities associated with operating a plumbing contracting business.

Important technical examination subjects include:

  • General Plumbing Knowledge: Plumbing terminology, system components, code provisions, and installation principles.
  • Water Supply Systems: Water distribution, piping materials, valves, pressure, and fixture connections.
  • Sanitary Drainage: Drainage piping, slopes, cleanouts, and residential sewer connections.
  • Venting Systems: Vent sizing, vent connections, system layouts, and installation requirements.
  • Plumbing Fixtures: Residential fixture installations, equipment connections, and related requirements.
  • Natural Gas Systems: Fuel gas piping, pressure regulation, pipe sizing, appliance connections, and testing.
  • Trade Mathematics: Measurements, calculations, geometry, conversions, and piping applications.
  • Construction Safety: Workplace protection, equipment safety, and applicable construction regulations.

Successful preparation involves understanding how plumbing systems operate, interpreting technical requirements, and identifying relevant information in examination references.

Candidates should develop familiarity with code tables, plumbing diagrams, mathematical formulas, and common residential installation situations.

Open Book Test

The Oklahoma residential plumbing and natural gas licensing examinations are open-book tests. Candidates may use examination-approved reference materials according to the applicable PSI testing rules.

Open-book testing requires both technical understanding and efficient reference navigation. Knowing where information appears in a codebook can be valuable, but candidates must also understand how to interpret requirements and apply them to examination questions.

The International Residential Code provides important provisions involving residential water supply, drainage, venting, fuel gas piping, and plumbing fixtures. Becoming familiar with its chapter organization, indexes, technical tables, and diagrams supports examination preparation.

The Oklahoma Uniform Building Code Commission adopted the 2024 International Residential Code with applicable state amendments effective September 14, 2026.

The Oklahoma Construction Industries Board established an examination transition period for affected residential plumbing and natural gas examinations from September 14 through December 14, 2026. During this period, candidates may take the affected examinations using either the previously adopted or newly adopted code editions.

Beginning December 15, 2026, affected examinations will use the newly adopted codes exclusively.

Examination-room references and permitted markings are governed by PSI rules. The books included in this package are study resources; candidates should distinguish between useful preparation materials and the specific references permitted for their selected examination.

Regular practice locating code sections, reading tables, and solving technical problems helps develop the reference familiarity needed for an open-book examination.

State Requirements

Plumbing and natural gas work in Oklahoma is regulated through the state's plumbing licensing laws and industry regulations. The Oklahoma Construction Industries Board oversees licensing and related professional requirements.

Residential plumbing work involves the installation, maintenance, alteration, and repair of plumbing systems within the scope of the applicable license.

Residential systems include water distribution piping, sanitary drainage, venting, plumbing fixtures, water heaters, and associated components. Natural gas work involves piping materials, equipment connections, pressure regulation, and installation safety.

The International Residential Code addresses construction provisions applicable to qualifying residential buildings, including one- and two-family dwellings and townhouses within its scope.

Oklahoma's adopted code requirements incorporate applicable state amendments, making code familiarity important for plumbing professionals preparing to work within the state's regulated construction industry.

Journeyman and contractor licensing are distinct. A licensed plumbing journeyman performs regulated work under a licensed plumbing contractor, while contractor licensing carries additional business and professional responsibilities.

Construction safety is also essential. Residential plumbing professionals may encounter excavation hazards, ladders, power tools, heavy materials, and other conditions addressed by OSHA construction safety standards.
