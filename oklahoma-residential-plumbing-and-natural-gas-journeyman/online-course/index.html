Exam Details

The Oklahoma Residential Journeyman Plumbing and Natural Gas examination evaluates technical knowledge involving residential plumbing systems, fuel gas installations, trade calculations, and construction safety.

The Oklahoma Construction Industries Board administers plumbing licensing, and PSI provides the applicable licensing examination.

  • Exam Name: Oklahoma Residential Journeyman Plumbing and Natural Gas
  • Licensing Authority: Oklahoma Construction Industries Board
  • Testing Provider: PSI
  • Number of Scored Questions: 85
  • Time Allowed: 180 minutes
  • Passing Score: 75%
  • Minimum Correct Answers: 64
  • Exam Format: Open book

The examination covers important technical areas associated with residential plumbing and natural gas work, including:

  • General Plumbing Knowledge and Regulations: Plumbing terminology, definitions, installation principles, and code requirements.
  • Piping and Valves: Piping materials, fittings, valves, and related installation practices.
  • Fixtures and Equipment: Residential plumbing fixtures, water heaters, and equipment connections.
  • Water Supply: Water distribution, piping sizes, pressure, and fixture supply systems.
  • Drains and Sewers: Drainage piping, slopes, cleanouts, building drains, and sewer connections.
  • Venting: Vent sizing, vent connections, and plumbing drainage protection.
  • Traps and Special Waste: Traps, interceptors, indirect waste, and related provisions.
  • Isometric Analysis: Plumbing drawings, piping layouts, and technical interpretation.
  • Fuel Gas: Gas piping, pipe sizing, appliance connections, and system safety.
  • Construction Safety: Workplace hazards, protective equipment, and applicable safety regulations.

These subjects combine technical code knowledge with practical plumbing experience. Candidates should understand how residential systems function, recognize common installation requirements, and become comfortable interpreting plumbing diagrams and calculations.

With 180 minutes available for 85 scored questions, candidates have approximately two minutes and seven seconds per question. Familiarity with examination references and consistent study habits can support effective time management.

Open-book examinations require technical understanding and efficient reference navigation. Candidates need to identify the subject of a question, select the appropriate reference, and interpret relevant information within the examination time limit.

The International Residential Code is an important resource for residential plumbing and natural gas requirements. Candidates should become familiar with its plumbing chapters, fuel gas provisions, sizing tables, technical definitions, and indexes.

For the Oklahoma Residential Journeyman Plumbing and Natural Gas examination, the code transition period runs from September 14 through December 14, 2026. During this period, affected candidates may test using either the previously adopted or newly adopted code edition.
