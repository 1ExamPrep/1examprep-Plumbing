Open Book Test

The Oklahoma Residential Plumbing and Natural Gas Journeyman Exam is an open book test. Candidates are responsible for bringing their own approved references to the examination center. For code questions, the examination is based on the specific code edition and references listed for the exam.

Reference materials may be highlighted, underlined, and indexed before the examination session. References may not be written in before or during the examination. Candidates may use permanent tabs only. Temporary tabs, including Post-it style notes, are not allowed and must be removed before the exam begins. Candidates may not bring additional loose papers with approved references.

For downloaded references, the material must be spiral bound, hole-punched and placed in a binder, or stapled in the left-hand corner. Candidates may use a silent, non-printing, non-programmable calculator in the examination center, and a magnifying glass may be provided upon request.

Because this is an open book examination, preparation should include reference navigation. Licensing Steps

Oklahoma residential plumbing and natural gas journeyman licensing is handled through the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board. Candidates applying for a Residential Plumbing and Natural Gas Journeyman license must meet the state’s eligibility requirements, submit the required application materials, receive approval to test, schedule the required examination through PSI, and complete the remaining licensing requirements after passing.

The licensing process begins with confirming that the applicant is pursuing the correct plumbing license category. Residential Plumbing and Natural Gas Journeyman licensing is different from contractor-level licensing, Plumbing and Natural Gas Contractor licensing, plumbing-only classifications, natural gas-only classifications, and other trade licenses. Candidates should match the license category to the type of work they intend to perform before applying.

Applicants submit the required application and supporting documentation to the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board. The Board reviews the application and determines whether the candidate is eligible for examination. Once approved, the candidate schedules the PSI examination and prepares using the listed references.

Passing the Residential Plumbing and Natural Gas Journeyman exam is an important step toward licensure. After licensure, the license holder may perform work within the authorized scope of the journeyman classification while working under the proper contractor category. Journeyman-level authority is different from contractor-level authority because a journeyman license is tied to qualified trade work, while contractor licensing is tied to business responsibility and contracting authority.

Maintaining license compliance is an ongoing responsibility. Residential plumbing and natural gas journeymen should keep their license in good standing, follow Oklahoma renewal requirements, work within the correct license classification, and understand the limits of journeyman-level authority. Candidates should review current Oklahoma Construction Industries Board application materials before applying so they understand documentation, examination, and work-scope requirements.
