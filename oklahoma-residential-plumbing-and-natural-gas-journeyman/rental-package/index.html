Exam Details

The Oklahoma Residential Journeyman Plumbing and Natural Gas examination evaluates knowledge of residential plumbing installations, fuel gas systems, construction mathematics, and workplace safety.

The Oklahoma Construction Industries Board oversees plumbing licensing, and PSI administers the examination.

  • Exam Name: Oklahoma Residential Journeyman Plumbing and Natural Gas
  • Licensing Authority: Oklahoma Construction Industries Board
  • Testing Provider: PSI
  • Number of Scored Questions: 85
  • Time Allowed: 180 minutes
  • Passing Score: 75%
  • Minimum Correct Answers: 64
  • Exam Format: Open book

The examination addresses several important technical areas:

  • General Plumbing Knowledge: Plumbing definitions, code provisions, installation principles, and calculations.
  • Piping and Valves: Pipe connections, materials, fittings, and valve applications.
  • Fixtures and Equipment: Plumbing fixture requirements and residential equipment installations.
  • Water Supply: Water distribution systems, pressure, sizing, and supply piping.
  • Drainage and Sewers: Sanitary drainage systems, slopes, cleanouts, and building drains.
  • Venting: Vent piping, sizing, connections, and drainage system protection.
  • Traps and Special Waste: Trap requirements, interceptors, and indirect waste provisions.
  • Isometric Analysis: Piping layouts, measurements, drawings, and calculations.
  • Fuel Gas: Natural gas piping, appliance connections, sizing, and testing.
  • Safety: Construction workplace hazards and protective requirements.

Effective preparation requires both technical understanding and familiarity with reference materials. Candidates should practice interpreting residential code provisions, completing calculations, and identifying information in technical tables.

State Requirements

Residential plumbing and natural gas work in Oklahoma is regulated through the state's plumbing licensing framework. The Oklahoma Construction Industries Board oversees applicable licensing requirements and professional regulations.

Residential plumbing work includes installation, repair, alteration, and maintenance of authorized plumbing systems serving residential buildings.

Important technical responsibilities involve water distribution, sanitary drainage, venting, plumbing fixtures, piping materials, and fuel gas installations.

Natural gas systems require additional knowledge of piping materials, regulators, valves, appliance connections, pressure testing, and safe installation practices.

The International Residential Code establishes construction requirements for qualifying residential buildings, including relevant plumbing and fuel gas provisions.

Journeyman licensing differs from contractor licensing. Journeymen perform regulated plumbing work within Oklahoma's licensing framework, while independent contracting requires the applicable contractor authorization.
