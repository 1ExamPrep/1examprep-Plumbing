Exam Details

The Utah Plumbing Apprentice Test Out 2A examination is part of the Utah Plumbing & Heating Contractors Association Apprentice Test-Out Exam Program and is administered through Prov.

The Test Out 2A examination contains 60 questions. Candidates have 3 hours to complete the examination.

The examination is identified as the Utah Plumbing Competency Exam – 2A. The published learning objectives are based on plumbing codes, standards, mathematics, and trade knowledge associated with the applicable apprenticeship curriculum.

The official Test Out 2A learning objectives identify the following chapters of the 2018 International Plumbing Code:

  • Chapter 6
  • Chapter 7
  • Chapter 8
  • Chapter 9
  • Chapter 10

These chapters cover major plumbing-system subjects including water supply and distribution, sanitary drainage, indirect and special waste, vents, traps, interceptors, and separators.

The published Test Out 2A learning objectives also identify Onsite Waste Water Systems, including Utah Administrative Code Rule 317-4 Sections 1 through 6, along with related plumbing trade and mathematics subjects.

With 60 questions and three hours available, candidates have an average of approximately three minutes per question. Efficient code navigation can help preserve time for calculation-based questions and more detailed technical lookups.

Open Book Test

The Utah Plumbing Apprentice Test Out 2A examination is an open book test. Prov identifies specific reference materials that may be used during testing.

Candidates are encouraged to prepare their approved testing references in advance by highlighting important text and using permanent tabs on important pages. This preparation can make it easier to move between chapters, tables, definitions, and frequently used code sections.

A strong open-book strategy begins with identifying the subject of the question. A water-distribution question should immediately direct attention toward the appropriate plumbing code chapter. Sanitary drainage questions point toward drainage provisions, while venting questions should lead candidates toward the vent chapter.

Questions involving indirect waste, special waste, traps, interceptors, separators, or onsite wastewater require candidates to distinguish between related plumbing concepts and select the correct reference quickly.

Candidates should also become comfortable using Utah-specific amendments. Model codes establish the base requirements, while state amendments can modify how those provisions apply in Utah. Learning to check the applicable amendment after finding a model-code provision is a useful habit during preparation.

Permanent tabs should be organized by meaningful topics rather than placed on every page. Major IPC chapters, definitions, commonly used tables, and state amendments are practical locations for navigation tabs.

Licensing Steps

The Utah Plumbing Apprentice Test Out 2A examination is an apprenticeship competency examination. It is not the final Utah Journeyman Plumber licensing examination. Its purpose is to allow qualifying apprenticeship students to demonstrate existing knowledge of specified curriculum material.

  1. Enter or plan to enter a Utah plumbing apprenticeship program. The apprentice test-out program is intended for individuals pursuing plumbing apprenticeship education in Utah.
  2. Meet the Test Out program eligibility requirements. Candidates must be enrolled in or planning to enroll in an eligible plumbing apprentice program.
  3. Maintain apprentice plumbing registration or licensure as applicable. Utah regulates apprentice plumbers through the Division of Professional Licensing.
  4. Prepare for Test Out 2A. Study water supply, drainage, waste, venting, traps, onsite wastewater, mathematics, and the related plumbing codes.
  5. Become familiar with the applicable references. Practice navigating the International Plumbing Code, International Mechanical Code, International Fuel Gas Code, and Utah amendments.
  6. Register for the examination. Follow the current Prov process for the Utah Plumbing Apprentice Test-Out Exam Program.
  7. Complete the Test Out 2A examination. Candidates answer 60 questions within the three-hour testing period.
  8. Continue through apprenticeship education. Successful completion of an eligible test-out exam allows the qualifying student to bypass the corresponding coursework.
  9. Continue accumulating required plumbing experience. Apprentices must satisfy Utah's applicable training and experience requirements on the path toward journeyman licensure.
  10. Complete journeyman licensing requirements when eligible. Utah requires qualified applicants to complete the applicable theory and practical examinations and other licensing requirements.

State Requirements

The Utah Plumbing Apprentice Test-Out Exam Program is intended for candidates who are enrolled in or planning to enroll in plumbing apprenticeship education in Utah. The program provides a way for candidates who already understand designated apprenticeship material to demonstrate competency through testing.

Passing Test Out 2A does not issue a Utah plumbing license. It addresses a portion of apprenticeship education and allows an eligible candidate to move past the associated coursework within the participating program.

Utah apprentice plumbers are regulated through the Utah Division of Professional Licensing. Apprentices continue accumulating supervised plumbing experience while completing the education and training required for later journeyman qualification.

Journeyman plumber licensure is a separate process. Utah provides qualifying pathways involving apprenticeship training, classroom education, documented plumbing experience, and successful completion of required journeyman examinations.

Students preparing for Test Out 2A should therefore view the examination as one step in a broader professional training path. Strong knowledge of water supply, drainage, venting, waste systems, traps, plumbing mathematics, and code navigation will also support later stages of apprenticeship education.

Test Information and Study Materials

A productive Test Out 2A study plan should begin with Chapter 6 of the 2018 International Plumbing Code, which addresses water supply and distribution. Candidates should review water service, distribution systems, piping, valves, pressure considerations, materials, sizing concepts, and installation requirements.

Water-supply questions can combine code lookup with practical plumbing knowledge. Understanding how water moves through a building's distribution system makes the detailed code provisions easier to interpret.

Chapter 7 covers sanitary drainage. Candidates should review drainage piping, sizing concepts, fittings, cleanouts, building drains, building sewers, changes in direction, and other requirements associated with moving wastewater through a plumbing system.

Chapter 8 addresses indirect and special waste. Study how indirect waste differs from directly connected drainage, where air gaps or air breaks may be required, and how specialized waste connections are handled.

Chapter 9 focuses on vents. Venting is a major plumbing concept because drainage and vent systems work together. Candidates should understand vent purposes, connections, sizing concepts, permitted arrangements, and terminology used throughout the venting provisions.

Chapter 10 covers traps, interceptors, and separators. Review trap requirements, prohibited arrangements, trap seals, interceptors, separators, and related installation concepts.

Test Out 2A preparation also includes onsite wastewater systems. Candidates should review the identified Utah requirements and understand the general relationship between building plumbing, sewer connections, and onsite wastewater disposal.

Mathematics should remain part of the study routine. Plumbing apprentices should be comfortable working with measurements, dimensions, pipe calculations, grade, offsets, and other math encountered in plumbing installation and code problems.

Code study should not be limited to reading. Select a topic and practice locating it without already knowing the page number. Start with the table of contents or index, find the relevant chapter and section, and then identify any related exceptions or Utah amendments.

For example, when practicing a venting question, first identify that it belongs to Chapter 9. Then locate the specific vent type or requirement. With repeated practice, candidates can reduce the amount of time needed to identify the correct portion of the code.

Test Information and Study Materials (continued)

Likewise, a sanitary drainage question should lead directly toward Chapter 7, while a water-distribution question should suggest Chapter 6. Developing these topic-to-chapter connections is one of the most useful ways to prepare for an open-book examination.

Candidates should also distinguish between a code-navigation error and a plumbing-knowledge error. If a question is missed because the wrong chapter was selected, additional navigation practice may help. If the correct provision was located but misunderstood, more technical study may be needed.

Timed practice is useful as the examination approaches. Three hours for 60 questions may appear generous, but difficult calculations and technical searches can consume time quickly. Straightforward questions should be handled efficiently so additional time remains for more complex problems.
