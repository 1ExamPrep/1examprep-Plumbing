Exam Details

The Reading Pennsylvania journeyman plumber licensing process uses the International Code Council (ICC) G25-N Journeyman Plumber Exam. This exam is associated with the National Standard Journeyman Plumber exam and is used to evaluate journeyman-level plumbing code knowledge. Candidates should be prepared for multiple-choice questions that require them to locate, understand, and apply information from the plumbing code.

The G25 National Standard Journeyman Plumber exam is listed as a 70-question exam with a 3-hour time limit. Open Book Test

The ICC G25-N Journeyman Plumber exam is an open book test. Open book testing allows candidates to use approved reference material during the exam, but it does not make the exam easy. The time limit still requires candidates to move through questions efficiently, identify the correct code section, and avoid spending too much time searching for basic information.

For an open book plumbing exam, the code book becomes part of the testing strategy. Candidates should study the International Plumbing Code, 2018 before exam day, not just bring it to the testing center. Important preparation habits include reviewing the table of contents, practicing with the index, learning where major plumbing topics are located, and becoming comfortable with definitions and tables. Candidates should also understand how chapters relate to one another, especially when a question requires knowledge of drainage, venting, fixtures, and water supply rules together.

Tabs and highlighting can be useful study tools when they comply with current exam rules. Candidates should review the latest ICC testing instructions before exam day to make sure their book preparation follows the allowed format. The best open book preparation combines code familiarity, organized review, and repeated practice locating answers under timed conditions.

Licensing Steps

Reading, Pennsylvania has local plumbing trade license requirements for journeyman plumbers. Applicants for the journeyman plumber license must meet the City of Reading’s requirements and pass the ICC G25-N Journeyman Plumber Exam. Passing exam results must be submitted with the license application.

A practical licensing path begins with reviewing the City of Reading plumbing trade license application requirements. Candidates should confirm that they meet the experience or apprenticeship requirements for the journeyman plumber license classification. Reading’s journeyman plumber requirements include completion of an approved apprenticeship training program or qualifying apprenticeship service under the supervision of a registered master plumber, along with successful completion of the ICC G25-N Journeyman Plumber Exam.

After confirming eligibility, candidates should obtain the required reference book, study the International Plumbing Code, 2018, schedule the ICC G25-N exam, and prepare using a structured code-based study plan. Once the exam is passed, the candidate should submit the completed Reading plumbing trade license application with proof of passing exam results and any required supporting documentation. Applicants should keep copies of their exam results, apprenticeship records, work experience documents, and submitted application materials.

Test Information and Study Materials

The International Plumbing Code, 2018 should be the center of your study plan for the Reading Pennsylvania Journeyman Plumber (ICC - G25-N) exam. Candidates should begin by learning how the book is organized. Reviewing the table of contents, chapter headings, definitions, and index helps create a roadmap for future study. Since the exam is open book, knowing how to use the reference quickly is just as important as understanding the trade concepts.

General regulations introduce the structure and application of the plumbing code. Plumbing fixtures include requirements related to fixture installation, fixture counts, accessibility, and related plumbing connections. Water heaters require attention to code provisions related to installation, safety, piping, and associated plumbing requirements. Water supply and distribution topics may involve potable water systems, backflow protection, pipe sizing concepts, materials, and installation requirements.

Sanitary drainage and venting are especially important for journeyman plumber candidates. These sections require careful review because drainage and vent systems often involve multiple connected rules. Candidates should study drainage piping, cleanouts, traps, vent connections, vent sizing concepts, and the relationship between fixtures and drainage systems. Traps, interceptors, separators, and storm drainage should also be reviewed because these topics include specific code definitions and installation requirements.

Plan reading is another important part of preparation. Candidates should practice applying code requirements to drawings, layouts, and practical plumbing scenarios. Plan-based questions may require the candidate to interpret a plumbing layout, identify system components, and determine how code rules apply. Studying the code book alongside plumbing diagrams or practice questions can help strengthen this skill.

A strong study routine should include short, consistent review sessions. Candidates should not wait until the final days before the exam to begin using the book. Repeated practice helps build speed and reduces stress during testing. Candidates should practice locating information, reviewing tables, reading definitions carefully, and recognizing when a code exception changes the answer. Open book success depends on preparation, organization, and familiarity with the approved reference material.
