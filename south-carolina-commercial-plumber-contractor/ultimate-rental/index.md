## State Requirements

South Carolina regulates commercial general and mechanical contracting through the South Carolina Contractor’s Licensing Board. Commercial plumbing falls within the mechanical contractor licensing structure.

Candidates pursuing the commercial Plumbing classification must complete the applicable qualification process. The examination route includes the required technical examination and the South Carolina Business Management and Law for Commercial Contractors examination.

The qualifying individual and the contractor business have related responsibilities. The qualifying party demonstrates the technical qualification connected to the requested classification, while the contractor business must satisfy the organizational, financial, and application requirements associated with commercial contractor licensure.

Applicants should be prepared to document relevant work experience and provide the information required for the requested contractor license group. License groups may affect the financial or bonding documentation associated with the application.

Commercial and residential plumbing licensing are separate pathways. This package is intended for candidates pursuing the South Carolina commercial contractor Plumbing classification and should not be treated as a residential plumbing licensing package.

The included Application Service supports the contractor application process, but applicants remain responsible for providing accurate, complete, and timely information. Final approval and license issuance remain under the authority of the applicable South Carolina licensing agency.
