## State Requirements

South Carolina regulates commercial general and mechanical contractors through the South Carolina Contractor’s Licensing Board. Commercial plumbing falls within the mechanical contractor licensing structure.

Candidates pursuing the commercial Plumbing classification must complete the applicable qualification process. The examination route includes the Commercial Plumber Contractor technical exam and the South Carolina Business Management and Law for Commercial Contractors exam.

The qualifying individual and the contractor business have related but separate responsibilities. The qualifying party demonstrates the trade qualification connected to the license classification, while the business must satisfy the organizational, financial, and application requirements associated with commercial contractor licensure.

Applicants should be prepared to provide the experience documentation, business information, financial information, and other materials required for the requested license classification and group.

Commercial and residential plumbing licenses follow different pathways. This package is developed for candidates pursuing the South Carolina commercial contractor Plumbing classification.

The included Application Service supports the licensing process, but applicants remain responsible for providing accurate information and completing any required actions. Final approval remains under the authority of the applicable South Carolina licensing agency.

Business Formation creates the legal entity selected for the contracting business. Forming an LLC or Corporation does not replace contractor licensing, examination, insurance, tax, local registration, or other compliance responsibilities.

EIN Filing provides the federal identification number commonly used for business banking, tax administration, payroll, and other business functions. The EIN does not serve as a contractor license.
