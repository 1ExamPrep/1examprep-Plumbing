## How does the Commercial Plumber Contractor exam work?

The Commercial Plumber Contractor license comes from the South Carolina Contractor's Licensing Board and Residential Builders Commission (LLR). The board approves your application first; then you schedule the exam and bring only the references the candidate bulletin allows.

- South Carolina licenses commercial plumbing contractors through the Contractor's Licensing Board and residential plumbers through the Residential Builders Commission.
- Both boards sit within the Department of Labor, Licensing and Regulation.
