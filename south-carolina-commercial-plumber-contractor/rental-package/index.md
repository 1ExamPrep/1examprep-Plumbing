## State Requirements

The South Carolina Contractor’s Licensing Board regulates general and mechanical contractors performing commercial construction. Commercial plumbing is regulated within the mechanical contractor licensing structure.

All individuals seeking qualifying-party certification must pass the South Carolina Business Management and Law for Commercial Contractors exam. Applicants using the examination pathway must also pass the appropriate technical examination before submitting the qualifying-party application.

The current qualifying-party instructions require examination applicants to document at least two years of full-time work experience performed within the previous five years in the classification requested. Experience should primarily reflect commercial work and must be documented using the Board’s current requirements.

The qualifying party demonstrates the technical qualifications associated with the contractor classification. The contractor business must separately satisfy the applicable business, financial, organizational, and licensing requirements.

Commercial and residential plumbing licensing pathways are different. This package is developed for the South Carolina commercial contractor process and the Commercial Plumber Contractor technical exam.

## Test Information and Study Materials

The package combines two types of preparation: guided online instruction and direct reference-book practice. Students should avoid treating these as separate resources. The strongest approach is to keep the highlighted and tabbed books open while completing the related course sections.

For business exam preparation, learn how the NASCLA contractor guide is organized. Review the major sections covering business startup, contractor operations, project management, and administrative responsibilities. Practice using the index and tabs to locate topics rather than attempting to memorize the entire book.

For commercial plumbing preparation, begin with the definitions and organization of the International Plumbing Code. Review the principal chapters for fixtures, water systems, drainage, venting, and related system requirements. Pay careful attention to tables, exceptions, and notes.

Use the International Fuel Gas Code to study fuel gas piping, appliance requirements, combustion air, and venting. Practice identifying the information needed before selecting a sizing table or code provision.

Work through the mathematics reference with a calculator and paper. Show each step, label units, and review mistakes to determine whether the problem involved arithmetic, measurement conversion, formula selection, or question interpretation.

Study the South Carolina 811 Excavator Manual as a sequence of responsibilities. Understand what happens before excavation, how markings are interpreted, what precautions apply near underground facilities, and which actions are required if a facility is disturbed or damaged.
