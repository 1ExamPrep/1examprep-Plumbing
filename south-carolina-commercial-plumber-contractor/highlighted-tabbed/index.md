## State Requirements

The South Carolina Contractor’s Licensing Board regulates general and mechanical contractors engaged in commercial construction. Commercial plumbing is included within the mechanical contractor licensing structure.

A contractor applicant must designate a qualifying party for the classification requested. The Primary Qualifying Party is the individual principally responsible for directing or reviewing work performed by the licensed business within that classification. Qualifying-party certification and contractor business licensure are connected parts of the licensing process.

Individuals applying through examination must pass the appropriate technical exam before submitting the qualifying-party application. South Carolina also requires the Business Management and Law for Commercial Contractors examination before an individual requests qualifying-party certification.

Applicants using the examination pathway must provide proof of at least two years of full-time experience completed within the previous five years in the classification being requested. Experience documentation should demonstrate work relevant to commercial plumbing and should be submitted in the form required by the Board.

The contractor business must also satisfy the application requirements for its selected license group. Group limits affect the size of projects the contractor may undertake and may determine the financial statement, working-capital, net-worth, or surety-bond documents required with the application.

Commercial and residential plumbing licenses are not the same. This product is prepared for the South Carolina Commercial Plumber Contractor exam and is not presented as a residential plumbing exam package.
