Exam Details

The South Carolina Residential Plumbing technical exam is part of the licensing process for applicants seeking a Residential Plumber license through the South Carolina Residential Builders Commission. The exam is administered through PSI after the applicant has been approved for examination eligibility by the Commission.

The Residential Plumbing technical exam contains 65 multiple-choice questions. Applicants must obtain a passing score of 67.7% on the technical examination. The exam is designed to measure minimum knowledge needed to perform residential plumbing work for the public in a competent and responsible manner.

Residential plumbing exam content may include plumbing fixtures, water supply and distribution, sanitary drainage, venting, traps, cleanouts, materials, piping methods, equipment, system testing, code definitions, safety, and related residential plumbing trade knowledge. Students should be ready to use the code books to locate requirements and apply them correctly to exam questions.

South Carolina residential specialty contractor applicants are also required to pass the Business Management and Law for Residential Builders examination unless their specific licensing path provides otherwise. That exam is separate from the Residential Plumbing technical exam and focuses on business management, licensing laws, contracts, project management, estimating, financial management, taxes, liens, insurance, bonding, labor requirements, environmental rules, and related contractor responsibilities.

This product is a highlighted and tabbed book package for the Residential Plumbing technical exam references listed in this page. Students preparing for the full licensing process should also prepare for the business and law requirement when it applies to their application path.

Open Book Test

The South Carolina Residential Plumbing technical exam is an open book test. Candidates may use permitted reference books during the exam. Books may be highlighted, underlined, and indexed. Permanent tabs are allowed. Books must not contain loose papers, removable sticky notes, extra attached pages, or unapproved notes.

This package is highlighted and tabbed to support open-book exam preparation. Highlights can help draw attention to important sections, while tabs can help students move quickly to major chapters, topics, and reference areas. Students should still practice using the books before exam day so the highlighted and tabbed layout becomes familiar.

An open-book plumbing exam still requires strong preparation. Candidates may need to identify the correct book, locate a chapter, read a table, understand a definition, apply a plumbing rule, or choose between similar answer options. The books are useful only when students know how to use them under exam conditions.

Students should practice moving between the International Plumbing Code, the International Residential Code, and OSHA 29 CFR Part 1926. Plumbing system questions may point to the plumbing code. Residential dwelling questions may point to the residential code. Safety questions may point to OSHA. Learning when to use each book is a major part of open-book exam readiness.

Licensing Steps

South Carolina Residential Plumber applicants must apply through the South Carolina Residential Builders Commission. Applicants seeking licensure by examination must submit the required application materials, document qualifying experience, and receive approval before scheduling the required examination through PSI.

Once the Commission approves an applicant for testing, the applicant receives examination eligibility and may schedule the required exam with PSI. Applicants should follow the Commission’s instructions for forms, supporting documentation, experience affidavits, fees, and exam eligibility requirements.

The standard examination path includes the Residential Plumbing technical exam and the Business Management and Law for Residential Builders exam. The technical exam measures residential plumbing trade knowledge. The business and law exam measures knowledge of contractor business practices, licensing responsibilities, contracts, project management, financial responsibilities, insurance, bonding, and legal obligations.

Some applicants may qualify for an examination waiver based on specific licenses or credentials recognized by South Carolina. Applicants who believe they may qualify for a waiver should follow the current Commission instructions and submit the required documentation for review.

After passing the required examination and meeting state licensing requirements, applicants must submit the required final documents and fees to the Commission. Depending on the scope of work and state requirements, a surety bond may be required for residential plumbing work that exceeds the applicable threshold.

State Requirements

The South Carolina Residential Builders Commission handles Residential Plumber licensing for residential plumbing work within the Commission’s jurisdiction. Individuals seeking licensure must meet the Commission’s application, experience, and examination requirements before a license can be issued.

Residential plumbing work may involve water supply systems, drainage systems, venting systems, plumbing fixtures, piping, equipment, materials, and related installations in residential structures. Candidates preparing for the license should be ready to demonstrate practical plumbing knowledge and the ability to use approved references during the open-book technical exam.

Applicants pursuing licensure by examination must receive Commission approval before scheduling exams through PSI. The Commission determines eligibility, and PSI administers the examinations. Students should prepare for both the technical trade exam and the business and law exam when both are required for their licensing path.

Residential plumbing candidates should also understand that licensing is not only about passing the exam. The state licensing process may involve application review, experience verification, examination results, final documentation, fees, and bonding requirements where applicable. Proper preparation helps students approach the process with a clearer understanding of the steps involved.
