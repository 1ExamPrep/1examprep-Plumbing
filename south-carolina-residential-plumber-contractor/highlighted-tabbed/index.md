## Licensing Steps

South Carolina Residential Plumber applicants must apply through the South Carolina Residential Builders Commission. Applicants seeking licensure by examination must submit the required application materials, document qualifying experience, and receive approval before scheduling the required examination through PSI.

Once the Commission approves an applicant for testing, the applicant receives examination eligibility and may schedule the required exam with PSI. Applicants should follow the Commission’s instructions for forms, supporting documentation, experience affidavits, fees, and exam eligibility requirements.

The standard examination path includes the Residential Plumbing technical exam and the Business Management and Law for Residential Builders exam. The technical exam measures residential plumbing trade knowledge. The business and law exam measures knowledge of contractor business practices, licensing responsibilities, contracts, project management, financial responsibilities, insurance, bonding, and legal obligations.

Some applicants may qualify for an examination waiver based on specific licenses or credentials recognized by South Carolina. Applicants who believe they may qualify for a waiver should follow the current Commission instructions and submit the required documentation for review.

After passing the required examination and meeting state licensing requirements, applicants must submit the required final documents and fees to the Commission. Depending on the scope of work and state requirements, a surety bond may be required for residential plumbing work that exceeds the applicable threshold.
