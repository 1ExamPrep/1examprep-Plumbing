Exam Details

The South Carolina Residential Plumbing technical exam is part of the licensing process for applicants seeking a Residential Plumber license through the South Carolina Residential Builders Commission. The examination is administered through PSI after the applicant has been approved for examination eligibility by the Commission.

The Residential Plumbing technical exam contains 80 questions. Candidates are allowed 240 minutes to complete the exam. A score of 52 correct answers, or 65%, is required to pass the technical portion.

The Residential Plumbing exam is designed to measure minimum knowledge required for residential plumbing work. Exam content may include plumbing fixtures, water supply and distribution, drainage systems, venting, traps, cleanouts, materials, equipment, code requirements, safety, and related residential plumbing trade knowledge.

South Carolina residential plumbing applicants are also required to pass the Business Management and Law for Residential Builders examination unless their individual licensing path provides otherwise. The business and law exam is separate from the Residential Plumbing technical exam and focuses on business management, licensing laws, contracts, project management, estimating, financial management, taxes, liens, insurance, bonding, labor requirements, environmental rules, and related contractor responsibilities.

This product is a book package for the Residential Plumbing technical exam references listed above. Students preparing for the full licensing process should also plan for the business and law requirement if it applies to their application path.

Open Book Test

The South Carolina Residential Plumbing technical exam is an open book test. Candidates may use permitted reference books during the exam. Books may be highlighted, underlined, and indexed, and permanent tabs are allowed. Books must not contain loose papers, removable sticky notes, extra attached pages, or unapproved notes.

Open book exams require preparation and organization. Students should practice finding information in the International Plumbing Code, the International Residential Code, and OSHA 29 CFR Part 1926 before exam day. Simply bringing books into the exam room is not enough. Candidates should know which reference is most likely to contain the answer for each type of question.

Plumbing code questions may require students to locate requirements for fixtures, piping, drainage, venting, water supply, traps, cleanouts, materials, or installation methods. Residential code questions may require students to understand how plumbing requirements connect to one- and two-family dwelling construction. Safety questions may require students to locate OSHA construction standards and apply them to jobsite conditions.

Students should become comfortable with indexes, definitions, chapter titles, tables, diagrams, and code organization. A strong open-book strategy includes repeated practice moving through the references quickly and accurately.

Licensing Steps

South Carolina Residential Plumber applicants must apply through the South Carolina Residential Builders Commission. Applicants seeking licensure by examination must submit the required application materials, document qualifying experience, and receive approval before scheduling the required examinations through PSI.

Once the Commission approves an applicant for testing, the applicant receives examination eligibility and may schedule the required exam with PSI. Examination eligibility is valid for one year, and candidates are allowed three attempts to pass the required examination within a 12-month period.

The standard examination path includes the Residential Plumbing technical exam and the Business Management and Law for Residential Builders exam. The technical exam measures residential plumbing trade knowledge, while the business and law exam measures knowledge of contractor business practices, state requirements, contracts, project management, financial responsibilities, and legal obligations.

Some applicants may qualify for an examination waiver based on specific licenses or credentials recognized by South Carolina. Applicants who believe they may qualify for a waiver should follow the current instructions from the South Carolina Residential Builders Commission and submit the required documentation for review.

After passing the required examination and meeting the state’s licensing requirements, applicants must submit the required final documents and fees to the Commission. For work that exceeds the applicable residential threshold, the state may require a surety bond as part of the licensing process.

State Requirements

The South Carolina Residential Builders Commission handles Residential Plumber licensing for residential plumbing work within the Commission’s jurisdiction. Individuals seeking licensure must meet the Commission’s application, experience, and examination requirements before a license can be issued.

Residential plumbing work may involve water supply systems, drainage systems, venting, fixtures, piping, equipment, and related plumbing installations for residential structures. Candidates preparing for the license should be ready to demonstrate practical plumbing knowledge, code understanding, and the ability to use approved references during the open-book exam.

Applicants pursuing licensure by examination must be approved before scheduling exams through PSI. The Commission determines eligibility, and PSI administers the examinations. Students should prepare for both the technical trade exam and the business and law exam when both are required for their licensing path.

Applicants should follow the current South Carolina Residential Builders Commission instructions for application forms, experience affidavits, eligibility approval, exam scheduling, fees, and required post-exam documentation.
