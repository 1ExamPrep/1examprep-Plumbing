## How does plumber licensing work in Georgia?

The Georgia State Construction Industry Licensing Board, Division of Master Plumbers and Journeyman Plumbers licenses plumbers here. The board approves your application first; then you schedule the exam and bring only the references the candidate bulletin allows.

- Georgia licenses master plumbers in Class I (Restricted) and Class II (Unrestricted), plus journeyman plumbers.
