## How does plumber licensing work in Idaho?

The Idaho Division of Occupational and Professional Licenses (DOPL), Plumbing Board licenses plumbers here. The board approves your application first; then you schedule the exam and bring only the references the candidate bulletin allows.

- Idaho licenses journeyman, contractor and specialty plumbers through the Plumbing Board at DOPL.
- Specialty licenses cover narrower work, such as appliance or water pump installation, with their own exams.
