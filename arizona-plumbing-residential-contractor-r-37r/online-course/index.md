## Exam Details

The Arizona Plumbing, Residential Contractor R-37R trade examination evaluates knowledge related to residential plumbing systems and associated construction practices. The qualifying party for the contractor license may be required to complete the trade examination as part of the Arizona licensing process unless an authorized waiver applies.

The examination contains 100 questions. Candidates are allowed 240 minutes to complete the test, and a minimum score of 70 percent is required to pass.

Exam subjects include drainage, waste and vent systems; indirect waste; water supply systems; backflow prevention; roof drains and storm drainage; plumbing fixtures; water heaters; accessibility; general regulations; specialty plumbing; interceptors and traps; isometric analysis; fuel gas; septic and sewer systems; fire sprinklers; and excavation.

Additional technical knowledge may come from the listed mathematics, pipe-trade, boiler, safety, utility-location, and hydronic heating references. Candidates should be prepared to work with formulas, code tables, definitions, diagrams, system layouts, sizing methods, installation rules, and accepted trade practices.

Questions may ask candidates to identify the correct material, determine a pipe size, select a required installation method, interpret a code provision, evaluate a drainage or vent arrangement, calculate a measurement, identify a safety requirement, or determine which sprinkler standard applies to a residential occupancy.

Provides requirements for automatic sprinkler systems, including system components, design, installation, piping, water supplies, inspection, and testing.
- **NFPA 13D - Installation of Sprinkler Systems in One- and Two-Family Dwellings and Manufactured Homes, 2019**
Addresses residential sprinkler systems for one- and two-family dwellings and manufactured homes.
- **NFPA 13R - Sprinkler Systems for Residential Occupancies up to and Including Four Stories in Height, 2019**
Covers qualifying residential sprinkler applications, including system design, components, installation, and water-supply requirements.
- **On-site Wastewater Treatment Facility Provisions in Unified Water Quality Permit Rule, Arizona Administrative Code Title 18, Chapter 9, 2006**
Contains Arizona provisions associated with onsite wastewater treatment facilities and related disposal systems.
- **Mathematics for Plumbers and Pipefitters, 8th Edition**

- **IPT’s Pipe Trades Handbook, Robert A. Lee, 2016**
Provides formulas, tables, fitting information, measurements, layout guidance, material data, and practical pipe-trade information.
- **Boiler Operator’s Guide, 5th Edition**
Explains boiler components, controls, combustion, operation, maintenance, water treatment, safety, and troubleshooting principles.
- **Arizona 811 - Arizona Blue Stake Brochure**
Provides Arizona safe-digging and underground utility-location guidance for excavation and trenching work.
- **Modern Hydronic Heating for Residential and Light Commercial Buildings, John Siegenthaler, P.E., 2022**
Covers hydronic system design, boilers, piping arrangements, heat emitters, circulators, controls, zoning, radiant heating, and system performance.
