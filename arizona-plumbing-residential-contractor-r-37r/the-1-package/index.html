Exam Details

The Arizona Registrar of Contractors administers contractor licensing in Arizona, and PSI administers the state’s trade-specific contractor examinations. Applicants pursuing the R-37R Plumbing, Residential classification must complete the examination and licensing requirements that apply to the proposed qualifying party and business entity.

The qualifying party is the individual whose trade experience, knowledge, skills, and examination qualifications support the contractor license. Examination requirements must be completed by the person who will serve as the qualifying party unless an applicable waiver is granted.

The R-37R trade examination focuses on knowledge associated with residential plumbing work. Preparation may include water supply and distribution, sanitary drainage, waste and vent systems, plumbing fixtures, fuel-gas piping, mechanical systems, fire sprinklers, hydronic heating, boilers, wastewater treatment, construction safety, utility-location practices, and plumbing calculations.

Questions may require direct code research, interpretation of technical requirements, practical trade knowledge, or mathematical problem-solving. Candidates may need to identify the correct pipe size, calculate an offset, determine a drainage or venting requirement, recognize a safe installation method, or select the correct standard for a residential sprinkler system.

Arizona contractor applicants may also be required to complete the Arizona Statutes and Rules Exam course. This online requirement is separate from the plumbing trade examination and addresses Arizona laws and rules affecting licensed contractors. An applicant’s specific examination requirements depend on the licensing path and any waiver approved by the Arizona Registrar of Contractors.

Passing the required examination does not automatically issue a contractor license. The completed examination requirements, business information, qualifying-party documentation, background-check records, bond, financial-protection requirements, and other application materials must be submitted for state review.

PSI offers computer-based trade examinations through approved test centers and may provide remote-proctored testing options. Candidates must follow the scheduling, identification, security, reference-material, and testing rules associated with the examination format selected.

Open Book Test

The Arizona R-37R Plumbing, Residential trade examination is an open-book test using approved reference materials. Candidates may consult permitted books during the examination, but the size and variety of the reference library make preparation and organization essential.

An open-book format does not eliminate the need for technical knowledge. Candidates must still understand plumbing principles, recognize applicable systems, complete calculations, interpret code language, and distinguish between similar requirements found in different references.

All included books are highlighted and Tabbed. Highlighting draws attention to useful code sections, definitions, formulas, tables, safety requirements, and installation provisions. Tabs help candidates move between major chapters and subject areas more efficiently.

Candidates should practice identifying the correct reference before beginning a search. Fuel-gas questions may require the International Fuel Gas Code, while water distribution, drainage, fixture, and venting questions may require the International Plumbing Code. Mechanical or hydronic-heating questions may involve the International Mechanical Code or Modern Hydronic Heating.

Residential fire-sprinkler questions may require NFPA 13, NFPA 13D, or NFPA 13R. Candidates should understand the purpose of each standard so they can select the correct book based on the building or system described in the question.

All reference materials remain subject to PSI’s current inspection and security rules. Loose papers, removable notes, unauthorized attachments, electronic devices, and unapproved resources are not allowed. Candidates should practice with the prepared books regularly so the tab locations and book organization become familiar before exam day.

State Requirements

The R-37R license is a residential specialty classification. It applies to plumbing work performed within the residential scope established by the Arizona Registrar of Contractors. Commercial plumbing work may require a separate commercial or dual classification.

The proposed qualifying party must demonstrate the experience, knowledge, and skills required for the classification. Experience records may need to describe the plumbing work performed, the types of systems involved, supervisory responsibilities, and the length of relevant trade experience.

The legal entity applying for the license must be properly formed and identified. The company name, ownership information, qualifying-party records, EIN documentation, bond documents, and contractor application should remain consistent throughout the process.

Arizona requires licensed residential contractors to provide financial protection. This includes a contractor license bond based on the applicable license category and anticipated volume of work. Residential contractors must also participate in the Residential Contractors’ Recovery Fund or provide an authorized alternative form of protection.

Applicants must complete the required examinations before the contractor application can be accepted for processing. Passing examination results must be submitted within the acceptance period established by the Arizona licensing process.

After the license is issued, the contractor must operate within the authorized classification, maintain the required bond, renew the license, and keep business and qualifying-party information current. Changes involving ownership, legal structure, address, bond, or qualifying party may require additional filings.

The contractor license belongs to the approved business entity. It does not automatically transfer with a sale, ownership change, or reorganization of the business. Proper business formation and consistent licensing records are therefore important parts of the application process.

Test Information and Study Materials

Effective R-37R preparation should combine code navigation, trade calculations, practical plumbing knowledge, safety review, and timed question practice. Begin by learning the purpose of each book so the correct reference can be selected quickly.

International Plumbing Code study may include fixtures, water supply, pipe sizing, backflow protection, drainage, waste and vent systems, traps, cleanouts, indirect waste, and storm drainage. Candidates should practice reading complete code sections, including exceptions, tables, and cross-references.

Fuel-gas preparation may involve piping materials, sizing, pressure limitations, shutoff valves, appliance connections, combustion air, venting, testing, and inspection. Mechanical study may include boilers, hydronic systems, equipment clearances, ventilation, exhaust, and combustion requirements.

Fire-sprinkler preparation should include familiarity with NFPA 13, NFPA 13D, and NFPA 13R. Candidates should learn which standard applies to the type of residential building or sprinkler system described.

Trade mathematics can include fractions, decimals, conversions, area, volume, slope, pressure, pipe offsets, fitting allowances, and measurement. Hydronic-heating study may include boilers, circulators, piping arrangements, heat emitters, expansion control, air separation, and system controls.

OSHA preparation should focus on hazards commonly encountered during plumbing work, including trenches, excavations, ladders, tools, electrical hazards, personal protective equipment, material handling, and jobsite safety. Arizona 811 material supports safe utility-location procedures before excavation begins.

A practical study schedule can combine topic review, short code-navigation drills, calculation exercises, practice questions, and longer timed sessions. Candidates should complete direct questions first, mark questions requiring extensive research, and return to them after answering faster items.
