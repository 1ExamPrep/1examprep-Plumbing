State Requirements

The North Carolina State Board of Examiners of Electrical Contractors administers electrical contractor licensing in North Carolina. The Board regulates electrical contractor classifications, examinations, licensing, renewals, and discipline under North Carolina electrical contracting law and Board rules.

The Special Restricted Plumbing and Heating electrical contracting license, identified as SP-PH, authorizes the licensee to install, maintain, or repair electric wiring or devices only as an incident to the licensee’s plumbing, heating, or air conditioning business. This classification is limited in scope and is not the same as a Limited, Intermediate, or Unlimited electrical contractor license.

The state scope for the SP-PH classification limits the licensee to wiring directly related to plumbing, heating, or air conditioning equipment. The wiring is restricted to circuits or parts of circuits that operate at voltages not exceeding 600 volts phase-to-phase. Candidates should understand the role of overcurrent protective isolating devices, equipment identification, equipment enclosures, and the point where the special restricted contractor’s work may begin.

The SP-PH classification also includes limitations tied to electrical work involving equipment served by plumbing, heating, or air conditioning systems. Candidates should understand that the license does not authorize unrestricted electrical contracting or general electrical work outside the classification scope. These scope limits are important for both exam preparation and real-world compliance.

North Carolina electrical contracting licenses issued by the Board expire one year after issuance. Licensees are responsible for renewing on time and keeping license information current. Anyone pursuing the SP-PH classification should review Board licensing procedures, renewal responsibilities, classification limits, and contractor requirements before operating as a licensed electrical contractor.
