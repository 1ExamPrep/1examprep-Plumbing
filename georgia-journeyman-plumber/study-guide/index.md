## How does the Journeyman Plumber exam work?

The Journeyman Plumber license comes from the Georgia State Construction Industry Licensing Board, Division of Master Plumbers and Journeyman Plumbers. The board approves your application first; then you schedule the exam and bring only the references the candidate bulletin allows.

- Georgia licenses master plumbers in Class I (Restricted) and Class II (Unrestricted), plus journeyman plumbers.
- Georgia candidates also study the NASCLA business and law guide written for the Georgia board.
