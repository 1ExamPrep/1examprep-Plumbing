Exam Details

The Georgia Journeyman Plumber examination evaluates the plumbing knowledge and practical understanding needed for licensure under Georgia's State Construction Industry Licensing Board, Division of Master and Journeyman Plumbers.

The examination addresses plumbing installation, water distribution, sanitary drainage, venting, fuel gas systems, plumbing fixtures, code interpretation, and related technical responsibilities.

Georgia Journeyman Plumber Examination Information:

  • License Classification: Journeyman Plumber
  • State: Georgia
  • Licensing Authority: Georgia State Construction Industry Licensing Board, Division of Master and Journeyman Plumbers
  • Testing Provider: PSI
  • Examination Format: Computer-based examination
  • Testing Method: Open book with authorized references
  • Examination Questions: 120 questions, including scored and unscored questions
  • Time Allowed: 5 hours
  • Passing Standard: Minimum score of 70

Examination preparation involves understanding plumbing codes and applying trade knowledge to situations involving system installation, pipe sizing, fixture requirements, drainage, venting, and fuel gas piping.

Mathematical skills also play an important role in plumbing work. Candidates benefit from reviewing calculations involving measurements, pipe dimensions, slopes, volumes, and other practical plumbing applications.

Georgia's examinations also address applicable accessibility and regulatory requirements. Familiarity with these subjects supports both examination preparation and professional plumbing knowledge.

Open Book Test

The Georgia Journeyman Plumber License Exam is an open-book test. Candidates may consult authorized reference materials during the examination according to PSI testing requirements.

This examination format allows candidates to use approved plumbing codes and technical publications while answering questions. However, an open-book examination still requires thorough preparation, efficient reference navigation, and a working understanding of plumbing concepts.

For example, a plumbing drainage question may require knowledge of the International Plumbing Code and the ability to locate relevant sizing or installation requirements. A fuel gas question may involve identifying applicable provisions in the International Fuel Gas Code.

Other questions may involve plumbing mathematics, pipe installation, accessibility, excavation practices, or general trade knowledge.

Effective open-book preparation strategies include:

  • Learning how the International Plumbing Code is organized.
  • Reviewing fuel gas code chapters and relevant technical tables.
  • Practicing plumbing calculations and measurement conversions.
  • Becoming familiar with plumbing fixtures and piping systems.
  • Reviewing Georgia plumbing and fuel gas amendments.
  • Practicing reference lookups within examination time limits.
  • Strengthening knowledge of installation methods and troubleshooting principles.

For examinations based on the 2024 Georgia Plumbing Code and 2024 Georgia Fuel Gas Code, candidates should study the applicable 2024 International Codes together with Georgia's corresponding amendments.

The examination provider determines which reference titles and editions are permitted in the testing room. Candidates must follow PSI rules regarding authorized publications and book preparation.

Licensing Steps

Becoming a licensed Georgia Journeyman Plumber involves meeting state experience requirements, submitting documentation, obtaining examination approval, and successfully completing the required licensing examination.

  1. Review Georgia Licensing Requirements: Understand the Journeyman Plumber classification and the qualifications established by Georgia's plumbing licensing authority.
  2. Complete Qualifying Plumbing Experience: Gain the required supervised practical experience performing plumbing work covered by the Georgia State Plumbing Code.
  3. Document Your Experience: Prepare information identifying your plumbing work, supervising licensed Master Plumber, and the practical responsibilities you performed.
  4. Gather Professional References: Collect the plumbing experience verification and professional references required for your application.
  5. Prepare Your Application: Organize identification documents, applicable background check materials, experience records, and other supporting information.
  6. Submit Your Licensing Application: Complete the appropriate application through Georgia's professional licensing system.
  7. Obtain Examination Authorization: Receive approval from the licensing authority before scheduling the examination.
  8. Prepare for the Exam: Study plumbing codes, fuel gas requirements, mathematics, system installation, and technical trade subjects using your reference books.
  9. Schedule Your Examination: Register with PSI after receiving the required examination authorization.
  10. Pass the Examination: Demonstrate your plumbing knowledge by meeting Georgia's required passing standard.
  11. Complete Licensure: Follow the licensing authority's instructions for license issuance and any remaining requirements.

State Requirements

Georgia Journeyman Plumber licensing is regulated by the State Construction Industry Licensing Board, Division of Master and Journeyman Plumbers.

Experience Requirement: Georgia requires at least one year of qualifying full-time plumbing experience for Journeyman Plumber applicants under the licensing requirements effective July 1, 2026.

Supervised Experience: Qualifying work must be performed under the supervision of a licensed Master Plumber Class I or Master Plumber Class II and must demonstrate practical competency in plumbing activities.

Practical Plumbing Knowledge: Qualifying experience includes relevant work involving water supply and distribution, sanitary drainage, venting, fixtures, equipment, and applicable fuel gas systems.

Experience Verification: Applicants must provide the required documentation and verification of qualifying supervised plumbing work.

Examination Requirement: Applicants must pass a Board-approved examination with a minimum score of 70.

Application Documentation: Applicants must provide identification, experience information, professional references, and other records required for their application.

Licensing Authority: The Georgia Division of Master and Journeyman Plumbers evaluates applications and determines eligibility for examination and licensure.

A Journeyman Plumber License is distinct from a Master Plumber License. Georgia reserves the ability to operate a plumbing contracting business for appropriately licensed Master Plumbers. Journeyman licensure recognizes trade qualifications but does not independently authorize the holder to operate a plumbing contracting business.

The Georgia Journeyman Plumber examination provides an opportunity for qualified plumbing professionals to demonstrate the technical knowledge associated with their trade responsibilities.
