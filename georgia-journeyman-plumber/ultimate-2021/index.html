Exam Details

Georgia plumbing licensure examinations are administered through PSI for the Georgia Construction Industry Licensing Board, Division of Master and Journeyman Plumbers. The Candidate Information Bulletin outlines a computer-based exam with multiple-choice questions and emphasizes that some questions draw on field experience and trade practices—not only book lookups.

For Georgia Journeyman Plumber candidates, the bulletin describes a two-part examination format with a total testing window of five (5) hours:

  • Part 1: Two and one-half (2.5) hours
  • Break
  • Part 2: Two and one-half (2.5) hours

The bulletin also explains that the exam includes scored questions plus additional pretest questions (used to evaluate future exam content). You’ll see more questions than are counted toward your score, which is another reason a steady, repeatable pace matters.

Scoring is reported as a final scaled score, and Georgia’s bulletin explains that the minimum passing standard is converted to a common scaled score of 70 across exam forms. That’s why it’s important to build both knowledge and execution—because even when the exam form changes, the passing standard remains consistent.

Open Book Test

You confirmed the exam is open book, and Georgia’s bulletin and reference guidance emphasize reference use for the Journeyman Plumber examination. Open book is not “easy mode.” It’s a different skill set: being able to find the right answer quickly, interpret it correctly, and avoid being thrown off by exceptions, definitions, and table-driven requirements.

Open-book success comes from a three-part strategy:

  • Identify the topic fast: drainage, venting, water supply, fixtures, gas piping concepts, safety, math, or administrative requirements.
  • Navigate with purpose: use tabs to jump into the right chapter area and use the index to locate specific terms when questions are phrased in trade language.
  • Confirm the exact requirement: read the code language carefully and check for exceptions, notes, and table footnotes before locking in your answer.

That’s exactly why this package includes permanent Quick Reference Tabs. They help you navigate quickly through the Code book during your exam and make it easier to stay organized while you practice. When you use the tabs consistently during study, your hands and eyes learn the code layout—so lookups feel faster and less stressful on test day.

Licensing Steps

Georgia plumbing licensure is regulated through the Georgia Construction Industry Licensing Board, Division of Master and Journeyman Plumbers. While individual eligibility details depend on your background and application status, the general exam pathway includes:

  1. Choose your license level: Journeyman Plumber vs. Master classifications.
  2. Apply for licensure by examination: Complete the required application process for the Journeyman Plumber exam.
  3. Receive approval and scheduling instructions: Once eligible, schedule your exam through PSI within the available testing windows.
  4. Prepare with a code-based study plan: Build code comprehension, navigation speed, and timed practice performance.
  5. Take the exam: Complete both exam parts within the time limits and meet the passing score standard.
  6. Complete any post-exam licensing steps: Follow the Board’s process for license issuance after passing.

Test Information and Study Materials

The Georgia Journeyman Plumber exam is built around applied trade knowledge and code-driven decision-making. That means you need to prepare in a way that mirrors how the exam asks questions: scenario-based prompts, practical installation situations, and code interpretation details where one exception or definition can change the correct answer.

This Ultimate package supports a complete prep workflow:

  • Code comprehension training: Learn how to read code language precisely—what is required, what is permitted, and what is prohibited. Many candidates lose points not because they can’t find the section, but because they misread the rule or overlook a key sentence.
  • Navigation speed: Train your hands and eyes to move through the IPC quickly. Use the tabs for chapter-level navigation and the index for term-based searching. The goal is to reduce “search time” so you can spend time answering questions.
  • Practice questions and answer verification: Use the study guide’s 750-question set to build familiarity with how questions are phrased. Then confirm each answer in the IPC to build the habit of verification—the same habit that wins points in open-book exams.
  • Exam simulation and technique: The package includes test-taking techniques and a study plan that emphasizes steady improvement, timed practice, and confidence-building repetition.

High-value topic areas to drill for journeyman plumbing exams:

  • Drainage systems: understanding how code requirements support flow, prevent clogging and siphonage, and maintain system function.
  • Venting rules: venting logic, vent system requirements, and how venting supports trap seals and system performance.
  • Water supply and distribution: interpreting requirements tied to supply systems and installation considerations.
  • Fixtures and connections: knowing common fixture requirements, installation rules, and scenario-based code application.
  • Gas piping concepts: reinforcement of fuel gas topics that commonly overlap with plumbing work and exam content.
  • Math and layout questions: measurement and calculation questions where accuracy matters and pacing is critical.
  • Isometrics analysis: strengthening your ability to interpret system layouts and apply rules to diagram-driven questions.
  • Safety fundamentals: OSHA awareness and jobsite safety principles that support compliant, professional practice.
