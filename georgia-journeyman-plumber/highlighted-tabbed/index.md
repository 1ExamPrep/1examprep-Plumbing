## State Requirements

Georgia Journeyman Plumber licensing is administered by the State Construction Industry Licensing Board, Division of Master and Journeyman Plumbers. Applicants must meet experience, application, reference, and examination requirements.

**Plumbing Experience:** Georgia's requirements effective July 1, 2026, establish a minimum of one year of full-time qualifying plumbing experience for Journeyman Plumber applicants.

**Professional Supervision:** Qualifying experience must be obtained under the supervision of a licensed Master Plumber Class I or Class II.

**Practical Competency:** Experience must demonstrate relevant plumbing responsibilities, including work involving water supply and distribution, sanitary drainage, venting, plumbing fixtures, or applicable fuel gas systems.

**Professional References:** Applicants must submit the required references from licensed plumbing professionals who can attest to their qualifications and experience.

**Examination Requirement:** Applicants must successfully complete the approved licensing examination with a minimum score of 70.

**Application Documentation:** The licensing process requires supporting records, including experience information, appropriate identification, background check documentation, and other documents applicable to the applicant.

**License Scope:** Journeyman Plumber licensure is distinct from Master Plumber licensure. In Georgia, the operation of a plumbing contracting business requires the appropriate Master Plumber license.

Journeyman Plumber licensing recognizes the professional qualifications of plumbing tradespeople and establishes an important step toward advanced responsibilities and future plumbing career development.
