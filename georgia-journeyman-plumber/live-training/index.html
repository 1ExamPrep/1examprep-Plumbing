Georgia Journeyman Plumber Exam Details

The Georgia Journeyman Plumber examination is part of the statewide plumbing licensing process administered by the Georgia State Construction Industry Licensing Board, Division of Master and Journeyman Plumbers.

The examination is administered through PSI and evaluates knowledge of plumbing systems, applicable regulations, installation procedures, plumbing calculations, and professional trade practices.

Georgia Journeyman Plumber Exam Overview:

  • License Classification: Statewide Journeyman Plumber
  • Licensing Authority: Georgia State Construction Industry Licensing Board, Division of Master and Journeyman Plumbers
  • Examination Provider: PSI
  • Examination Format: Computer-based examination
  • Exam Type: Open Book
  • Passing Requirement: Minimum score of 70
  • Examination Subjects: Plumbing trade knowledge, applicable regulations, installation practices, testing, maintenance, safety, and technical calculations

Candidates should develop a study plan that includes both technical plumbing knowledge and familiarity with the reference materials associated with their examination.

Open Book Test

The Georgia Journeyman Plumber licensing examination is an open book test. Candidates may use approved reference materials during the examination, subject to PSI testing rules and the Georgia Board's reference policies.

Open-book testing requires more than having books available. Candidates must understand how to locate information efficiently, interpret code provisions, and apply technical requirements to specific plumbing situations.

Important reference-navigation skills include:

  • Finding information using plumbing code chapter headings and indexes.
  • Locating pipe-sizing tables and installation requirements.
  • Understanding drainage, venting, and water supply provisions.
  • Identifying applicable fuel gas requirements.
  • Using plumbing mathematics references to solve technical problems.
  • Interpreting Georgia amendments to national and international model codes.

The Georgia Board permits approved examination references to be tabbed and highlighted. Reference materials containing handwritten notes or comments are not permitted.

Practicing reference navigation before test day can help candidates develop a systematic approach to technical questions rather than spending unnecessary time searching through unfamiliar books.

Licensing Steps

Obtaining a Georgia Journeyman Plumber license involves meeting the state's qualifications, completing the licensing application process, receiving examination approval, and passing the required examination.

  1. Review Georgia licensing requirements. Understand the qualifications established by the Georgia State Construction Industry Licensing Board for journeyman plumber applicants.
  2. Document qualifying plumbing experience. Gather information demonstrating experience in plumbing work involving systems and activities covered by Georgia plumbing requirements.
  3. Gather application documents. Prepare the required identity information, employment and experience records, professional references, background check information, and additional supporting documentation.
  4. Submit your licensing application. Complete the appropriate application through Georgia's licensing process and provide the required documentation and applicable fees.
  5. Receive examination approval. The licensing authority reviews applications and determines eligibility to take the journeyman plumber examination.
  6. Schedule your examination. Once approved, follow the instructions provided for registering and scheduling the examination through PSI.
  7. Prepare for the test. Study plumbing codes, installation practices, technical references, and practical examination subjects using an organized preparation schedule.
  8. Complete the licensing examination. Take the required examination and satisfy the passing-score requirement.
  9. Complete any remaining licensing requirements. Follow the Board's instructions for obtaining your journeyman plumber license.

Keeping your application materials organized and preparing consistently can help you move through the examination and licensing process more effectively.

Georgia State Requirements

Georgia regulates journeyman plumber licensing through state law and the State Construction Industry Licensing Board.

Under Georgia's requirements effective July 1, 2026, journeyman plumber applicants must document at least one year of qualifying full-time plumbing experience under the supervision of a licensed Master Plumber Class I or Class II. Qualifying work includes practical experience involving plumbing systems and activities covered by the state's minimum plumbing code.

This experience can involve water supply and distribution systems, sanitary drainage, venting systems, plumbing fixtures, fuel gas piping, plumbing system testing, troubleshooting, and related installation activities.

Applicants must also satisfy applicable documentation and identity requirements and achieve the required minimum examination score of 70.

Georgia requires plumbing license applicants to provide professional references and supporting information demonstrating their qualifications. The licensing authority evaluates the applicant's records and determines whether the requirements for licensure have been satisfied.

A Georgia journeyman plumber works within the professional scope established by state licensing law. A journeyman license is distinct from a Master Plumber Class I or Class II license, which carries separate qualifications and contracting authority.

Test Information and Study Materials

The Georgia Journeyman Plumber examination evaluates practical trade knowledge and the application of plumbing regulations. Successful preparation involves reviewing how plumbing systems are installed, tested, maintained, and repaired.

Important plumbing examination topics include:

  • Water Supply Systems: Water distribution, pipe sizing, pressure, materials, valves, and installation methods.
  • Drainage and Venting: Drainage systems, traps, vents, drainage fixture units, and code requirements.
  • Plumbing Fixtures: Fixture installation, connections, clearances, and accessibility requirements.
  • Fuel Gas Systems: Gas piping, system sizing, appliance connections, and applicable safety requirements.
  • Plumbing Mathematics: Area, volume, measurements, offsets, slopes, pipe dimensions, and mathematical calculations.
  • Testing and Inspection: Plumbing system testing procedures, identifying defects, and evaluating installation compliance.
  • Safety Practices: Jobsite safety, excavation awareness, hazard prevention, and safe work practices.
  • Applicable Regulations: Plumbing code provisions, Georgia amendments, and relevant professional responsibilities.

A productive study routine combines reading with practical application. Candidates should regularly review reference tables, work through plumbing calculations, identify relevant code sections, and reinforce installation principles.

Georgia's adoption of the 2024 International Plumbing Code and International Fuel Gas Code, together with the state's amendments, makes these code materials particularly important to preparation.
