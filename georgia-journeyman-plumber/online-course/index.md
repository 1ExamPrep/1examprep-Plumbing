## State Requirements

Georgia Journeyman Plumber licensing is regulated by the State Construction Industry Licensing Board, Division of Master and Journeyman Plumbers. State law establishes qualifications involving practical experience, professional supervision, supporting documentation, and successful examination performance.

**Qualifying Experience:** Georgia requires at least one year of full-time plumbing experience for Journeyman Plumber applicants under the requirements effective July 1, 2026.

**Licensed Supervision:** Qualifying experience must be obtained under the supervision of a licensed Master Plumber Class I or Master Plumber Class II.

**Relevant Plumbing Work:** Experience includes practical involvement with plumbing systems such as potable water supply and distribution, sanitary drainage, venting, fixtures, equipment, and applicable fuel gas systems.

**Technical Competency:** Applicants should demonstrate familiarity with plumbing layouts, pipe sizing, material selection, inspections, testing, diagnosis, and troubleshooting appropriate to the work performed.

**Experience Documentation:** Applicants must provide the required verification of qualifying experience and professional references.

**Identity and Background Documentation:** The application process requires identification and other supporting materials established by the licensing authority.

**Examination Requirement:** Applicants must pass the Board-approved Journeyman Plumber examination with a minimum score of 70.

A Journeyman Plumber license is different from a Master Plumber license. Georgia reserves plumbing business operation for appropriately licensed Master Plumbers. Journeyman licensure recognizes a plumber's professional trade qualifications but does not independently authorize operation of a plumbing contracting business.

Understanding these distinctions is important when planning your career path, documenting experience, and selecting the appropriate licensing examination.
