## State Requirements

Georgia Journeyman Plumber licensing is administered by the State Construction Industry Licensing Board, Division of Master and Journeyman Plumbers.

**Plumbing Experience:** Georgia requires at least one year of qualifying full-time plumbing experience under the licensing requirements effective July 1, 2026.

**Licensed Supervision:** Qualifying experience must be obtained under the supervision of a licensed Master Plumber Class I or Master Plumber Class II.

**Relevant Trade Work:** Experience includes plumbing activities involving water supply, sanitary drainage, venting, plumbing fixtures, piping systems, and applicable fuel gas installations.

**Practical Competency:** Applicants must demonstrate qualifying work experience involving relevant installation, maintenance, repair, testing, or troubleshooting activities.

**Experience Verification:** Applicants must provide appropriate documentation and supervisory verification of their plumbing experience.

**Application Documentation:** The licensing process includes identity verification and supporting records required by the Board.

**Examination Requirement:** Applicants must pass the Board-approved Journeyman Plumber examination with a minimum score of 70.

**License Scope:** Journeyman Plumber licensing is distinct from Master Plumber licensing. Georgia requires the appropriate Master Plumber license to operate a plumbing contracting business.

Meeting these qualifications is an important part of pursuing professional plumbing licensure in Georgia. Examination preparation helps candidates review the technical knowledge associated with their trade responsibilities.
