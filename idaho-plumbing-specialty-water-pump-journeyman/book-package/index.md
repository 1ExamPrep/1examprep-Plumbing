## Test Information and Study Materials

Your best results on a code-based open-book exam come from practicing how to use the code, not just reading it. The UPC is your tool—so your preparation should focus on building comfort with the code’s structure and training efficient “find-and-confirm” habits.

- **Learn the code’s structure first.** Before you chase details, understand how the UPC is organized: chapters, section numbering, and where general requirements and definitions are located. Navigation speed improves dramatically when you know the “map.”
- **Study in “lookup mode.”** Use practice questions as navigation drills: read the question, identify the topic, locate the section, confirm the requirement, then answer. This builds real exam skill faster than passive reading.
- **Tab by major topics.** Tabs are helpful when they act like signposts. Keep tabs focused on major sections you repeatedly use. Too many tabs can slow you down and create clutter that makes the book harder to use.
- **Highlight for speed.** Highlight keywords, thresholds, and exceptions—items that help your eyes land on what matters. Avoid highlighting entire paragraphs; heavy highlighting reduces scan speed.
- **Train to spot exceptions.** Code questions often hinge on a small exception or condition. In practice, make it a habit to look for “except,” “shall not,” “unless,” and similar language so you don’t miss the detail that changes the correct answer.
- **Practice with a timer that matches your exam.** Since the Specialty Journeyman exam is 2.5 hours, practice shorter timed sets. Your goal is to learn how long a “reasonable lookup” takes before you choose to move on.
- **Review missed questions by learning the location.** Don’t only learn the correct answer—learn where it lives in the code. Open-book speed improves when your brain remembers “where to find it.”
- **Stay exam-compliant while studying.** Since loose paper isn’t allowed in reference material, build your organization directly into the code book through allowed tabs, highlighting, and notes.
