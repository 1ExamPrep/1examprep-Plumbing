Exam Details

The Oklahoma Residential Contractor Plumbing and Natural Gas examination evaluates technical knowledge involving residential plumbing installations, natural gas systems, calculations, and construction safety.

The Oklahoma Construction Industries Board administers plumbing contractor licensing, while PSI provides the applicable licensing examinations.

  • Exam Name: Oklahoma Residential Contractor Plumbing and Natural Gas
  • Licensing Authority: Oklahoma Construction Industries Board
  • Testing Provider: PSI
  • Number of Scored Questions: 85
  • Time Allowed: 180 minutes
  • Passing Score: 75%
  • Minimum Correct Answers: 64
  • Exam Format: Open book

Important examination topics include:

  • General Plumbing Knowledge: Plumbing terminology, materials, installation principles, and applicable regulations.
  • Piping and Valves: Piping systems, fittings, valves, supports, and joining methods.
  • Fixtures and Equipment: Residential plumbing fixtures, water heaters, and equipment connections.
  • Water Supply: Water distribution, pressure, pipe sizing, and fixture supply requirements.
  • Drains and Sewers: Drainage piping, slopes, cleanouts, and building drains.
  • Venting: Vent sizing, vent connections, and drainage system protection.
  • Traps and Special Waste: Plumbing traps, interceptors, and indirect waste requirements.
  • Isometric Analysis: Plumbing drawings, piping layouts, and system interpretation.
  • Fuel Gas: Gas piping, regulators, sizing, appliance connections, and testing.
  • Safety: Construction hazards and workplace protection requirements.

These subjects combine practical plumbing knowledge with the ability to interpret code provisions and apply technical information. Candidates benefit from studying each subject while developing familiarity with the reference materials associated with the examination.

With 180 minutes available for 85 scored questions, candidates have approximately two minutes and seven seconds per question. Regular technical review and reference-navigation practice support effective examination time management.

The Oklahoma Residential Contractor Plumbing and Natural Gas examination is an open-book test. Candidates may consult approved examination references according to PSI testing requirements.

Open-book testing requires technical understanding and efficient reference navigation. Candidates must identify the relevant plumbing subject, locate the appropriate information, and apply technical requirements accurately.

The International Residential Code is an important reference for residential plumbing and natural gas installations. Its provisions address water supply, sanitary drainage, venting, fixtures, fuel gas piping, and related residential construction requirements.

Mathematics for Plumbers and Pipefitters supports preparation involving trade calculations, measurements, and piping applications. OSHA construction regulations address workplace safety requirements relevant to plumbing professionals.

The examination transition period for Oklahoma Residential Contractor Plumbing and Natural Gas runs from September 14 through December 14, 2026. During this period, candidates may take the affected examination using either the previously adopted or newly adopted building code.

Beginning December 15, 2026, affected examinations will be based exclusively on the newly adopted codes.

State Requirements

Residential plumbing and natural gas contracting in Oklahoma operates within the state's regulated plumbing industry. The Oklahoma Construction Industries Board administers applicable licensing laws and administrative regulations.

Residential plumbing work includes activities involving water distribution, sanitary drainage, venting, plumbing fixtures, and related piping systems within the authorized scope of the license.

Natural gas installations involve additional technical requirements concerning fuel gas piping, regulators, valves, sizing, appliance connections, pressure testing, and safe system operation.

The International Residential Code establishes requirements for qualifying residential buildings, including one- and two-family dwellings and townhouses within its scope.

Oklahoma applies state amendments to adopted construction codes. Understanding residential plumbing provisions and related installation requirements is an important part of professional technical knowledge.

Residential contractor licensing is separate from residential journeyman licensing. Contractors have additional responsibilities associated with contracting operations and maintaining active professional authorization.

For active Oklahoma plumbing contractors, the Construction Industries Board requires a $5,000 corporate surety bond and proof of at least $50,000 in commercial general liability insurance.

Construction safety is another important responsibility. Residential plumbing projects may involve excavation, ladders, tools, heavy equipment, and workplace hazards addressed by OSHA construction safety regulations.
