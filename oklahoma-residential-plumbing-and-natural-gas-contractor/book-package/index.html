Exam Details

The Oklahoma Residential Contractor Plumbing and Natural Gas examination evaluates technical knowledge associated with residential plumbing systems, natural gas installations, piping, trade mathematics, and construction safety.

The Oklahoma Construction Industries Board oversees the state's plumbing licensing program, and PSI administers the licensing examination.

  • Exam Name: Oklahoma Residential Contractor Plumbing and Natural Gas
  • Licensing Authority: Oklahoma Construction Industries Board
  • Testing Provider: PSI
  • Number of Scored Questions: 85
  • Time Allowed: 180 minutes
  • Passing Score: 75%
  • Minimum Correct Answers: 64
  • Exam Format: Open book

The technical examination covers the following principal subject areas:

  • General Plumbing Knowledge and Regulations: Plumbing definitions, installation principles, measurements, testing, and code requirements.
  • Piping, Valves, and Controls: Piping materials, fittings, valves, supports, and related installation practices.
  • Fixtures and Equipment: Residential plumbing fixtures, equipment connections, and installation requirements.
  • Water Supply: Water distribution, pipe sizing, pressure, valves, and supply systems.
  • Drains and Sewers: Sanitary drainage, pipe slopes, cleanouts, and building drains.
  • Venting: Vent piping, sizing, connections, and system requirements.
  • Traps and Special Waste: Trap requirements, interceptors, and indirect waste provisions.
  • Isometric Analysis: Plumbing drawings, layouts, measurements, and calculations.
  • Fuel Gas: Natural gas piping, sizing, appliance connections, and testing.
  • Safety: Construction hazards and workplace protection.

Preparing across these subjects helps candidates build a stronger understanding of residential plumbing installations and the application of technical code provisions.

Open Book Test

The Oklahoma Residential Contractor Plumbing and Natural Gas examination is an open-book test. Candidates may use approved examination references while following the testing provider's requirements.

Open-book testing requires more than bringing reference materials into the examination room. Candidates must understand the questions, select appropriate code provisions, interpret technical requirements, and manage their examination time.

The International Residential Code provides information concerning residential plumbing systems, fuel gas piping, drainage, venting, and installation requirements. Mathematics for Plumbers and Pipefitters supports calculation skills, while OSHA regulations address construction workplace safety.

Oklahoma adopted the 2024 International Residential Code with applicable state amendments effective September 14, 2026.

The Oklahoma Construction Industries Board established an examination transition period from September 14 through December 14, 2026 for affected examinations, including Residential Contractor Plumbing and Natural Gas.

During this period, candidates may test using either the previously adopted or newly adopted code. Beginning December 15, 2026, affected examinations will use the newly adopted codes exclusively.

Approved examination references must comply with PSI requirements governing editions, highlighting, tabs, binding, and permitted materials.
