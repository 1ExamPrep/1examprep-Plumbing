## Oklahoma State Requirements

Oklahoma regulates plumbing contractor licensing through the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board. Applicants must satisfy qualifying experience requirements and complete the applicable examinations.

**Age Requirement:** Applicants must be at least 18 years old.

**Experience Requirements:** Plumbing contractor applicants must satisfy the experience qualifications established for journeyman plumbers, along with one additional year of qualifying plumbing experience.

Journeyman qualification generally requires three years of plumbing trade experience while employed by a licensed plumbing contractor. Qualifying military plumbing experience or an eligible out-of-state plumbing license may also support an application under the state's requirements.

**Education Substitutions:** Certain approved plumbing education programs may substitute for a portion of the experience requirement. Approved programs containing at least 1,000 classroom hours may qualify for a two-year substitution, while approved programs containing at least 500 classroom hours may qualify for a one-year substitution.

**Examination Requirements:** Contractor applicants must pass the applicable plumbing trade examination and the required Business and Law examination.

**Bonding and Insurance:** Oklahoma establishes bonding and insurance requirements for activating a plumbing contractor license, with applicable provisions for qualifying exemptions.

**Continuing Education:** Licensed plumbing contractors must satisfy Oklahoma's continuing education requirements, including six hours during the applicable 36-month licensing period.

These requirements distinguish contractor licensing from journeyman plumbing qualifications and reflect the additional responsibilities associated with operating a plumbing contracting business.

## Test Information and Study Materials

The Oklahoma Residential Contractor Plumbing and Natural Gas examination evaluates ten areas of trade knowledge. Understanding the examination content breakdown can help candidates organize their preparation and devote appropriate attention to important subjects.

**Examination Content Breakdown:**

- **General Plumbing Knowledge and Regulations - 10 Questions:** Plumbing definitions, general code requirements, area and volume calculations, testing methods, structural penetrations, and pipe offsets.
- **Piping, Valves, and Control Valves - 8 Questions:** Piping connections, cast iron, copper, steel, plastic piping, joining methods, and valves.
- **Fixtures and Equipment - 8 Questions:** Plumbing fixture requirements, fixture installation, and water heater installations.
- **Water Supply - 10 Questions:** Water supply and distribution piping, potable water protection, and backflow prevention.
- **Drains and Sewers - 10 Questions:** Drainage piping, building sewers, cleanouts, drainage fixture units, and drain connections.
- **Vents - 13 Questions:** Plumbing vent installation, wet venting, and associated venting requirements.
- **Traps, Interceptors, Indirect Waste, and Special Waste - 5 Questions:** Traps, ejector pumps, and indirect waste applications.
- **Isometric Analysis - 7 Questions:** Interpretation of plumbing isometric drawings and piping diagrams.
- **Fuel Gas - 10 Questions:** Gas piping, valves, pipe sizing, supports, system testing, installation methods, joining procedures, and threaded connections.
- **Safety - 4 Questions:** Construction safety practices, ladders, and fall protection systems.

Venting represents the largest individual examination category, followed by important subjects involving plumbing knowledge, water supply, drainage, and fuel gas systems.
