Oklahoma Residential Plumbing and Natural Gas Contractor Exam Details

The Oklahoma Residential Contractor Plumbing and Natural Gas examination is administered by PSI under the authority of the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board (CIB).

The examination assesses knowledge of residential plumbing systems, water distribution, drainage, venting, fuel gas installations, piping materials, plumbing calculations, and construction safety.

Examination Overview:

  • Exam Name: Residential Contractor Plumbing and Natural Gas.
  • Licensing Authority: Oklahoma Construction Industries Board.
  • Testing Provider: PSI.
  • Number of Questions: 85 scored questions.
  • Time Allowed: 180 minutes (3 hours).
  • Passing Score: 70%, effective November 1, 2026.
  • Exam Type: Open Book.
  • Primary Code Reference: International Residential Code, 2024 Edition, with Oklahoma amendments.
  • Examination Subjects: Plumbing regulations, piping, fixtures, water supply, drainage, venting, fuel gas systems, mathematics, and safety.

The technical examination contains 85 scored questions distributed across ten subject categories. Candidates should prepare for both code-based questions and practical plumbing problems requiring technical understanding.

Oklahoma plumbing contractor licensing also requires a separate Business and Law examination. Candidates must satisfy both the trade and Business and Law examination requirements as part of the contractor licensing process.

Open Book Test

The Oklahoma Residential Plumbing and Natural Gas Contractor examination is an open book test. Candidates may consult approved technical references during testing under PSI examination rules.

Open-book testing requires an understanding of how plumbing codes and technical references are organized. Candidates must be able to identify the applicable requirements, locate relevant information, and apply that information to practical installation questions.

The examination permits approved reference materials to be highlighted, underlined, and indexed before testing. Permanent reference tabs are permitted, while temporary tabs such as removable sticky notes are prohibited. Handwritten notes and additional loose papers are not allowed.

Students preparing for this examination should practice locating plumbing code provisions, using mathematical formulas, interpreting piping diagrams, and identifying safety requirements.

Important open-book study strategies include:

  • Learning the organization of the 2024 International Residential Code.
  • Reviewing plumbing and fuel gas chapters and technical tables.
  • Practicing water supply and drainage system calculations.
  • Locating pipe sizing, installation, and venting requirements.
  • Studying permitted piping materials and joining methods.
  • Using mathematics references to solve plumbing problems.
  • Developing efficient methods for answering timed questions.

Oklahoma began transitioning residential plumbing examinations to the 2024 International Residential Code on September 14, 2026. During the transition period, candidates may test under the applicable 2018 or 2024 code editions. Beginning December 15, 2026, the examinations use the newly adopted 2024 codes exclusively.

Preparing with the 2024 edition supports familiarity with the updated code requirements associated with Oklahoma's current residential plumbing standards.

Oklahoma State Requirements

Oklahoma regulates plumbing contractor licensing through the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board. Applicants must satisfy qualifying experience requirements and complete the applicable examinations.

Age Requirement: Applicants must be at least 18 years old.

Experience Requirements: Plumbing contractor applicants must satisfy the experience qualifications established for journeyman plumbers, along with one additional year of qualifying plumbing experience.

Journeyman qualification generally requires three years of plumbing trade experience while employed by a licensed plumbing contractor. Qualifying military plumbing experience or an eligible out-of-state plumbing license may also support an application under the state's requirements.

Education Substitutions: Certain approved plumbing education programs may substitute for a portion of the experience requirement. Approved programs containing at least 1,000 classroom hours may qualify for a two-year substitution, while approved programs containing at least 500 classroom hours may qualify for a one-year substitution.

Examination Requirements: Contractor applicants must pass the applicable plumbing trade examination and the required Business and Law examination.

Bonding and Insurance: Oklahoma establishes bonding and insurance requirements for activating a plumbing contractor license, with applicable provisions for qualifying exemptions.

Continuing Education: Licensed plumbing contractors must satisfy Oklahoma's continuing education requirements, including six hours during the applicable 36-month licensing period.

These requirements distinguish contractor licensing from journeyman plumbing qualifications and reflect the additional responsibilities associated with operating a plumbing contracting business.

Test Information and Study Materials

The Oklahoma Residential Contractor Plumbing and Natural Gas examination evaluates ten areas of trade knowledge. Understanding the examination content breakdown can help candidates organize their preparation and devote appropriate attention to important subjects.

Examination Content Breakdown:

  • General Plumbing Knowledge and Regulations - 10 Questions: Plumbing definitions, general code requirements, area and volume calculations, testing methods, structural penetrations, and pipe offsets.
  • Piping, Valves, and Control Valves - 8 Questions: Piping connections, cast iron, copper, steel, plastic piping, joining methods, and valves.
  • Fixtures and Equipment - 8 Questions: Plumbing fixture requirements, fixture installation, and water heater installations.
  • Water Supply - 10 Questions: Water supply and distribution piping, potable water protection, and backflow prevention.
  • Drains and Sewers - 10 Questions: Drainage piping, building sewers, cleanouts, drainage fixture units, and drain connections.
  • Vents - 13 Questions: Plumbing vent installation, wet venting, and associated venting requirements.
  • Traps, Interceptors, Indirect Waste, and Special Waste - 5 Questions: Traps, ejector pumps, and indirect waste applications.
  • Isometric Analysis - 7 Questions: Interpretation of plumbing isometric drawings and piping diagrams.
  • Fuel Gas - 10 Questions: Gas piping, valves, pipe sizing, supports, system testing, installation methods, joining procedures, and threaded connections.
  • Safety - 4 Questions: Construction safety practices, ladders, and fall protection systems.

Venting represents the largest individual examination category, followed by important subjects involving plumbing knowledge, water supply, drainage, and fuel gas systems.

Students should concentrate on understanding plumbing system relationships, interpreting technical drawings, solving mathematical problems, and applying code requirements to installation scenarios.

Because the examination is open book, reference-navigation practice is valuable. Working with the approved code edition and repeatedly reviewing relevant tables can help candidates develop a more efficient approach to finding technical information.
