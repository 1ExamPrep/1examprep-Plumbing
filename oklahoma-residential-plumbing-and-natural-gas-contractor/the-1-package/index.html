Exam Details

The Oklahoma Residential Contractor Plumbing and Natural Gas examination evaluates technical knowledge associated with residential plumbing systems, fuel gas installations, trade calculations, and construction safety.

The Oklahoma Construction Industries Board oversees plumbing contractor licensing, while PSI administers the applicable examination.

  • Exam Name: Oklahoma Residential Contractor Plumbing and Natural Gas
  • Licensing Authority: Oklahoma Construction Industries Board
  • Testing Provider: PSI
  • Scored Questions: 85
  • Time Allowed: 180 minutes
  • Passing Score: 75%
  • Minimum Correct Answers: 64
  • Examination Format: Open book

Technical preparation covers important subjects involving general plumbing knowledge, piping and valves, plumbing fixtures, water supply, sanitary drainage, venting, traps, isometric drawings, fuel gas systems, and workplace safety.

State Requirements

Residential plumbing and natural gas contracting in Oklahoma operates within the state's regulated plumbing industry. The Oklahoma Construction Industries Board administers licensing requirements under applicable plumbing laws and administrative regulations.

Plumbing contractor applicants must meet journeyman-level qualifications and complete an additional year of qualifying plumbing experience. The state's licensing framework also recognizes specified experience and approved educational pathways.

The residential contractor classification concerns authorized plumbing work involving water distribution, sanitary drainage, venting, fixtures, piping installations, and residential fuel gas systems.

Contractor licensing involves responsibilities beyond journeyman-level trade work. Active plumbing contractor licensing also requires applicable bond and insurance documentation.

Oklahoma's contractor activation requirements include a $5,000 corporate surety bond and proof of at least $50,000 in commercial general liability insurance, subject to applicable provisions and authorized alternatives.

Contractors should also understand business organization, tax identification, contract administration, workplace safety, and professional compliance responsibilities.
