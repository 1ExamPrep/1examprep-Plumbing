The Oklahoma Residential Contractor Plumbing and Natural Gas examination evaluates knowledge involving residential plumbing systems, fuel gas installations, technical calculations, and construction safety.

The Oklahoma Construction Industries Board administers plumbing contractor licensing, while PSI provides the applicable examinations.

Oklahoma adopted the 2024 International Residential Code with applicable state amendments effective September 14, 2026.

The Oklahoma Construction Industries Board established a code examination transition period from September 14 through December 14, 2026 for affected examinations, including Residential Contractor Plumbing and Natural Gas.

During this period, candidates may test using either the previously adopted or newly adopted code. Beginning December 15, 2026, affected examinations use the newly adopted codes exclusively.
