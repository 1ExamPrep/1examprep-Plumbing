Exam Details

The Oklahoma Residential Contractor Plumbing and Natural Gas examination evaluates technical knowledge associated with residential plumbing systems, natural gas installations, plumbing calculations, and construction safety.

The Oklahoma Construction Industries Board regulates plumbing contractor licensing, while PSI administers the examination.

  • Exam Name: Oklahoma Residential Contractor Plumbing and Natural Gas
  • Licensing Authority: Oklahoma Construction Industries Board
  • Testing Provider: PSI
  • Number of Scored Questions: 85
  • Time Allowed: 180 minutes
  • Passing Score: 75%
  • Minimum Correct Answers: 64
  • Examination Format: Open book

Important examination subjects include:

  • General Plumbing Knowledge: Plumbing terminology, installation principles, code provisions, and testing procedures.
  • Piping and Valves: Piping materials, fittings, valves, supports, and installation methods.
  • Fixtures and Equipment: Residential plumbing fixtures and associated equipment connections.
  • Water Supply: Distribution piping, water pressure, pipe sizing, and fixture supplies.
  • Drains and Sewers: Drainage piping, slopes, cleanouts, and sanitary sewer systems.
  • Venting: Vent sizing, connections, installation arrangements, and system requirements.
  • Traps and Waste Systems: Traps, interceptors, indirect waste, and related provisions.
  • Isometric Analysis: Plumbing drawings, piping arrangements, and technical calculations.
  • Fuel Gas: Gas piping, regulators, sizing, appliance connections, and testing.
  • Safety: Construction hazards and workplace protection requirements.

Candidates should understand how residential plumbing systems function and how applicable code requirements influence installation, inspection, maintenance, and repair.
