The CR-78 classification applies to Solar Plumbing, Liquid Systems Only work within its authorized residential and commercial scope.   • International Mechanical Code, 2018
    This model code covers mechanical-system requirements within its scope. It can support study of equipment, installation provisions, ventilation, combustion-related topics, hydronic systems, piping, and other mechanical considerations connected with contractor work.
  • Mathematics for Plumbers and Pipefitters, 8th Edition
    This trade-mathematics reference supports the calculation skills used by plumbing and piping professionals. Study areas may include whole numbers, fractions, decimals, percentages, measurement, geometry, area, volume, ratios, offsets, conversions, and applied jobsite calculations.

    This handbook provides practical technical information for pipe-trade work. It is useful for reviewing piping data, measurements, formulas, materials, fittings, layout concepts, and field-oriented information commonly used by plumbing and pipefitting professionals.

    This manual focuses on the principles, components, design considerations, and installation practices associated with solar water-heating and solar pool-heating systems. It supports preparation for solar thermal questions involving liquid-based systems and their operation.
