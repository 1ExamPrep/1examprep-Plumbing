State Requirements

The Arizona Registrar of Contractors issues commercial, residential, and dual contractor licenses. The CR-78 Solar Plumbing, Liquid Systems Only classification is a dual residential and commercial classification. It allows the scopes of work permitted by the commercial C-78 Solar Plumbing Liquid Systems Only license and the residential R-37R Solar Plumbing Liquid Systems Only license.

The C-78 commercial scope allows the licensee to install, alter, and repair solar water heating systems operating at temperatures not exceeding 220 degrees Fahrenheit, including thermosyphon, direct open-loop, and indirect closed-loop systems, but excludes air as a transfer medium. The scope also includes installation of solar heating systems for swimming pools that tie into and operate from the conventional pool system, while excluding non-solar plumbing, electrical, and mechanical systems and components.

Test Information and Study Materials

Effective CR-78 preparation should combine trade review, code study, math practice, solar system review, and open-book navigation. Students should begin by learning what each reference is used for. For safety topics, spend time with the OSHA construction standards. Solar plumbing work can involve ladders, roof-adjacent work, equipment handling, pipe installation, material handling, excavation-related work, and general jobsite hazards. Safety questions can be direct, so students should practice finding OSHA rules quickly.

For code topics, separate plumbing and mechanical study time. The International Plumbing Code and International Mechanical Code are large references, and students should practice finding definitions, chapters, tables, material requirements, system requirements, and installation provisions before test day. Code questions often depend on knowing where to look, not just knowing the general concept.

For math topics, review the calculations used in plumbing and pipefitting. Students should be comfortable with fractions, decimals, measurements, offsets, area, volume, basic geometry, pipe-related calculations, and formula use. Math confidence can save time during the exam and reduce second-guessing when a question requires a calculated answer.

For pipe trade topics, use IPT’s Pipe Trades Handbook to review fittings, valves, pipe materials, dimensions, symbols, flanges, threads, and reference tables. Solar plumbing liquid systems depend on correct pipe layout, system components, and installation details. Knowing how to find reference data quickly can help with both exam preparation and trade understanding.

For solar topics, focus on liquid-based solar water and pool heating systems. Students should review collectors, piping layouts, pumps, valves, controls, heat transfer, direct and indirect systems, open-loop and closed-loop systems, thermosyphon principles, freeze protection concepts, system operation, and troubleshooting.
