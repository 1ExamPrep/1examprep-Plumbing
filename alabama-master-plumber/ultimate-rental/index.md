## State Requirements

The **Alabama Plumbers and Gas Fitters Examining Board** oversees professional certification and registration for plumbing and gas fitting activities throughout Alabama.

To become eligible for Alabama Master Plumber certification through the standard in-state pathway, applicants must have at least one year of qualifying experience as a certified Journeyman Plumber.

Beginning January 1, 2026, master-level applicants must satisfy the applicable trade examination requirement and the additional Business Law examination requirement. The Board provides rules governing when an applicant who has already completed a qualifying Business Law examination must take it again.

Following successful completion of the required examinations, candidates must complete certification within the Board's applicable 90-calendar-day period.

Alabama also requires companies performing regulated plumbing work to register with the Board before bidding on or performing such work. Each registered company must have a qualified Principal Master responsible for its plumbing activities.

These requirements emphasize the distinction between individual professional certification and business registration. Understanding both responsibilities is important for experienced plumbers planning to operate or supervise plumbing contracting activities.
