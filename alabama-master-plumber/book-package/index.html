Exam Details

The Alabama Master Plumber examination is administered by Prov for applicants pursuing certification through the Alabama Plumbers and Gas Fitters Examining Board. The published examination outline identifies 100 questions with 4.17 hours allowed for completion.

  • Exam: Alabama Master Plumber
  • Testing Administrator: Prov
  • Number of Questions: 100
  • Time Allowed: 4.17 hours
  • Passing Score: 70%
  • Exam Format: Computerized examination
  • Reference Format: Open book using authorized references

The published Master Plumber examination outline covers multiple subject areas. General Knowledge includes 14 questions, Drawings and Plan Reading includes 8 questions, Sizing and Design includes 15 questions, System Installation includes 15 questions, Trade Math includes 6 questions, Tools includes 5 questions, Water Heaters includes 8 questions, Roof Drain Systems includes 6 questions, Fixtures and Fittings includes 6 questions, Drain Waste and Vents includes 6 questions, Safety includes 5 questions, and Alabama Plumbing and Gas Law includes 6 questions.

This broad examination structure means candidates should prepare across both code-based and practical plumbing topics. Sizing and design, installation, general knowledge, and water heaters represent substantial portions of the exam, but success also requires attention to plan reading, calculations, drainage, venting, fixtures, safety, and state law.

Effective January 1, 2026, Alabama requires master-level applicants to successfully complete an additional Business and Law examination before obtaining master certification. The Business and Law examination is separate from the Master Plumber technical exam and has its own preparation requirements and reference materials.

Open Book Test

The Alabama Master Plumber examination is an open book test. Candidates may use authorized reference materials during the examination in accordance with Prov testing rules. This makes familiarity with the books an important part of preparation.

An open-book exam should not be approached as though every question can simply be researched from scratch. With 100 questions and a limited testing period, candidates need to know which reference is likely to contain the answer, where important sections are located, and how to move efficiently between chapters, tables, indexes, and definitions.

Permanent tabs, careful highlighting, and repeated code-navigation practice can make authorized references easier to use. Candidates should organize their study books before test day and become comfortable locating common plumbing subjects such as drainage requirements, water distribution provisions, fixture rules, venting requirements, water heaters, pipe sizing, fuel gas provisions, and Alabama regulatory material.

Prov's reference-material rules permit certain preparation of authorized books while restricting materials that could introduce unauthorized information. Candidates should follow the current testing rules when preparing their references and ensure their books remain compliant with exam-room requirements.

Licensing Steps

Master Plumber certification in Alabama is regulated by the Alabama Plumbers and Gas Fitters Examining Board. Candidates must progress through the Board's certification structure and satisfy its experience and examination requirements before becoming certified at the master level.

  1. Hold Journeyman Plumber certification. Alabama requires an individual seeking eligibility for the Master Plumber examination to be certified as a Journeyman Plumber for at least one year.
  2. Complete the Master examination application. Applicants must use the appropriate master-level examination application and provide the information required by the Board.
  3. Document current experience. The current Master examination application requires one year of current work experience as a journeyman for the Master Plumber examination.
  4. Receive authorization to test. After the Board processes the application and determines eligibility, the applicant receives examination eligibility information for scheduling.
  5. Schedule and take the Master Plumber examination. Eligible candidates schedule their examination through Prov and complete the required technical exam.
  6. Pass the Business and Law examination. Effective January 1, 2026, master-level applicants must also successfully complete the separate Business and Law examination.
  7. Complete certification requirements. After passing the required examinations, the applicant must complete the Board's certification process within the applicable timeframe.

The Board states that candidates who pass an examination have 90 calendar days to obtain certification. Candidates should complete the required post-examination steps promptly so that a passed examination does not expire under the Board's certification rules.

State Requirements

Alabama law requires plumbing work to be performed within the certification structure established by the Alabama Plumbers and Gas Fitters Examining Board. The state recognizes apprentice, journeyman, and master classifications, with progressively greater qualification and examination requirements.

A Master Plumber is a person or legal entity that engages in or works at the actual installation, repair, or replacement of plumbing systems and has successfully satisfied the examination and certification requirements of the Board. A certified Master Plumber may also register with the Board as the principal for a plumbing business, firm, or corporation.

To become eligible to take the Alabama Master Plumber examination, an individual must be certified as a Journeyman Plumber for a minimum of one year. Current master-level examination materials also identify one year of current journeyman work experience as part of the application requirement.

As of January 1, 2026, all master-level certification applicants must successfully complete both the applicable Master Plumber or Master Gas Fitter examination and the separate Business and Law examination.

Businesses performing plumbing or gas-fitting work in Alabama are also subject to registration requirements. The Board requires companies performing regulated work to register and maintain a Principal Master designee responsible for the work performed by the business. Individual certification and company registration are separate regulatory requirements.

Alabama also requires continuing education for applicable journeyman and master certificate holders. Continuing education requirements became effective beginning with renewal requirements associated with 2026 certifications, subject to exemptions established by the Board's rules.

Test Information and Study Materials

The Alabama Master Plumber exam covers a wide range of subjects, so an organized study plan is more effective than simply reading the books from beginning to end. Start by dividing your preparation according to the published examination categories and devote additional practice to the subjects carrying the greatest number of questions.

Sizing and Design and System Installation each account for 15 questions, while General Knowledge accounts for 14. These three areas alone represent a substantial portion of the test. Candidates should be comfortable working with plumbing-system sizing, installation requirements, code tables, materials, fittings, general trade practices, and the relationships between different plumbing system components.

Drawings and Plan Reading requires candidates to interpret plumbing information from plans and related technical documents. Preparation should include identifying plumbing symbols, recognizing system layouts, understanding dimensions, and connecting drawings with code requirements and installation practices.

Trade Math preparation should focus on becoming comfortable with the mathematical procedures used in plumbing and pipefitting work. Regular calculation practice can help candidates work more efficiently and reduce mistakes during a timed examination.

Drain Waste and Vents, Fixtures and Fittings, Roof Drain Systems, and Water Heaters each involve technical requirements that can be located within the plumbing code and related references. Candidates should learn where these topics appear in the books and practice moving between definitions, general provisions, sizing tables, installation requirements, and exceptions.

Safety and Tools require practical trade familiarity in addition to code knowledge. Modern Plumbing can be particularly useful for reviewing broader trade concepts, equipment, terminology, and installation methods that may not be presented as instructional material in a code book.

The Alabama Plumbing and Gas Law portion should not be overlooked. Review Title 34, Chapter 37 and the applicable administrative rules so that you understand the state-specific material associated with certification, Board authority, regulated work, and professional responsibilities.
