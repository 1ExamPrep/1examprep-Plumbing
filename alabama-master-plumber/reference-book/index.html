Exam Details

The Alabama Master Plumber examination is administered through Prov for candidates seeking certification through the Alabama Plumbers and Gas Fitters Examining Board. The Master Plumber examination is open book and timed, allowing candidates to use authorized references during testing.

Alabama requires applicants to complete the appropriate application with the Alabama Plumbers and Gas Fitters Examining Board before scheduling the examination. Once an applicant is approved for testing, the candidate can proceed with scheduling through Prov.

The Master Plumber examination contains 100 questions and allows 4.17 hours for completion. A passing score of 70% is required. The examination covers a broad range of plumbing subjects and requires candidates to understand both practical trade concepts and code-based requirements.

Sizing and design questions may require knowledge of tables, calculations, system layout, and applicable technical requirements. Installation questions may involve materials, fittings, system arrangement, accepted practices, and code compliance. Other sections test familiarity with common plumbing components, drainage, venting, safety, tools, and Alabama-specific laws and regulations.

As of January 1, 2026, master-level applicants must also successfully complete a separate Business and Law examination. This requirement applies in addition to the applicable Master Plumber examination and forms part of the current path toward master-level certification.

Licensing Steps

The Alabama Plumbers and Gas Fitters Examining Board oversees Master Plumber certification in the state. Candidates must complete the required application and examination steps before master certification can be issued.

  1. Meet the Master Plumber eligibility requirement. Alabama requires an individual seeking eligibility for the Master Plumber examination to be certified as a Journeyman Plumber for a minimum of one year.
  2. Complete the current Master examination application. Applicants must submit the appropriate master-level application to the Alabama Plumbers and Gas Fitters Examining Board.
  3. Provide the required application information. The application process requires candidates to provide the documentation and information requested by the Board.
  4. Receive approval to test. The Board reviews the application and determines examination eligibility. Candidates must be approved before scheduling their examination.
  5. Schedule the examination through Prov. Approved applicants can schedule the applicable examination with Prov.
  6. Complete the Master Plumber examination. Candidates must earn the required passing score on the technical Master Plumber exam.
  7. Complete the Business and Law examination. Effective January 1, 2026, master-level applicants must also pass the separate Business and Law examination.
  8. Complete the certification process. After satisfying the examination requirements, candidates must complete the Board's required steps to obtain their Master Plumber certification.

The Alabama Board states that candidates who pass the required examination have 90 calendar days to obtain certification. Completing the post-exam certification process promptly is therefore an important part of the overall licensing path.

State Requirements

Plumbing and gas fitting in Alabama are regulated by the Alabama Plumbers and Gas Fitters Examining Board. The state uses apprentice, journeyman, and master certification classifications, with applicants progressing through the applicable experience and examination requirements.

To become eligible for the Alabama Master Plumber examination, an individual must be certified as a Journeyman Plumber for at least one year. The Master Plumber classification applies to individuals who have fulfilled the Board's examination requirements and hold a valid, current master plumber certificate.

A Master Plumber may also register with the Board as the principal for a business, firm, or corporation. Alabama business registration requirements are separate from an individual's master-level certification and apply when operating within the state's regulated plumbing structure.

Beginning January 1, 2026, applicants pursuing a new master-level certification must complete both the applicable master trade exam and a Business and Law examination. Candidates preparing for the Master Plumber examination should account for both requirements when planning their certification path.

After passing the required examination, candidates must complete the certification process within the timeframe established by the Board. The Board identifies a 90-calendar-day period following successful examination completion for obtaining certification.

Test Information and Study Materials

Master Plumber candidates should build a study plan around the published exam subjects rather than treating every chapter and every book equally. Start with major areas such as sizing and design, system installation, general plumbing knowledge, water heaters, drainage, and venting, then build additional review around plan reading, trade math, tools, safety, and Alabama law.

For sizing and design, practice locating tables and understanding how code requirements affect plumbing system capacity and layout. Learn to read table headings, notes, definitions, and related provisions rather than focusing on individual numbers without context.

For system installation, review requirements for materials, joints, fittings, supports, equipment, piping, drainage components, vents, fixtures, and water distribution systems. Knowing how technical provisions fit together can make code questions easier to evaluate.

Trade mathematics deserves regular practice. Work with the types of calculations used by plumbers and pipefitters so that formulas, conversions, measurements, and problem setup become familiar. Mathematical proficiency is also useful when working with sizing and design questions.

Plan-reading preparation should focus on recognizing plumbing system layouts, dimensions, symbols, and the relationship between a drawing and the technical requirements governing the installation. Practical experience is valuable, but deliberate review can strengthen the ability to interpret information under exam conditions.

For the open-book portion of preparation, practice locating answers in the actual references. Use the index, chapter organization, definitions, and permanent navigation methods allowed under current testing rules. The objective is to know both the subject matter and where supporting information is located.
