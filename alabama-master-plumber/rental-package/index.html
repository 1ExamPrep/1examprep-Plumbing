Exam Details

The Alabama Master Plumber examination is administered by Prov for the Alabama Plumbers and Gas Fitters Examining Board. The examination evaluates professional plumbing knowledge, technical calculations, installation practices, and familiarity with applicable plumbing requirements.

The Master Plumber trade examination and Plumbing Business and Law examination are separate tests covering different professional knowledge areas.

Alabama Master Plumber Trade Examination

  • Exam Name: Alabama Master Plumber Examination
  • Testing Provider: Prov
  • Exam Format: Computerized, open book
  • Number of Questions: 100
  • Time Allowed: Approximately 250 minutes
  • Passing Score: 70%

The examination evaluates general plumbing knowledge, plan reading, sizing and design, system installation, trade mathematics, tools, water heaters, roof drainage, fixtures and fittings, drainage and venting, and safety.

General knowledge, system installation, and sizing and design represent particularly important examination subjects. However, effective preparation involves reviewing the complete examination content rather than concentrating only on the largest categories.

Alabama Plumbing Business and Law Examination

  • Exam Name: Alabama Plumbing Business and Law Examination
  • Testing Provider: Prov
  • Exam Format: Open book
  • Number of Questions: 40
  • Time Allowed: 2 hours
  • Passing Score: 70%

The Business and Law examination addresses business organization, construction contracts, estimating, financial management, licensing requirements, employment responsibilities, project management, risk management, and relevant Alabama laws.

Both examinations contribute to the knowledge requirements associated with Master Plumber certification, making preparation in technical and business subjects an important part of the process.

Licensing Steps

Obtaining Alabama Master Plumber certification involves professional experience, application procedures, required examinations, and certification through the Alabama Plumbers and Gas Fitters Examining Board.

  1. Meet the Experience Requirements: Applicants following the standard Alabama certification pathway must be certified as a Journeyman Plumber for at least one year and satisfy the applicable work experience requirements.
  2. Review Certification Requirements: Become familiar with the Alabama Plumbers and Gas Fitters Examining Board's eligibility and examination procedures.
  3. Submit the Examination Application: Complete the required Master Plumber examination application and supporting documentation.
  4. Receive Examination Authorization: The Board reviews the application and determines eligibility for testing.
  5. Prepare for Both Examinations: Study plumbing technical subjects and Business and Law material using the rental reference books and included course access.
  6. Schedule Testing: Arrange the required examinations through Prov after receiving authorization.
  7. Complete the Examinations: Pass the applicable Master Plumber trade and Business and Law examinations.
  8. Obtain Certification: Complete the Board's certification procedures within the applicable 90-calendar-day period following successful examination completion.
  9. Maintain Your Credentials: Follow Alabama's applicable annual certification and professional responsibilities.

State Requirements

The Alabama Plumbers and Gas Fitters Examining Board regulates plumbing and gas fitting certification throughout Alabama.

Individuals pursuing Master Plumber certification through the standard in-state pathway must meet the Board's professional experience requirements, including at least one year as a certified Journeyman Plumber.

Effective January 1, 2026, master-level applicants must also complete the applicable Business Law examination in addition to the trade examination, subject to the Board's rules concerning previously completed examinations.

Alabama requires companies performing regulated plumbing activities to register with the Board. Registered businesses must have an appropriately certified Principal Master responsible for their plumbing operations.

Individual Master Plumber certification and business registration serve different purposes. Certification establishes an individual's professional qualifications, while business registration addresses the requirements associated with operating a plumbing company.

Understanding these responsibilities can support professional development for plumbers pursuing supervisory positions or planning to establish their own contracting businesses.
