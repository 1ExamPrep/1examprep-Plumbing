The Master Plumber trade examination and Plumbing Business and Law examination are separate tests covering different professional knowledge areas.

**Alabama Master Plumber Trade Examination**

- **Exam Name:** Alabama Master Plumber Examination
- **Testing Provider:** Prov

The examination evaluates general plumbing knowledge, plan reading, sizing and design, system installation, trade mathematics, tools, water heaters, roof drainage, fixtures and fittings, drainage and venting, and safety.

## State Requirements

The **Alabama Plumbers and Gas Fitters Examining Board** regulates plumbing and gas fitting certification throughout Alabama.

Individuals pursuing Master Plumber certification through the standard in-state pathway must meet the Board's professional experience requirements, including at least one year as a certified Journeyman Plumber.

Effective January 1, 2026, master-level applicants must also complete the applicable Business Law examination in addition to the trade examination, subject to the Board's rules concerning previously completed examinations.

Alabama requires companies performing regulated plumbing activities to register with the Board. Registered businesses must have an appropriately certified Principal Master responsible for their plumbing operations.

Individual Master Plumber certification and business registration serve different purposes. Certification establishes an individual's professional qualifications, while business registration addresses the requirements associated with operating a plumbing company.

Understanding these responsibilities can support professional development for plumbers pursuing supervisory positions or planning to establish their own contracting businesses.
