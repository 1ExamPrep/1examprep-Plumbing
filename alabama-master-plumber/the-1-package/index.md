## Exam Details

Alabama Master Plumber certification requires successful completion of the applicable trade examination and, for applicants subject to the requirements effective January 1, 2026, the Business Law examination.

Both examinations are administered by **Prov** for the **Alabama Plumbers and Gas Fitters Examining Board**.

**Alabama Master Plumber Trade Examination**

- **Exam Name:** Alabama Master Plumber Examination
- **Testing Provider:** Prov
- **Exam Format:** Computerized, open book
- **Number of Questions:** 100

The Business and Law examination covers business organization, contracts, estimating, financial management, labor laws, licensing laws, lien laws, project management, risk management, safety and environmental responsibilities, Board rules, and payroll and employment taxes.

## State Requirements

Alabama plumbing certification is regulated by the **Alabama Plumbers and Gas Fitters Examining Board**.

Applicants seeking Master Plumber certification must satisfy the applicable professional experience requirements and complete the examinations established by the Board.

Under the standard certification pathway, candidates must hold Journeyman Plumber certification and demonstrate at least one year of current qualifying experience.

Effective January 1, 2026, new master-level applicants must successfully complete both the applicable trade examination and the Business Law examination.

Alabama also requires businesses performing regulated plumbing activities to register with the Board. Plumbing businesses must maintain an appropriately certified Principal Master responsible for their professional plumbing operations.

Establishing an LLC or corporation and obtaining professional plumbing certification are separate processes. Business formation creates a legal entity, while certification and registration address the professional authorization required for regulated plumbing activities.
