## Exam Details

The **Alabama Master Plumber examination** is administered by Prov on behalf of the Alabama Plumbers and Gas Fitters Examining Board. The examination measures professional knowledge related to plumbing installation, system design, trade calculations, materials, and applicable construction requirements.

The Master Plumber trade examination is computer-based and open book. Candidates may use authorized references during testing while following the examination provider's rules.

- **Exam Name:** Alabama Master Plumber Examination
- **Licensing Authority:** Alabama Plumbers and Gas Fitters Examining Board
- **Testing Provider:** Prov
- **Exam Format:** Computerized, open book
- **Number of Questions:** 100
- **Time Allowed:** 4.17 hours, approximately 250 minutes
- **Passing Score:** 70%
- **Exam Subjects:** Plumbing systems, code requirements, design, installation, calculations, fixtures, drainage, venting, and safety

The examination evaluates multiple areas of professional plumbing knowledge. Major subjects include general plumbing principles, drawings and plan reading, sizing and design, installation practices, trade mathematics, tools, water heaters, roof drainage, fixtures and fittings, drainage and venting, and workplace safety.

Two important examination categories are **Sizing and Design** and **System Installation**, each accounting for 16 questions. General Knowledge accounts for another 15 questions. Together, these three subjects represent a substantial portion of the examination.

Candidates should build familiarity with all examination topics rather than concentrating exclusively on the largest categories. Plumbing mathematics, fixture requirements, water heaters, and drainage systems are also important components of professional plumbing knowledge.

**Important for 2026 Applicants:** Beginning January 1, 2026, Alabama requires master-level applicants to successfully complete an additional Business Law examination to obtain master certification. The Business Law examination is separate from the Master Plumber trade examination and uses its own authorized reference materials.

2. **Complete the Examination Application:** Submit the appropriate Master Plumber examination application and required documentation to the Alabama Plumbers and Gas Fitters Examining Board.

7. **Complete Certification:** Following successful completion of the required examinations, obtain certification through the Board within the applicable 90-calendar-day period.
