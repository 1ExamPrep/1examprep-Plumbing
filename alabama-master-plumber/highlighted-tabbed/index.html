Exam Details

The Alabama Master Plumber examination is administered by Prov on behalf of the Alabama Plumbers and Gas Fitters Examining Board. The examination measures professional knowledge related to plumbing installation, system design, trade calculations, materials, and applicable construction requirements.

The Master Plumber trade examination is computer-based and open book. Candidates may use authorized references during testing while following the examination provider's rules.

  • Exam Name: Alabama Master Plumber Examination
  • Licensing Authority: Alabama Plumbers and Gas Fitters Examining Board
  • Testing Provider: Prov
  • Exam Format: Computerized, open book
  • Number of Questions: 100
  • Time Allowed: 4.17 hours, approximately 250 minutes
  • Passing Score: 70%
  • Exam Subjects: Plumbing systems, code requirements, design, installation, calculations, fixtures, drainage, venting, and safety

The examination evaluates multiple areas of professional plumbing knowledge. Major subjects include general plumbing principles, drawings and plan reading, sizing and design, installation practices, trade mathematics, tools, water heaters, roof drainage, fixtures and fittings, drainage and venting, and workplace safety.

Two important examination categories are Sizing and Design and System Installation, each accounting for 16 questions. General Knowledge accounts for another 15 questions. Together, these three subjects represent a substantial portion of the examination.

Candidates should build familiarity with all examination topics rather than concentrating exclusively on the largest categories. Plumbing mathematics, fixture requirements, water heaters, and drainage systems are also important components of professional plumbing knowledge.

Important for 2026 Applicants: Beginning January 1, 2026, Alabama requires master-level applicants to successfully complete an additional Business Law examination to obtain master certification. The Business Law examination is separate from the Master Plumber trade examination and uses its own authorized reference materials.

Licensing Steps

Becoming an Alabama Master Plumber involves meeting the state's professional eligibility requirements, completing the examination process, and obtaining certification through the Alabama Plumbers and Gas Fitters Examining Board.

  1. Meet the Experience Requirement: Applicants must hold Alabama Journeyman Plumber certification and have at least one year of qualifying current work experience as a Journeyman Plumber.
  2. Complete the Examination Application: Submit the appropriate Master Plumber examination application and required documentation to the Alabama Plumbers and Gas Fitters Examining Board.
  3. Receive Examination Authorization: The Board reviews eligibility and provides instructions for scheduling the required examinations.
  4. Prepare Your Study Materials: Use the highlighted and tabbed reference books to review plumbing regulations, construction codes, installation principles, and trade mathematics.
  5. Schedule the Examination: Once authorized, arrange the Master Plumber examination through Prov.
  6. Pass the Required Examinations: Complete the Master Plumber trade examination and the additional Business Law examination required for master-level certification.
  7. Complete Certification: Following successful completion of the required examinations, obtain certification through the Board within the applicable 90-calendar-day period.
  8. Maintain Your Professional Credentials: Follow the Board's applicable annual certification and professional registration requirements.

Organizing your exam preparation before scheduling testing allows you to review the references thoroughly and develop familiarity with the examination subjects.

State Requirements

The Alabama Plumbers and Gas Fitters Examining Board regulates plumbing and gas fitting professionals throughout Alabama. Individuals seeking Master Plumber certification must satisfy the Board's eligibility, examination, and certification requirements.

Applicants pursuing Master Plumber certification generally progress through the state's established plumbing certification structure, including qualifying experience as a certified Journeyman Plumber.

Master Plumber applicants must demonstrate the knowledge required for advanced plumbing responsibilities, including planning, laying out, and supervising plumbing installation and construction work.

Beginning January 1, 2026, applicants seeking master-level certification must satisfy both the applicable trade examination and Business Law examination requirements.

Alabama also regulates plumbing businesses. Companies performing covered plumbing work must register with the Board and maintain an appropriately certified Principal Master responsible for their plumbing operations.

These requirements emphasize the importance of combining technical plumbing knowledge with an understanding of professional responsibilities.

Test Information and Study Materials

Preparing for the Alabama Master Plumber examination requires understanding important trade concepts and practicing how to apply technical information.

Plumbing Codes and Regulations

Begin your preparation with the International Plumbing Code and the Alabama regulatory references. Review how code provisions are organized and understand the relationship between construction standards and professional plumbing responsibilities.

Become familiar with terminology, code chapters, and technical requirements affecting plumbing systems.

Water Supply and Distribution

Study water distribution principles, pipe sizing, system layout, fittings, and installation requirements. Understanding how plumbing systems provide water to fixtures and equipment is an important part of professional plumbing knowledge.

Drainage and Venting

Review drainage systems, traps, venting methods, piping arrangements, and installation practices. Learn how system components work together and how plumbing codes regulate drainage and venting installations.

Plumbing Mathematics

Practice measurements, calculations, geometry, and mathematical applications involving plumbing systems. Use Mathematics for Plumbers and Pipefitters to reinforce fundamental calculations and improve problem-solving skills.

Fixtures and Water Heaters

Study plumbing fixture requirements, water heater installations, piping connections, and related code provisions. Focus on understanding the technical relationships between equipment, fixtures, and plumbing systems.

Construction Drawings

Develop familiarity with plumbing plans, drawings, dimensions, and symbols. Practice interpreting technical information used in plumbing system layout and installation.

Tools and Safety

Review common plumbing tools, equipment, safe work practices, and installation considerations. Understanding proper equipment use supports both examination preparation and professional jobsite responsibilities.

Developing a Study Routine

Create a study schedule that includes technical reading, reference navigation, and regular review. Divide large subjects into manageable study sessions and spend additional time on topics that require improvement.
