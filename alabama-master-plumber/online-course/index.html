Exam Details

The Alabama Master Plumber examination is administered by Prov on behalf of the Alabama Plumbers and Gas Fitters Examining Board. The examination evaluates advanced plumbing knowledge, practical installation principles, mathematical calculations, and familiarity with applicable plumbing regulations.

Understanding the examination format and subject areas helps candidates establish a productive study strategy.

  • Exam Name: Alabama Master Plumber Examination
  • Licensing Authority: Alabama Plumbers and Gas Fitters Examining Board
  • Testing Provider: Prov
  • Exam Format: Computerized, open book
  • Number of Questions: 100
  • Time Allowed: 4.17 hours, approximately 250 minutes
  • Primary Subjects: General plumbing knowledge, installation, sizing, design, trade mathematics, fixtures, drainage, venting, water heaters, and safety

The Alabama Master Plumber trade examination covers eleven subject areas. The examination content includes:

  • General Knowledge: 15 questions
  • Drawings and Plan Reading: 9 questions
  • Sizing and Design: 16 questions
  • System Installation: 16 questions
  • Trade Mathematics: 7 questions
  • Tools: 5 questions
  • Water Heaters: 9 questions
  • Roof Drain Systems: 6 questions
  • Fixtures and Fittings: 6 questions
  • Drain, Waste, and Vents: 6 questions
  • Safety: 5 questions

Sizing and design, system installation, and general plumbing knowledge represent 47 questions, making these important areas for focused review. However, candidates should study the complete examination outline, including mathematics, plan reading, water heaters, and other technical subjects.

2026 Examination Requirement: Effective January 1, 2026, applicants seeking Alabama master-level certification must also pass a separate Business Law examination. This requirement applies in addition to the Master Plumber trade examination.

The Business Law examination contains 40 questions and allows 2 hours for completion. It addresses business organization, contracts, estimating, financial management, licensing laws, project management, and related professional responsibilities.

The Alabama Master Plumber examination is an Open Book Test. Candidates may use authorized reference materials during testing, subject to the rules established by Prov.

Although reference materials are allowed, an open-book examination requires strong preparation. Candidates must understand plumbing principles and know how to locate technical information efficiently within the available examination time.

Licensing Steps

Obtaining Alabama Master Plumber certification requires meeting the state's eligibility requirements, completing the examination process, and receiving certification through the Alabama Plumbers and Gas Fitters Examining Board.

  1. Meet the Journeyman Experience Requirement: Applicants seeking Master Plumber certification must have at least one year of qualifying current experience as an Alabama-certified Journeyman Plumber.
  2. Review the Master Plumber Requirements: Become familiar with the certification rules established by the Alabama Plumbers and Gas Fitters Examining Board.
  3. Submit the Examination Application: Complete the required Master Plumber examination application and provide the applicable supporting documentation.
  4. Receive Examination Eligibility: The Board reviews the application and provides examination scheduling authorization to eligible candidates.
  5. Prepare for the Trade Examination: Use organized study resources to review plumbing codes, system design, technical calculations, and installation requirements.
  6. Complete the Business Law Requirement: Prepare for and pass the additional Business Law examination required for master-level certification.
  7. Schedule Testing Through Prov: After receiving authorization, schedule the required examinations through the designated testing provider.
  8. Complete the Certification Process: Following successful completion of both examinations, obtain certification from the Board within the applicable 90-calendar-day period.

Following a consistent study routine can help candidates prepare for the technical portions of the examination while managing the administrative requirements associated with certification.

State Requirements

Alabama regulates plumbing professionals through the Alabama Plumbers and Gas Fitters Examining Board. The Board establishes certification requirements for individuals performing plumbing and gas fitting activities within the state.

To qualify for the Alabama Master Plumber examination through the standard in-state progression, applicants must hold Journeyman Plumber certification for at least one year and meet the applicable current work experience requirement.

The Master Plumber certification represents a higher level of professional responsibility and is associated with advanced plumbing work, supervision, contracting, and business operations.

Effective January 1, 2026, the state requires master-level applicants to complete both the appropriate trade examination and a Business Law examination.

Alabama also requires companies performing plumbing work to register with the Board. Registered businesses must designate an appropriately certified Principal Master responsible for the plumbing operations of the company.

This distinction between individual certification and business registration is important for professionals planning to operate their own plumbing contracting business.

Developing knowledge of both technical plumbing requirements and professional business responsibilities can support long-term career development.

Reference Books

The Alabama Master Plumber trade examination uses several reference materials addressing plumbing codes, technical calculations, installation methods, and Alabama regulatory requirements.

The following six references form the study-material foundation for the Alabama Master Plumber Contractor Online Exam Prep:

  • 2022 Code of Alabama, Title 34, Chapter 37, Plumbers and Gas Fitters
    This statutory reference covers Alabama laws governing plumbers and gas fitters. It addresses professional certification, regulatory responsibilities, and the legal framework associated with plumbing activities in Alabama. Familiarity with these provisions helps candidates understand their professional obligations.
  • Administrative Code Chapter 720, 2018
    This reference contains administrative rules established by the Alabama Plumbers and Gas Fitters Examining Board. It covers certification procedures, regulatory requirements, and professional responsibilities. Reviewing these rules supports preparation for questions involving Alabama plumbing regulations.
  • International Fuel Gas Code, 2021
    The 2021 International Fuel Gas Code addresses fuel gas piping systems, appliance installations, equipment connections, and applicable safety requirements. It provides technical information concerning fuel gas systems and construction standards relevant to plumbing professionals.
  • International Plumbing Code, 2021
    This plumbing code addresses water distribution, sanitary drainage, venting, fixtures, water heaters, and technical installation requirements. It is an important reference for understanding plumbing system design, code provisions, and professional installation practices.
  • Mathematics for Plumbers and Pipefitters, 8th Edition
    This trade mathematics textbook provides instruction in mathematical principles used in plumbing and pipefitting. Topics include measurements, geometry, calculations, and practical mathematical applications involving piping systems and construction work.
  • Modern Plumbing, 8th Edition
    Modern Plumbing introduces plumbing materials, tools, system components, installation methods, fixtures, and practical trade principles. It supports an understanding of plumbing construction and reinforces the technical concepts used throughout the profession.

Students should become familiar with the organization of these references and the types of information covered by each book. Understanding which material addresses a particular plumbing subject can make technical study sessions more productive.

The separate Business Law examination also uses additional references, including the NASCLA Contractors Guide to Business, Law and Project Management, 14th Edition, and the applicable Alabama supplement.

Test Information and Study Materials

Effective Alabama Master Plumber exam preparation involves more than reading plumbing codes. Candidates should understand how plumbing systems function, how technical requirements apply to installations, and how to interpret questions involving construction drawings and mathematical calculations.

General Plumbing Knowledge

Review the fundamentals of plumbing systems, materials, installation practices, and professional terminology. Understanding basic principles provides a foundation for more advanced examination topics.

Plumbing System Sizing and Design

Study water supply systems, drainage sizing, venting requirements, pipe dimensions, and the factors affecting system performance. Become familiar with applicable code tables and methods used when planning plumbing installations.

System Installation

Review installation techniques, plumbing materials, piping connections, and construction requirements. Develop an understanding of how installation practices relate to code provisions and properly functioning plumbing systems.

Trade Mathematics

Practice measurements, dimensional calculations, geometric principles, and mathematical applications involving plumbing installations. Technical calculations are an important component of professional plumbing knowledge.

Drawings and Plan Reading

Develop familiarity with plumbing drawings, construction plans, diagrams, and symbols. Practice interpreting dimensions and understanding how plumbing system information is represented in construction documents.

Water Heaters and Fixtures

Study water heater installations, equipment connections, fixture requirements, and the technical considerations associated with water distribution and fixture placement.

Drainage, Waste, and Venting

Review sanitary drainage systems, traps, vents, piping arrangements, and applicable installation principles. Understanding the relationship between drainage and venting components is essential for professional plumbing practice.

Plumbing Safety and Tools

Become familiar with common plumbing tools, equipment, safe installation techniques, and workplace safety principles. Review the importance of safe practices during installation, maintenance, and repair activities.
