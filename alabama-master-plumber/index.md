### Is the Master Plumber exam open book?

It depends on the exam. Many plumbing trade exams allow the code book, but some are closed book. Your candidate bulletin lists the references allowed; if books are allowed, highlighted and tabbed sets save the most time.

### Who issues the Master Plumber license in Alabama?

It comes from the Alabama Plumbers and Gas Fitters Examining Board. Buying a package here does not register you or make you eligible; the board approves you first, and the exam vendor schedules the test.
