Exam Details

The Maryland Journey Plumber-Gas Fitter examination is administered by PSI Services LLC for the Maryland State Board of Plumbing.

The examination evaluates trade knowledge involving plumbing installations, water supply systems, backflow prevention, drainage, venting, gas appliances, piping, and mathematics.

  • Exam Name: Maryland Journey Plumber/Gas Fitter
  • Testing Provider: PSI Services LLC
  • Licensing Authority: Maryland State Board of Plumbing
  • Examination Format: Multiple-choice questions
  • Number of Questions: 100
  • Time Limit: 240 minutes (4 hours)
  • Passing Score: 70%
  • Exam Type: Open book
  • Online Practice Access: 3 months

The PSI Journey Plumber/Gas Fitter content outline divides the examination into nine technical categories:

  • Plumbing Fundamentals and Safety – 8 Questions: Plumbing principles, terminology, general regulations, and safety practices.
  • Materials, Uses, and Specifications – 6 Questions: Plumbing materials, fittings, joining methods, and installation requirements.
  • Traps, Interceptors, and Backwater Valves – 4 Questions: Fixture traps, interceptors, drainage protection, and backwater prevention.
  • Plumbing Fixtures, Fixture Fittings, and Plumbing Appliances – 4 Questions: Fixture installation, connections, and applicable requirements.
  • Water Supply Systems – 8 Questions: Water distribution, piping, supply protection, and installation principles.
  • Backflow Prevention – 10 Questions: Cross-connection protection and backflow prevention equipment.
  • Drain, Waste, and Vent Systems – 13 Questions: Sanitary drainage, venting, drainage piping, and system requirements.
  • Gas Appliances and Piping – 40 Questions: Fuel gas installations, appliance connections, piping, combustion air, and venting.
  • Plumbing Mathematics – 7 Questions: Trade calculations, dimensions, quantities, and mathematical problem-solving.

Gas Appliances and Piping is the largest examination category, accounting for 40% of the exam. Drain, Waste, and Vent Systems accounts for 13 questions, while Backflow Prevention represents 10 questions.

Understanding this distribution helps prioritize study time. Candidates should develop strong fuel gas knowledge while maintaining familiarity with every plumbing category.

Open Book Test

The Maryland Journey Plumber-Gas Fitter (PSI) examination is an open book test. Candidates may bring approved reference materials into the examination room under PSI testing rules.

The examination allows four hours for 100 questions. Knowing how to navigate approved references can help you manage your testing time more efficiently.

The approved reference materials include:

  • International Plumbing Code, 2018
  • International Fuel Gas Code, 2018
  • Mathematics for Plumbers and Pipefitters, 8th Edition, by Lee Smith
  • Any standard backflow text
  • Code of Federal Regulations - 29 CFR Part 1926 (OSHA), with current amendments, or the permitted PSI selections

PSI permits approved reference books containing highlighting, underlining, and acceptable indexing tabs. Books must not contain handwritten notes, additional loose papers, or attached study materials. Study guides are not permitted inside the testing room.

Reference materials are not supplied at the testing center, making familiarity with the books an important part of examination preparation.

Effective open-book study strategies include learning the organization of the plumbing and fuel gas codes, practicing reference navigation, reviewing important tables, and developing confidence interpreting technical requirements.

Licensing Steps

Maryland plumber and gas fitter licensing is regulated by the Maryland State Board of Plumbing. Applicants must meet the appropriate training and examination qualifications before receiving a journey-level license.

  1. Register as an Apprentice: Obtain the applicable Maryland apprentice plumber/gas fitter license.
  2. Complete Qualifying Training: Accumulate the required apprenticeship experience under the direction and control of a licensed master plumber.
  3. Complete Backflow Education: Fulfill the required backflow prevention device testing training.
  4. Document Your Qualifications: Organize apprenticeship records, experience verification, and training documentation.
  5. Apply Through PSI: Submit the examination application and supporting information through the online PSI examination system.
  6. Prepare for the Examination: Review the approved references and practice the nine examination subject areas.
  7. Schedule and Take the Test: Complete your examination after receiving the required approval.
  8. Apply for Licensure: Submit the Maryland journey plumber/gas fitter license application after satisfying the examination requirements.

Organizing your experience records, training documentation, and study schedule can help you approach both the examination and the licensing application process more efficiently.

State Requirements

The Maryland State Board of Plumbing establishes the qualifications for Journey Plumber/Gas Fitter licensing.

The established Maryland licensing pathway requires:

  • Four Years as an Apprentice: Applicants must have held an apprentice plumber/gas fitter license for at least four years.
  • 7,500 Hours of Training: Applicants must complete qualifying plumbing training under the direction and control of a licensed master plumber.
  • 32 Hours of Backflow Prevention Training: Completion of approved backflow prevention device testing training is required.
  • Passing Examination: Applicants must pass the comprehensive written examination.
  • Licensing Application: Applicants must submit the required information to the Maryland State Board of Plumbing.

Maryland examination guidance provides for credit toward qualifying experience from certain approved educational study. The examination application process requires documentation demonstrating eligibility.

A Maryland Journey Plumber/Gas Fitter performs plumbing and gas fitting work within the scope of the state's licensing rules and under the applicable direction and control of a licensed master plumber.

The journeyman credential represents an important stage in professional development, recognizing the knowledge and training needed to perform skilled plumbing and gas fitting work.

Reference Books

The following technical references support preparation for the Maryland Journey Plumber-Gas Fitter examination.

  • International Plumbing Code, 2018
    Published by the International Code Council, the 2018 International Plumbing Code covers plumbing installation requirements, fixtures, water supply systems, sanitary drainage, vents, traps, interceptors, and related safety provisions. It provides important technical information for interpreting plumbing code questions and understanding plumbing system requirements.
  • International Fuel Gas Code, 2018
    The 2018 International Fuel Gas Code establishes requirements involving fuel gas piping systems, gas appliances, combustion air, venting, installation, and equipment safety. It is especially important for the Gas Appliances and Piping category, which represents 40 questions on the Maryland examination.
  • Mathematics for Plumbers and Pipefitters, 8th Edition
    Written by Lee Smith, this trade mathematics textbook explains calculations used in plumbing and pipefitting applications. It supports review of fractions, measurements, geometry, pipe-related calculations, and practical mathematical reasoning encountered in the plumbing trade.
  • Code of Federal Regulations - 29 CFR Part 1926 (OSHA)
    These federal construction safety regulations address workplace hazards, protective equipment, ladders, excavation, tools, fall protection, and other construction safety considerations. They help reinforce knowledge of safe work practices in plumbing and gas fitting environments.

Each reference addresses a different part of examination preparation. The International Plumbing Code supports general plumbing knowledge, while the International Fuel Gas Code addresses specialized fuel gas requirements.

Mathematics for Plumbers and Pipefitters strengthens trade calculation skills, and OSHA construction safety regulations support preparation involving workplace safety and hazard recognition.

Traps, Interceptors, and Backwater Valves

Traps and interceptors perform important functions in plumbing systems, while backwater valves help address reverse-flow conditions in drainage installations.

Review the purpose of these components, their relationship to drainage systems, and the general installation requirements covered by the plumbing code.

Plumbing Fixtures and Appliances

Study plumbing fixtures, fixture fittings, fixture connections, and plumbing appliance requirements.

Become familiar with common installation principles involving water supply connections, drainage connections, and fixture operation.

Water Supply Systems

Water supply systems distribute potable water throughout residential and commercial buildings.

Review supply piping, water distribution arrangements, fixture connections, water pressure principles, and protection of potable water systems.

Understanding how these elements interact supports stronger comprehension of water supply requirements.

Backflow Prevention

Backflow Prevention accounts for 10 examination questions, making it one of the larger plumbing categories.

Review cross-connection control, backflow prevention devices, air gaps, and the importance of preventing contamination of potable water supplies.

Develop familiarity with the purposes of different protective arrangements and the circumstances addressed by backflow requirements.

Drain, Waste, and Vent Systems

Drain, Waste, and Vent Systems represents 13 questions.

Study sanitary drainage, waste piping, fixture drains, vent piping, drainage fittings, and system performance concepts.

Understanding the relationship between drainage and venting helps explain how plumbing systems transport wastewater while maintaining appropriate system operation.

Gas Appliances and Piping

Gas Appliances and Piping accounts for 40 questions, making it the highest-priority subject for many candidates.

Review fuel gas piping materials, sizing principles, appliance connections, shutoff devices, installation requirements, combustion air, and venting.

Use the International Fuel Gas Code to strengthen familiarity with technical provisions governing fuel gas systems and appliances.

Plumbing Mathematics

Plumbing Mathematics accounts for seven examination questions.

Practice calculations involving measurements, dimensions, fractions, pipe lengths, and common trade mathematics concepts.

Concentrate on understanding the mathematical method used to solve a problem rather than memorizing isolated answers.

Construction Safety

Plumbing and gas fitting work involves construction environments where safe work practices are essential.

Review OSHA construction safety concepts, including appropriate protective equipment, tools, ladders, excavation hazards, and general workplace responsibilities.

Understanding workplace safety helps connect technical trade knowledge with responsible professional practices.
