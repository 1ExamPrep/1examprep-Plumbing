## Exam Details

The **ICC G27 National Standard Master Plumber with Gas** examination contains **100 multiple-choice questions** and has a **4-hour time limit**.

The official G27 examination outline divides the test into eight major content areas:

- **Administration General Regulations – 36%**
- **Plumbing Fixtures – 13%**
- **Water Supply and Distribution – 9%**
- **Sanitary Drainage – 8%**
- **Plumbing Vent Requirements – 6%**
- **Traps, Interceptors, and Separators – 5%**
- **Storm Drainage – 2%**
- **Gas Piping – 21%Administration General Regulations** is the largest content area at 36%. This portion includes general installation requirements, code definitions and terms, plan reading, testing, and materials. Candidates should devote significant preparation time to this area because these fundamentals affect many different plumbing and fuel-gas installations.

**Gas Piping** is the second-largest content area at 21%. This portion covers gas piping for service and distribution, gas-piping requirements associated with specific appliances and equipment, sources of combustion air and duct openings, and requirements involving fuel-burning appliances, vents, and connectors.

**Plumbing Fixtures** represent 13% of the examination and include water heaters, water closets and urinals, specialty fixtures, bathtubs, showers, sinks, and lavatories.

**Water Supply and Distribution** account for 9% and include service distribution and piping, water supply and pressure, and protection of the potable water supply.

**Sanitary Drainage** represents 8% of the test and includes building sanitary drainage, sumps and ejectors, and indirect and special waste systems.

**Plumbing Vent Requirements** account for 6%, while **Traps, Interceptors, and Separators** account for 5%. **Storm Drainage** represents the remaining 2% of the official examination outline.
