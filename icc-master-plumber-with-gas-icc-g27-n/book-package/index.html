Exam Details

The ICC Master Plumber w/Gas - G27-N exam is part of the ICC Contractor/Trades examination program. The National Standard Master Plumber with Gas exam is listed as an open-book exam with 100 multiple-choice questions and a 4-hour time limit. Candidates preparing for this exam should focus on both master-level plumbing knowledge and efficient reference use.

The exam content includes administration and general regulations, plumbing fixtures, water supply and distribution, sanitary drainage, plumbing vent requirements, traps, interceptors and separators, storm drainage, and gas piping. These categories reflect the broad working knowledge expected from master plumber with gas candidates who install, supervise, service, repair, or support plumbing and fuel gas systems.

Administration and general regulations may include code scope, permits, inspections, approved materials, definitions, system testing, installation requirements, protection of piping, and general responsibilities. Candidates should understand how the IPC and IFGC are organized and how general code provisions apply before moving into system-specific chapters.

Plumbing fixture questions may involve fixture types, clearances, installation requirements, fixture connections, water closets, lavatories, sinks, bathtubs, showers, drinking fountains, service sinks, and related code provisions. Candidates should be comfortable locating fixture tables and understanding how fixture requirements connect to drainage, venting, and water supply.

Water supply and distribution questions may involve water service piping, distribution piping, pipe materials, valves, fixture supply, backflow prevention, protection against contamination, hot water distribution, pressure-related requirements, and sizing concepts. Candidates should practice using the code book to locate water supply rules quickly and accurately.

Sanitary drainage and plumbing vents are major study areas for the G27-N exam. Sanitary drainage topics may include fixture units, drainage pipe sizing, slope, cleanouts, fittings, building drains, building sewers, indirect waste, branch drains, stacks, and waste piping. Venting topics may include trap arms, individual vents, common vents, wet vents, circuit vents, vent sizing, vent terminals, developed length, and system protection.

Traps, interceptors, and separators may include trap seals, prohibited traps, trap protection, grease interceptors, oil separators, solids interceptors, access requirements, and related installation responsibilities. Storm drainage may include roof drainage, storm piping, secondary drainage, leaders, conductors, and related drainage provisions.

Gas piping is the fuel gas portion of the exam. Candidates should review pipe sizing concepts, approved materials, fittings, joints, valves, regulators, appliance connections, sediment traps, pressure testing, purging, pipe support, protection from damage, prohibited locations, and fuel supply requirements. Gas piping questions may require careful use of the International Fuel Gas Code, 2018 and a strong understanding of safe installation practices.

The G27-N exam may be accepted differently by different licensing authorities. A city, county, state, municipality, or local agency may determine whether this exam is required for a specific master plumber with gas license classification. Passing an ICC exam does not automatically create a license unless the applicable authority recognizes the exam as part of its licensing process.

Open Book Test

The ICC Master Plumber w/Gas - G27-N exam is an open-book test. Open-book testing allows candidates to use approved references during the exam, but it does not remove the need for focused preparation. Candidates must know how to navigate the International Plumbing Code and International Fuel Gas Code efficiently, identify which reference applies to the question, and locate the correct requirement without losing time.

Open-book plumbing and gas exams reward candidates who understand code organization. Candidates should become familiar with the table of contents, indexes, definitions, chapter layouts, fixture tables, water distribution provisions, drainage tables, venting sections, trap requirements, storm drainage provisions, gas piping sections, fuel gas tables, and appliance connection rules. The goal is not simply to bring books into the exam room. The goal is to know how to use the books quickly and accurately.

The International Plumbing Code, 2018 is especially useful for plumbing fixtures, water supply, sanitary drainage, vents, traps, interceptors, separators, storm drainage, and general plumbing requirements. The International Fuel Gas Code, 2018 is especially useful for gas piping, fuel supply, pipe sizing concepts, pressure testing, regulators, valves, appliance connections, combustion air, and fuel gas safety requirements.

During study, candidates should practice deciding which reference applies before searching for an answer. A question involving drainage, venting, fixtures, water heaters, or water distribution may belong in the IPC. A question involving gas piping, fuel supply, regulators, appliance gas connections, combustion air, or venting of gas-fired equipment may belong in the IFGC. Strong reference-selection habits help candidates avoid losing time in the wrong book.

Because the exam has 100 questions and a 4-hour time limit, pacing matters. Candidates should practice answering questions under timed conditions. A strong open-book strategy includes identifying the topic first, using the index when needed, moving quickly to familiar chapters, reading the section carefully, and avoiding too much time on a single difficult question.
