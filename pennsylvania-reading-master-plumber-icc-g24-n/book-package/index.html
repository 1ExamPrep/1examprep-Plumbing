Open Book Test

The ICC G24-N Master Plumber exam is an open book test. Open book testing allows candidates to use approved reference material during the exam, but it does not remove the need for preparation. Candidates still need to understand the code, recognize where information is located, and apply the correct section within the exam time limit.

For an open book master plumber exam, the code book becomes part of the testing strategy. Candidates should learn the table of contents, use the index regularly during study, review chapter headings, and become familiar with major code tables. The exam may require candidates to move quickly between definitions, general requirements, fixture provisions, water supply rules, drainage sections, venting requirements, and plan reading details.

Tabs and highlighting can help organize the book when they comply with current testing rules. Licensing Steps

Reading, Pennsylvania has local plumbing trade license requirements for master plumbers. Applicants for the master plumber license must meet the City of Reading’s requirements and pass the ICC G24-N Master Plumber Exam. Passing exam results must be submitted with the local license application.

A practical licensing path begins with reviewing the City of Reading plumbing trade license application requirements for the master plumber classification. Reading’s master plumber requirements include holding a valid journeyman plumber license, being employed as a journeyman plumber by a registered master plumber for at least two years, and successfully passing the ICC G24-N Master Plumber Exam. A copy of passing results must accompany the license application.

After confirming eligibility, candidates should obtain the required reference book, study the International Plumbing Code, 2018, schedule the ICC G24-N exam, and prepare using a structured code-based study plan. Once the exam is passed, the candidate should submit the completed Reading plumbing trade license application with proof of passing results and all required supporting documentation.

Candidates should keep copies of their license records, exam results, employment documentation, and application materials. Master plumber licensing is a professional step that may involve responsibility for supervision, code compliance, and plumbing work performed under the license, so applicants should follow the local application process carefully.

Test Information and Study Materials

General regulations introduce important code concepts and explain how plumbing requirements are applied. Plumbing fixtures include requirements connected to fixture installation, fixture counts, clearances, connections, and related code provisions. Water heater topics require attention to installation requirements, safety provisions, piping, and related plumbing code rules.

Water supply and distribution topics may involve potable water systems, backflow protection, sizing concepts, materials, installation requirements, and protection of the water supply. These topics are important because they connect public health, code compliance, and proper plumbing system performance.

Sanitary drainage and venting are major areas for master plumber preparation. Candidates should study drainage piping, cleanouts, traps, vent connections, vent sizing concepts, and the relationship between fixtures and drainage systems. Venting provisions often require careful reading because several code sections can apply to one system layout.

Traps, interceptors, separators, and storm drainage should also be reviewed carefully. These topics include specific definitions, installation conditions, sizing requirements, and code rules that may be tested through direct questions or plan-based scenarios. Plan reading requires candidates to apply code knowledge to drawings, layouts, and practical plumbing system situations.

A strong study routine should include repeated code navigation. Candidates should practice finding definitions, locating tables, reading exceptions, and identifying the correct section for each topic. Open book exams reward candidates who know how to use the book efficiently. Candidates who wait until exam day to become familiar with the code book may lose valuable time searching for information.
