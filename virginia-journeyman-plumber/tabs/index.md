## How does the Journeyman Plumber exam work?

The Journeyman Plumber license comes from the Virginia Board for Contractors (DPOR). PSI gives the exam. The board approves your application first; then you schedule the exam and bring only the references the candidate bulletin allows.

- Virginia licenses journeyman, master and residential plumbing tradesmen through DPOR; PSI gives the exams.
- Virginia also certifies plumbing inspectors and plans examiners using ICC P1, P2 and P3 exams.
