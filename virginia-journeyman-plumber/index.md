## How does the Journeyman Plumber exam work?

The Journeyman Plumber license comes from the Virginia Board for Contractors (DPOR). PSI gives the exam. The board approves your application first; then you schedule the exam and bring only the references the candidate bulletin allows.

- Virginia licenses journeyman, master and residential plumbing tradesmen through DPOR; PSI gives the exams.
- Virginia also certifies plumbing inspectors and plans examiners using ICC P1, P2 and P3 exams.

### Is the Journeyman Plumber exam open book?

It depends on the exam. Many plumbing trade exams allow the code book, but some are closed book. Your candidate bulletin lists the references allowed; if books are allowed, highlighted and tabbed sets save the most time.

### Who issues the Journeyman Plumber license in Virginia?

It comes from the Virginia Board for Contractors (DPOR). Buying a package here does not register you or make you eligible; the board approves you first, and the exam vendor schedules the test.
