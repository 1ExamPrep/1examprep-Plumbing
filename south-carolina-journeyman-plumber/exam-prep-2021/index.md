## How does the Journeyman Plumber exam work?

The Journeyman Plumber license comes from the South Carolina Contractor's Licensing Board and Residential Builders Commission (LLR). The board approves your application first; then you schedule the exam and bring only the references the candidate bulletin allows.

- South Carolina licenses commercial plumbing contractors through the Contractor's Licensing Board and residential plumbers through the Residential Builders Commission.
- Both boards sit within the Department of Labor, Licensing and Regulation.
