The Plumbing (C-36) Contractor license comes from the Contractors State License Board (CSLB). The board approves your application first; then you schedule the exam and bring only the references the candidate bulletin allows.

- California has no statewide journeyman plumber license, so the journeyman packages suit local and union exams built on the UPC.
