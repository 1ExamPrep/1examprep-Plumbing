## Exam Details

The California Plumbing (C-36) Contractor Examination evaluates knowledge of plumbing project planning, estimating, underground installations, rough systems, finish plumbing, service, repair, remodeling, and safety.

The official CSLB C-36 examination study guide, applicable to examinations scheduled on or after **March 1, 2025**, identifies five major examination categories:

- **Planning and Estimating — 22%:** Understanding project scope, code compliance, pipe sizing, venting requirements, shop drawings, specifications, job site inspections, water conservation, and estimating materials, equipment, labor, and overhead.
- **Underground and Rough Systems — 22%:** Excavation, underground installations, piping layout, fuel gas piping, insulation, and system testing.
- **Finish Plumbing Installations — 20%:** Plumbing fixtures, appliances, water heaters, shut-off valves, specialty installations, and industrial or commercial piping systems.
- **Service, Repair, and Remodel Plumbing Systems — 19%:** Diagnosing plumbing problems, isolating leaks, repairing piping, retrofitting systems, and disposing of plumbing materials.
- **Safety — 17%:** Personnel protection, job site safety, and environmental safety practices.

These percentages represent approximate examination content distribution. **Planning and Estimating and Underground and Rough Systems are the two largest categories, each accounting for approximately 22%.**

The examination uses multiple-choice questions with four answer choices and one best answer. Some questions require mathematical calculations, and a calculator is provided during testing. There is no penalty for guessing.

## State Requirements

The California Contractors State License Board regulates plumbing contractors under the C-36 specialty contractor classification.

California Code of Regulations, Title 16, Section 832.36 describes plumbing work involving the supply of safe water, proper disposal of fluid waste, and the installation, maintenance, and replacement of associated piping and equipment.

The classification includes water supply systems, drainage and waste systems, fuel gas piping, water heaters, certain gas appliances, backflow prevention devices, pressure-regulating valves, and other qualifying plumbing equipment.

Applicants must satisfy applicable CSLB experience, application, examination, and license issuance requirements. Licensed plumbing contractors must also follow relevant California building, plumbing, mechanical, environmental, and workplace safety regulations.

Plumbing projects can involve excavation, underground utilities, water quality, sanitary systems, and potentially hazardous working conditions. Understanding appropriate installation methods and safety practices is an important part of professional plumbing work.
