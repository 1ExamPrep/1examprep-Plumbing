## Exam Details

The California Plumbing (C-36) Contractor Examination evaluates knowledge of plumbing project planning, estimating, underground systems, rough plumbing, finish installations, repairs, remodeling, and safety.

The CSLB C-36 examination study guide for examinations scheduled on or after March 1, 2025, identifies five major categories:

- **Planning and Estimating — 22%:** Project scope, code compliance, pipe sizing, venting, drawings, water conservation, and estimating materials, labor, equipment, and overhead.
- **Underground and Rough Systems — 22%:** Excavation, underground installations, piping layout, fuel gas systems, insulation, and testing.
- **Finish Plumbing Installations — 20%:** Fixtures, appliances, water heaters, shut-off valves, specialty installations, and commercial piping.
- **Service, Repair, and Remodel Plumbing Systems — 19%:** Diagnosing problems, locating leaks, repairing piping, retrofitting systems, and material disposal.
- **Safety — 17%:** Personnel protection, job site safety, and environmental safety.

These percentages represent approximate examination weighting. **Planning and Estimating and Underground and Rough Systems are the two largest categories, each representing 22%.**

The examination uses multiple-choice questions with four answer choices and one best answer. Some questions require calculations, and a calculator is provided during testing. There is no penalty for guessing.

## State Requirements

The California Contractors State License Board regulates plumbing contractors under the C-36 specialty contractor classification.

California Code of Regulations, Title 16, Section 832.36 describes plumbing work involving a safe supply of water, proper disposal of fluid waste, and associated piping and equipment.

The classification includes qualifying water supply systems, drainage and waste systems, gas and liquid piping, water heaters, certain gas appliances, backflow prevention devices, regulating valves, and related equipment.

Applicants must satisfy applicable experience, application, examination, and license issuance requirements. Licensed contractors must also comply with relevant plumbing, building, mechanical, environmental, and safety regulations.

Plumbing projects may involve excavation, underground utilities, pressurized piping, sanitary systems, and other hazards requiring appropriate precautions and technical knowledge.
