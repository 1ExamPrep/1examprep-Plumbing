Exam Details

The California Plumbing (C-36) Contractor Examination evaluates technical knowledge of plumbing planning, underground systems, rough installations, fixtures, maintenance, repairs, and safety.

The official CSLB C-36 examination study guide for examinations scheduled on or after March 1, 2025, identifies five categories:

  • Planning and Estimating — 22%: Project scope, code compliance, pipe sizing, venting, construction plans, water conservation, and estimating materials, labor, equipment, and overhead.
  • Underground and Rough Systems — 22%: Excavation, underground installations, piping layouts, fuel gas piping, insulation, and system testing.
  • Finish Plumbing Installations — 20%: Plumbing fixtures, appliances, water heaters, shut-off valves, specialty installations, and commercial piping systems.
  • Service, Repair, and Remodel Plumbing Systems — 19%: Diagnosing problems, isolating leaks, repairing piping, retrofitting systems, and disposing of plumbing materials.
  • Safety — 17%: Personnel safety, job site protection, and environmental safety.

These percentages represent approximate examination content weighting. Planning and Estimating and Underground and Rough Systems are the two largest categories, each accounting for 22%.

The examination uses multiple-choice questions with four possible answers and one best answer. Some questions involve mathematical calculations, and a calculator is provided during testing. There is no penalty for guessing.

The included C-36 Trade Course follows these official examination categories to support focused preparation for the technical portion of California plumbing contractor licensing.

State Requirements

California regulates plumbing contractors through the CSLB under the C-36 specialty contractor classification.

California Code of Regulations, Title 16, Section 832.36 describes plumbing work involving safe water supply, disposal of fluid waste, and associated piping and equipment.

The classification covers qualifying water distribution, drainage and waste systems, fuel gas piping, water heaters, plumbing fixtures, and related installations.

Applicants must meet applicable experience, application, examination, and license issuance requirements. Licensed contractors must comply with relevant plumbing, building, mechanical, environmental, and workplace safety regulations.

California requires a contractor license bond for active licensure. The standard contractor bond has a $25,000 face amount, while the premium is the cost paid for the bond.

Additional bonds, workers' compensation insurance, and other coverage may be required depending on the business and licensing circumstances.

California Law and Business Exam Preparation Included

The California Law and Business Exam Prep Online Course and business study books are included with this package.

Plumbing contractors need business knowledge alongside trade expertise. Operating a contracting company involves financial planning, employment responsibilities, insurance, customer contracts, and safety management.

The official CSLB Law and Business examination study guide identifies seven categories:

  • Business Organization and Licensing — 13%: Company organization, licensing requirements, advertising, and subcontracting.
  • Business Finances — 15%: Cash management, budgeting, taxes, and financial reporting.
  • Employment Requirements — 20%: Hiring, supervising, training, employee records, and payroll.
  • Insurance and Liens — 12%: Workers' compensation, business insurance, liens, and related remedies.
  • Contract Requirements and Execution — 21%: Bidding, cost control, project organization, contracts, and payments.
  • Public Works — 5%: Prevailing wage, bonding, and insurance requirements.
  • Safety — 14%: Training, reporting, general safety, and hazardous materials.

Contract Requirements and Execution is the largest approximate Law and Business category at 21%, followed by Employment Requirements at 20%.

The examination is multiple choice and closed book. The included business course follows the same four-layer preparation method as the C-36 trade course.
