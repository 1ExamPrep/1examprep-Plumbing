## Exam Details

The California Plumbing (C-36) Contractor Examination evaluates plumbing project planning, estimating, underground installations, rough piping, finish plumbing, service, repair, remodeling, and workplace safety.

The official CSLB C-36 examination study guide for examinations scheduled on or after March 1, 2025, identifies five major categories:

- **Planning and Estimating — 22%:** Understanding project scope, code compliance, pipe sizing, venting requirements, construction plans, water conservation, and estimating materials, labor, equipment, and overhead.
- **Underground and Rough Systems — 22%:** Excavation, underground plumbing installations, piping layout, fuel gas piping, insulation, and system testing.
- **Finish Plumbing Installations — 20%:** Installing plumbing fixtures, appliances, water heaters, shut-off valves, specialty systems, and commercial or industrial piping.
- **Service, Repair, and Remodel Plumbing Systems — 19%:** Troubleshooting plumbing problems, identifying leaks, repairing and retrofitting systems, and disposing of plumbing materials.
- **Safety — 17%:** Personnel protection, job site safety, and environmental safety practices.

These percentages represent approximate examination content distribution. **Planning and Estimating and Underground and Rough Systems are the two largest categories, each accounting for approximately 22%.**

The examination uses multiple-choice questions with four possible answers and one best answer. Some questions involve mathematical calculations, and a calculator is provided during testing. There is no penalty for guessing.

## State Requirements

The California Contractors State License Board regulates plumbing contractors under the C-36 specialty contractor classification.

California Code of Regulations, Title 16, Section 832.36 describes plumbing work involving safe water supply, proper disposal of fluid waste, piping systems, water heaters, fuel gas piping, and associated equipment.

The classification encompasses qualifying water distribution, drainage, waste, venting, fuel gas, and related plumbing work.

Applicants must satisfy applicable CSLB experience, application, examination, and license issuance requirements. Licensed contractors must follow relevant building, plumbing, mechanical, environmental, and construction safety regulations.

Plumbing projects may involve excavation, underground utilities, sanitary systems, and pressurized piping. Contractors are responsible for applying appropriate work practices and complying with applicable safety requirements.
