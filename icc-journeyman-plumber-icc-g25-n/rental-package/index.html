Test Information and Study Materials

Journeyman Plumber exam preparation should focus on applying the 2018 International Plumbing Code to plumbing installation scenarios. Students should study definitions, general regulations, plumbing fixtures, water supply and distribution, sanitary drainage, venting, traps, interceptors, indirect waste, storm drainage, materials, joints, supports, testing, inspections, and safety provisions.

Fixture requirements are a major part of plumbing code study. Students should review fixture types, minimum fixture requirements, fixture unit concepts, fixture installation rules, clearances, accessibility-related fixture concepts where applicable, and requirements for public and private plumbing facilities. Questions may depend on the type of occupancy or the specific fixture arrangement described in the problem.

Water supply and distribution should also receive careful attention. Students should review potable water protection, pipe sizing concepts, supply fixture units, pressure concerns, valves, backflow prevention concepts, cross-connection control, water hammer arrestors, hot water distribution, water heaters where applicable, and installation requirements. Water distribution questions often involve tables, system conditions, or material limitations.

Sanitary drainage is one of the most important journeyman plumber study areas. Students should review drainage fixture units, horizontal drainage piping, vertical stacks, slope, cleanouts, branch intervals, pipe sizing, materials, fittings, changes in direction, and connection rules. These questions may require careful table use and attention to the details given in the exam question.

Venting is another key topic. Students should study trap arms, vent connections, vent sizing, stack vents, vent stacks, branch vents, circuit venting concepts, wet venting concepts, air admittance concepts where applicable, relief vents, and vent termination. Venting questions can be challenging because the answer often depends on distance, pipe size, fixture type, drainage arrangement, or the specific venting method allowed by code.

Traps, interceptors, and separators should be reviewed in detail. Students should understand trap requirements, prohibited traps, trap seals, trap protection, grease interceptors, oil separators, and installation conditions. These topics connect directly to plumbing system performance, sanitary protection, and code compliance.

Test Information and Study Materials (continued)

Indirect waste and special waste topics may appear in plumbing exam preparation. Students should review indirect waste receptors, air gaps, air breaks, food service equipment connections, condensate disposal concepts, clear waste, and special piping conditions. These topics often require students to know where the code places requirements and how to apply them to specific equipment or fixture scenarios.

Storm drainage and roof drainage should not be overlooked. Students should review storm drainage piping, roof drains, secondary drainage, scuppers, gutters, conductors, leaders, sizing concepts, and related requirements. Even when plumbing candidates are more familiar with sanitary systems, storm drainage is an important part of the plumbing code.

Testing and inspection requirements are also important. Students should understand drainage system testing, water supply testing, inspection requirements, material approval, system support, protection from damage, and code compliance procedures. These topics help connect classroom preparation with practical field expectations.

Because the package includes 6 months of course access, students can build a steady study schedule instead of cramming. A strong routine may include reviewing one topic at a time, practicing reference lookup, completing practice questions, reviewing missed answers, and returning to weak areas. Repetition helps students remember where information is located and improves confidence with the book.
