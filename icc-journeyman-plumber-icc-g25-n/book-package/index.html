Exam Details

The ICC Journeyman Plumber - G25-N exam is part of the ICC Contractor/Trades examination program. The National Standard Journeyman Plumber exam is listed as an open-book exam with 70 multiple-choice questions and a 3-hour time limit. Candidates preparing for this exam should focus on both plumbing trade knowledge and efficient use of the International Plumbing Code, 2018.

The exam content includes general regulations, fixtures, water heaters, water supply and distribution, sanitary drainage, vents, traps, interceptors and separators, storm drainage, and plan reading. These categories reflect the working knowledge expected from journeyman plumber candidates who install, service, repair, or support plumbing systems.

General regulations may include code administration, permits, inspections, materials, approved installation methods, definitions, system requirements, and general plumbing responsibilities. Candidates should understand how the IPC is arranged and how general provisions apply before moving into system-specific chapters.

Fixture questions may involve fixture requirements, installation, clearances, fittings, connections, water closets, lavatories, sinks, bathtubs, showers, drinking fountains, service sinks, and related plumbing fixture provisions. Candidates should be comfortable locating fixture tables and understanding how fixture requirements connect to drainage, venting, and water supply.

Water heater topics may include installation requirements, safety devices, temperature and pressure relief valves, discharge piping, access, location, connections, and related code provisions. Water heaters are a common plumbing system component, so candidates should understand both installation details and safety-related requirements.

Water supply and distribution questions may involve pipe materials, sizing concepts, valves, pressure, protection against contamination, backflow prevention, hot water distribution, fixture supply, water service piping, and installation requirements. Candidates should practice using tables and locating water distribution rules quickly.

Sanitary drainage and venting are major study areas for the G25-N exam. Sanitary drainage topics may include drainage pipe sizing, fixture units, slope, cleanouts, fittings, building drains, building sewers, waste piping, indirect waste, and drainage system layout. Venting topics may include vent sizing, vent connections, wet venting, circuit venting, individual vents, common vents, vent terminals, developed length, trap arm limitations, and system protection.

Storm drainage may include roof drainage, storm piping, secondary drainage concepts, leaders, conductors, and related drainage provisions. Plan reading may require candidates to interpret plumbing drawings, identify symbols, understand fixture layouts, and connect plan information to code requirements.

The G25-N exam may be accepted differently by different licensing authorities. A city, county, state, municipality, or local agency may determine whether this exam is required for a specific journeyman plumber license classification. Passing an ICC exam does not automatically create a license unless the applicable authority recognizes the exam as part of its licensing process.

Open Book Test

The ICC Journeyman Plumber - G25-N exam is an open-book test. Open-book testing allows candidates to use approved references during the exam, but it does not remove the need for focused preparation. Candidates must know how to navigate the International Plumbing Code, 2018 efficiently, identify the topic being tested, and locate the correct requirement without losing time.

Open-book plumbing exams reward candidates who understand code organization. Candidates should become familiar with the table of contents, index, definitions, chapter layout, fixture tables, water distribution provisions, drainage tables, venting sections, trap requirements, storm drainage provisions, and plan reading concepts. The goal is not simply to bring the book into the exam room. The goal is to know how to use the book quickly and accurately.

Because the exam has 70 questions and a 3-hour time limit, pacing matters. Candidates should practice answering questions under timed conditions. A strong open-book strategy includes identifying the topic first, using the index when needed, moving quickly to familiar chapters, reading the section carefully, and avoiding too much time on a single difficult question. Marking difficult questions and returning to them later can help protect exam pacing.

Exam-day reference rules may control which books are allowed, how books may be marked, whether tabs are permitted, whether notes are allowed, and what condition books must be in. Candidates should follow the current testing rules for the G25-N exam and the requirements of the jurisdiction connected to their license.

Test Information and Study Materials

Journeyman plumber preparation should be organized around the major exam categories and the way plumbing requirements are arranged in the International Plumbing Code. Candidates should study general regulations, fixtures, water heaters, water distribution, sanitary drainage, vents, traps, interceptors, separators, storm drainage, and plan reading. Random reading is less effective than a focused plan that connects each topic to the correct code chapter or table.

For general regulations, candidates should review code administration, scope, definitions, permits, inspections, approved materials, system testing, protection of piping, and general installation rules. These topics help candidates understand the framework of the plumbing code and how plumbing work is evaluated for compliance.

For fixtures, candidates should review fixture requirements, clearances, connections, materials, public and private fixture provisions, fixture supply, fixture drainage, and related installation requirements. Candidates should practice locating fixture tables and understanding how fixture requirements connect to water supply, drainage, and venting.

For water heaters, candidates should study installation location, access, safety devices, relief valves, discharge piping, temperature control, connections, and related provisions. Water heater questions often involve safety requirements, so careful reading is important.

For water supply and distribution, candidates should review water service piping, distribution piping, pipe materials, valves, protection against contamination, backflow prevention, fixture supply, hot water distribution, pressure-related requirements, and sizing concepts. Candidates should become comfortable using tables and identifying where water supply rules are located.

For sanitary drainage, candidates should review fixture units, drainage pipe sizing, slope, cleanouts, building drains, building sewers, fittings, indirect waste, branch drains, stacks, offsets, and drainage system arrangement. Sanitary drainage questions often require candidates to apply a table or read a section carefully before selecting an answer.

For vents, candidates should study trap arms, vent connections, vent sizing, individual vents, common vents, wet vents, circuit vents, branch vents, vent stacks, stack vents, vent terminals, and developed length. Venting is one of the most important study areas because small wording differences can change the correct answer.

For traps, interceptors, and separators, candidates should review trap seal requirements, prohibited traps, trap protection, grease interceptors, oil separators, solids interceptors, and access requirements. For storm drainage, candidates should review roof drainage, secondary drainage, leaders, conductors, horizontal storm piping, and related provisions. For plan reading, candidates should practice interpreting plumbing layouts, identifying fixtures, reviewing pipe routing, and connecting drawing information to code requirements.

A strong study routine should include timed reference practice. Candidates should practice looking up plumbing terms, locating fixture provisions, reviewing drainage and venting tables, finding water supply requirements, and answering questions using the IPC. Open-book exam success depends on more than knowing plumbing work. It depends on using the reference efficiently, reading questions carefully, and applying the correct code requirement.
