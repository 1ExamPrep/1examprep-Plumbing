## How does plumber licensing work in Utah?

The Utah Division of Professional Licensing (DOPL) licenses plumbers here. The board approves your application first; then you schedule the exam and bring only the references the candidate bulletin allows.

- Utah DOPL licenses journeyman, residential journeyman and master plumbers, and master plumbers take a law and rule exam.
- Utah apprentices can test out of training levels 1A to 2B with their own exams.

### Can you bring books into the Utah exam?

It depends on the exam. Many plumbing trade exams allow the code book, but some are closed book. Your candidate bulletin lists the references allowed; if books are allowed, highlighted and tabbed sets save the most time.
