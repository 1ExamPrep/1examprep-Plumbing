Fixtures and faucets should also be reviewed in detail. The IPC includes requirements for plumbing fixtures, fixture installation, minimum facilities, faucets, connections, and related provisions. Water distribution topics require familiarity with water supply rules, pipe sizing, pressure considerations, materials, valves, protection of potable water, and related code sections.
