## Exam Details

The Arizona Registrar of Contractors issues residential, commercial, and dual contractor licenses. The C-77 Plumbing, Including Solar classification is the commercial specialty classification for qualifying plumbing work that includes solar-thermal systems.

The qualifying party is the individual whose experience, trade knowledge, and examination qualifications support the contractor license. This person must satisfy the applicable Arizona requirements and remain associated with the licensed business in the qualifying capacity recognized by the state.

C-77 exam preparation may include commercial water distribution, sanitary drainage, venting, plumbing fixtures, fuel-gas systems, mechanical equipment, boilers, hydronic heating, fire-sprinkler provisions, wastewater systems, solar water heating, trade mathematics, estimating, and OSHA safety.

Water-distribution preparation may involve service piping, fixture demand, pressure, elevation, friction loss, pipe sizing, valves, backflow protection, water heaters, thermal expansion, insulation, supports, and testing.

Sanitary drainage and venting preparation may include building drains, building sewers, fixture drains, traps, cleanouts, interceptors, vent sizing, vent connections, slope, developed length, approved fittings, piping materials, and testing procedures.

Fuel-gas preparation may involve approved materials, pipe sizing, appliance demand, shutoff valves, regulators, sediment traps, pressure testing, combustion air, equipment connections, and venting.

Mechanical and hydronic preparation may include boilers, circulating pumps, expansion tanks, air separators, heat exchangers, valves, controls, heat emitters, piping arrangements, system filling, purging, pressure control, and temperature protection.

Fire-sprinkler preparation may address system types, water supplies, piping arrangements, sprinklers, valves, inspection, testing, and installation provisions. The appropriate NFPA reference depends on the type of building, occupancy, and sprinkler system described in the question.

On-site wastewater preparation may involve treatment-facility provisions, disposal systems, installation concepts, setbacks, site conditions, and requirements contained in Arizona’s administrative-code material.

Solar-thermal preparation may include flat-plate collectors, evacuated-tube collectors, storage tanks, heat exchangers, direct and indirect systems, active and passive circulation, pumps, controllers, sensors, valves, expansion tanks, piping, insulation, and heat-transfer fluids.

Candidates should understand freeze protection, stagnation, overheating, pressure relief, thermal expansion, collector orientation, tilt, shading, roof penetrations, structural support, equipment access, startup, shutdown, and maintenance considerations.

## State Requirements

The C-77 classification is an Arizona commercial specialty contractor license for qualifying plumbing work that includes solar applications. It does not provide residential contracting authority.

The proposed qualifying party must demonstrate the experience, knowledge, and skills necessary to supervise or perform the work covered by the classification. Experience documentation may need to describe commercial water systems, drainage, vents, fuel gas, boilers, hydronic heating, plumbing equipment, solar water heating, commercial projects, and field supervision.

The qualifying party must remain associated with the applicant business in the capacity recognized by the Arizona Registrar of Contractors. Experience records, examination information, and the person’s business role should remain consistent throughout the application process.

The business entity applying for the license must be properly formed and identified. The company name, ownership information, qualifying-party records, EIN documentation, contractor bond, and license application should remain consistent.

Arizona contractor applicants must complete applicable background requirements and provide the identity, experience, examination, business, and disclosure information required for state review.

A commercial contractor license bond is required. The applicable bond amount depends on the anticipated gross volume of commercial work and the license category.

Because C-77 is a commercial-only classification, the residential financial-protection requirements associated with Arizona residential and dual licenses do not apply.

Passing the required examinations is only one part of the licensing process. A person or company may not operate as a licensed C-77 contractor until the Arizona Registrar of Contractors approves the complete application and issues the active license.

After licensing, the contractor must remain within the authorized C-77 scope, maintain the required bond, renew the license, and keep business and qualifying-party information current. Changes involving ownership, entity structure, address, bond, or qualifying party may require additional filings.
