Exam Details

The ICC G27-N Master Plumber with Gas exam is part of the ICC Contractor/Trades testing program. The G27-N exam title is Master Plumber with Gas, and it is listed with the 2018 code edition. The exam is administered through Pearson VUE for ICC Contractor/Trades testing.

The G27-N Master Plumber with Gas exam contains 100 multiple-choice questions and has a 4-hour time limit. The exam references are the International Plumbing Code, 2018 and the International Fuel Gas Code, 2018. Candidates should be comfortable using both books because the exam covers plumbing code knowledge and fuel gas code knowledge.

The exam content areas include administration and general regulations, plumbing fixtures, water supply and distribution, sanitary drainage, plumbing vent requirements, traps, interceptors and separators, storm drainage, and gas piping. The largest listed content areas include administration and general regulations, along with gas piping. This means candidates should not treat the fuel gas book as a minor add-on. The gas portion is a meaningful part of the G27-N Master Plumber with Gas exam and should be studied carefully.

For the plumbing portion, candidates should be prepared to answer questions involving installation requirements, code definitions, plan reading, testing, materials, water heaters, water closets, urinals, specialty fixtures, bathtubs, showers, sinks, lavatories, service distribution piping, water supply pressure, protection of potable water supply, building sanitary drainage, sumps and ejectors, indirect and special waste systems, vent connections, vent grades, vent terminals, trap installation, interceptors, separators, and storm drainage.

For the fuel gas portion, candidates should focus on gas piping for service and distribution, appliance-specific gas piping requirements, combustion air, duct openings, fuel-burning appliances, vents, and connectors. These topics require both code familiarity and the ability to move through tables, definitions, and installation provisions efficiently.

Test Information and Study Materials

The ICC G27-N Master Plumber with Gas exam is a code-based test. Candidates should prepare by learning the layout of the references, not by relying only on general trade experience. Field knowledge is valuable, but exam questions are answered according to the listed code references. That means the wording in the book matters. Definitions matter. Tables matter. Exceptions matter. Footnotes matter. A strong study plan should include repeated review of the highlighted sections, tab navigation practice, and timed lookup drills.

Start by reviewing the major chapters and tabs in the International Plumbing Code, 2018. Pay attention to definitions, general regulations, fixture requirements, water distribution, drainage, venting, and special waste topics. Then move into the International Fuel Gas Code, 2018 and review gas piping, sizing concepts, combustion air, appliance requirements, and venting rules. Since the G27-N exam combines plumbing and gas, the strongest preparation plan gives meaningful time to both books.

When studying, do not simply read from front to back once. Use the books as working tools. Look up terms in the index. Move from a question topic to the correct chapter. Practice finding related tables. Review highlighted code sections more than once. Use the tabs until the book layout becomes familiar. The more comfortable you become with the references, the more efficiently you can use the open-book format.

It is also helpful to create a study routine around the exam content areas. For example, spend one session on plumbing fixtures, another on water supply, another on drainage, another on venting, and another on fuel gas piping. This keeps preparation organized and helps prevent the common mistake of spending too much time on familiar topics while ignoring weaker areas.
