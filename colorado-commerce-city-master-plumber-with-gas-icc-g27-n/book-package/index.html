Exam Details

The ICC G27-N exam is titled Master Plumber with Gas. Commerce City’s ICC exam code list places G27-N in the PMG category and identifies the 2018 code edition for this exam. The exam is administered through ICC’s contractor and trades testing program and is used by jurisdictions as a qualification measure for plumbing and gas-related trade knowledge.

The G27-N exam contains 100 multiple-choice questions and has a 4-hour time limit. The exam is open book and is based on the 2018 International Plumbing Code and the 2018 International Fuel Gas Code. Candidates should prepare to use both references during the exam and should understand when a question belongs in the plumbing code and when it belongs in the fuel gas code.

The exam outline includes Administration General Regulations at 36%, Plumbing Fixtures at 13%, Water Supply and Distribution at 9%, Sanitary Drainage at 8%, Plumbing Vent Requirements at 6%, Traps, Interceptors, and Separators at 5%, Storm Drainage at 2%, and Gas Piping at 21%. This distribution shows that the exam places major emphasis on general regulations, plumbing system knowledge, and gas piping.

Administration General Regulations is the largest content area. Candidates should review general installation requirements, code definitions and terms, plan reading, testing, and materials. These topics affect how plumbing and gas systems are installed, inspected, interpreted, and approved. A master-level candidate should be comfortable reading code language, identifying applicable requirements, and applying the correct provisions to field conditions.

Plumbing fixture topics include water heaters, water closets, urinals, specialty fixtures, bathtubs, showers, sinks, and lavatories. Water supply and distribution topics include service distribution and piping, water supply and pressure, and protection of the potable water supply. Sanitary drainage topics include building sanitary drainage, sumps and ejectors, and indirect and special waste systems.

The ICC G27-N Master Plumber with Gas exam is an open book test. Candidates are allowed to use approved references during the exam, which makes book navigation one of the most important parts of preparation. Open book testing does not remove the need to study. It requires the candidate to know where information is located and how to apply it quickly.

For the plumbing portion of the exam, candidates should practice using the International Plumbing Code to locate definitions, fixture requirements, water supply rules, drainage provisions, venting requirements, trap rules, interceptor and separator provisions, storm drainage requirements, material standards, and testing requirements. Many plumbing questions require careful reading because the correct answer may depend on fixture type, pipe size, slope, developed length, system condition, or installation location.

For the gas portion of the exam, candidates should practice using the International Fuel Gas Code to locate gas piping provisions, pipe sizing tables, appliance connection requirements, shutoff valve rules, regulator requirements, pressure testing provisions, combustion air rules, venting requirements, connector provisions, and fuel-burning appliance requirements. Gas questions often require attention to exact conditions, including appliance input, piping material, pressure, length, vent type, and combustion air method.

Open-book preparation should include repeated lookup practice. Start by learning the chapter layout of each book. Then practice finding common topics through the table of contents, index, definitions, section headings, and tables. As your comfort improves, add timed practice so you can build speed without sacrificing accuracy. The best exam-day use of the books comes from preparation that makes navigation feel familiar before the test begins.

Test Information and Study Materials

A strong study plan for the Commerce City Colorado Master Plumber with Gas ICC G27-N exam should begin with the official content areas. Administration General Regulations and Gas Piping are the two largest categories, so candidates should give both areas steady attention. Plumbing Fixtures, Water Supply and Distribution, Sanitary Drainage, Plumbing Vent Requirements, Traps, Interceptors, Separators, and Storm Drainage should also be reviewed thoroughly.

Start with the International Plumbing Code. Review the table of contents and learn how the chapters are organized. Spend time with definitions because plumbing code terms often determine how a requirement applies. Then move into general regulations, materials, fixture requirements, water supply, drainage, venting, traps, interceptors, separators, and storm drainage. Practice using the index and section headings until you can locate topics without wasting time.

Plumbing fixture study should include water closets, urinals, lavatories, sinks, bathtubs, showers, specialty fixtures, and water heaters. Candidates should understand fixture installation requirements, clearances, connections, water supply requirements, drainage connections, and applicable code conditions. Fixture questions often appear straightforward, but the correct answer may depend on the specific fixture type or installation condition.

Water supply and distribution study should include service piping, distribution piping, pressure, protection of potable water, backflow prevention concepts, materials, valves, and system sizing principles. Sanitary drainage study should include building drains, building sewers, drainage fixture units, indirect waste, special waste, sumps, ejectors, pipe sizing, slope, and cleanout requirements. Venting study should include vent connections, grades, terminals, developed length, wet venting concepts, and protection of trap seals.

Traps, interceptors, and separators should be studied as a separate topic. Candidates should review trap installation, prohibited traps, trap seal protection, grease interceptors, oil separators, and other special waste handling requirements. Storm drainage should also be reviewed even though it is a smaller portion of the exam, because storm drainage questions may require table use, roof drainage concepts, or system-sizing knowledge.

Pipe sizing deserves special attention because table use can take time without practice.

Because the G27-N exam uses two references, candidates should practice deciding which book applies before looking up the answer.
