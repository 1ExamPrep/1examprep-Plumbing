## How does the Journeyman Plumber exam work?
The Journeyman Plumber license comes from the Minnesota Department of Labor and Industry (DLI). The board approves your application first; then you schedule the exam and bring only the references the candidate bulletin allows.

    - The Minnesota Department of Labor and Industry licenses journeyworker and master plumbers.
    - Minneapolis and St. Paul have their own licenses for gas work and plumbing contractors.
