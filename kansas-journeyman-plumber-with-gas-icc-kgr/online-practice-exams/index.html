Exam Details

The KGR Kansas Standard Journeyman Plumber with Gas examination is part of the International Code Council contractor and trade examination program.

The exam contains 70 multiple-choice questions and has a 3-hour time limit. It measures knowledge across plumbing and fuel gas subject areas using the 2018 editions of the International Plumbing Code and International Fuel Gas Code.

The published examination content areas include:

  • Administration and General Regulations: 34%
  • Plumbing Fixtures: 11%
  • Water Supply and Distribution: 11%
  • Sanitary Drainage: 9%
  • Plumbing Vent Requirements: 7%
  • Traps, Interceptors, and Separators: 7%
  • Storm Drainage: 2%
  • Gas Piping: 19%

Administration and General Regulations is the largest published content area. Topics include general installation requirements, code definitions and terminology, plan reading, testing, and materials. Candidates should therefore be comfortable with more than individual plumbing-system chapters. General code knowledge can affect questions throughout the examination.

Gas Piping represents 19 percent of the published exam content. The gas portion can include service and distribution piping, requirements affecting specific appliances and equipment, sources of combustion air, duct openings, fuel-burning appliances, vents, and connectors.

Plumbing-system subjects account for the remainder of the examination. Candidates should understand fixture requirements, water heaters, water closets, urinals, specialty fixtures, sinks, lavatories, water supply and pressure, potable-water protection, sanitary drainage, sumps and ejectors, indirect waste, vent connections, vent terminals, traps, interceptors, separators, and storm drainage.

Because the examination combines two code books, candidates should practice identifying the subject before opening a reference. Developing that habit can reduce time spent searching in the wrong book.

Open Book Test

The Kansas Journeyman Plumber with Gas ICC KGR examination is an open book test. The approved references identified for the examination are the 2018 International Plumbing Code and the 2018 International Fuel Gas Code.

An open-book format allows candidates to consult approved references during testing, but it should not be treated as a substitute for preparation. With 70 questions and three hours available, candidates have an average of approximately two and a half minutes per question if time is divided evenly.

Efficient reference navigation can therefore make a significant difference in how comfortably you manage your testing time. Learn the general organization of each book before the exam. Become familiar with chapter names, important tables, definitions, major system categories, and commonly used terminology.

For plumbing questions, practice starting with the International Plumbing Code and narrowing your search based on the subject. For fuel gas questions, develop the habit of moving directly to the International Fuel Gas Code rather than searching through the plumbing code first.

Use practice questions to train the decision-making process. Read the question carefully, identify what it is actually testing, determine the likely reference, and then locate the relevant provision when needed. This is more efficient than searching for individual words without understanding the topic.

Licensing Steps

Kansas plumber licensing is administered through cities and counties rather than through one statewide journeyman plumbing licensing office. The exact licensing process therefore depends on the local jurisdiction in which you intend to work.

  1. Identify your Kansas licensing jurisdiction. Determine which city or county issues the journeyman plumbing credential for the area where you plan to work.
  2. Review the local licensing requirements. Confirm that the jurisdiction accepts or requires the ICC Kansas Standard Journeyman Plumber with Gas KGR examination.
  3. Meet the applicable experience requirements. Kansas law establishes minimum documented field-experience requirements for issuance of a journeyman plumbing certificate.
  4. Prepare for the examination. Study the 2018 International Plumbing Code and 2018 International Fuel Gas Code and practice the published content areas.
  5. Register for the ICC examination. Complete the applicable registration and scheduling process for the KGR exam.
  6. Take the open-book examination. The test contains 70 multiple-choice questions and allows three hours for completion.
  7. Complete the local license application. After meeting examination requirements, submit any required documentation and licensing materials to the applicable Kansas city or county.
  8. Maintain your license. Follow the renewal and continuing-education requirements established by the licensing jurisdiction and Kansas law.

State Requirements

Kansas law provides the statutory framework for journeyman plumbing credentials, while cities and counties handle licensing administration. Before issuing a journeyman certificate, the issuing jurisdiction must verify documented proof of a minimum of two years of field experience.

Field experience includes qualifying work performed under the direct supervision of an individual holding a valid journeyman or master certificate. Kansas law also allows qualifying trade-related schooling to satisfy part of the experience requirement.

No more than one year of the required field experience may be satisfied through trade-related schooling. When schooling is used toward that requirement, the applicable statutory language identifies a minimum of 930 program hours documented by a certificate of completion.

Because plumbing licensing is handled locally, candidates should confirm the specific administrative requirements imposed by their city or county. Requirements can include an application, proof of experience, examination documentation, local fees, renewal requirements, and continuing education.

Candidates should also confirm that the KGR examination is the correct ICC exam for their jurisdiction before registering. Local code adoption and licensing policies determine which examination a city or county accepts for a particular license classification.

Test Information and Study Materials

Preparing for the Kansas KGR examination is most effective when code reading is combined with repeated practice. The exam requires candidates to move between general regulations, plumbing-system requirements, and fuel gas provisions, so preparation should reflect that variety.

Start with Administration and General Regulations. At 34 percent of the published content outline, this is the largest category on the examination. Review general installation requirements, definitions, plan reading, testing requirements, and materials. These subjects can influence how you interpret questions throughout the exam.

Develop strong plumbing fixture knowledge. Plumbing Fixtures represents 11 percent of the examination. Review water heaters, water closets, urinals, specialty fixtures, bathtubs, showers, sinks, and lavatories. Pay attention to installation requirements and how general code rules interact with individual fixtures.

Review water supply and distribution. This category also accounts for 11 percent of the exam. Study service and distribution piping, water supply, pressure, and protection of the potable water system. Learn where relevant requirements and tables are located in the International Plumbing Code.

Practice sanitary drainage questions. Sanitary Drainage represents 9 percent of the published outline. Review building sanitary drainage, sumps and ejectors, and indirect and special waste systems. Practice recognizing whether a question involves drainage sizing, installation, or a special drainage condition.

Study vents, traps, interceptors, and separators. Vent Requirements and Traps, Interceptors, and Separators each represent 7 percent of the exam. These subjects require candidates to understand how different parts of the drainage system work together and how installation requirements apply to specific conditions.

Include storm drainage in your preparation. Storm Drainage represents a smaller percentage of the exam, but candidates should still be prepared for questions in this category.

Give substantial attention to fuel gas. Gas Piping represents 19 percent of the published examination content. Review fuel gas service and distribution piping, appliance-related requirements, combustion air, duct openings, vents, and connectors. Learn the structure of the International Fuel Gas Code so you can reach the correct subject efficiently.

Test Information and Study Materials (continued)

Practice switching between references. A combined plumbing-and-gas examination requires an additional skill: deciding which code to open first. Train yourself to classify each practice question before researching it. That simple habit can save significant time during an open-book examination.

Use timed practice. The actual KGR exam allows three hours for 70 questions. Practice groups of questions under realistic time limits. Timed sessions can reveal whether you are spending too long on difficult questions or performing too many unnecessary code lookups.

Review every missed question. When you answer a question incorrectly, identify the subject, locate the applicable code material, understand why the correct response applies, and note what caused the mistake. This turns errors into useful study opportunities.

Use your three-month access period strategically. During the early weeks, concentrate on learning the structure of the codes and strengthening weaker subjects. During the middle portion of your access period, increase the volume of practice questions. As your test date approaches, add timed sessions and focus on speed, accuracy, and efficient reference use.
