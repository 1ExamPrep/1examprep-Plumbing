Exam Details

The Utah Plumbing Apprentice Test Out 1A examination is part of the Utah Plumbing & Heating Contractors Association apprentice test-out program. The examination program has been administered through Prov.

The Test Out 1A examination contains 60 questions. Candidates are allowed 3 hours to complete the examination. The written examination is graded against a 70% cut score.

The examination is identified as the Utah Plumbing Competency Exam - 1A and is designed around learning objectives normally covered during the first semester of plumbing apprenticeship education.

The official Test Out 1A materials identify the following references as permitted during testing:

  • International Fuel Gas Code, 2018
  • International Mechanical Code, 2018
  • International Plumbing Code, 2018
  • Utah Amendments to the International Fuel Gas Code, 2018
  • Utah Amendments to the International Mechanical Code, 2018
  • Utah Amendments to the International Plumbing Code, 2018

The published learning objectives for Test Out 1A include material associated with Chapters 1, 2, and 3 of the 2018 International Plumbing Code, along with first-semester plumbing knowledge addressed through the apprenticeship curriculum.

Candidates should therefore be comfortable with introductory plumbing-code organization, terminology, general regulations, and the relationship between the International Plumbing Code and Utah-specific amendments.

Open Book Test

The Utah Plumbing Apprentice Test Out 1A examination is an open book test. The listed code references are permitted during testing, making familiarity with the books an important part of preparation.

Candidates are encouraged to prepare their reference materials before the examination by highlighting important text and using permanent tabs. Permanent tabs are permitted, while movable or repositionable tabs such as temporary flags or Post-it-style tabs are not permitted.

With 60 questions and three hours available, candidates have an average of approximately three minutes per question. That amount of time makes efficient code navigation valuable. A candidate who understands how the International Plumbing Code, International Mechanical Code, International Fuel Gas Code, and Utah amendments are organized can move more efficiently than someone seeing the references for the first time on exam day.

During preparation, practice identifying the type of information being requested before opening a book. Determine whether the question is primarily a plumbing-code issue, mechanical-code issue, fuel-gas issue, or a Utah-specific amendment. Then move to the applicable reference.

It is also important to understand that a Utah amendment may change or supplement a provision in the corresponding International Code. Candidates should therefore become comfortable checking the applicable Utah amendments when studying model-code requirements.

Licensing Steps

The Test Out 1A examination is an apprenticeship education test-out exam, not the final Utah journeyman plumber licensing examination. Its purpose is to allow qualifying students who already understand the first-semester material to demonstrate competency and potentially bypass that semester of apprenticeship coursework.

  1. Enter or plan to enter a Utah plumbing apprenticeship program. The apprentice test-out examinations are intended for students enrolling in plumbing apprenticeship education within Utah.
  2. Meet the Test Out 1A eligibility requirements. Candidates must be enrolled in, or planning to enroll in, a plumbing apprenticeship program.
  3. Register as an apprentice plumber as applicable. Utah regulates apprentice plumbers through the Division of Professional Licensing and requires apprentices to identify their proposed plumbing supervisor.
  4. Prepare for the first-semester competency examination. Review the code references and first-semester plumbing concepts associated with Test Out 1A.
  5. Practice open-book code navigation. Become familiar with the 2018 IPC, IMC, IFGC, and Utah amendments rather than attempting to locate every provision for the first time during testing.
  6. Take the Utah Plumbing Apprentice Test Out 1A examination. The exam contains 60 questions and allows three hours.
  7. Earn the required passing score. The written test-out examination uses a 70% cut score.
  8. Continue through the applicable apprenticeship program. Successful Test Out 1A candidates may bypass the corresponding first-semester coursework and continue progressing through apprenticeship education.
  9. Accumulate required plumbing experience and education. Apprentices working toward journeyman licensure must satisfy Utah's applicable training and experience requirements.
  10. Complete journeyman licensing requirements when eligible. Utah journeyman plumber applicants must eventually satisfy the applicable experience requirements and pass the required theory and practical examinations.

State Requirements

The Utah Plumbing Apprentice Test Out program is specifically intended for individuals who are enrolled in or planning to enroll in plumbing apprenticeship education in Utah. It is not a substitute for the entire apprenticeship or journeyman licensing process.

The purpose of the Test Out 1A examination is to evaluate whether a student already possesses the knowledge associated with the first semester of plumbing apprenticeship education. Successful completion allows the student to bypass that semester of coursework within the participating apprenticeship framework.

Utah separately regulates the Apprentice Plumber license through the Division of Professional Licensing. State law does not require a Division-administered licensing examination merely to become an apprentice plumber. An apprentice applicant must identify the proposed supervisor of the apprenticeship and satisfy the applicable registration requirements.

For future Journeyman Plumber licensure, Utah provides an experience pathway that includes at least four years of full-time experience as a licensed apprentice while enrolled in an approved training program that includes at least 576 classroom hours. Utah also recognizes an experience pathway involving eight years of full-time experience as a licensed apprentice outside an approved training program, along with other qualifying pathways established by law and rule.

Journeyman plumber applicants must also pass Utah's required plumber theory and practical examinations. Passing Test Out 1A does not replace those future licensing examinations.

Students should view Test Out 1A as an opportunity to demonstrate existing first-semester competency and move forward in apprenticeship education while continuing to build the work experience, classroom training, and technical knowledge required for later journeyman licensure.

  • International Plumbing Code, 2018

  • Utah Amendments to the International Fuel Gas Code, 2018
    Provides Utah-specific modifications to the 2018 International Fuel Gas Code. Candidates should study the amendments together with the base IFGC to understand state-specific requirements.
  • Utah Amendments to the International Mechanical Code, 2018
    Provides Utah-specific changes to the 2018 International Mechanical Code and should be reviewed alongside the corresponding model code.
  • Utah Amendments to the International Plumbing Code, 2018
    Provides Utah-specific modifications to provisions in the 2018 International Plumbing Code. Using the amendment document with the IPC helps candidates recognize when Utah requirements differ from the model-code language.

Test Information and Study Materials

A productive Test Out 1A study plan should begin with the 2018 International Plumbing Code. The published learning objectives identify Chapters 1, 2, and 3, making these chapters a practical starting point for first-semester code study.

Become familiar with the organization of the code before trying to memorize individual requirements. Learn how chapters, sections, definitions, tables, exceptions, and referenced provisions are arranged. Understanding code structure makes it easier to locate information under timed conditions.

Definitions deserve careful attention. Plumbing codes use technical terms with specific meanings, and misunderstanding a definition can lead to mistakes even when a candidate is looking at the correct code section.

Study the general regulations and administrative structure of the plumbing code as part of your preparation. Early IPC chapters establish important terminology and general requirements that provide a foundation for more advanced plumbing subjects encountered later in apprenticeship training.

The Utah amendments should be studied alongside each corresponding model code. When reviewing an IPC, IMC, or IFGC provision, check whether Utah has amended that subject. This habit helps candidates understand how state-specific requirements interact with nationally published codes.

Reference navigation should be practiced under timed conditions. Select a topic, decide which code is likely to address it, locate the appropriate section, and then check the relevant Utah amendment. The more often this process is repeated, the more natural it becomes.

Candidates should also use permanent tabs strategically. Tabs are most useful when they identify major chapters, frequently used sections, definitions, or amendment areas rather than attempting to label every page.

When a practice question is missed, determine why. The problem may come from misunderstanding plumbing terminology, selecting the wrong code, overlooking a Utah amendment, or spending too much time searching. Identifying the cause makes the next study session more productive.
