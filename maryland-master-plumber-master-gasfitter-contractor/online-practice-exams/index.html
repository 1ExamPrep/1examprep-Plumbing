Exam Details

The Maryland Master Plumber/Gas Fitter examination is administered by PSI Services LLC through the licensing examination program of the Maryland State Board of Plumbing.

The examination evaluates knowledge of plumbing installations, water supply systems, drainage and venting, backflow prevention, gas appliances, fuel gas piping, plumbing mathematics, and safety.

  • Exam Name: Maryland Master Plumber/Gas Fitter
  • Testing Provider: PSI Services LLC
  • Licensing Authority: Maryland State Board of Plumbing
  • Examination Format: Multiple-choice questions
  • Number of Questions: 100
  • Time Limit: 240 minutes (4 hours)
  • Passing Score: 70%
  • Exam Type: Open book
  • Online Practice Access: 3 months

The PSI Master Plumber/Gas Fitter content outline identifies ten examination categories:

  • Plumbing Fundamentals and Safety – 5 Questions: Plumbing principles, terminology, regulations, and safety.
  • Materials, Uses, and Specifications – 6 Questions: Piping materials, fittings, joining methods, and installation requirements.
  • Traps, Interceptors, and Backwater Valves – 4 Questions: Drainage components and protective devices.
  • Plumbing Fixtures, Fixture Fittings, and Plumbing Appliances – 4 Questions: Fixtures, connections, installation, and equipment requirements.
  • Water Supply Systems – 8 Questions: Potable water distribution, pressure, piping, and supply protection.
  • Backflow Prevention – 10 Questions: Cross-connection control and backflow prevention methods.
  • Drain, Waste, and Vent Systems – 13 Questions: Sanitary drainage, venting, and related installation requirements.
  • Special Topics – 3 Questions: Specialized plumbing requirements and applications.
  • Gas Appliances and Piping – 40 Questions: Fuel gas systems, piping, appliances, combustion air, and venting.
  • Plumbing Mathematics – 7 Questions: Calculations, measurements, and trade mathematics.

Gas Appliances and Piping is the largest examination category, accounting for 40% of the test. Drain, Waste, and Vent Systems accounts for 13%, while Backflow Prevention represents 10%.

Open Book Test

The Maryland Master Plumber-Gas Fitter (PSI) examination is an open book test. Candidates may bring approved reference publications into the examination room under PSI testing rules.

Approved references include the 2018 International Plumbing Code, 2018 International Fuel Gas Code, Mathematics for Plumbers and Pipefitters, 8th Edition, any standard backflow text, and the designated OSHA construction safety publications.

Reference books may contain highlighting, underlining, and acceptable indexing tabs. Handwritten notes, additional loose or attached papers, and study guides are not permitted.

PSI permits designated permanent indexing tabs. Tabs that can be removed without damaging the page are not accepted, and tabs containing paper inserts are not permitted.

Approved reference books are not supplied at the testing center.

Effective open-book preparation includes:

  • Learning the organization of the International Plumbing Code.
  • Practicing navigation through the International Fuel Gas Code.
  • Reviewing important plumbing and gas piping tables.
  • Understanding water supply, drainage, and venting terminology.
  • Strengthening knowledge of backflow prevention.
  • Practicing plumbing mathematics and pipe-related calculations.
  • Reviewing OSHA construction safety requirements.
  • Developing efficient time-management habits.

Open-book examinations require more than finding individual answers. Candidates benefit from understanding the subject being tested and recognizing which publication addresses the relevant requirement.

Licensing Steps

Maryland Master Plumber/Gas Fitter licensing is administered by the Maryland State Board of Plumbing.

The licensing process establishes qualifications for professionals seeking master-level authority to perform plumbing and gas fitting services.

  1. Obtain Journey-Level Licensing: Hold the appropriate journey plumber/gas fitter license.
  2. Complete Required Experience: Accumulate qualifying work experience under the direction and control of a licensed master plumber/gas fitter.
  3. Maintain Backflow Certification: Complete the approved backflow prevention device testing training required for examination eligibility.
  4. Organize Documentation: Prepare employment verification, license information, and training records.
  5. Register With PSI: Submit the examination application and supporting documents through PSI's online examination system.
  6. Prepare for the Examination: Review the approved references and study the ten examination categories.
  7. Schedule and Complete the Examination: Arrange testing after receiving PSI approval.
  8. Apply for Master Licensure: Complete the Maryland State Board of Plumbing application after passing the examination.

PSI requires applicants to submit documentation demonstrating examination eligibility before scheduling the test.

Preparing your employment verification and training records early helps create a more organized licensing process.

State Requirements

The Maryland State Board of Plumbing regulates Master Plumber/Gas Fitter licensing and establishes professional qualification requirements.

The standard Maryland qualification pathway includes:

  • Two Years as a Licensed Journey Plumber/Gas Fitter: Applicants must have held the applicable journey-level license for at least two years.
  • 3,750 Hours of Qualifying Experience: Applicants must complete practical plumbing experience under the direction and control of a licensed master plumber/gas fitter.
  • Experience Verification: Applicants must provide documentation supporting the required professional experience.
  • Backflow Prevention Training: PSI examination eligibility requires evidence of an approved, non-expired 32-hour backflow prevention device testing course.
  • Passing Examination: Applicants must achieve at least 70% on the comprehensive examination.
  • License Application: Applicants must complete the Maryland State Board of Plumbing licensing process.

Additional documentation provisions apply to candidates with qualifying experience obtained outside Maryland or through certain local licensing jurisdictions.

Maryland recognizes specific reciprocal licensing arrangements involving qualifying credentials from designated jurisdictions.

Only an appropriately insured master plumber may enter into contracts to perform plumbing or gas fitting services under Maryland's state licensing rules.

State licensing requirements also distinguish between the jurisdiction of the Maryland State Board of Plumbing and certain local jurisdictions with separate plumbing licensing systems.

Understanding the responsibilities associated with master-level licensing is an important part of preparing for professional plumbing and gas fitting work.

Reference Books

The following references support preparation for the Maryland Master Plumber-Gas Fitter examination.

  • International Plumbing Code, 2018
    Published by the International Code Council, the 2018 International Plumbing Code establishes requirements for plumbing fixtures, water supply systems, sanitary drainage, venting, traps, interceptors, backflow prevention, and related installations. It provides technical guidance for interpreting plumbing code questions and understanding system requirements.
  • International Fuel Gas Code, 2018
    Published by the International Code Council, the 2018 International Fuel Gas Code addresses fuel gas piping, appliance connections, combustion air, ventilation, equipment installation, and venting. It is particularly important for preparation involving Gas Appliances and Piping, the largest examination category.
  • Mathematics for Plumbers and Pipefitters, 8th Edition
    Written by Lee Smith, this textbook explains mathematical concepts used in plumbing and pipefitting applications. It supports preparation involving measurements, fractions, geometry, pipe dimensions, trade calculations, and practical mathematical problem-solving.
  • Code of Federal Regulations - 29 CFR Part 1926 (OSHA)
    These federal construction safety regulations address personal protective equipment, ladders, scaffolding, excavation safety, fall protection, tools, and other workplace hazards. They support preparation involving plumbing safety and construction work practices.

The International Plumbing Code and International Fuel Gas Code provide important installation requirements, while the mathematics textbook supports trade calculations.

OSHA construction safety regulations reinforce knowledge of workplace hazards and safe working practices.

Studying these references alongside online practice questions helps connect technical information with practical plumbing and gas fitting applications.
