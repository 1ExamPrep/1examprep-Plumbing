## State Requirements

Utah Master Plumber licensing is administered by the Division of Professional Licensing within the Utah Department of Commerce.

**Supervisory Experience:** One qualification pathway requires 4,000 hours of plumbing experience plus 4,000 hours of supervisory plumbing experience while licensed as a Journeyman Plumber.

**Education-Based Pathway:** Another pathway requires an eligible Associate of Applied Science degree or comparable recognized qualification and 2,000 hours of supervisory plumbing experience while licensed as a Journeyman Plumber.

**Law and Rule Examination:** Master Plumber applicants must pass the Utah Master Plumber Law and Rule Examination with a minimum score of 75%.

**Practical Examination:** Applicants who previously passed the Utah Plumber Practical Examination as part of Journeyman Plumber licensing do not need to repeat that practical examination.

**Professional Licensing:** Master Plumbers must maintain the appropriate professional license and comply with Utah's construction trades regulations.

**Workplace Safety:** Plumbing professionals must understand applicable occupational safety responsibilities, including requirements relevant to construction worksites.

**Labor Compliance:** Plumbing businesses with employees must comply with applicable employment, workers' compensation, and reporting laws.

**Code Compliance:** Plumbing installations must satisfy applicable Utah construction code requirements and state amendments.

These requirements reflect the combination of technical experience, supervision, and regulatory understanding associated with Master Plumber licensing.
