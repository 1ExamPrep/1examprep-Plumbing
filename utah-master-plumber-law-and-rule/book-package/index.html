Exam Details

The Utah Master Plumber Law and Rule Examination evaluates knowledge of state licensing laws, plumbing-specific regulations, labor requirements, workplace safety, Utah plumbing code amendments, and reporting responsibilities.

Plumbing licensing is administered by the Utah Department of Commerce, Division of Professional Licensing (DOPL). The examination is delivered by Prov.

Utah Master Plumber Law and Rule Exam Overview:

  • Examination: Utah Master Plumber Law and Rule
  • State: Utah
  • Licensing Authority: Utah Division of Professional Licensing
  • Examination Provider: Prov
  • Examination Format: Computer-based multiple-choice examination
  • Testing Method: Open book
  • Number of Questions: 40
  • Time Allowed: 120 minutes
  • Passing Score: 75%
  • Testing Options: Authorized testing centers and remote proctored written testing

The examination consists of seven subject areas covering regulatory and professional responsibilities.

  • General Statutes and Regulations: 12 questions
  • Safety: 8 questions
  • Labor Laws: 5 questions
  • Safety Reporting Requirements: 5 questions
  • Plumbing-Specific Regulations: 4 questions
  • Utah Code Amendments: 4 questions
  • Other State Regulations: 2 questions

This examination emphasizes the responsibilities of licensed plumbing professionals rather than concentrating exclusively on installation calculations or plumbing system design.

Preparation involves understanding the requirements of Utah statutes, recognizing relevant administrative rules, and applying legal and safety principles to professional plumbing situations.

Obtaining a Utah Master Plumber license involves meeting the applicable professional qualifications, passing the required examination, and completing the state licensing process.

State Requirements

Utah Master Plumber licensing is administered by the Division of Professional Licensing within the Utah Department of Commerce.

Supervisory Experience: One qualification pathway requires 4,000 hours of plumbing experience plus 4,000 hours of supervisory plumbing experience while licensed as a Journeyman Plumber.

Education-Based Pathway: Another pathway requires an eligible Associate of Applied Science degree or comparable recognized qualification and 2,000 hours of supervisory plumbing experience while licensed as a Journeyman Plumber.

Law and Rule Examination: Master Plumber applicants must pass the Utah Master Plumber Law and Rule Examination with a minimum score of 75%.

Practical Examination: Applicants who previously passed the Utah Plumber Practical Examination as part of Journeyman Plumber licensing do not need to repeat that practical examination.

Professional Licensing: Master Plumbers must maintain the appropriate professional license and comply with Utah's construction trades regulations.

Workplace Safety: Plumbing professionals must understand applicable occupational safety responsibilities, including requirements relevant to construction worksites.

Labor Compliance: Plumbing businesses with employees must comply with applicable employment, workers' compensation, and reporting laws.

Code Compliance: Plumbing installations must satisfy applicable Utah construction code requirements and state amendments.

These requirements reflect the combination of technical experience, supervision, and regulatory understanding associated with Master Plumber licensing.
