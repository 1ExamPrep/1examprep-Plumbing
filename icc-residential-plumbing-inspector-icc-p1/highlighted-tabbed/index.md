## Test Information and Study Materials

Effective preparation for the ICC Residential Plumbing Inspector - P1 exam should begin with reference navigation. Candidates should review plumbing system installation, approved materials, support, protection from corrosion, protection from physical damage, protection from freezing, testing, inspections, damage to structural members, cutting and notching limitations, and required access. Residential plumbing inspectors need to recognize whether piping systems are installed safely and protected from conditions that could damage the system or the building.

Fixture installation should receive focused study. Candidates should review plumbing fixture requirements, fixture fittings, lavatories, water closets, bathtubs, showers, sinks, clothes washers, dishwashers, hose bibbs, floor drains, waste receptors, appliances, clearances, fixture connections, and installation requirements. Water heater provisions are another major study area. Candidates should review water heater location, installation, access, working space, safety pans, discharge piping, temperature and pressure relief valves, thermal expansion, fuel-burning appliance connections where applicable, seismic support where required by adopted provisions, and related safety requirements. Residential plumbing inspectors often need to evaluate whether a water heater is installed in a safe, accessible, and code-compliant manner.

Water supply and distribution topics should include service piping, distribution piping, potable water protection, backflow prevention, valves, pressure, pipe sizing, hot water delivery, identification, material approval, joints, connections, and protection from contamination. Candidates should understand how the code protects potable water and how residential water distribution systems must be installed and tested.

Sanitary drainage is a central part of P1 preparation. Candidates should review drainage pipe materials, drainage fixture units, building drains, building sewers, horizontal branches, stacks, cleanouts, slopes, fittings, traps, trap arms, sumps, ejectors, indirect waste, and special drainage conditions.
