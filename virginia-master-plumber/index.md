It depends on the exam. Many plumbing trade exams allow the code book, but some are closed book. Your candidate bulletin lists the references allowed; if books are allowed, highlighted and tabbed sets save the most time.

### Who issues the Master Plumber license in Virginia?

It comes from the Virginia Board for Contractors (DPOR). Buying a package here does not register you or make you eligible; the board approves you first, and the exam vendor schedules the test.
