## State Requirements

Washington maintains separate certifications for **PL03 Pump & Irrigation** and **PL03A Domestic Well**, even though candidates take the Domestic Pump or Irrigation Specialty Plumber examination.

The **PL03 Pump & Irrigation** specialty is limited to installation, maintenance, and repair of equipment used to acquire, treat, store, or move water suitable for drinking or irrigation purposes.

The PL03 pathway requires at least **4,000 hours, or two years, of qualifying specialty work experience** under the supervision of an appropriately certified plumber. Washington also notes that work in this trade requires the appropriate electrical certification.

The **PL03A Domestic Well** specialty is limited to installation, maintenance, and repair of equipment used to acquire, treat, store, or move drinking or irrigation water using a pump that does not exceed **100 gallons per minute or 7.5 horsepower, single phase**.

The PL03A pathway requires at least **2,000 hours, or one year, of qualifying specialty work experience** under the supervision of an appropriately certified plumber. Electrical certification requirements also apply to electrical work associated with this trade.

Because plumbing and electrical work can overlap in pump installations, Washington maintains combination plumber/electrician certification pathways for qualifying pump specialties. A plumber certificate by itself does not automatically authorize electrical work that requires electrical certification.

Candidates should pay close attention to the scope of the plumbing certificate they are pursuing. PL03 and PL03A are related but are not interchangeable. The limits of each certification determine the types of pumps, water systems, equipment, and installations that may be worked on under that credential.

Washington plumber certifications for domestic pump and pump-and-irrigation specialties renew on the plumber's birthdate on a three-year cycle. Continuing education requirements apply as part of Washington's plumber certification system.
